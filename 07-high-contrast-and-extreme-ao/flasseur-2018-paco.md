# Exoplanet detection in angular differential imaging by statistical learning of the nonstationary patch covariances

**Flasseur et al. (2018)** · *A&A 618, A138* · [Free full text](https://www.aanda.org/articles/aa/full_html/2018/10/aa32745-18/aa32745-18.html) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201832745](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201832745)

## Summary

Presents PACO, a detection method for angular differential imaging data that learns a local statistical model of the background, including spatial correlations and average stellar speckles, instead of subtracting reference images. A non-stationary multivariate Gaussian model is learned patch by patch and detection is performed by binary hypothesis testing, preserving photometry. Tests on VLT/SPHERE-IRDIS data show that a single threshold gives consistent detection performance at all separations and that false-alarm rate, detection probability and photometric and astrometric accuracies can be assessed without Monte-Carlo injections. A fast approximate version is also described.
