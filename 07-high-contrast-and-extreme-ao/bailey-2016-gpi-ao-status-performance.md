# Status and performance of the Gemini Planet Imager adaptive optics system

**Bailey et al. (2016)** · *Proc. SPIE 9909, 99090V* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.08689) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2233172](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2233172)

## Summary

Uses science data and AO telemetry from the first 1.5 years of the GPI Exoplanet Survey to quantify the performance of the Gemini Planet Imager AO system. In a typical 60 s H-band exposure GPI reaches 5-sigma raw contrast of 1e-4 at 0.4 arcsec, with final contrasts for 1 h sequences more than ten times better. Contrast is found to be limited by bandwidth (servo-lag) error over much of the PSF, and factor analysis explains 60-70 percent of raw-contrast variance with seeing and wavefront-error metrics; the effect of higher loop gains is also examined.
