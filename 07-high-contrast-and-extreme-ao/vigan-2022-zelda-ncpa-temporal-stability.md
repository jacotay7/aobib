# Calibration of quasi-static aberrations in exoplanet direct-imaging instruments with a Zernike phase-mask sensor. IV. Temporal stability of non-common path aberrations in VLT/SPHERE

**Vigan et al. (2022)** · *A&A 660, A140* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.10470) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202142635](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202142635)

## Summary

Uses the ZELDA Zernike wavefront sensor to study the temporal stability of instrumental aberrations in VLT/SPHERE from long, high-cadence sequences on the internal source. Two regimes of NCPA decorrelation are identified: a fast component with a timescale of a few seconds and a few nanometers amplitude caused by internal turbulence in the enclosure, and a slow quasi-linear drift of a few 1e-3 nm RMS per second over minutes to hours. Coronagraphic image reconstruction shows both affect image differences, with the fast internal turbulence dominating, and the work stresses observing symmetrically around the meridian.
