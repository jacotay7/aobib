# PynPoint: an image processing package for finding exoplanets

**Amara & Quanz (2012)** · *MNRAS 427, 948* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.6637) · DOI: [10.1111/j.1365-2966.2012.21918.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2966.2012.21918.x)

## Summary

Presents PynPoint, a Python package that uses principal component analysis to detect exoplanets and estimate their flux in high-contrast imaging data. Tests insert simulated planets into VLT/NACO angular differential imaging data taken with an apodizing phase plate coronagraph. Within a common statistical framework the method gives lower detection thresholds than LOCI, with the largest gain at small inner working angles (about a factor 5 at 0.29 arcsec), and more stable flux estimates.
