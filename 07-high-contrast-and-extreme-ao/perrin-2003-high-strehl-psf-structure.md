# The Structure of High Strehl Ratio Point-Spread Functions

**Perrin et al. (2003)** · *ApJ 596, 702* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0306468) · DOI: [10.1086/377689](https://doi.org/10.1086/377689)

## Summary

Analyzes the symmetries of the point-spread function of a space telescope or an AO system corrected to Strehl ratios of about 70 percent and higher. The PSF is expanded to arbitrary order in powers of the Fourier transform of the residual phase over an arbitrary, possibly apodized, aperture. At high Strehl the first-order term is antisymmetric and produces speckles pinned to the Airy rings, while one second-order term reduces to the Marechal approximation and another produces a non-negative halo limiting dynamic range in the darkest regions. The paper shows how these symmetries, and the effects of secondary obscuration and apodization, can be exploited in instrument design and observing strategy.
