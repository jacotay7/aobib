# The Subaru Coronagraphic Extreme Adaptive Optics System: Enabling High-Contrast Imaging on Solar-System Scales

**Jovanovic et al. (2015)** · *PASP 127, 890* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.00017) · DOI: [10.1086/682989](https://doi.org/10.1086/682989)

## Summary

Describes SCExAO, a multi-band high-contrast imaging platform at the Subaru Telescope that also serves as a technology testbed for extremely large telescopes. Following partial low-order correction by the facility AO188 system, a visible pyramid wavefront sensor and a 2000-actuator deformable mirror provide the high-order correction feeding small-inner-working-angle coronagraphs such as PIAA and vector vortex designs. Non-common-path low-order aberrations are measured with a coronagraphic low-order wavefront sensor, and fast low-noise near-infrared cameras enable speckle nulling and coherent differential imaging. Preliminary on-sky and laboratory results are presented.
