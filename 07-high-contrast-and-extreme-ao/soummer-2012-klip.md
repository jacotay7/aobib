# Detection and Characterization of Exoplanets and Disks Using Projections on Karhunen-Loève Eigenimages

**Soummer et al. (2012)** · *ApJL 755, L28* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.4197) · DOI: [10.1088/2041-8205/755/2/L28](https://doi.org/10.1088/2041-8205/755/2/L28)

## Summary

Describes KLIP, a PSF subtraction method for high-contrast imaging based on principal component analysis. A Karhunen-Loève transform of a reference PSF library produces an orthogonal basis of eigenimages onto which the science image is projected and subtracted. For detection the method gives suppression comparable to LOCI with greater robustness to parameters and faster computation, and for characterization it enables forward modeling of astrophysical sources to reduce astrometric and photometric biases. Performance is illustrated with archival HST data.
