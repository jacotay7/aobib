# Speckle Noise and Dynamic Range in Coronagraphic Images

**Soummer et al. (2007)** · *ApJ 669, 642* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/0706.1739) · DOI: [10.1086/520913](https://doi.org/10.1086/520913)

## Summary

Derives the statistical properties of residual starlight in coronagraphic images and the effect of a coronagraph on speckle and photon noise. The statistical model is generalized to include static, quasi-static and fast atmospheric speckles, motivated by observations showing that quasi-static speckles are the main limitation of high-contrast imaging. The results describe how wavefront control, coronagraphy, active speckle reduction and differential calibration affect dynamic range. The study targets ground-based extreme AO imaging but applies to space with different parameters.
