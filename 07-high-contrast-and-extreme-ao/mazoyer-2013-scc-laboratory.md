# Estimation and correction of wavefront aberrations using the self-coherent camera: laboratory results

**Mazoyer et al. (2013)** · *A&A 557, A9* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.5561) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201321706](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201321706)

## Summary

Presents a method to estimate phase and amplitude aberrations upstream of a coronagraph from the complex speckle field in the downstream focal plane, measured with the self-coherent camera. Simulations and laboratory tests with a four-quadrant phase mask and a 32x32-actuator deformable mirror demonstrate stable closed-loop compensation of quasi-static phase and amplitude aberrations. Contrasts better than 1e-6 between 2 and 12 lambda/D, and 3e-7 RMS between 7 and 11 lambda/D, were reached, limited mainly by deformable-mirror amplitude defects and detector dynamic range.
