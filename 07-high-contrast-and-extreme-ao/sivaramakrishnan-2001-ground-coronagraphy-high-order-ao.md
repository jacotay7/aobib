# Ground-based Coronagraphy with High-Order Adaptive Optics

**Sivaramakrishnan et al. (2001)** · *ApJ 552, 397* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0103012) · DOI: [10.1086/320444](https://doi.org/10.1086/320444)

## Summary

Summarizes coronagraph theory and introduces a dimensionless fine-tuning parameter describing the Lyot stop size in natural units of the optical train and wavelength. Simulations of coronagraphs matched to AO systems on the 1.2 m Calypso, 5 m Palomar Hale and 8 m Gemini telescopes identify useful parameter ranges under various atmospheric conditions. AO correction is modeled as a tapered high-pass spatial-frequency filter, a representation validated against K-band coronagraphic data from the 241-channel Palomar AO system and PHARO camera.
