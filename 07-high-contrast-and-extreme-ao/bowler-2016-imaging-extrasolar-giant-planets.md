# Imaging Extrasolar Giant Planets

**Bowler (2016)** · *PASP 128, 102001* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.02731) · DOI: [10.1088/1538-3873/128/968/102001](https://doi.org/10.1088/1538-3873/128/968/102001)

## Summary

Review of high-contrast adaptive optics imaging of giant planets, covering high-order AO systems, dedicated planet-finding instruments with coronagraphs, and observing and post-processing strategies for speckle suppression. It summarizes observational results and large surveys and presents a statistical meta-analysis of 384 young stars, finding an occurrence rate of 0.6 (+0.7/-0.5) percent for 5-13 Jupiter-mass companions at 30-300 AU under hot-start models. It concludes with prospects for extreme AO and next-generation ground and space telescopes.
