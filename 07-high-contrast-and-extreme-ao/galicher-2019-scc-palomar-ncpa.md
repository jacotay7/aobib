# Minimization of non-common path aberrations at the Palomar telescope using a self-coherent camera

**Galicher et al. (2019)** · *A&A 631, A143* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.09130) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201936282](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201936282)

## Summary

First on-sky demonstration of the self-coherent camera, installed on the stellar double coronagraph instrument at the Palomar Hale telescope to minimize non-common path aberrations limiting a vortex coronagraph. On the internal source, SCC-based correction improved the coronagraphic detection limit by factors of 4 to 20 between 1.5 and 5 lambda/D relative to the standard Palomar procedure. On sky, the SCC calibration improved contrast by a factor of 5 between 2 and 4 lambda/D, showing that the technique can be retrofitted to an existing instrument.
