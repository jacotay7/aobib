# Performance of the Gemini Planet Imager's adaptive optics system

**Poyneer et al. (2016)** · *Applied Optics 55, 323* · [Free full text](https://www.osti.gov/biblio/1240924) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.55.000323](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.55.000323)

## Summary

Gives a definitive description of the algorithms and technologies of the Gemini Planet Imager adaptive optics subsystem as built, which was designed specifically for high-contrast imaging. It analyzes 564 AO measurements from the GPI Exoplanet Survey campaign, showing that the modal gain optimizer tracks changing atmospheric conditions. Science observations show image quality improves with the spatially filtered wavefront sensor and with linear-quadratic-Gaussian control of vibrations. The error budget indicates that AO bandwidth error is the largest term for all targets and conditions. The free copy is the LLNL accepted manuscript on OSTI.
