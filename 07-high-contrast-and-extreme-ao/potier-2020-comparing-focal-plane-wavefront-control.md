# Comparing focal plane wavefront control techniques: Numerical simulations and laboratory experiments

**Potier et al. (2020)** · *A&A 635, A192* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.04336) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201937015](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201937015)

## Summary

Compares two focal-plane wavefront sensing and control techniques in closed loop under identical laboratory conditions in visible light: pair-wise probing combined with electric field conjugation (PW+EFC) and the self-coherent camera (SCC). Simulations optimize pair-wise sensing and predict PW+EFC performance including model errors. On the THD2 testbed, both techniques dig a dark hole in space-like conditions within a few iterations and reach the bench limit of about 5e-9 contrast in a narrow (<0.25 percent) band.
