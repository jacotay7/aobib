# Overview of focal plane wavefront sensors to correct for the Low Wind Effect on SUBARU/SCExAO

**Vievard et al. (2019)** · *Proc. AO4ELT6 (Québec, 2019)* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.10179)

## Summary

Reviews focal-plane wavefront sensors under development on SCExAO to correct the low wind effect, which produces piston, tip and tilt discontinuities in each pupil quadrant defined by the spiders and is not detected by pyramid or Shack-Hartmann sensors. The methods compared are the Zernike asymmetric pupil sensor (already shown on sky), Fast and Furious using deformable-mirror commands as temporal phase diversity, a neural network trained on SCExAO telemetry, and linearized analytic phase diversity treating the pupil as four sub-apertures. Latest results and real-time implementation considerations are presented.
