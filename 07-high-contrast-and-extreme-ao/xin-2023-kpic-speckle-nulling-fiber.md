# On-sky speckle nulling through a single-mode fiber with the Keck Planet Imager and Characterizer

**Xin et al. (2023)** · *JATIS 9, 035001* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.11893) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.9.3.035001](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.9.3.035001)

## Summary

Presents the first on-sky demonstration of speckle nulling through a single-mode fiber, using KPIC and NIRSPEC exposures both to measure speckle phase for quasi-real-time wavefront control and as science data. Iterations of measurement and correction reduced on-sky leaked starlight in the targeted spectral order by a factor of 2.6-2.8 at 2.0 lambda/D in K band, corresponding to a similar reduction in exposure time to a given signal-to-noise ratio. Performance is limited by instability of the speckle phase, with null depth degrading by a factor two within one measurement time.
