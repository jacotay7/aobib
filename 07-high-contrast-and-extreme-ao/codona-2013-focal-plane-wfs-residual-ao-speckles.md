# Focal Plane Wavefront Sensing using Residual Adaptive Optics Speckles

**Codona & Kenworthy (2013)** · *ApJ 767, 100* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.0527) · DOI: [10.1088/0004-637X/767/2/100](https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/767/2/100)

## Summary

Presents the theory and first on-sky demonstration of measuring the complex starlight halo in the science focal plane using rapidly changing residual atmospheric speckles as probes, instead of deformable-mirror probes. AO wavefront sensor measurements at the 6.5 m MMT are used to estimate the evolving residual speckle field, which is combined with short synchronized Clio mid-infrared images to interferometrically recover the static diffraction halo including non-common path effects. In an operational system this could continuously feed correction of quasi-static errors.
