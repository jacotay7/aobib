# High-order myopic coronagraphic phase diversity (COFFEE) for wave-front control in high-contrast imaging systems

**Paul et al. (2013)** · *Optics Express 21, 31751* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.5459) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.21.031751](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.21.031751)

## Summary

Extends COFFEE, a Bayesian coronagraphic phase-diversity method that estimates quasi-static aberrations from two focal-plane images recorded by the science camera, to low- and high-order aberrations with nanometric precision for any coronagraph. A myopic estimation accounts for imperfect knowledge of the diversity phase. Simulations in a SPHERE-like configuration show that compensation with COFFEE reaches the 1e-6 contrast required at a few resolution elements, and a non-linear energy-minimization scheme reaches better than 1e-7.
