# Self-coherent camera as a focal plane wavefront sensor: simulations

**Galicher et al. (2010)** · *A&A 509, A31* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/0911.2465) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/200912902](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/200912902)

## Summary

Proposes the self-coherent camera (SCC) as a focal-plane wavefront sensor for active correction and for differential imaging. Stellar speckles are spatially encoded in the science image by interference with a reference beam, exploiting the incoherence between the star and its environment. The paper discusses an intrinsic limitation of deformable mirrors, studies the SCC parameters, proposes a design associating the SCC with a Lyot-stop coronagraph, and simulates its use with a four-quadrant phase mask for detecting Earth-like planets.
