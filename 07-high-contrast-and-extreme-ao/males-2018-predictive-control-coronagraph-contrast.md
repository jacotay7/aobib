# Ground-based adaptive optics coronagraphic performance under closed-loop predictive control

**Males & Guyon (2018)** · *JATIS 4, 019001* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.07189) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.4.1.019001](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.4.1.019001)

## Summary

Develops a semi-analytic framework for post-coronagraph raw contrast in a closed-loop AO system, starting from temporal power spectra of Fourier modes under frozen-flow turbulence and applying closed-loop transfer functions. Including a simple predictive controller, the authors find gains of over a factor 1400 in raw PSF contrast at 1 lambda/D on bright stars and more than 30 on an I = 7.5 star such as Proxima Centauri. They argue predictive control is a key technology for characterizing terrestrial planets with giant segmented mirror telescopes.
