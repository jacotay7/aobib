# High-order wavefront sensing and control for the Roman Coronagraph Instrument (CGI): architecture and measured performance

**Cady et al. (2025)** · *JATIS 11, 021408* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.23738) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.11.2.021408](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.11.2.021408)

## Summary

Describes the algorithmic and system architecture of high-order wavefront sensing and control (HOWFSC) for the Coronagraph Instrument on the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope, the first space instrument with deformable mirrors and active wavefront control. The implementation uses ground-in-the-loop operations to offload computationally expensive steps. During thermal vacuum testing, CGI achieved better than 5e-8 total raw contrast with two coronagraph architectures covering 3-9 and 6-20 lambda/D, each with a 360-degree dark hole, with limits attributed to available test time rather than a performance floor.
