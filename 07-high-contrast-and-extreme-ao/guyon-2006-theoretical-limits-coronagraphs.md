# Theoretical Limits on Extrasolar Terrestrial Planet Detection with Coronagraphs

**Guyon et al. (2006)** · *ApJS 167, 81* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0608506) · DOI: [10.1086/507630](https://doi.org/10.1086/507630)

## Summary

Reviews coronagraph designs for direct imaging of terrestrial exoplanets and develops a linear-algebra model of coronagraphy that explains existing designs and sets a fundamental performance limit. The maximum theoretical throughput is found to equal one minus the non-aberrated non-coronagraphic PSF, and the paper describes how a coronagraph reaching this limit could be designed. Performance degrades rapidly with stellar angular diameter, penalizing designs with the smallest inner working angles. Simulations including zodiacal and exozodiacal light estimate yields for a 4 m telescope.
