# Increasing the raw contrast of VLT/SPHERE with the dark hole technique. II. On-sky wavefront correction and coherent differential imaging

**Potier et al. (2022)** · *A&A 665, A136* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.11244) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202244185](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202244185)

## Summary

First on-sky dark-hole speckle minimization on VLT/SPHERE, using an iterative algorithm with pair-wise probing estimation and electric field conjugation control applied before science observations to remove speckles from non-common path aberrations. Under median conditions the standard deviation of normalized raw intensity in the corrected region is reduced by up to a factor 5 relative to the standard calibration, so that 1 min exposures reach contrasts comparable to median SPHERE performance after 1 h of post-processed observations. An alternative calibration exploiting starlight coherence improves post-processed contrast by about a factor 3.
