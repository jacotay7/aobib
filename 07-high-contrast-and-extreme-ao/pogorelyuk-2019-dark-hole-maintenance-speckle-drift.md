# Dark Hole Maintenance and A Posteriori Intensity Estimation in the Presence of Speckle Drift in a High-contrast Space Coronagraph

**Pogorelyuk & Kasdin (2019)** · *ApJ 873, 95* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.01880) · DOI: [10.3847/1538-4357/ab0461](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/ab0461)

## Summary

Addresses maintaining dark-hole contrast over long integrations in a space coronagraph when slowly drifting wavefront errors accumulate and bias the stellar PSF. The paper shows that speckle drift can be estimated from dark-hole intensity measurements combined with small deformable-mirror dithers that add phase diversity, and proposes a scheme based on an extended Kalman filter and electric field conjugation to maintain the dark hole during integration. For post-processing, an a posteriori approach estimates the realized drift and the planet intensity incoherent with the speckles.
