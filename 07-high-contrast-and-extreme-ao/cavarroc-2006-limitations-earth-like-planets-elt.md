# Fundamental limitations on Earth-like planet detection with extremely large telescopes

**Cavarroc et al. (2006)** · *A&A 447, 397* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0509713) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361:20053916](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361:20053916)

## Summary

Analyzes fundamental limitations for detecting Earth-like planets at 1e-10 contrast with extremely large telescopes using a coronagraph behind very high-order wavefront correction plus calibration and subtraction of stellar residuals. The analytical model includes dynamic phase residuals after correction, static instrumental aberrations and differential aberrations introduced by the calibration unit, assuming a filled circular pupil and a perfect coronagraph. The analysis and simulations find static aberrations to be the main limitation and derive phase-aberration requirements.
