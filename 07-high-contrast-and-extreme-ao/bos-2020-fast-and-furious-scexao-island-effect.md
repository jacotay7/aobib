# On-sky verification of Fast and Furious focal-plane wavefront sensing: Moving forward toward controlling the island effect at Subaru/SCExAO

**Bos et al. (2020)** · *A&A 639, A52* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.12097) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202037910](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202037910)

## Summary

Demonstrates that the Fast and Furious focal-plane wavefront sensing algorithm can measure and correct island-effect and low-wind-effect aberrations, deploying it on SCExAO with the internal near-infrared camera in H band. With the internal source, random low-wind-effect phase screens of 0.4-2 micron peak-to-valley were corrected to a Strehl ratio approximation above 90 percent. On sky in 1.3-1.4 arcsec seeing, closed-loop operation reduced the variance of the normalized first Airy ring from 0.27 to 0.03, and simultaneous visible PSFs showed that aberrations common to both paths were corrected.
