# Review of high-contrast imaging systems for current and future ground-based and space-based telescopes II. Common path wavefront sensing/control and coherent differential imaging

**Jovanovic et al. (2018)** · *Proc. SPIE 10703* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.07043) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2314260](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2314260)

## Summary

Second paper of the series summarizing the 2017 Optimal Optical Coronagraph workshop. It gives an overview of common-path (focal-plane and coronagraphic) wavefront sensing and control techniques and of coherent differential imaging, reporting recent results and comparing the strengths and weaknesses of each approach. The authors outline milestones needed to close the contrast gap towards imaging terrestrial planets in reflected light from space and from the ground.
