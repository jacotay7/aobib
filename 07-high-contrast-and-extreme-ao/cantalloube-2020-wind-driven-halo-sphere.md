# Wind-driven halo in high-contrast images. I. Analysis of the focal-plane images of SPHERE

**Cantalloube et al. (2020)** · *A&A 638, A98* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.05794) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201937397](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201937397)

## Summary

Reviews the causes of the wind-driven halo, a radial extension of the PSF along the wind direction (butterfly pattern) that appears when turbulence evolves faster than the AO loop can correct, and which affects about 30 percent of VLT/SPHERE data. The paper presents a method to characterize the halo directly from science images and demonstrates its effect on raw contrast and on final contrast after post-processing. Simulations and on-sky SPHERE data confirm the extracted parameters describe the halo and track its temporal, spatial and spectral variation.
