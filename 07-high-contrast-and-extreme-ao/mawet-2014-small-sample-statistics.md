# Fundamental Limitations of High Contrast Imaging Set by Small Sample Statistics

**Mawet et al. (2014)** · *ApJ 792, 97* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.2247) · DOI: [10.1088/0004-637X/792/2/97](https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/792/2/97)

## Summary

Examines how the small number of independent resolution elements at small angular separations affects detection thresholds and confidence levels in high-contrast imaging. Assuming Gaussian statistics as at large separations can produce up to an order of magnitude error in contrast at fixed confidence, with the penalty increasing towards small inner working angles. The paper presents a statistical treatment based on the Student t-distribution that preserves the confidence level and gives practical recommendations for computing contrast curves and interpreting survey results.
