# Technical description and performance of the phase II version of the Keck Planet Imager and Characterizer

**Jovanovic et al. (2025)** · *JATIS 11, 015005* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.01863) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.11.1.015005](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.11.1.015005)

## Summary

Describes the phase II version of the Keck Planet Imager and Characterizer, a set of upgrades to the Keck II AO system and NIRSPEC enabling diffraction-limited R > 30000 spectroscopy of exoplanets via single-mode fiber injection. Phase I combined fiber injection and extraction units with an H-band pyramid wavefront sensor; phase II (2022) added a 1000-actuator deformable mirror, beam shaping optics, a vortex mask and other upgrades, with y-band operation, an atmospheric dispersion corrector and laser frequency combs added in 2024. Laboratory characterization of throughput and stability, efforts to compensate static and non-common path errors, and commissioning results are presented.
