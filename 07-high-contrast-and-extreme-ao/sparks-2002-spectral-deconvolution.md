# Imaging Spectroscopy for Extrasolar Planet Detection

**Sparks & Ford (2002)** · *ApJ 578, 543* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0209078) · DOI: [10.1086/342401](https://doi.org/10.1086/342401)

## Summary

Proposes coupling a coronagraph with an integral field spectrograph and exploiting the wavelength scaling of the diffraction and speckle pattern to remove starlight while preserving the planet's flux and spectrum, a method called spectral deconvolution. The paper also discusses cross-correlation when the stellar spectrum is known. An idealized simulation gives a 20-sigma detection of a Jovian planet at 2 pc with a 2 m coronagraphic space telescope despite the planet's peak being 1 percent of the stellar PSF wings.
