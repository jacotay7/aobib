# Methods and limitations of focal plane sensing, estimation, and control in high-contrast imaging

**Groff et al. (2016)** · *JATIS 2, 011009* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.2.1.011009) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.2.1.011009](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.2.1.011009)

## Summary

Reviews focal-plane wavefront sensing, estimation and control for coronagraphs, whose performance is very sensitive to quasi-static aberrations. Speckles are suppressed using an estimate of the final focal-plane electric field obtained from probe images that modulate the field, avoiding non-common path errors, followed by a control law; characterization requires suppression over the full bandpass. The paper compares probing, estimation and control algorithms, discusses how these choices affect control solutions and contrast, and describes their practical implementation.
