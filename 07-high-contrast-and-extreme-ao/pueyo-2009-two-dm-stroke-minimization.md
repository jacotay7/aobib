# Optimal dark hole generation via two deformable mirrors with stroke minimization

**Pueyo et al. (2009)** · *Applied Optics 48, 6296* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.5111) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.48.006296](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.48.006296)

## Summary

Describes a wavefront control algorithm for generating dark holes on both sides of the image plane with two deformable mirrors in series while minimizing the stroke required from each mirror. The first deformable mirror corrects amplitude aberrations and the second creates a flat wavefront in the pupil plane. First results from the Princeton high-contrast imaging laboratory, equipped with two Boston Micromachines Kilo-DMs, demonstrate a symmetric dark hole in monochromatic light.
