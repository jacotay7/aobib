# Direct Imaging and Spectroscopy of Extrasolar Planets

**Currie et al. (2023)** · *Protostars and Planets VII, ASP Conf. Ser. 534, 799* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.05696) · DOI: [10.26624/UCTD5384](https://doi.org/10.26624/UCTD5384)

## Summary

Protostars and Planets VII chapter summarizing the state of exoplanet direct imaging and spectroscopy. It describes the instruments and software used for direct detections, lists the confirmed and candidate directly imaged planets, and reviews what imaging has revealed about giant-planet atmospheres, demographics of outer planetary systems and planet formation. It closes with an outlook on new instruments and future ground and space facilities aimed at imaging habitable-zone rocky planets.
