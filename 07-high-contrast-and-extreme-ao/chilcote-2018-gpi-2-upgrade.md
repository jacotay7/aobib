# Upgrading the Gemini Planet Imager: GPI 2.0

**Chilcote et al. (2018)** · *Proc. SPIE 10702* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.07145) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2313771](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2313771)

## Summary

Presents the proposed upgrade of the Gemini Planet Imager in connection with its planned move from Gemini South to Gemini North. Based on analysis of GPI's as-built performance, the upgrades include a pyramid wavefront sensor, broadband low-spectral-resolution prisms and new apodized-pupil Lyot coronagraph designs, intended to improve detection capability while retaining facility-class operation and enabling new science programs.
