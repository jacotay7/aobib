# Speckle temporal stability in XAO coronagraphic images. II. Refine model for quasi-static speckle temporal evolution for VLT/SPHERE

**Martinez et al. (2013)** · *A&A 554, A41* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1302.7211) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201220820](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201220820)

## Summary

Characterizes the temporal evolution of quasi-static speckles using SPHERE during its final test phase in Europe, in a thermally controlled environment reproducing telescope conditions, confirming earlier results from ESO's High-Order Test bench. Analysis of residual speckles in direct and differential coronagraphic images shows the quasi-static wavefront error grows following a linear power law, about 0.7 angstrom per minute in these data. The model can be used to set the timescale for sensing and correcting non-common path aberrations and to predict angular differential imaging gains.
