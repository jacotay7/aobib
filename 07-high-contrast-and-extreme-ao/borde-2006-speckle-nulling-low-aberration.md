# High-Contrast Imaging from Space: Speckle Nulling in a Low-Aberration Regime

**Bordé & Traub (2006)** · *ApJ 638, 488* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0510597) · DOI: [10.1086/498669](https://doi.org/10.1086/498669)

## Summary

Develops two analytical focal-plane wavefront sensing and control methods for creating dark holes with an ideal coronagraph and small aberrations, following Malbet, Yu and Shao (1995). Speckle field nulling is a fast FFT-based algorithm requiring identical actuator influence functions, while speckle energy minimization obtains the optimal deformable mirror shape by matrix inversion and allows trading dark-hole area against contrast. Complex aberrations are measured from three science-camera images, avoiding non-common path errors. Simulations indicate contrast improvements of several orders of magnitude.
