# Review of high-contrast imaging systems for current and future ground- and space-based telescopes I: coronagraph design methods and optical performance metrics

**Ruane et al. (2018)** · *Proc. SPIE 10698, 106982S* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.07042) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2312948](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2312948)

## Summary

First of three papers summarizing the 2017 Optimal Optical Coronagraph workshop at the Lorentz Center. It surveys methods and optimization tools used to design coronagraph masks for ground-based telescopes and space mission concepts such as WFIRST, HabEx and LUVOIR. It also proposes a common set of performance metrics applicable to designs, testbeds and deployed instruments, and shows how they relate instrument properties to point-source detection significance in the presence of realistic noise.
