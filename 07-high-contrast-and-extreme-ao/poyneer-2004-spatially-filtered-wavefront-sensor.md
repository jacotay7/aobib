# Spatially filtered wave-front sensor for high-order adaptive optics

**Poyneer & Macintosh (2004)** · *JOSA A 21, 810* · [Free full text](https://exaoc.ucolick.org/documents/Papers/XAOPI%20papers/poyneer_sfwfs.pdf) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.21.000810](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.21.000810)

## Summary

Because the turbulent phase is not band-limited, the sampled measurements of an AO wavefront sensor suffer from aliasing, which increases residual error and scattered light in the PSF. The spatially filtered wavefront sensor (SFWFS) places a field stop in a focal plane before the sensor, acting as a low-pass filter that strongly reduces the high-spatial-frequency phase seen by the sensor at moderate to high Strehl ratio. The paper studies its properties for open- and closed-loop correction of atmospheric turbulence, segmented-mirror errors and broadband light: in closed loop the filter reduces high-spatial-frequency phase power by factors of 10^3 to 10^8, which in full AO simulations translates into up to a 625-fold reduction of aliasing error power over a given frequency range and up to a 100-fold reduction of PSF intensity (with an apodized pupil) out to lambda/2d. The concept was adopted in the GPI and SPHERE extreme AO systems.
