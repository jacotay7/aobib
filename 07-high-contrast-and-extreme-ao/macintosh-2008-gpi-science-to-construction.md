# The Gemini Planet Imager: from science to design to construction

**Macintosh et al. (2008)** · *Proc. SPIE 7015, 701518* · [Free full text](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/945746) · DOI: [10.1117/12.788083](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.788083)

## Summary

The Gemini Planet Imager (GPI), then under construction for the 8 m Gemini South telescope, combines a 1500-subaperture AO system using a MEMS deformable mirror, an apodized-pupil Lyot coronagraph, a high-accuracy infrared interferometric calibration system and a near-infrared integral field spectrograph. Its goal is the detection and characterisation of self-luminous extrasolar planets at planet/star contrast ratios of 10^-7. The paper follows the evolution of the instrument from science requirements through modelling to the final detailed design, gives an overview of the subsystems and presents models of the predicted performance. The free copy is the LLNL preprint (LLNL-CONF-405081) on OSTI.
