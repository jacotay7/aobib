# High-order adaptive optics requirements for direct detection of extrasolar planets: Application to the SPHERE instrument

**Fusco et al. (2006)** · *Optics Express 14, 7515* · [Free full text](https://opg.optica.org/oe/fulltext.cfm?uri=oe-14-17-7515) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.14.007515](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.14.007515)

## Summary

Feasibility and design study of the extreme adaptive optics (XAO) subsystem of the SPHERE planet-finder instrument for the VLT, which became SAXO. The paper argues that the level of AO correction sets the exposure time needed for planet detection, and that the ability of the AO system to calibrate static instrumental defects ultimately limits detectivity. The XAO system must therefore correct both atmospheric turbulence and the instrument's internal aberrations, and the study derives the corresponding high-order AO requirements and system choices.
