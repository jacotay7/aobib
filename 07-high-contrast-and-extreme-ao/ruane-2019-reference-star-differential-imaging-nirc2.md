# Reference Star Differential Imaging of Close-in Companions and Circumstellar Disks with the NIRC2 Vortex Coronagraph at the W. M. Keck Observatory

**Ruane et al. (2019)** · *AJ 157, 118* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.04090) · DOI: [10.3847/1538-3881/aafee2](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-3881/aafee2)

## Summary

Demonstrates with Keck/NIRC2 vortex coronagraph observations in L' band that reference star differential imaging (RDI) improves sensitivity to point sources at small angular separations compared with angular differential imaging. For the low-mass companions HIP 79124 C (192 mas) and HIP 78233 B (141 mas, first imaging detection), RDI raises detection significance by up to a factor 5 relative to ADI, and pre-selection of reference frames gives up to a further factor 3. Observations of the disks around MWC 758 and 2MASS J16042165-2130284 show RDI maps scattered light without self-subtraction artifacts.
