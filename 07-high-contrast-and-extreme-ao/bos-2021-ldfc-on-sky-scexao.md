# First on-sky demonstration of spatial Linear Dark Field Control with the vector-Apodizing Phase Plate at Subaru/SCExAO

**Bos et al. (2021)** · *A&A 653, A42* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.06286) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202040134](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202040134)

## Summary

Reports the first on-sky proof-of-concept of spatial linear dark field control, combined with an asymmetric-pupil vector-apodizing phase plate coronagraph on SCExAO. With artificial static aberrations applied on the deformable mirror, LDFC improved raw contrast by a factor 3-7 over the dark hole and reduced residual wavefront error from about 90 to 40 nm RMS. With only atmospheric residuals and instrumental aberrations, it suppressed aberrations evolving on timescales slower than 0.1-0.4 Hz, reducing power at 1e-2 Hz by factors of about 20, 7 and 4 at 2.5, 5.5 and 8.5 lambda/D.
