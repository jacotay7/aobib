# Lyot-based Low Order Wavefront Sensor for Phase-mask Coronagraphs: Principle, Simulations and Laboratory Experiments

**Singh et al. (2014)** · *PASP 126, 586* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.7201) · DOI: [10.1086/677048](https://doi.org/10.1086/677048)

## Summary

Presents the Lyot-based low-order wavefront sensor (LLOWFS) for small-inner-working-angle phase-mask coronagraphs, which lack the reflective focal-plane mask used by earlier coronagraphic low-order sensors. Starlight diffracted by the phase mask is reflected by the Lyot stop onto a camera, and a linear reconstructor measures tip, tilt and other low-order aberrations. Simulations with realistic post-AO residuals give measurement accuracy of about 1e-2 lambda/D per mode at 1.6 microns for a four-quadrant phase mask, and laboratory open-loop pointing accuracy of 1e-2 lambda/D at 638 nm is demonstrated.
