# On-sky dark hole diggin' with implicit Electric Field Conjugation on MagAO-X

**Haffert et al. (2026)** · *arXiv preprint 2607.08146* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08146)

## Summary

Demonstrates on-sky removal of non-common path aberrations with implicit electric field conjugation (iEFC) on MagAO-X, whose second-stage AO includes a dedicated deformable mirror for coronagraphic focal-plane control placed after the science/AO beam splitter, recently upgraded to a Boston Micromachines Kilo-DM. The iEFC interaction matrix was calibrated on the internal source and used on sky, showing repeatability between off-sky and on-sky alignment. Tests on HR 4796A and Alpha Centauri in 0.5 arcsec seeing reduced the NCPA by factors of 2 to 20.
