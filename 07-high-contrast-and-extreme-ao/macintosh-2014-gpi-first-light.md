# First light of the Gemini Planet Imager

**Macintosh et al. (2014)** · *PNAS 111, 12661* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.7520) · DOI: [10.1073/pnas.1304215111](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1304215111)

## Summary

Reports first-light results of the Gemini Planet Imager, a facility instrument combining a high-order adaptive optics system, a diffraction-suppressing coronagraph and a low-resolution integral field spectrograph for direct imaging of extrasolar planets. During commissioning the system reached an estimated H-band Strehl ratio of 0.89 and 5-sigma contrasts of 1e-6 at 0.75 arcsec and 1e-5 at 0.35 arcsec. Beta Pictoris b was detected in a single 60 s exposure with minimal post-processing, and the new astrometry was used to refine the planet's orbit.
