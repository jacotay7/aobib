# Post-coronagraphic tip-tilt sensing for vortex phase masks: The QACITS technique

**Huby et al. (2015)** · *A&A 584, A74* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.06158) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201527102](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201527102)

## Summary

Proposes QACITS (Quadrant Analysis of Coronagraphic Images for Tip-tilt Sensing), which retrieves tip-tilt errors from the final coronagraphic image of a vortex phase mask coronagraph. Under small aberrations, the vortex mask behavior from entrance pupil to Lyot stop is described with Zernike polynomials, giving the theoretical basis of the method. Simulations, including a Keck-like 24 percent central obstruction, give tip-tilt precision of 5e-2 lambda/D for lambda/14 RMS wavefront error and 1e-2 lambda/D for lambda/70 RMS, and laboratory results validate the principle without requiring hardware changes.
