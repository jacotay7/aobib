# Current laboratory performance of starlight suppression systems, and potential pathways to desired Habitable Worlds Observatory exoplanet science capabilities

**Mennesson et al. (2024)** · *JATIS 10, 035004* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.18036) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.10.3.035004](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.10.3.035004)

## Summary

Summarizes the best polychromatic (10-20 percent bandwidth) contrast demonstrated in laboratory testbeds by internal coronagraphs and external starshades, for off-axis and on-axis, monolithic and segmented apertures. Each system is characterized by raw contrast, off-axis core throughput and post-calibration contrast. The results are placed in the context of the Habitable Worlds Observatory by simulating exposure times for detecting and spectroscopically characterizing an Earth-Sun twin at 12 pc with a 6 m telescope, identifying key performance improvements and trades.
