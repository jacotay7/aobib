# Temporal Evolution of Coronagraphic Dynamic Range and Constraints on Companions to Vega

**Hinkley et al. (2007)** · *ApJ 654, 633* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0609337) · DOI: [10.1086/509063](https://doi.org/10.1086/509063)

## Summary

Studies the temporal behavior of speckle noise using 284 AO-corrected, coronagraphic H-band images of Vega from the 3.63 m AEOS telescope. The speckle pattern is found to be highly stable in position and time because the AO system corrects the stellar wavefront down to the level where instrumental errors dominate, so longer exposure alone does not improve detection limits. Subtracting static and quasi-static speckles in the rotating pupil and deformable-mirror frames improves dynamic range by 1.5-2 magnitudes and constrains companions to Vega.
