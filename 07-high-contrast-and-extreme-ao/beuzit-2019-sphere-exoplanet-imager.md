# SPHERE: the exoplanet imager for the Very Large Telescope

**Beuzit et al. (2019)** · *A&A 631, A155* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.04080) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201935251](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201935251)

## Summary

Reference description of SPHERE, the high-contrast imager at the ESO Very Large Telescope, presented after four years of operations. The instrument comprises the SAXO extreme adaptive optics system, a stabilized common-path interface, several coronagraphs, and three science instruments: the near-infrared integral field spectrograph (IFS), the dual-band imager and spectrograph IRDIS, and the visible polarimeter ZIMPOL. The paper describes the complete system design and its measured on-sky performance.
