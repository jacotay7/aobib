# Review of small-angle coronagraphic techniques in the wake of ground-based second-generation adaptive optics systems

**Mawet et al. (2012)** · *Proc. SPIE 8442, 844204* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.5481) · DOI: [10.1117/12.927245](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.927245)

## Summary

Reviews high-contrast imaging at very small angular separations (within about four resolution elements) in terms of four interacting elements: coronagraph choice, wavefront control, observing strategy and post-processing. It summarizes lessons from zeroth- and first-generation AO systems and early experience with second-generation extreme AO instruments, and compiles contrast results from laboratory demonstrations for space mission concepts. The authors stress the exchange of techniques between ground and space projects.
