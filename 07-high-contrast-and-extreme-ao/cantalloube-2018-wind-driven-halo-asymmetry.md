# Origin of the asymmetry of the wind driven halo observed in high-contrast images

**Cantalloube et al. (2018)** · *A&A 620, L10* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.13171) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201834311](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201834311)

## Summary

Explains the asymmetry of the wind-driven halo seen in extreme-AO coronagraphic images such as those from SPHERE. The halo itself results from the lag between AO correction and turbulence moving across the pupil; this letter shows that its asymmetry arises from interference between the AO servo-lag error and scintillation, mainly from the fast jet-stream layer near 12 km altitude. End-to-end simulations reproduce the effect, which the authors note should be included in simulators, instrument upgrades, predictive control and post-processing.
