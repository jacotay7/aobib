# An Introduction to High Contrast Differential Imaging of Exoplanets and Disks

**Follette (2023)** · *PASP 135, 093001* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.01354) · DOI: [10.1088/1538-3873/aceb31](https://doi.org/10.1088/1538-3873/aceb31)

## Summary

Tutorial introduction to high-contrast imaging for newcomers, covering the technique that isolates light from faint planets and circumstellar disks near bright host stars. It provides an overview of terminology, observational considerations, data-reduction strategies and analysis techniques used to identify, vet and characterize planet and disk candidates, with emphasis on differential imaging methods.
