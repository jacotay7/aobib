# MagAO-X: project status and first laboratory results

**Males et al. (2018)** · *Proc. SPIE 10703* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.04315) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2312992](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2312992)

## Summary

Overview of MagAO-X, an extreme adaptive optics system for the 6.5 m Magellan Clay telescope whose main science goal is high-contrast imaging of accreting protoplanets at H-alpha. With 2040 actuators running at up to 3630 Hz, it is designed to deliver Strehl ratios above 70 percent, 19 mas resolution and contrast better than 1e-4 at 656 nm. The paper reviews the system design and reports project status and first laboratory results.
