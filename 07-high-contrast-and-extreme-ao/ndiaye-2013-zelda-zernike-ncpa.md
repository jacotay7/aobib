# Calibration of quasi-static aberrations in exoplanet direct-imaging instruments with a Zernike phase-mask sensor

**N'Diaye et al. (2013)** · *A&A 555, A94* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.5143) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201219797](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201219797)

## Summary

Proposes measuring non-common path aberrations between the visible extreme-AO wavefront sensor and the near-infrared coronagraph path with a Zernike phase-contrast sensor, the concept later built as ZELDA. A focal-plane phase mask of size about lambda/D encodes upstream pupil aberrations into intensity variations in the downstream pupil image, and a rigorous formalism gives quasi-linear, accurate reconstruction. For a 44 nm RMS static phase map, simulations predict a reduction of chromatic NCPA by a factor 3-10 in the presence of AO residuals, lowering quasi-static speckles by a factor 10-100.
