# Calibration of the island effect: Experimental validation of closed-loop focal plane wavefront control on Subaru/SCExAO

**N'Diaye et al. (2018)** · *A&A 610, A18* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.03963) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201731985](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201731985)

## Summary

Proposes closed-loop focal-plane wavefront control with the asymmetric pupil Fourier wavefront sensor to calibrate island-effect aberrations, i.e. differential piston, tip and tilt between pupil quadrants separated by spiders. The aberrations are described as independent modes in each quadrant of a four-quadrant aperture and calibrated before closing the loop. Simulations, laboratory and on-sky tests on SCExAO validate the approach in the small-aberration regime, with a 37 percent relative increase in visible image Strehl ratio on the VAMPIRES module after near-infrared calibration.
