# High-contrast imager for complex aperture telescopes (HiCAT): 8. Dark zone demonstration with simultaneous closed-loop low-order wavefront sensing and control

**Soummer et al. (2022)** · *Proc. SPIE 12180, 1218026* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.13026) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2630444](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2630444)

## Summary

Presents laboratory results from the HiCAT testbed, a system-level demonstration of segmented-aperture coronagraphy with wavefront control for a future large space telescope. The testbed uses a 37-segment deformable mirror controllable in piston, tip and tilt, two continuous deformable mirrors for high-order wavefront sensing and control, and a low-order subsystem with a tip-tilt stage, target-acquisition camera and Zernike wavefront sensor to correct drifts. Static and dynamic (with injected drifts) operations are explored, and contrast, working angles, bandpass and limiting factors are reported.
