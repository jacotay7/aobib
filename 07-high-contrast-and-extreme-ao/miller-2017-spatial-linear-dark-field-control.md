# Spatial linear dark field control: stabilizing deep contrast for exoplanet imaging using bright speckles

**Miller et al. (2017)** · *JATIS 3, 049002* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.04259) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.3.4.049002](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.3.4.049002)

## Summary

Introduces spatial linear dark field control (LDFC) for maintaining a high-contrast dark field without the modulation required by phase-diversity estimators such as electric field conjugation. After the dark hole is created by conventional methods, LDFC closes a loop on the linear response of the bright field outside the dark hole to wavefront perturbations that affect both regions. The paper describes the operating principles and presents simulations showing correction within the dark field without interrupting science acquisition.
