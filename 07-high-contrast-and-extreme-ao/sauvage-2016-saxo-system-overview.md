# SAXO: the extreme adaptive optics system of SPHERE (I) system overview and global laboratory performance

**Sauvage et al. (2016)** · *JATIS 2, 025003* · [Free full text](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/4fxff-axc63) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.2.2.025003](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.2.2.025003)

## Summary

SAXO is the extreme AO system of SPHERE, the VLT instrument for detection, photometry and spectral characterisation of giant exoplanets, and it must correct atmospheric turbulence and internal errors with very high accuracy. The paper describes the system and the performance demonstrated during the assembly, integration and test phases: first at Observatoire de Paris for SAXO alone, then at IPAG Grenoble in interaction with the full instrument, and finally after reintegration at Paranal. The targeted performance includes Strehl ratios of about 90% in H band and 43% in V band under realistic turbulence and wind conditions, loop closure down to R magnitude 15 and robustness to high wind speed, with measured image FWHM of 40 mas in H band and 18.5 mas in V band.
