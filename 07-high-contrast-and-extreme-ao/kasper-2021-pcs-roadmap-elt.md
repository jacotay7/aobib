# PCS - A Roadmap for Exoearth Imaging with the ELT

**Kasper et al. (2021)** · *The Messenger 182* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.11196) · DOI: [10.18727/0722-6691/5221](https://doi.org/10.18727/0722-6691/5221)

## Summary

Describes the Planetary Camera and Spectrograph (PCS), a planned ELT instrument dedicated to detecting and characterizing nearby exoplanets from sub-Neptune to Earth size by combining extreme adaptive optics, coronagraphy and spectroscopy, including searches for biosignatures such as molecular oxygen. The article presents the primary science goals, the instrument concept, and the research and development roadmap planned for the following years.
