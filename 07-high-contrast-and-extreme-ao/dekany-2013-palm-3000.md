# PALM-3000: Exoplanet Adaptive Optics for the 5 m Hale Telescope

**Dekany et al. (2013)** · *ApJ 776, 130* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.1216) · DOI: [10.1088/0004-637X/776/2/130](https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/776/2/130)

## Summary

Describes PALM-3000, the second-generation adaptive optics facility on the 5.1 m Hale telescope at Palomar, designed for high-contrast near-infrared imaging and spectroscopy of brown dwarfs and planetary-mass companions while also supporting natural guide stars to V ~ 17. It uses a 66x66-actuator deformable mirror and had demonstrated 141 nm RMS residual wavefront error in 1 arcsec seeing. The system can provide phase correction over a 6.4 arcsec region at 2.2 microns or full electric-field correction over about half of it, and targets 1e-7 contrast at 1 arcsec after post-processing.
