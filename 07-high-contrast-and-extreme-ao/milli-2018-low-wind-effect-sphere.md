# Low wind effect on VLT/SPHERE: impact, mitigation strategy, and results

**Milli et al. (2018)** · *Proc. SPIE 10703, 107032A* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.05370) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2311499](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2311499)

## Summary

Describes the low wind effect, a pupil-plane phase disturbance on large telescopes with spider-obstructed pupils that appears at low wind speed and is attributed to radiative cooling of the spiders creating air temperature inhomogeneities. Because conventional AO wavefront sensors do not see it, it degrades AO-corrected images. The paper analyzes the effect's strength in four years of VLT/SPHERE operations versus wind and temperature conditions, and quantifies the improvement in image quality, contrast and wavefront error after a low-emissivity coating was applied to the UT3 spiders in 2017.
