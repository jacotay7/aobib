# Speckle Statistics in Adaptively Corrected Images

**Fitzgerald & Graham (2006)** · *ApJ 637, 541* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0510503) · DOI: [10.1086/498339](https://doi.org/10.1086/498339)

## Summary

Tests the predicted distribution function of speckle intensity in AO-corrected images, which sets noise limits for detecting faint companions. Using a fast readout mode at Lick Observatory with 5 ms exposures every 14.5 ms at 2.2 microns, the observations temporally oversample and spatially Nyquist-sample the speckle pattern. The measured intensity distributions at various image locations agree with the predicted modified Rician form, and a method to map the coherent and speckle intensity components across the image is demonstrated, with application to PSF calibration.
