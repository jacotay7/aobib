# Exoplanet imaging data challenge: benchmarking the various image processing methods for exoplanet detection

**Cantalloube et al. (2020)** · *Proc. SPIE 11448, 114485A* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.05080) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2574803](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2574803)

## Summary

Describes the Exoplanet Imaging Data Challenge, a community effort to compare image-processing methods for exoplanet detection on common data sets from several ground-based high-contrast instruments with injected synthetic planets, hosted on Zenodo and CodaLab. The first phase (September 2019 - October 2020) covered point-source detection in single-wavelength pupil-tracking (ADI) data and in multispectral ADI data. The paper reports the approach, organizational lessons, limitations and preliminary results of participant submissions.
