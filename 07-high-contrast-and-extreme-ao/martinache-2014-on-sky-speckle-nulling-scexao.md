# On-Sky Speckle Nulling Demonstration at Small Angular Separation with SCExAO

**Martinache et al. (2014)** · *PASP 126, 565* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.0824) · DOI: [10.1086/677141](https://doi.org/10.1086/677141)

## Summary

Reports the first on-sky demonstration of speckle nulling, carried out at the Subaru Telescope with SCExAO. Even without a high-order, high-bandwidth closed-loop AO system at the time and in poor-to-moderate conditions, speckle nulling suppressed static and slowly varying speckles in the presence of a brighter dynamic speckle halo. The result suggests that focal-plane high-contrast algorithms developed in the laboratory can be applied to ground-based systems.
