# High-Contrast Observations in Optical and Infrared Astronomy

**Oppenheimer & Hinkley (2009)** · *ARA&A 47, 253* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.4466) · DOI: [10.1146/annurev-astro-082708-101717](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-astro-082708-101717)

## Summary

Annual Reviews article defining high-contrast observations as those requiring special techniques to detect a source near another at least 1e5 times brighter. It surveys the scientific motivation, mainly comparative planetary science, and gives an overview of techniques in use or under development, including adaptive optics, coronagraphy and speckle suppression. It also discusses ideas and studies for future high-contrast facilities.
