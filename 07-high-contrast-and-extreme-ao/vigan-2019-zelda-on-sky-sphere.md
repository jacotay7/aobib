# Calibration of quasi-static aberrations in exoplanet direct-imaging instruments with a Zernike phase-mask sensor. III. On-sky validation in VLT/SPHERE

**Vigan et al. (2019)** · *A&A 629, A11* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.11241) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201935889](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201935889)

## Summary

Reports on-sky validation of non-common path aberration compensation with the ZELDA Zernike wavefront sensor in VLT/SPHERE. On the internal source, aberrations between 1 and 15 cycles/pupil are reduced by a factor of five, giving a raw-contrast gain of 2-3 below 300 mas. On sky, closed-loop compensation reduces aberrations by about a factor two and improves coronagraphic raw contrast by up to 2.5, with a loss of sensor sensitivity only partly explained by Strehl differences. Coronagraphic image modeling shows the remaining limitation is the apodized-pupil Lyot coronagraph design rather than residual NCPA.
