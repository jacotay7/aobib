# MCAO for Gemini South

**Ellerbroek et al. (2003)** · *Proc. SPIE 4839, 55* · [Free full text](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/88247/) · DOI: [10.1117/12.459692](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.459692)

## Summary

The paper describes the design of the MCAO system for the 8 m Gemini South telescope, which later became GeMS. It targets near-diffraction-limited, uniform correction in the near-infrared over a 2 arcmin field using three deformable mirrors conjugated to 0, 4.5 and 9 km, five sodium laser guide stars with 16x16 Shack-Hartmann sensors, and three natural-guide-star tip/tilt sensors to measure tip/tilt and tilt anisoplanatism. It summarizes science applications, performance estimates, and the requirements and design status of the AO module, laser, beam transfer and launch optics, real-time controller and aircraft safety system.
