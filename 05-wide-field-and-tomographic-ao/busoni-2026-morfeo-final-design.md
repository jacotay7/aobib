# MORFEO: Advancing Towards Final Design

**Busoni et al. (2026)** · *Proc. AO4ELT8 (Viña del Mar, 2025)* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13258) · DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.21127865](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21127865)

## Summary

The paper gives an updated overview of MORFEO, the first-generation MCAO module of the ELT that feeds the MICADO camera with a diffraction-limited, uniform 53x53 arcsec field, as it approaches its final design review. It covers the MCAO and SCAO modes, the opto-mechanical architecture, the post-focal deformable mirrors, the laser guide star objective zoom system needed to follow sodium-layer variations, and the natural guide star low-order and reference sensing. The pseudo-open-loop control strategy implemented on split hard and soft real-time computers is described. End-to-end performance estimates obtained with the SPECULA simulation framework are reported as compliant with the Strehl ratio and sky-coverage requirements under median conditions.
