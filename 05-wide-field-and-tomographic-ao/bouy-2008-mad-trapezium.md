# Multi-conjugate adaptive optics images of the Trapezium cluster

**Bouy et al. (2008)** · *A&A 477, 681* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/0709.3994) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361:20078599](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361:20078599)

## Summary

The paper presents early scientific results obtained with MAD, the first MCAO instrument used on sky. Deep J, H and Ks images over a 1x1 arcmin field of the Trapezium cluster were used to search for new members and multiple systems. The images reach a contrast of about 6 magnitudes at 0.4 arcsec from bright stars, and 128 sources were detected, including 10 new faint objects. The authors conclude that the data demonstrate both the technical feasibility and the scientific potential of MCAO.
