# Optimal wave-front reconstruction strategies for multiconjugate adaptive optics

**Fusco et al. (2001)** · *JOSA A 18, 2527* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/11766761_Optimal_wave-front_reconstruction_strategies_for_multiconjugate_adaptive_optics) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.18.002527](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.18.002527)

## Summary

Derives an optimal phase-reconstruction approach for multi-conjugate adaptive optics: a minimum-mean-square-error estimator that minimizes the mean residual phase variance over the field of view of interest. It uses the Cn2 profile and accounts for there always being fewer deformable mirrors than turbulent layers. The paper shows that the optimal solution amounts to a full tomographic reconstruction of the turbulence volume followed by a projection onto the mirrors for the chosen field. This is compared with a simpler approach that assumes the layers sit at the mirror altitudes. The optimal approach gives good performance even with one or two mirrors; for a 4 m telescope it reaches Strehl ratios above 20% over a 150 by 150 arcsecond field.
