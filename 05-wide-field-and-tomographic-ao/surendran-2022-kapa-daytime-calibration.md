# Daytime calibration and testing of the Keck All sky Precision Adaptive optics tomography system

**Surendran et al. (2022)** · *Proc. SPIE 12185, 121851V* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.14433) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2628264](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2628264)

## Summary

KAPA (Keck All sky Precision Adaptive optics), started in 2018, upgrades the Keck I AO system to laser tomography AO with a four-laser-guide-star asterism, a new laser, real-time controller and wavefront sensor. The paper describes the daytime calibration and test infrastructure built to assess the tomographic algorithms. It includes an asterism simulator with fibre sources for four LGS and two NGS at the AO bench focus, and an upgraded telescope simulator reproducing focal anisoplanatism and wind-driven turbulence using a phase screen adjustable in effective altitude.
