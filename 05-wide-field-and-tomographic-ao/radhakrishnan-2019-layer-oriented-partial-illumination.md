# Operation of a layer-oriented multiconjugate adaptive optics system in the partial illumination regime

**Radhakrishnan et al. (2019)** · *JATIS 5, 049002* · [Free full text](https://www.research.unipd.it/bitstream/11577/3452150/1/049002_1.pdf) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.5.4.049002](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.5.4.049002)

## Summary

In MCAO, the metapupils at the altitudes of the deformable mirrors are only partially illuminated by the guide-star beams, so the turbulence information is incomplete. The paper reports a working solution to this problem for layer-oriented MCAO. It was demonstrated in the laboratory and on sky with the LINC-NIRVANA instrument at the Large Binocular Telescope, and the approach is reported to be simple and stable.
