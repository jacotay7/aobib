# Ground layer adaptive optics for the W. M. Keck Observatory: feasibility study

**Lu et al. (2018)** · *Proc. SPIE 10703* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.08832) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2314417](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2314417)

## Summary

The paper explores the utility and feasibility of a GLAO system at Keck to feed existing and future multi-object spectrographs and wide-field imagers, and summarizes science cases from exoplanets to high-redshift galaxies. Initial simulations predict FWHM improvements of 1.5x at 500 nm and 2x at 1.5 um. MOSFIRE is identified as well suited to a GLAO feed except for its lack of an atmospheric dispersion corrector, while LRIS and DEIMOS lack either flexure compensation or an ADC needed for narrow slits matched to GLAO image quality.
