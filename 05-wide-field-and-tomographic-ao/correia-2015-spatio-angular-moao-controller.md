# Spatio-angular minimum-variance tomographic controller for multi-object adaptive-optics systems

**Correia et al. (2015)** · *Applied Optics 54, 5281* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.07777) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.54.005281](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.54.005281)

## Summary

The paper formulates a spatio-angular linear-quadratic Gaussian (SA-LQG) tomographic controller for open-loop MOAO systems. It uses stochastic time-progression models and approximate sparse measurement operators, so that wavefronts are estimated directly in the science directions without explicit estimation of the turbulence volume, reducing computational cost. Applied to the Raven MOAO demonstrator with two science channels, the SA-LQG improves the limiting magnitude by about two magnitudes for both Strehl ratio and ensquared energy compared with static reconstruction. This corresponds to roughly a five-fold improvement in sky coverage.
