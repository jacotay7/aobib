# Etudes des galaxies lointaines et optiques adaptatives tomographiques pour les ELTs (Study of distant galaxies and tomographic adaptive optics for the ELTs)

**Neichel (2008)** · *PhD thesis, Université Paris VII (Denis Diderot)* · [Free full text](https://theses.hal.science/tel-00366529)

## Summary

This thesis has three parts. The first studies the kinematics and morphology of distant galaxies with integral-field spectroscopy (GIRAFFE, IMAGES programme), motivating the need for AO-corrected multi-object spectroscopy on ELTs. The second part studies tomographic AO systems (MCAO, MOAO, LTAO, GLAO) for ELTs, focusing on the fundamental limitations of tomographic reconstruction. Using analytical developments in the Fourier domain, it derives ways to optimise such systems. The third part applies these results to a first AO design for EAGLE, a multi-object integral-field spectrograph proposed for the European ELT. The manuscript is mainly in French.
