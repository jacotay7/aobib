# Identifying optical turbulence profiles for realistic tomographic error in adaptive optics

**Farley et al. (2019)** · *MNRAS 488, 213* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.03511) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stz1669](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stz1669)

## Summary

The paper evaluates tomographic error for 10,691 turbulence profiles measured by Stereo-SCIDAR at ESO Paranal, using a fast analytical AO simulation. The tomographic error is found to be most directly linked to how turbulence is distributed into discrete stratified layers. Standard reference profiles, built by averaging many measurements into continuous distributions, mostly give higher tomographic error than is typical for real profiles. The authors propose that representative profiles for tomographic AO design should be selected according to their tomographic error rather than by averaging.
