# Atmospheric tomography with separate minimum variance laser and natural guide star mode control

**Gilles & Ellerbroek (2010)** · *Proc. AO4ELT1 (Paris, 2009), 07002* · [Free full text](https://ao4elt.edpsciences.org/articles/ao4elt/abs/2010/01/ao4elt_07002/ao4elt_07002.html) · DOI: [10.1051/ao4elt/201007002](https://doi.org/10.1051/ao4elt/201007002)

## Summary

This paper introduces a computationally efficient atmospheric tomography wavefront control architecture in which laser guide star (high-order) and natural guide star (tip/tilt and low-order) modes are estimated separately, each in a minimum-variance sense. The architecture applies to laser tomography systems in general, including MCAO, LTAO and MOAO. Monte Carlo simulations of the TMT MCAO system show a benefit over an earlier ad hoc split MCAO architecture. The authors call for further analysis over representative ensembles of natural guide star asterisms with optimized frame rates and modal gains.
