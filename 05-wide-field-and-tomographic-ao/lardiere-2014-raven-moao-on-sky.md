# Multi-object adaptive optics on-sky results with Raven

**Lardière et al. (2014)** · *Proc. SPIE 9148 (Adaptive Optics Systems IV), 91481G* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/269320299_Multi-object_adaptive_optics_on-sky_results_with_Raven) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2055480](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2055480)

## Summary

Reports lab and first on-sky results of Raven, a multi-object adaptive optics technical and science demonstrator built at the University of Victoria AO Lab that saw first light at the Subaru telescope in May 2014. Raven has three open-loop natural-guide-star wavefront sensors, a central laser-guide-star sensor and two independent science channels feeding the IRCS spectrograph, and supports SCAO, open-loop GLAO and MOAO with tomographic reconstructors such as Learn and Apply or a model-based method. Lab results agree with simulations. On sky, ensquared energy in a 140 mas slit was 17%, 30% and 41% for GLAO, MOAO and SCAO, which the authors present as confirming that MOAO gives correction between GLAO and SCAO in any direction of the field.
