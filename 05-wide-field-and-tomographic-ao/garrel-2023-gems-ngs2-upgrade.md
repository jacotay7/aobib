# Double sky coverage, GeMS upgrade NGS2 in operation

**Garrel et al. (2023)** · *Proc. AO4ELT7 (Avignon, 2023)* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-04402846) · DOI: [10.13009/AO4ELT7-2023-115](https://doi.org/10.13009/AO4ELT7-2023-115)

## Summary

The paper reports on NGS2, an upgrade of the natural guide star wavefront sensor of GeMS installed in 2019 in partnership with ANU. NGS2 uses an EMCCD imaging the full acquisition field and measures tip/tilt and anisoplanatic modes from up to three regions of interest at 800 Hz, replacing the original sensor limited to stars of magnitude about 15.5. The performance of the new system is compared with the previous one. The paper also describes the repurposing of a Gemini peripheral wavefront sensor to track sodium-layer altitude drifts and the resulting changes to GeMS operations.
