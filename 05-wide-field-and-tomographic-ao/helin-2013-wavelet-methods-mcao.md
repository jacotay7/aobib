# Wavelet methods in multi-conjugate adaptive optics

**Helin & Yudytskiy (2013)** · *Inverse Problems 29, 085003* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1302.3734) · DOI: [10.1088/0266-5611/29/8/085003](https://doi.org/10.1088/0266-5611/29/8/085003)

## Summary

The paper introduces a reconstruction method for the atmospheric tomography problem in MCAO based on compactly supported wavelets, exploiting their locality in both space and frequency. The reconstruction is formulated as a Bayesian MAP estimate and solved with a conjugate-gradient algorithm, with an accelerated preconditioned variant. Performance is demonstrated with ESO's end-to-end simulation tool OCTOPUS. This work is a precursor of the finite element-wavelet hybrid algorithm (FEWHA) developed for ELT-scale tomography.
