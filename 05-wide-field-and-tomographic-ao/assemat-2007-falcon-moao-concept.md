# The FALCON concept: multi-object adaptive optics and atmospheric tomography for integral field spectroscopy - principles and performance on an 8-m telescope

**Assémat et al. (2007)** · *MNRAS 376, 287* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0612538) · DOI: [10.1111/j.1365-2966.2007.11422.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2966.2007.11422.x)

## Summary

The paper describes FALCON, a concept for a multi-object integral-field spectrograph with adaptive optics for the VLT, targeting 0.25 arcsec resolution and R>5000 in J and H bands over a 10x10 arcmin field. Instead of correcting the whole field, FALCON applies multi-object AO (MOAO): an independent AO correction in each integral-field unit, computed by atmospheric tomography from natural guide stars around each target. The authors show that combining MOAO and tomography would allow internal kinematics of galaxies up to z=2 to be measured with about 50% sky coverage, even near the Galactic pole. Application of the concept to extremely large telescopes is also discussed.
