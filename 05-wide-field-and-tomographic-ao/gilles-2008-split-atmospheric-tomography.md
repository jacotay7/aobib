# Split atmospheric tomography using laser and natural guide stars

**Gilles & Ellerbroek (2008)** · *JOSA A 25, 2427* · [Free full text](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/zewvt-r2330) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.25.002427](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.25.002427)

## Summary

Integrated minimum-variance LGS tomography reconstructs from a concatenated vector of tip/tilt-removed high-order LGS measurements plus a few low-order NGS measurements needed for the tip/tilt and tilt-anisoplanatism modes the LGS cannot sense. The paper proposes instead a split control architecture with two independent closed loops driven by the LGS and NGS measurements, and compares it with the integrated architecture in wave-optics Monte Carlo simulations of the TMT LGS MCAO system (NFIRAOS). Three iterative tomography algorithms are analysed: Fourier-domain preconditioned conjugate gradient, plain conjugate gradient, and a new layer-oriented block Gauss-Seidel conjugate gradient. With enough iterations all three give essentially the same residual RMS wavefront error in both architectures, while the split approach offers a simpler minimum-variance formulation, simpler control of NGS modes and reduced coupling between LGS- and NGS-controlled modes. Computation and memory requirements for the split approach are reported and judged feasible with contemporary hardware.
