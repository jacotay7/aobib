# Optimized modal tomography in adaptive optics

**Tokovinin et al. (2001)** · *A&A 378, 710* · [Free full text](https://www.aanda.org/articles/aa/pdf/2001/41/aa1401.pdf) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361:20011213](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361:20011213)

## Summary

The paper analyses modal MCAO systems that correct a finite number of Zernike modes, using second-order statistics for natural and laser guide stars. An optimized command matrix is computed from atmospheric and noise covariances to minimize the residual phase variance averaged over the field, together with an efficient method for computing modal covariances and their projections. The code reproduces known anisoplanatism and cone-effect results and is applied to tomographic estimation from several off-axis guide stars. The tomographic error grows quadratically and then linearly with constellation radius, driven by incomplete beam overlap at high altitude. With an optimized command matrix, two-DM correction is found to be practically independent of the conjugation altitude of the second mirror.
