# ATLAS: the Laser Tomographic Adaptive Optics module for the E-ELT

**Fusco et al. (2010)** · *Proc. AO4ELT1 (Paris, 2009), 02002* · [Free full text](https://ao4elt.edpsciences.org/articles/ao4elt/abs/2010/01/ao4elt_02002/ao4elt_02002.html) · DOI: [10.1051/ao4elt/201002002](https://doi.org/10.1051/ao4elt/201002002)

## Summary

ATLAS was the laser tomographic AO module studied for the E-ELT, aiming to deliver diffraction-limited PSFs in K band over a significant fraction of the sky (typically more than 60%). The paper presents a first concept of the module with its opto-mechanical implementation and the associated performance. Results show the feasibility, versatility and relative simplicity of the concept as a candidate for early implementation on the telescope.
