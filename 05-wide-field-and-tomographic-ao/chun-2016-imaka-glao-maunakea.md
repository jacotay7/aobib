# 'imaka: a ground-layer adaptive optics system on Maunakea

**Chun et al. (2016)** · *Proc. SPIE 9909, 990902* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.01804) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2233208](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2233208)

## Summary

The paper presents the integration status of 'imaka, a GLAO pathfinder on the University of Hawaii 2.2 m telescope on Maunakea. The system exploits Maunakea's thin ground layer and weak free atmosphere to push the corrected field to about one third of a degree, with an expected FWHM of about 0.33 arcsec in the visible and near-infrared. The unique design aspects, the driving science cases and their impact on the system, and the plan for on-sky demonstration are discussed.
