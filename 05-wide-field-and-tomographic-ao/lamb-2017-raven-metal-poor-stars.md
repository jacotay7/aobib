# Using the multi-object adaptive optics demonstrator RAVEN to observe metal-poor stars in and towards the Galactic Centre

**Lamb et al. (2017)** · *MNRAS 465, 3536* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.02712) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stw2865](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stw2865)

## Summary

The paper determines chemical abundances of five metal-poor stars in and towards the Galactic bulge from H-band spectroscopy taken with the RAVEN MOAO demonstrator and the IRCS spectrograph on Subaru. Three bulge stars have -2.1<[Fe/H]<-1.5 and enhanced alpha elements, and two stars in M22 are also analysed. The authors describe this as the first science demonstration of MOAO with high-resolution infrared spectroscopy and discuss the feasibility of the technique for future instruments on large telescopes.
