# Tilt anisoplanatism in laser-guide-star-based multiconjugate adaptive optics

**Flicker et al. (2003)** · *A&A 400, 1199* · [Free full text](https://www.aanda.org/articles/aa/pdf/2003/12/aah3939.pdf) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361:20030022](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361:20030022)

## Summary

The paper presents a method to estimate the long-exposure PSF degradation caused by tilt anisoplanatism in laser-guide-star MCAO from control-loop data. Tilt anisoplanatism arises because LGS cannot measure tip/tilt and the quadratic null modes must be measured with a few natural guide stars. The algorithm is tested with Monte Carlo simulations of the separately driven low-order null-mode system, using a symmetric three-NGS asterism of 30 arcsec radius. It reconstructs the PSF with relative errors below 10% in H and K bands down to NGS magnitude mR=21, and below 5% at mR=19.
