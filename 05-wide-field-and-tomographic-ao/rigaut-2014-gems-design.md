# Gemini multiconjugate adaptive optics system review - I. Design, trade-offs and integration

**Rigaut et al. (2014)** · *MNRAS 437, 2361* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.6199) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stt2054](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stt2054)

## Summary

This is the first of two papers reviewing GeMS, the Gemini South MCAO system and the first sodium multi-laser-guide-star AO system. GeMS uses five laser guide stars and two deformable mirrors to deliver near-diffraction-limited near-infrared images with uniform quality over about two square arcminutes. The paper describes the system, explains the design choices and trade-offs, discusses the main issues encountered from project start in 1999 to first light in 2011, and briefly presents first-light results.
