# Increasing the size of the isoplanatic patch with multiconjugate adaptive optics

**Beckers (1988)** · *ESO Conference and Workshop Proceedings 30, 693* · [Free full text](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1988ESOC...30..693B)

## Summary

This conference paper introduced multiconjugate adaptive optics (MCAO) for night-time astronomy as a way to overcome the small isoplanatic patch of conventional AO. It proposes using an array of artificial guide stars to sense the three-dimensional distribution of atmospheric turbulence, and several deformable mirrors, each optically conjugated to a different turbulent layer, to correct it. Using a simple N-layer model of the atmosphere, the author estimates that the corrected area grows roughly as 4N^2 while the total number of correction elements stays approximately constant. The paper is commonly cited as the origin of the MCAO concept.
