# A gradient-based method for atmospheric tomography

**Saxenhuber & Ramlau (2016)** · *Inverse Problems and Imaging 10, 781* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/306087552_A_gradient-based_method_for_atmospheric_tomography) · DOI: [10.3934/ipi.2016021](https://doi.org/10.3934/ipi.2016021)

## Summary

Presents a fast iterative algorithm for atmospheric tomography, the limited-angle tomography problem behind deriving mirror commands in wide-field AO systems with several guide stars, whose cost grows quickly with telescope size. The algorithm includes real-life effects such as tip-tilt indetermination, the cone effect and laser spot elongation, and two models for the tip-tilt components of the incoming wavefront are discussed and incorporated. The authors propose a fast step-size choice for the gradient iteration and compare it with existing choices. Numerical results are shown for two AO systems on a 42 m telescope using ESO's end-to-end simulator OCTOPUS.
