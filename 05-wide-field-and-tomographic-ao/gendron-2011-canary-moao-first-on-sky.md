# MOAO first on-sky demonstration with CANARY

**Gendron et al. (2011)** · *A&A 529, L2* · [Free full text](https://www.aanda.org/articles/aa/pdf/2011/05/aa16658-11.pdf) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201116658](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201116658)

## Summary

This letter reports the first on-sky demonstration of multi-object adaptive optics (MOAO), obtained with the CANARY demonstrator at the 4.2 m William Herschel Telescope in September 2010. CANARY was built to demonstrate MOAO feasibility ahead of multi-object near-infrared integral-field spectrographs for extremely large telescopes. A deformable mirror driven in open loop is controlled through a tomographic reconstruction from three widely separated off-axis natural guide star wavefront sensors, also operating in open loop. Comparing closed-loop AO, MOAO and GLAO with infrared images and simultaneous wavefront data, the authors measured H-band Strehl ratios of 0.20 in MOAO and 0.25 in closed-loop AO in similar seeing, with MOAO performing between GLAO and closed-loop AO.
