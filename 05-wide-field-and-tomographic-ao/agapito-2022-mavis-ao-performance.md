# MAVIS: performance estimation of the adaptive optics module

**Agapito et al. (2022)** · *Proc. SPIE 12185* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.02663) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2629363](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2629363)

## Summary

MAVIS is a visible-wavelength MCAO instrument planned for the VLT, whose AO module uses three deformable mirrors, eight laser guide stars, up to three natural guide stars and eleven wavefront sensors. The module must provide high correction at low galactic latitude and high sky coverage at the galactic pole over a 30 arcsec imager field and an integral-field unit. The paper presents updated performance estimates for the phase B design from analytical and end-to-end simulations under more realistic conditions.
