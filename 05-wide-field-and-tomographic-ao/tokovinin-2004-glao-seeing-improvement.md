# Seeing improvement with ground-layer adaptive optics

**Tokovinin (2004)** · *PASP 116, 941* · [Free full text](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1086/424805/pdf) · DOI: [10.1086/424805](https://doi.org/10.1086/424805)

## Summary

The paper develops a simple analytical method to compute GLAO-compensated image quality over the field and applies it to future GLAO systems using Rayleigh or sodium laser guide stars. It shows that even a single Rayleigh laser can provide useful correction over a wide field. The dependence of GLAO performance on the turbulence profile is studied. Most residual anisoplanatism and image ellipticity is attributed to partially corrected turbulence in a 'gray zone' from a few hundred metres to 1-2 km above the ground.
