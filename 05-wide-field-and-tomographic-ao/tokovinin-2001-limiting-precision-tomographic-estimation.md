# Limiting precision of tomographic phase estimation

**Tokovinin & Viard (2001)** · *JOSA A 18, 873* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/249334147_Limiting_precision_of_tomographic_phase_estimation) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.18.000873](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.18.000873)

## Summary

Studies the optimal linear estimation of the wavefront toward a target from the phases measured on several surrounding natural guide stars, in the context of multi-conjugate AO, using a Wiener-filter-type estimator. The minimum tomographic error depends on the turbulence profile but, contrary to common belief at the time, is almost insensitive to strong layers; it is characterized by a new parameter, the equivalent profile thickness δK for K guide stars (typically 0.5 km for five stars), and the guide-star constellation radius should not exceed r0/δK. Exact profile knowledge is not needed: filters built from a model profile lose less than 10% of field size. The paper also outlines how to measure the turbulence profile from wavefront-sensor data and finds that noise propagation is significant but manageable, so tomography guide stars should be at least as bright as those for classical AO.
