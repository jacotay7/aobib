# Performance modeling for the RAVEN multi-object adaptive optics demonstrator

**Andersen et al. (2012)** · *PASP 124, 469* · [Free full text](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1086/665924/pdf) · DOI: [10.1086/665924](https://doi.org/10.1086/665924)

## Summary

RAVEN is an MOAO technology and science demonstrator for the Subaru telescope with three natural guide star wavefront sensors and two science pick-off arms patrolling a field of about 2 arcmin. The paper presents early performance simulations with two independent tools, MAOS and OOMAO, which agree well. Trade-offs between performance, sky coverage and wavefront sensor field of view led to a 10x10 order AO system. The requirement of 30% ensquared energy in a 140 mas slit is met with three NGS and exceeds 40% when the Subaru laser guide star is used on axis, for NGS as faint as mR=14.5.
