# Multi time-step wavefront reconstruction for tomographic adaptive-optics systems

**Ono et al. (2016)** · *JOSA A 33, 726* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.02849) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.33.000726](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.33.000726)

## Summary

The paper proposes a multi time-step tomographic reconstruction that uses wavefront sensor measurements from the current and previous time steps to reduce tomographic error, together with a method to estimate the wind speed and direction of each turbulent layer. End-to-end simulations of an MOAO system on a 30 m telescope show a Strehl ratio gain of 1.5-1.8 over a 10 arcmin field compared with classical tomography, assuming perfect wind knowledge. Laboratory tests on the Raven demonstrator measured layer winds with errors below 2 m/s and gave Strehl gains of 1.2-1.5, consistent with simulations.
