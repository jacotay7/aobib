# Analysis of on-sky MOAO performance of CANARY using natural guide stars

**Vidal et al. (2014)** · *A&A 569, A16* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.6008) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201322991](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201322991)

## Summary

The paper analyses the first on-sky data of CANARY, recorded at the William Herschel Telescope in September 2010. It describes the command and calibration algorithms and the observing conditions, and uses synchronized slope buffers from four natural guide star wavefront sensors together with near-infrared images. A method to establish the MOAO error budget is presented, giving median tomographic and open-loop errors of about 216 nm and 110 nm, plus an unexpected quasi-static field aberration of about 110 nm. The experimental budgets agree with numerical simulations and with the measured H-band Strehl ratios of 10-20%.
