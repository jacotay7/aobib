# Isoplanatism in a multiconjugate adaptive optics system

**Tokovinin et al. (2000)** · *JOSA A 17, 1819* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/12294906_Isoplanatism_in_a_multiconjugate_adaptive_optics_system) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.17.001819](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.17.001819)

## Summary

Analyses turbulence correction over a wide field with several deformable mirrors conjugated to different altitudes. The residual phase variance is computed for an optimized linear correction, including a finite outer scale and the finite spatial resolution of the mirrors. In the limit of infinitely large apertures and Kolmogorov turbulence, the authors derive a general isoplanatic angle θM for a system with M mirrors; like Fried's θ0, it depends only on the turbulence profile, scales with wavelength and does not depend on telescope diameter. θM quantifies the field-of-view gain from adding mirrors and gives their optimal conjugation heights. With measured profiles, three mirrors give a 7–10 times gain, corresponding to typical and best isoplanatic field radii of 16 and 30 arcsec at 0.5 μm.
