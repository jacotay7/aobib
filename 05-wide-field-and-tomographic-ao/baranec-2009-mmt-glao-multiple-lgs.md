# On-sky wide-field adaptive optics correction using multiple laser guide stars at the MMT

**Baranec et al. (2009)** · *ApJ 693, 1814* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/0812.0352) · DOI: [10.1088/0004-637X/693/2/1814](https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/693/2/1814)

## Summary

The paper reports results from the first astronomical AO system using multiple laser guide stars, at the 6.5 m MMT. In GLAO mode, the average of wavefront measurements from five Rayleigh laser beacons, supplemented by image motion from a faint star, is applied to the adaptive secondary mirror. Correction is uniform within the 2 arcmin beacon constellation, reducing stellar image widths by up to 53%, from 0.70 to 0.33 arcsec at 2.14 um. Further commissioning was expected to deliver 0.1-0.2 arcsec near-infrared resolution in most seeing conditions.
