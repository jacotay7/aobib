# MAORY AO performances

**Agapito et al. (2020)** · *Proc. SPIE 11448* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.14484) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2561338](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2561338)

## Summary

The paper presents the AO configuration and simulated performance of MAORY, the ELT MCAO module, during its design phase. The requirement is 30% Strehl ratio in K band (50% goal) over half of the sky at the South Galactic Pole. Numerical simulations are used to assess performance and its sensitivity to parameter variations, connecting the performance requirements to the system configuration and justifying the AO design choices.
