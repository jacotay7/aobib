# Methods for correcting tilt anisoplanatism in laser-guide-star-based multiconjugate adaptive optics

**Ellerbroek & Rigaut (2001)** · *JOSA A 18, 2539* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/11766762_Methods_for_correcting_tilt_anisoplanatism_in_laser-guide-star-based_multiconjugate_adaptive_optics) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.18.002539](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.18.002539)

## Summary

Explains that, just as a single laser guide star cannot measure overall tilt, a constellation of laser guide stars at a common range cannot detect tilt anisoplanatism, and that this error alone would seriously degrade an MCAO system using one tilt-only natural guide star with several tilt-removed laser stars. The authors give a heuristic low-order model of the main source of tilt anisoplanatism. It suggests four remedies: tip-tilt from several natural guide stars, solving the laser tilt-uncertainty problem, extra higher-order measurements from a single natural star, or higher-order measurements from sodium and Rayleigh laser stars at different ranges. Numerical results for one MCAO configuration show the second option would work best if feasible, with the first and fourth also giving useful compensation. Using several natural tip-tilt stars became the standard approach in LGS MCAO systems.
