# The adaptive optics modes for HARMONI: from Classical to Laser Assisted Tomographic AO

**Neichel et al. (2016)** · *Proc. SPIE 9909, 990909* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.11931) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2231681](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2231681)

## Summary

HARMONI is the visible and near-infrared integral-field spectrograph of the ELT, designed to work at the diffraction limit with a single-conjugate AO (SCAO) and a laser tomographic AO (LTAO) system. The paper presents the system choices made for both modules, in particular the wavefront sensing strategy: a pyramid sensor for SCAO, the laser guide star sensor concept, the natural guide star path and the truth sensor. First potential implementations and initial performance estimates are presented.
