# ARGOS at the LBT. Binocular laser guided ground-layer adaptive optics

**Rabien et al. (2019)** · *A&A 621, A4* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.09938) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201833716](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201833716)

## Summary

ARGOS is the GLAO facility of the Large Binocular Telescope, using six Rayleigh laser guide stars in two constellations and the adaptive secondary mirrors of both 8.4 m apertures. Under regular conditions it reduces the PSF size by a factor of about 2-3 compared with seeing-limited operation, feeding the LUCI near-infrared imagers and multi-object spectrographs over a 4x4 arcmin field. The paper describes the final technical set-up and AO performance after commissioning. It also presents first science observations, from cluster colour-magnitude diagrams and embedded star formation to nearby galactic nuclei and high-redshift objects.
