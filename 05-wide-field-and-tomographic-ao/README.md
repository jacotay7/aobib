# 05 · Wide-Field & Tomographic AO (MCAO, GLAO, LTAO, MOAO)

Classical single-conjugate AO corrects well only within the isoplanatic patch around one guide star, typically a few arcseconds in the near-infrared. Wide-field AO gets past this limit by sensing the turbulence along several lines of sight, using natural and/or laser guide stars, and estimating its three-dimensional distribution (atmospheric tomography). Multi-conjugate AO (MCAO) applies the correction with several deformable mirrors conjugated to different altitudes, giving near-uniform correction over about an arcminute. Ground-layer AO (GLAO) corrects only the turbulence common to the whole field, giving partial correction over many arcminutes. Laser tomography AO (LTAO) uses several laser guide stars to correct a single direction and remove the cone effect. Multi-object AO (MOAO) applies an independent open-loop correction in each of several science directions.

This folder collects the founding concept papers, the theory and algorithms of tomographic reconstruction, layer-oriented versus star-oriented sensing, and papers on demonstrators and facility systems. The demonstrators and systems include MAD, GeMS, LINC-NIRVANA, MORFEO/MAORY, NFIRAOS, MAVIS, MMT, SAM, ARGOS, 'imaka, AOF/GALACSI, ATLAS, HARMONI, KAPA, FALCON/EAGLE, CANARY, RAVEN and MOSAIC. Sky-coverage studies and Fourier performance models of tomographic systems are cross-listed from folder 08.

Subtopics used here:

- **Concepts, history & reviews**: the MCAO concept, early ELT studies, reviews and theses
- **Tomographic reconstruction & control**: modal, MMSE/MAP, iterative and wavelet tomography, LQG and predictive tomographic control, tomographic error, tilt anisoplanatism
- **Layer-oriented & multi-pyramid MCAO**: layer-oriented wavefront sensing, multiple field-of-view, MAD-LO and LINC-NIRVANA
- **MCAO systems (MAD, GeMS, MORFEO, NFIRAOS, MAVIS)**: design and on-sky performance of night-time MCAO systems
- **Ground-layer AO (GLAO)**: concept, analytical models, and the MMT, SAM, ARGOS, 'imaka and Keck studies
- **Laser tomography AO (LTAO)**: ATLAS, HARMONI, KAPA and analytical LTAO performance
- **Multi-object AO (MOAO)**: FALCON, EAGLE, CANARY, RAVEN and MOSAIC
