# Modal tomography for adaptive optics

**Ragazzoni et al. (1999)** · *A&A 342, L53* · [Free full text](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1999A%26A...342L..53R)

## Summary

This letter outlines a modal approach to atmospheric tomography using multiple laser guide stars. The goal is to retrieve the three-dimensional distribution of turbulent layers and correct for the cone effect (focal anisoplanatism). The analytical framework expresses the wavefront from each guide star as projections of layer Zernike modes, and the authors discuss the advantages of this approach with respect to zonal tomography. A preliminary discussion addresses how to treat the involved matrices to limit noise propagation.
