# Improved image quality over 10' fields with the 'imaka ground layer adaptive optics experiment

**Abdurrahman et al. (2018)** · *AJ 156, 100* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.03885) · DOI: [10.3847/1538-3881/aad1f2](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-3881/aad1f2)

## Summary

The paper reports on-sky results from 'imaka, a GLAO demonstrator with a 24x18 arcmin field on the UH 2.2 m telescope. Over 15 nights with natural guide star asterisms about 16 arcmin across, median FWHM improved from 0.95 to 0.64 arcsec in R band, 0.81 to 0.48 arcsec in I band and 0.76 to 0.44 arcsec at 1 um. The AO-on PSF is uniform out to about 5 arcmin off-axis and about ten times more stable in time than the uncorrected PSF. Delivered image quality correlates with 'imaka telemetry and, more weakly, with DIMM and MASS seeing measurements.
