# Fast optimal wavefront reconstruction for multi-conjugate adaptive optics using the Fourier domain preconditioned conjugate gradient algorithm

**Vogel & Yang (2006)** · *Optics Express 14, 7487* · [Free full text](https://opg.optica.org/oe/fulltext.cfm?uri=oe-14-17-7487) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.14.007487](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.14.007487)

## Summary

The paper presents two implementations of the Fourier-domain preconditioned conjugate gradient (FD-PCG) algorithm for solving the large structured linear systems of minimum-variance atmospheric tomography in MCAO. It describes how to handle technical issues such as the cone coordinate transformation for laser guide stars and the subaperture grid spacing of the sensors. The FD-PCG approach is also extended to the deformable mirror fitting step of MCAO.
