# Adaptive-optics corrections available for the whole sky

**Ragazzoni et al. (2000)** · *Nature 403, 54* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/12675228_Adaptive-optics_corrections_available_for_the_whole_sky) · DOI: [10.1038/47425](https://doi.org/10.1038/47425)

## Summary

Reports an experimental on-sky test of atmospheric tomography with multiple guide stars, using three off-axis reference stars about 15 arcsec from a central star. Wavefronts measured on the off-axis stars were used to estimate the wavefront of the central star, and this estimate was compared with the wavefront measured on the central star directly. The tomographic estimate reduced the wavefront variations by about 92%. The authors conclude that this removes a serious barrier to extending adaptive optics correction to the whole sky using multiple guide stars.
