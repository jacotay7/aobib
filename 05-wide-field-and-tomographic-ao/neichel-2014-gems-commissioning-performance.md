# Gemini multiconjugate adaptive optics system review - II. Commissioning, operation and overall performance

**Neichel et al. (2014)** · *MNRAS 440, 1002* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.6906) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stu403](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stu403)

## Summary

The second GeMS review paper presents the commissioning, performance and operation of the Gemini South MCAO system. GeMS uses five laser guide stars on a 60 arcsec square constellation and two deformable mirrors to deliver near-diffraction-limited images between 0.95 and 2.5 um over a 120 arcsec field. The performance of each subsystem is derived from commissioning data, and typical FWHM, Strehl ratio and their variation over the field are reported. The main contributors limiting performance are discussed, along with observing overheads and planned upgrades.
