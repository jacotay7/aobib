# Wave-front error breakdown in laser guide star multi-object adaptive optics validated on-sky by Canary

**Martin et al. (2017)** · *A&A 598, A37* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.04704) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201629271](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201629271)

## Summary

The paper establishes a wavefront error breakdown for laser guide star MOAO using CANARY data from 2013, with three off-axis natural guide stars and four off-axis Rayleigh laser guide stars in open loop and a truth sensor on axis. On-sky results in MOAO, SCAO and GLAO modes are compared over a large data set, with a statistical study of seeing. The error breakdown is computed from an MOAO system model and from truth-sensor measurements, with a vertical decomposition of the tomographic error. CANARY obtained H-band Strehl ratios of 30.1%, 21.4% and 17.1% in SCAO, MOAO and GLAO respectively for median seeing, and the two analytic error estimates are highly correlated.
