# NFIRAOS

**Herriot et al. (2017)** · *Proc. AO4ELT5 (Tenerife, 2017)* · [Free full text](https://meetings.iac.es/AO4ELT5/media/proceedings/proceeding-129.pdf) · DOI: [10.26698/ao4elt5.0129](https://doi.org/10.26698/ao4elt5.0129)

## Summary

This paper describes NFIRAOS, the facility MCAO system for the Thirty Meter Telescope serving three client instruments. NFIRAOS has two deformable mirrors and six laser guide star wavefront sensors. It uses up to three low-order infrared wavefront sensors on each instrument, plus guide windows on the science detector, to correct turbulence, windshake, optical errors and plate-scale distortion. The system is cooled to -30 C, and the paper reports the design status, including the Xeon-based real-time computer approaching final design review.
