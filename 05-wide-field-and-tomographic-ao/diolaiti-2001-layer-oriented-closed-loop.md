# Closed loop performance of a layer-oriented multi-conjugate adaptive optics system

**Diolaiti et al. (2001)** · *A&A 372, 710* · [Free full text](https://www.aanda.org/articles/aa/pdf/2001/23/aa10630.pdf) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361:20010514](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361:20010514)

## Summary

The paper examines the dynamic behaviour of a layer-oriented MCAO system, in which light from several guide stars is optically co-added on detectors conjugated to the turbulent layers. It shows that such a system is stable and closes the loop. A simplified analysis of the achievable correction defines the conditions under which the layer-oriented approach is equivalent to other linear reconstruction schemes. Under certain assumptions it is found to be equivalent to an optimal reconstruction while retaining the practical advantages of the layer-oriented scheme.
