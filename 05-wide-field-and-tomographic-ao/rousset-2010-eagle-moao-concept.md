# EAGLE multi-object AO concept study for the E-ELT

**Rousset et al. (2010)** · *Proc. AO4ELT1 (Paris, 2009), 02008* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1002.2077) · DOI: [10.1051/ao4elt/201002008](https://doi.org/10.1051/ao4elt/201002008)

## Summary

EAGLE was the multi-object, spatially resolved near-infrared spectrograph concept for the E-ELT, relying on MOAO. The paper presents the AO results of its phase A study. With 84x84-actuator deformable mirrors, six laser guide stars and up to five natural guide stars (R<17) in a 7.3 arcmin patrol field, the analysis predicts 35% ensquared energy in a 75x75 mas spaxel in H band anywhere in the central 5 arcmin science field under median seeing. The computed sky coverage at galactic latitudes around 60 degrees is close to 90%.
