# Wide field adaptive optics laboratory demonstration with closed-loop tomographic control

**Costille et al. (2010)** · *JOSA A 27, 469* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-04511082) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.27.000469](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.27.000469)

## Summary

HOMER, a wide-field AO bench at ONERA, was used for the first experimental validations of MCAO and LTAO with a linear quadratic Gaussian (LQG) control approach. Closed-loop LTAO results show a significant performance gain from LQG control, which performs the tomographic reconstruction. The paper presents a calibration and model identification strategy, and the experimental results are consistent with end-to-end simulations. Performance is found to be robust against experimental uncertainties.
