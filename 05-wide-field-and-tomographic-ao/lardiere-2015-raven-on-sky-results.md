# On-sky results of Raven, a MOAO science demonstrator at Subaru Telescope

**Lardière et al. (2015)** · *Proc. AO4ELT4 (Los Angeles, 2015)* · [Free full text](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/9xx3208w) · DOI: [10.20353/K3T4CP1131703](https://doi.org/10.20353/K3T4CP1131703)

## Summary

Raven is an MOAO science demonstrator used on sky at the Subaru telescope from May 2014 to July 2015, developed by the University of Victoria AO Lab with NRC, NAOJ and Tohoku University. It has three open-loop wavefront sensors, a central laser guide star sensor and two science pick-off arms feeding the IRCS spectrograph, and supports SCAO, open-loop GLAO and MOAO modes. The paper gives an overview of the instrument design, compares the on-sky performance of the different AO modes and presents science results obtained with MOAO.
