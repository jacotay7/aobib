# Multiple field of view layer-oriented adaptive optics. Nearly whole sky coverage on 8 m class telescopes and beyond

**Ragazzoni et al. (2002)** · *A&A 396, 731* · [Free full text](https://www.aanda.org/articles/aa/pdf/2002/47/aa1848.pdf) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361:20021406](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361:20021406)

## Summary

The paper introduces multiple field of view (MFoV) layer-oriented adaptive optics. In layer-oriented wavefront sensing, multiconjugation is more efficient than conventional sensing, especially for high-altitude layers, but high layers can only be sensed over a limited field. The MFoV concept senses the ground layer using guide stars from a large annular field and the high layers with stars in a smaller field. The authors show that this combination of sampling and field coverage yields sky coverage approaching the whole sky on 8 m class telescopes using only natural guide stars, and discuss extension to larger apertures.
