# Multiconjugate Adaptive Optics for Astronomy

**Rigaut & Neichel (2018)** · *ARA&A 56, 277* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.03097) · DOI: [10.1146/annurev-astro-091916-055320](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-astro-091916-055320)

## Summary

This Annual Review article summarizes multiconjugate adaptive optics (MCAO), which uses several wavefront sensors, tomographic phase reconstruction and multiple deformable mirrors to deliver near-uniform correction over fields larger than one square arcminute. After recalling AO principles and limitations, it covers aspects specific to MCAO such as tomography and MCAO-specific error sources. Results from MAD, GeMS and the solar MCAO system at Big Bear are presented, including Strehl ratios up to about 40% in H band and FWHM down to about 52 mas. Photometric and astrometric accuracy in MCAO images are discussed, and the review closes with the prospects of MCAO for extremely large telescopes.
