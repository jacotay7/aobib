# On-sky testing of the Multi-Conjugate Adaptive Optics Demonstrator

**Marchetti et al. (2007)** · *The Messenger 129, 8* · [Free full text](https://www.eso.org/sci/publications/messenger/archive/no.129-sep07/messenger-no129-8-13.pdf)

## Summary

This article presents ESO's Multi-Conjugate Adaptive Optics Demonstrator (MAD) and its first on-sky tests at the VLT (UT3, Melipal) in 2007. MAD uses two deformable mirrors and can operate with either a star-oriented Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensor or a layer-oriented pyramid sensor, using several natural guide stars over a 2 arcmin field. The article describes the instrument and reports the correction performance over the field obtained in star-oriented mode. MAD was built as a pathfinder for wide-field AO on second-generation VLT instruments and the E-ELT.
