# Experimental results of ground-layer and tomographic wavefront reconstruction from multiple laser guide stars

**Lloyd-Hart et al. (2006)** · *Optics Express 14, 7541* · [Free full text](https://opg.optica.org/oe/fulltext.cfm?uri=oe-14-17-7541) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.14.007541](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.14.007541)

## Summary

The paper describes the first multi-laser wavefront sensing system designed for tomographic AO, at the 6.5 m MMT. Five Rayleigh beacons at 532 nm are range-gated between 20 and 29 km with dynamic refocus and imaged on a single Shack-Hartmann detector, while a separate sensor measures a natural star simultaneously. Open-loop data show that the average of the beacon wavefronts gives a good estimate of the ground-layer aberration. Full tomographic wavefront analysis is presented using additional image-motion measurements from three field stars, and plans for closed-loop GLAO and tomographic AO at the MMT are described.
