# SOAR Adaptive Module (SAM): seeing improvement with a UV laser

**Tokovinin et al. (2016)** · *PASP 128, 125003* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.05593) · DOI: [10.1088/1538-3873/128/970/125003](https://doi.org/10.1088/1538-3873/128/970/125003)

## Summary

SAM is the ground-layer AO module of the 4.1 m SOAR telescope, correcting low-altitude turbulence using a UV Rayleigh laser guide star. Commissioned in 2013 and in regular science operation since 2014, it feeds a CCD imager covering a 3 arcmin field or a speckle camera. SAM routinely delivers I-band resolution equal to the free-atmosphere seeing. The paper describes the system as a reference for users and instrumentalists and reviews its operation, performance and science programmes.
