# Effect of deformable mirror sequence on performance of multiconjugate adaptive optics

**van Dam et al. (2023)** · *Proc. AO4ELT7 (Avignon, 2023)* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-04419528) · DOI: [10.13009/AO4ELT7-2023-002](https://doi.org/10.13009/AO4ELT7-2023-002)

## Summary

The paper studies the optimal ordering of deformable mirrors along the optical path of an MCAO system. Analytical calculations and Fresnel-propagation simulations show that scintillation arises from spatial frequencies higher than high-altitude DMs can correct, so it is not affected by the DM sequence. End-to-end simulations show that placing the ground-layer DM last minimizes dynamic misregistration between DMs and wavefront sensors, improving loop stability and reducing wavefront error. The authors recommend ordering the DMs from highest to lowest conjugate altitude.
