# Towards the phase A review of MAORY, the multi-conjugate adaptive optics module for the E-ELT

**Diolaiti et al. (2010)** · *Proc. AO4ELT1 (Paris, 2009), 02007* · [Free full text](https://ao4elt.edpsciences.org/articles/ao4elt/abs/2010/01/ao4elt_02007/ao4elt_02007.html) · DOI: [10.1051/ao4elt/201002007](https://doi.org/10.1051/ao4elt/201002007)

## Summary

This paper describes the conceptual design of MAORY, the multi-conjugate adaptive optics module for the European ELT, as the phase A study approached its final review. It presents the optical and system design, including laser and natural guide star wavefront sensing and post-focal deformable mirrors working together with the telescope's adaptive mirror. Expected performance over the corrected field is also presented.
