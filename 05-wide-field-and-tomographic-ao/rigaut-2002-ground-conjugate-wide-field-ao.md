# Ground-conjugate wide field adaptive optics for the ELTs

**Rigaut (2002)** · *ESO Conference and Workshop Proceedings 58, 11* · [Free full text](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/2002ESOC...58...11R)

## Summary

This conference paper (Venice 2001, 'Beyond Conventional Adaptive Optics') presents the concept of ground-conjugate wide-field AO, now called ground-layer AO (GLAO). A single deformable mirror conjugated near the ground corrects the turbulence common to all directions, giving partial but uniform correction over a field of 10-20 arcmin. Using an analytical phase power spectral density model and Cerro Pachon balloon profiles, first performance estimates show significant FWHM reductions with respect to natural seeing. A GLAO system for a 30 m ELT is sketched.
