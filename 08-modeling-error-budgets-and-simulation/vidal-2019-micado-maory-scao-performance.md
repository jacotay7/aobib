# Analysis the MICADO-MAORY SCAO performance

**Vidal et al. (2019)** · *Proc. AO4ELT6 (Québec, 2019)* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-03020241)

## Summary

The paper reports end-to-end simulation studies of the single-conjugate AO mode shared by MICADO and MAORY at the ELT, which led to selecting a visible pyramid wavefront sensor as baseline. Developments implemented in COMPASS for fast, accurate ELT-scale simulation are described, including a diffractive pyramid model, ELT pupil, M4 geometry and influence functions and telescope perturbations. Pyramid-specific calibrations (modal basis, optical gains, NCPA, island effect) are explored, and detailed error budgets are given for five turbulence conditions and guide star magnitudes from 10 to 17, along with the case of extended objects such as double stars or Titan.
