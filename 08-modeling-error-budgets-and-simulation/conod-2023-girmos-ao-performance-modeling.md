# The Adaptive Optics System for the Gemini Infrared Multi-Object Spectrograph: Performance Modeling

**Conod et al. (2023)** · *PASP 135, 105001* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.15338) · DOI: [10.1088/1538-3873/acf61c](https://doi.org/10.1088/1538-3873/acf61c)

## Summary

GIRMOS is a near-infrared multi-object integral-field spectrograph for Gemini North that will add open-loop multi-object AO correction in each of its four arms on top of the ground-layer correction of the Gemini North AO system (GNAO). The paper presents the baseline parameters and simulated performance obtained by modeling both GNAO and GIRMOS. After trade-offs between performance, risk and cost, a 16x16 AO order was selected. The requirement of 57% ensquared energy within 0.1x0.1 arcsec at 2.0 um is met in median Maunakea conditions at 30 degrees from zenith.
