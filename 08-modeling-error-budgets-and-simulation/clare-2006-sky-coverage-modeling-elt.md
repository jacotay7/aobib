# Adaptive optics sky coverage modeling for extremely large telescopes

**Clare et al. (2006)** · *Applied Optics 45, 8964* · [Free full text](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/k4xz2-6g552) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.45.008964](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.45.008964)

## Summary

The paper refines a Monte Carlo sky coverage model for laser guide star AO systems by adding natural guide star statistics from published star count models, NGS measurement noise, telescope wind shake, the field variation of Strehl (and hence NGS sensor noise), sodium-layer range-tracking focus error, the mechanical bandwidths of the tip-tilt stage and DM, and temporal filtering of NGS measurements. The model is used to derive a tip-tilt error budget and design trades for the TMT NFIRAOS system. With the design at the time, the median tip-tilt error at the galactic pole in median seeing is computed to be 65 nm, or 1.8 mas rms.
