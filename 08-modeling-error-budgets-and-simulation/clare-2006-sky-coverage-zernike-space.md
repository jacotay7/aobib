# Sky coverage estimates for adaptive optics systems from computations in Zernike space

**Clare & Ellerbroek (2006)** · *JOSA A 23, 418* · [Free full text](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/edxgp-v1533) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.23.000418](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.23.000418)

## Summary

The paper proposes a sky coverage model for laser guide star AO systems in which the atmosphere is represented by a finite number of phase screens at different altitudes, each expanded in Zernike polynomials. The screens are transformed to the aperture plane and converted into LGS and NGS wavefront-sensor measurements, including the cone effect for guide stars at finite height, anisoplanatism for off-axis guide stars, and systems with multiple guide stars. The residual wavefront error is computed tomographically with minimum-variance estimators built from these transformation matrices and the known turbulence statistics, enabling fast Monte Carlo simulations over random NGS asterisms independent of telescope diameter. Example simulations show that a finite outer scale significantly reduces the median wavefront error, that adding LGSs reduces it, and that a larger NGS patrol field helps when the star density is low.
