# Modelling Fourier filtering wavefront sensors for PSD-based methods: the AOPERA tool

**Fétick et al. (2026)** · *arXiv:2609.10042* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10042)

## Summary

PSD-based AO models are fast and give a full error breakdown and focal-plane impact, but have difficulty describing non-linear wavefront sensors such as the pyramid or Zernike sensor. The paper presents a method, implemented in the Python tool AOPERA, to compute the residual phase power spectral density and PSF of an AO system including the sensitivity and non-linear behaviour of Fourier-filtering wavefront sensors. After a mathematical description, the implementation is compared with end-to-end simulations performed with OOPAO, showing similar fitting, temporal and noise error terms.
