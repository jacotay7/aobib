# Simulating METIS' SCAO System

**Feldt et al. (2023)** · *Proc. AO4ELT7 (Avignon, 2023)* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-04440473) · DOI: [10.13009/AO4ELT7-2023-007](https://doi.org/10.13009/AO4ELT7-2023-007)

## Summary

METIS, the mid-infrared ELT imager and spectrograph, is supported by a single-conjugate AO system with a 90x90 pyramid wavefront sensor working in H and K bands. The paper describes the COMPASS-based simulation environment used during the preliminary and final design phases to support the sensing, reconstruction and control concept. It presents the performance metrics, the main results, and special cases including non-common path aberrations, water vapour seeing and the low-wind effect.
