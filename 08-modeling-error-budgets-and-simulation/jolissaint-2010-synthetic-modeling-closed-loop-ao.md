# Synthetic Modeling of Astronomical Closed Loop Adaptive Optics

**Jolissaint (2010)** · *J. Eur. Opt. Soc. Rapid Publ. 5, 10055* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1009.1581) · DOI: [10.2971/jeos.2010.10055](https://doi.org/10.2971/jeos.2010.10055)

## Summary

The paper presents an analytical model of a single natural guide star AO system operating in closed loop, used to simulate the long-exposure PSF with the spatial-frequency (Fourier) approach and complementing an earlier open-loop model. All classical error terms are included: deformable-mirror fitting error, wavefront-sensor spatial aliasing and noise, and the correlated anisoplanatic and servo-lag errors. The model includes the deformable-mirror spatial transfer function, allows the actuator geometry to differ from the lenslet geometry, and accounts for dispersion between sensing and correction wavelengths. Intended applications are system design, science case analysis and AO data reduction; the formalism underlies the PAOLA modeling code.
