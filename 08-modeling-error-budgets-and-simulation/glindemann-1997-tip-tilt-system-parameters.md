# Relevant Parameters for Tip-Tilt Systems of Large Telescopes

**Glindemann (1997)** · *PASP 109, 682* · [Free full text](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1997PASP..109..682G) · DOI: [10.1086/133932](https://doi.org/10.1086/133932)

## Summary

Derives analytic expressions for the aberrations remaining after tip-tilt correction as a function of the tracking algorithm and tracking frequency, to provide design criteria for tip-tilt systems on large telescopes. It proposes a definition of the required tip-tilt bandwidth based on the residual high-order aberrations, and a simple method to remove the influence of coma on the image-centroid measurement. The effect of science-camera pixel size on image quality is examined, an approximation to the image-motion power spectrum is given and compared with measured spectra, and it is shown that rms image motion must be measured over sufficiently long periods (more than 10 minutes). The parameters are applied in a companion paper to the CHARM tip-tilt system at Calar Alto.
