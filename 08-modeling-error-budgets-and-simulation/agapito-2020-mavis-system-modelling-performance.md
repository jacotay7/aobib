# MAVIS: system modelling and performance prediction

**Agapito et al. (2020)** · *Proc. SPIE 11448* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.14487) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2561252](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2561252)

## Summary

MAVIS is a visible MCAO instrument for the VLT aiming at 15% Strehl in V band over a 30 arcsec field and sky coverage above 50% at the south galactic pole using up to three natural guide stars as faint as H=19. The paper reports the phase-A modeling work that combined analytical (Fourier-based) and end-to-end simulations to explore the MCAO parameter space. Baseline performance, a statistical sky coverage analysis and parameter sensitivity studies are presented.
