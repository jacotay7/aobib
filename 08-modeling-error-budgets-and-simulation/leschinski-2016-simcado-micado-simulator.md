# SimCADO: an instrument data simulator package for MICADO at the E-ELT

**Leschinski et al. (2016)** · *Proc. SPIE 9911, 991124* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.01480) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2232483](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2232483)

## Summary

Introduces SimCADO, a Python instrument data simulator for MICADO, the first-light diffraction-limited near-infrared imager of the ELT (about 7 mas resolution at 1.2 microns over a field of about 53 arcsec). SimCADO combines results of the consortium's detailed work-package simulations, including AO PSFs, into a model of the optical path from source to detector readout so that science and instrument teams can generate realistic simulated observations. The paper gives an overview of the package's structure and plans for further development; SimCADO later evolved into the general ScopeSim framework.
