# Sky coverage estimation for multiconjugate adaptive optics systems: strategies and results

**Fusco et al. (2006)** · *MNRAS 370, 174* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-00398160) · DOI: [10.1111/j.1365-2966.2006.10448.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2966.2006.10448.x)

## Summary

The paper proposes an improved sky coverage estimation algorithm for multi-conjugate AO systems. The method accounts for the actual corrected field of view, which depends on guide star positions and system characteristics, and for the wavefront sensing strategy (star-oriented or layer-oriented). It is applied to the ESO MCAO demonstrator MAD, highlighting the importance of guide star geometry, the generalized isoplanatic angle and the magnitude differences between guide stars.
