# HSIM: a simulation pipeline for the HARMONI integral field spectrograph on the European ELT

**Zieleniewski et al. (2015)** · *MNRAS 453, 3754* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.04441) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stv1860](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stv1860)

## Summary

Presents HSIM, a pipeline that simulates observations with the HARMONI integral-field spectrograph on the ELT by turning high-resolution input data cubes into mock observed cubes using models of the sky, telescope and instrument. A method is introduced to include the strongly wavelength-dependent AO point spread functions in the simulation. HSIM is positioned as a step beyond traditional exposure time calculators, supporting feasibility studies of observing programmes and instrument design trade-offs. Two applications are shown: point-source sensitivity estimates and simulations of star-forming emission-line galaxies at z of about 2-3.
