# Tip-Tilt Compensation: Resolution Limits for Ground-based Telescopes Using Laser Guide Star Adaptive Optics

**Olivier et al. (1993)** · *ApJ 407, 428* · [Free full text](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1993ApJ...407..428O) · DOI: [10.1086/172525](https://doi.org/10.1086/172525)

## Summary

Analyses tip-tilt compensation for laser guide star AO when a natural star is used as the tilt reference, including atmospheric properties and Galactic star-count models, and optimises the system operating parameters to find the fundamental limits on long-exposure resolution. For a 10-m telescope on Mauna Kea, about half the sky can be imaged in V band with long-exposure resolution below 60 mas if the tilt star image is uncorrected, and below 16 mas if the tilt star image is itself fully corrected. V-band resolution below 16 mas is predicted for unresolved objects brighter than magnitude 22 fully corrected by an LGS AO system.
