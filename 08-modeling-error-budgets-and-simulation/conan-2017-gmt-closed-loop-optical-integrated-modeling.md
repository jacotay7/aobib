# Closed-loop Optical Integrated Modeling

**Conan et al. (2017)** · *Proc. AO4ELT5 (Tenerife, 2017)* · [Free full text](https://meetings.iac.es/AO4ELT5/media/proceedings/proceeding-144.pdf) · DOI: [10.26698/AO4ELT5.0144](https://doi.org/10.26698/AO4ELT5.0144)

## Summary

For the Giant Magellan Telescope, adaptive optics is part of the telescope itself and must be modeled together with structure and controls from an early stage. The paper describes DOS (Dynamic Optical Simulation), an integrated modeling framework that couples finite element models of the telescope, optical propagation and control systems in Simulink. Optical propagation is handled by CEO (Cuda-Engined Optics), a GPU- and cloud-based geometric and Fourier propagation package interfaced through a Simulink toolbox. The processes for combining the FEM, optical prescription and control algorithms are described, along with examples of evaluating telescope dynamic performance, for instance under wind buffeting.
