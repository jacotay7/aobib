# Simulation of MCAO on (extremely) large telescopes

**Le Louarn et al. (2005)** · *Comptes Rendus Physique 6, 1070* · [Free full text](https://comptes-rendus.academie-sciences.fr/physique/articles/10.1016/j.crhy.2005.10.004/) · DOI: [10.1016/j.crhy.2005.10.004](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.crhy.2005.10.004)

## Summary

This review describes the hierarchy of methods used to model multi-conjugate AO systems. It starts with analytical error-budget formulae for order-of-magnitude estimates, then semi-analytical Fourier-domain methods that produce PSF estimates, and finally the modules needed for full end-to-end Monte Carlo simulation (atmosphere, wavefront sensors, reconstruction, deformable mirrors). A final section discusses the computational challenges of simulating MCAO on extremely large telescopes.
