# Performance of the Keck Observatory adaptive-optics system

**van Dam et al. (2004)** · *Applied Optics 43, 5458* · [Free full text](https://www2.keck.hawaii.edu/inst/ao/aodocs/ApplOptVol43_29oct2004.pdf) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.43.005458](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.43.005458)

## Summary

The paper characterizes the Keck II adaptive optics system operating in natural guide star mode with the NIRC2 near-infrared camera and builds a term-by-term wavefront error budget for it. Measurement-noise and bandwidth errors are obtained by modeling the control loops and by recording residual wavefront-sensor centroids. Sky tests gave average Strehl ratios of up to 0.37 at 1.58 um on bright guide stars and 0.19 for a magnitude 12 star. The measured image quality is shown to be consistent with the wavefront error predicted by the error budget, making the paper a worked example of validating an AO error budget against on-sky data.
