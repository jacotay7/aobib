# Analytical modeling of adaptive optics: foundations of the phase spatial power spectrum approach

**Jolissaint et al. (2006)** · *JOSA A 23, 382* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/7298860_Analytical_modeling_of_adaptive_optics_foundations_of_the_phase_spatial_power_spectrum_approach) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.23.000382](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.23.000382)

## Summary

Sets out the foundations of an analytical method for modelling adaptive optics performance, as a faster complement to end-to-end simulation for exploring the AO parameter space. The AO optical transfer function is computed from an analytical description of the spatial power spectrum of the residual phase. The method is implemented in PAOLA, an IDL code, and comparisons with end-to-end simulations support its validity.
