# Optimum imaging observation wavelength selection for astronomical telescopes with partial correction adaptive optics

**Rao & Jiang (2002)** · *A&A 390, 801* · [Free full text](https://www.aanda.org/articles/aa/pdf/2002/29/aa1566.pdf) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361:20020740](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361:20020740)

## Summary

Derives, from an analysis of the residual wavefront error of an AO system, the observing wavelength that maximises imaging resolution for a partially correcting AO system, separately for short- and long-exposure imaging. The choice depends on the system capability and on the working conditions. The method is applied to the 61-element AO system built by the Institute of Optics and Electronics (CAS) for the 1.2-m telescope of Yunnan Observatory, giving its optimum observing wavelength and the corresponding resolution.
