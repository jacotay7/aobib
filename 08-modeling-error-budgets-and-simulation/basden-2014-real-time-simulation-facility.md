# A real-time simulation facility for astronomical adaptive optics

**Basden (2014)** · *MNRAS 439, 2854* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.5105) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stu143](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stu143)

## Summary

The paper introduces the concept of real-time hardware-in-the-loop simulation for astronomical AO and argues for the need for such a facility. When coupled with an AO real-time control system, a real-time simulation provides a means to validate, verify and integrate ELT real-time controllers before commissioning at the telescope. The author argues that such a facility is necessary for the success of extremely large telescope AO systems.
