# Performance Simulations for KOLA: Achieving High-Resolution, Visible-Light AO Correction Over a 1 Arcminute Field

**Peck et al. (2025)** · *arXiv:2511.17488* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.17488)

## Summary

The paper presents performance simulations of KOLA, a proposed visible-light multi-conjugate AO system for the Keck I telescope using multiple laser guide stars and deformable mirrors to correct a 60 arcsec field. Simulations use MAOS, which the authors validate against on-sky data from the existing Keck I AO system. Trade studies on actuator count and LGS configuration are reported, with PSF metrics (Strehl, FWHM, encircled energy). A nominal design with 10 LGS, 3 tip-tilt NGS and a 3600-actuator adaptive secondary yields 15 mas resolution and 34% Strehl at 652 nm on axis.
