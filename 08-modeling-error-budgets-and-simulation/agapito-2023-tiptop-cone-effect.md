# TIPTOP: cone effect for single laser adaptive optics systems

**Agapito et al. (2023)** · *Proc. AO4ELT7 (Avignon, 2023)* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08168) · DOI: [10.13009/AO4ELT7-2023-012](https://doi.org/10.13009/AO4ELT7-2023-012)

## Summary

The paper adds the cone effect (focal anisoplanatism) of a single laser guide star to the Fourier-domain PSF prediction library TIPTOP, a term previously omitted because the phase seen through the cone is non-stationary. For each spatial frequency in a turbulent layer, the apparent magnification of the frequency seen by the LGS wavefront sensor is modeled, and the residual power is computed from the difference between the nominal and the magnified sinusoids, giving the cone-effect power spectrum. The estimate is compared with end-to-end simulation, and plans for validation with on-sky data are presented.
