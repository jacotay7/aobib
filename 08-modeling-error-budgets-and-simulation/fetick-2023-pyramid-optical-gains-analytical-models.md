# Including the pyramid optical gains into analytical models

**Fétick et al. (2023)** · *Proc. AO4ELT7 (Avignon, 2023)* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-04402859) · DOI: [10.13009/AO4ELT7-2023-013](https://doi.org/10.13009/AO4ELT7-2023-013)

## Summary

Fourier-filtering wavefront sensors such as the pyramid and Zernike sensors are highly sensitive but lose linearity quickly under strong residual turbulence, which analytical (PSD-based) PSF models usually cannot describe because they assume a linear system response. The paper develops a method to include wavefront-sensor non-linearity, through the optical gains, into the analytical formulas. The approach is tested against end-to-end simulations and improves the accuracy of the analytical description of Fourier-filtering sensors, with applications to AO design and performance estimation.
