# Numerical estimation of wavefront error breakdown in adaptive optics

**Ferreira et al. (2018)** · *A&A 616, A102* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.11071) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201832579](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201832579)

## Summary

The authors add to the GPU-based COMPASS end-to-end simulator a tool (ROKET) that decomposes the residual wavefront at every iteration of a single simulation run into its individual contributors, providing temporal buffers for each error term. The tool is used to study correlations between error sources, showing for example a significant correlation between wavefront measurement deviation and bandwidth error due to centroid gain, and recovering the known bandwidth-anisoplanatism correlation. A new analytical model of anisoplanatism and bandwidth errors expressed in deformable-mirror space, based on structure functions and error covariance matrices, is proposed and validated against the tool. The model reproduces Strehl ratio and encircled energy from the end-to-end simulation to about one percent and is intended as a step toward PSF reconstruction and turbulence parameter identification.
