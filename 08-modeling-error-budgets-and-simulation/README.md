# 08 · Modeling, Error Budgets & Simulation

Adaptive-optics systems are designed, specified and understood through models of different
fidelity. At one end are analytical error budgets, where the residual wavefront variance is split
into independent terms (fitting, aliasing, noise, servo lag, anisoplanatism, tip-tilt, calibration
errors) using scaling laws. In the middle are semi-analytical Fourier-domain (PSD-based) models,
which treat the AO loop as a spatial and temporal filter on the turbulent phase power spectrum and
produce long-exposure PSFs in seconds (PAOLA-type models, TIPTOP, AOPERA). At the other end are
end-to-end Monte Carlo simulators that propagate phase screens through wavefront sensors,
reconstructors, controllers and deformable mirrors (CAOS, YAO, MAOS, COMPASS, OOMAO/OOPAO, PASSATA/
SPECULA, DASP, Soapy, HCIPy), together with general optical-propagation libraries (PROPER, POPPY).

This folder collects papers, theses and software documentation on these modeling approaches, on
sky-coverage and tip-tilt modeling for laser-guide-star and wide-field systems, on performance
predictions for ELT-class instruments, and on validating models against on-sky data and using them
for PSF prediction. Several classic analytic-modeling references (Rigaut, Véran & Lai 1998;
Jolissaint, Véran & Conan 2006; Ellerbroek 2005) are not freely available and are listed in
`PAYWALLED.md` rather than here.

## Subtopics

- **Error budgets & scaling laws**: term-by-term wavefront error budgets, error-breakdown tools, reviews of modeling methods
- **Analytic & Fourier-domain performance models**: PSD-based models, Fourier tomography, fitting-error and WFS models, TIPTOP
- **Sky coverage & tip-tilt modeling**: NGS statistics, tip-tilt error budgets, asterism selection for LGS and MCAO systems
- **End-to-end simulation codes**: Monte Carlo simulators and toolkits (CAOS, YAO, MAOS, COMPASS, OOMAO, OOPAO, PASSATA, SPECULA, DASP, Soapy, AOtools, HCIPy, Arroyo, CEO)
- **Optical propagation & analysis tools**: PROPER, POPPY, analysis of simulation outputs (AOSAT)
- **Performance predictions for ELT-class systems**: simulation studies for MICADO/MAORY, METIS, KOLA, GIRMOS
- **Model validation & PSF prediction**: comparing model predictions with on-sky PSFs and calibrating models on data
