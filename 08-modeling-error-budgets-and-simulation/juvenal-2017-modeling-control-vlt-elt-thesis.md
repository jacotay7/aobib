# Modélisation et commande pour les optiques adaptatives des VLT et ELT : de l'analyse de performance à la validation ciel

**Juvénal (2017)** · *PhD thesis (in French)* · [Free full text](https://pastel.hal.science/tel-01661369) · DOI: [10.70675/c99b2b18z5e16z46eczb3a8z2b9eff971e90](https://doi.org/10.70675/c99b2b18z5e16z46eczb3a8z2b9eff971e90)

## Summary

This thesis (English title: Modeling and control for VLT and ELT adaptive optics: from performance assessment to on-sky validation) develops a general frequency-domain error-budget formalism for comparing linear AO controllers, first for single-conjugate AO and then extended to wide-field configurations. The tool decomposes controller performance using either simulation or on-sky data, based on disturbance power spectral densities and controller transfer functions. New control strategies built on turbulence and vibration disturbance models are assessed through their error budgets, and the results contributed to characterizing an LQG tip/tilt controller with vibration mitigation for the GeMS MCAO system at Gemini South.
