# Sky coverage assessment for the European ELT: a joint evaluation for MAORY/MICADO and HARMONI

**Plantet et al. (2022)** · *JATIS 8, 021509* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-03703779) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.8.2.021509](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.8.2.021509)

## Summary

Even with laser guide stars, ELT AO systems need natural guide stars to sense image jitter and some low-order aberrations, which limits sky coverage. The paper presents the sky coverage assessment methods used for HARMONI (laser tomography AO) and MAORY/MICADO (MCAO), both based on a semi-analytical description of the main error budget contributors that allows fast estimation of residual jitter. These methods are suited to statistical sky coverage studies over many fields and to exploring system parameters. Sky coverage of both instruments is computed for cosmological fields from the CANDELS catalogue, comparing the two types of tomographic AO systems.
