# COMPASS: COMputing Platform for Adaptive opticS Systems

**Gratadour et al. (2014)** · *Software (CUDA/C++/Python), Observatoire de Paris; reference paper Proc. SPIE 9148* · [Free full text](https://compass.pages.obspm.fr/website/) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2056358](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2056358)

## Summary

COMPASS is a GPU-accelerated end-to-end AO simulation platform developed at Observatoire de Paris (LESIA) for ELT-scale systems, with a Python interface (shesha) on top of CUDA/C++ libraries. It models multi-layer turbulence, ELT pupils with missing segments and spiders, Shack-Hartmann, pyramid, LGS (with elongation and cone effect) and geometric wavefront sensors, deformable mirrors and a range of real-time reconstruction methods. It has been used for MICADO and METIS SCAO design studies, among others; the platform was first described by Gratadour et al. (2014, Proc. SPIE 9148). The link points to the project website with documentation, tutorials and publication list.
