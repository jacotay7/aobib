# Durham extremely large telescope adaptive optics simulation platform

**Basden et al. (2007)** · *Applied Optics 46, 1089* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0611294) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.46.001089](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.46.001089)

## Summary

The paper describes the AO simulation platform developed at Durham University (later known as DASP) for modeling AO systems on proposed extremely large telescopes as well as current telescopes. The platform is modular and object oriented, can be highly parallelized with techniques suited to AO simulation, and can use hardware acceleration while still giving the user control during a run. A ground-layer AO simulation is presented as an example. The arXiv preprint is titled "The Durham ELT adaptive optics simulation platform".
