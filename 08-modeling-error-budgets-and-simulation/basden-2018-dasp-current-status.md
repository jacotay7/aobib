# The Durham Adaptive Optics Simulation Platform (DASP): Current status

**Basden et al. (2018)** · *SoftwareX 7, 63* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.08503) · DOI: [10.1016/j.softx.2018.02.005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.softx.2018.02.005)

## Summary

DASP is a Monte-Carlo modeling tool for astronomical and solar AO systems written mainly in Python and C with parallelization support. The paper gives an overview of its features, including recently added modules for Fresnel propagation and extended-object wavefront sensing, and its use in predicting the performance of extremely large telescope AO systems. The user tools for configuring and controlling simulations are also described.
