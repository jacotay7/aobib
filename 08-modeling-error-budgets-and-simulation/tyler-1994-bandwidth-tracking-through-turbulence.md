# Bandwidth considerations for tracking through turbulence

**Tyler (1994)** · *JOSA A 11, 358* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/230630777_Bandwidth_considerations_for_tracking_through_turbulence) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.11.000358](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.11.000358)

## Summary

Derives the servo bandwidth needed to track image motion through turbulence for a closed-loop system with a first-order transfer function. The one-axis rms jitter is shown to equal (fT/f3dB)(λ/D), where fT, the fundamental turbulence-tracking frequency (now often called the Tyler frequency), is the bandwidth at which the jitter equals the diffraction angle λ/D. Both centroid tilt (G-tilt) and least-squares plane tilt (Z-tilt) are evaluated; their tracking frequencies are nearly identical, about 0.33 and 0.37 D^(-1/6) λ^(-1) [∫ Cn2(z) V²(z) dz]^(1/2). The results are applied to turbulence models for systems of interest.
