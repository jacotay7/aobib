# High fidelity sky coverage analysis via time domain adaptive optics simulations

**Wang et al. (2009)** · *Applied Optics 48, 5076* · [Free full text](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/c73j9-ctx13) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.48.005076](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.48.005076)

## Summary

The authors describe a high-fidelity method for estimating the sky coverage of multi-conjugate AO systems based on the split tomography control architecture. AO simulation post-processing is used to evaluate system performance for hundreds of randomly generated natural guide star asterisms. A technique to model the effect of quadratic wavefront aberrations on the NGS PSFs is introduced, which is needed to capture asterism-to-asterism variations. Design and algorithm improvements that reduce residual tip/tilt and plate-scale errors are reported, including WFS pixel size choice, optimal pixel weighting, and type II control of plate-scale modes.
