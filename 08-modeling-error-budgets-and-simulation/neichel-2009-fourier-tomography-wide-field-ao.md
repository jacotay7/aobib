# Tomographic reconstruction for Wide Field Adaptive Optics systems: Fourier domain analysis and fundamental limitations

**Neichel et al. (2009)** · *JOSA A 26, 219* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/0811.2369) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.26.000219](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.26.000219)

## Summary

The paper analyzes atmospheric tomographic reconstruction for wide-field AO concepts (MCAO, MOAO, GLAO) in the Fourier domain. This approach yields simple analytical expressions for the reconstruction error of the different configurations and reconstruction algorithms, giving a comprehensive view of their fundamental limitations. The impact of model errors and statistical errors on the reconstructed phase is investigated. Examples of the expected performance of several wide-field AO systems on a 42 m telescope are given.
