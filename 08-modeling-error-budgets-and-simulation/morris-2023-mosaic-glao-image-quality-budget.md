# Defining the MOSAIC GLAO Image Quality Budget

**Morris et al. (2023)** · *Proc. AO4ELT7 (Avignon, 2023)* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-04428987) · DOI: [10.13009/AO4ELT7-2023-091](https://doi.org/10.13009/AO4ELT7-2023-091)

## Summary

MOSAIC, the ELT multi-object spectrograph, uses ground-layer AO to improve H-band encircled energy within a 600 mas aperture over a 7.4 arcmin field. Because GLAO correction is partial and the aperture is large, summing individual wavefront error terms in quadrature does not give a usable estimate of system performance or of subsystem image-quality requirements. The paper describes the approach adopted to build the MOSAIC image quality budget and uses Monte Carlo GLAO simulations at the ELT to test its limits. The results indicate that convolving the AO-corrected PSF with a high-order PSF representing instrumental and non-AO effects is sufficient to determine encircled energy for the instrument.
