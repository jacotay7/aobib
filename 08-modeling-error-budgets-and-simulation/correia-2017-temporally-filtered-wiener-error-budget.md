# Modeling astronomical adaptive optics performance with temporally filtered Wiener reconstruction of slope data

**Correia et al. (2017)** · *JOSA A 34, 1877* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.05090) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.34.001877](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.34.001877)

## Summary

Building on linear-systems modeling of AO in the spatial-frequency domain, the paper provides a tool for computing error budgets as residual temporally filtered phase power spectral densities and variances, and for fast simulation of AO-corrected PSFs. The authors propose a distributed Kalman filter that mitigates both aniso-servo-lag and aliasing errors while minimizing residual variance. Applications discussed include analytic PSF modeling, post-coronagraphic contrast prediction, filter optimization for real-time reconstruction, and PSF reconstruction from telemetry. Assuming perfect knowledge of wind velocities, about 60 nm rms error reduction is reported for 10 m-class high-order AO systems, with contrast gains of up to three orders of magnitude at 1-5 lambda/D for bright stars.
