# MORFEO wavefront error budget

**Agapito et al. (2026)** · *Proc. AO4ELT8 (Viña del Mar, 2025)* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07755) · DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.21127893](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21127893)

## Summary

The paper presents the structure of the wavefront error budget of MORFEO (formerly MAORY), the multi-conjugate AO module of the ESO ELT that feeds the MICADO camera with 12 wavefront sensors and three deformable mirrors. It details the main contributors to the residual error, including atmospheric turbulence, optical surfaces, alignment tolerances, control residuals and calibration uncertainties, and the methodology used to evaluate each term. External conditions, system configurations and key assumptions behind the budget are discussed, together with the interplay between requirements and design choices. The budget is used as input to end-to-end simulations of MORFEO-assisted observations.
