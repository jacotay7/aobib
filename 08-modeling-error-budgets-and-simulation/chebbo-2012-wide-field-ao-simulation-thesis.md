# Refined Adaptive Optics simulation with wide field of view for the E-ELT

**Chebbo (2012)** · *PhD thesis* · [Free full text](https://theses.hal.science/tel-00742873)

## Summary

The thesis addresses the simulation of wide-field AO systems (MOAO, MCAO, LTAO) on the E-ELT, where the number of degrees of freedom makes classical matrix inversion and matrix-vector multiplication impractical in simulation codes. A full simulation code based on iterative solutions of the least-squares or minimum mean square error problem with sparse matrices is developed in collaboration with ONERA. New filtering and LGS/NGS data fusion concepts are introduced to manage tip, tilt and defocus within tomographic reconstruction, and the code is intended to support the development of complex multi-DM, multi-field control laws.
