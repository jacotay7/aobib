# Performance characterization and near-real-time monitoring of MUSE adaptive optics modes at Paranal

**Wevers et al. (2022)** · *Proc. SPIE 12186, 121860T* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.07540) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2630835](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2630835)

## Summary

Quantifies the delivered performance of the narrow-field laser tomography AO mode (NFM-AO) of MUSE on the VLT (GALACSI with the Adaptive Optics Facility), extending earlier work on the wide-field ground-layer mode. Around 230 spectrophotometric standard-star observations from three years of NFM-AO operations are analysed, measuring Strehl ratio and core FWHM with two PSF models and correlating them with atmospheric conditions. The paper also describes a semi-automated framework for near-real-time performance monitoring during science operations. The results are intended to improve the performance predictions in the exposure time calculator and to help optimize the scientific output for given limiting atmospheric conditions.
