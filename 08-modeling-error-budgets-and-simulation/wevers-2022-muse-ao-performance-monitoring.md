# Performance characterization and near-realtime monitoring of MUSE adaptive optics modes at Paranal

**Wevers et al. (2022)** · *Proc. SPIE 12186, 121860T* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.07540) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2630835](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2630835)

## Summary

Quantifies the delivered performance of the MUSE adaptive optics modes on the VLT (GALACSI with the Adaptive Optics Facility): the wide-field ground-layer mode and the narrow-field laser tomography mode. Around 230 spectrophotometric standard-star observations from three years of operations are analysed, measuring Strehl ratio and core FWHM with two PSF models and correlating them with atmospheric conditions. The paper also describes a semi-automated framework for near-real-time performance monitoring during science operations. The results are intended to improve the PSF predictions in the exposure time calculator and the scheduling of observations under given atmospheric constraints.
