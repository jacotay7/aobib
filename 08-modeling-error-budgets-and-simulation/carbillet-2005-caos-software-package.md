# Modelling astronomical adaptive optics - I. The software package CAOS

**Carbillet et al. (2005)** · *MNRAS 356, 1263* · [Free full text](https://www-n.oca.eu/caos/caos-article-MNRAS.pdf) · DOI: [10.1111/j.1365-2966.2004.08524.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2966.2004.08524.x)

## Summary

CAOS (Code for Adaptive Optics Systems) is a modular IDL software package for end-to-end simulation of astronomical AO systems. Its modules cover a full atmospheric turbulence model, sodium laser guide star upward and downward propagation, Shack-Hartmann and pyramid wavefront sensors, reconstruction and temporal filtering, and correction with different correctors, as well as image formation, Fizeau interferometry and coronagraphy. The paper gives an overview of the physical modeling and illustrates its use by studying whether a dedicated tip-tilt sensor is worthwhile in a pyramid-based AO system on an 8 m telescope.
