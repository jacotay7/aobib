# Analytical modelling of adaptive optics systems: Role of the influence function

**Berdeu et al. (2023)** · *A&A 674, A112* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.10803) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202245327](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202245327)

## Summary

Standard analytical models of the AO fitting error depend only on the corrector cut-off frequency, so the effect of influence-function shape on the corrected PSF had required Monte Carlo simulation. The authors derive a general analytical model of the fitting-error structure function and, under further hypotheses, of the AO-corrected phase power spectral density, accounting for influence-function shape. Predictions agree with Monte Carlo simulations for piston, pyramid and Gaussian influence functions and for a measured ALPAO DM192 mirror. The work shows that the depth of correction and the transition to the uncorrected halo depend on the influence functions, and that the usual stationarity assumption for AO correction does not strictly hold.
