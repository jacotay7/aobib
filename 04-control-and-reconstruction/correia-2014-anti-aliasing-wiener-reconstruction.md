# Anti-aliasing Wiener filtering for wave-front reconstruction in the spatial-frequency domain for high-order astronomical adaptive-optics systems

**Correia & Teixeira (2014)** · *JOSA A 31, 2763* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.6055) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.31.002763](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.31.002763)

## Summary

Derives the Wiener (Strehl-maximizing) filter for wavefront reconstruction in the spatial-frequency domain and extends it to an anti-aliasing Wiener filter that accounts for high-order wavefront content folded in-band by the discrete sampling of the wavefront sensor. The forward measurement operators are represented with a continuous spatial-frequency model, and noise and aliasing propagation coefficients are compared with least-squares filters as a function of system order. For a 32x32 single-conjugate system the aliasing propagation coefficient is roughly 60% of the least-squares value and noise propagation about 80%, with contrast improvements of up to a factor of 2 in H band. The authors conclude that anti-aliasing filtering must be combined with optical spatial filtering for high-contrast imagers.
