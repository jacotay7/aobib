# On the optimal reconstruction and control of adaptive optical systems with mirror dynamics

**Correia et al. (2010)** · *JOSA A 27, 333* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-04511071) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.27.000333](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.27.000333)

## Summary

Establishes the general minimum-variance solution to the joint reconstruction and control problem of adaptive optics systems whose deformable mirrors have non-negligible dynamics, as expected for large, slow or resonant ELT mirrors. Using a continuous stochastic disturbance model and a state-space approach, the optimal solution takes the form of a discrete-time LQG regulator. Simpler suboptimal schemes, such as dynamics-free solutions or static minimum-variance reconstruction, are derived as special cases and their performance evaluated within the same framework. Results are given for weakly damped second-order oscillatory mirrors with large resonant responses.
