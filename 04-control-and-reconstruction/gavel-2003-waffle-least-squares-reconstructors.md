# Suppressing anomalous localized waffle behavior in least-squares wavefront reconstructors

**Gavel (2003)** · *Proc. SPIE 4839, 972* · [Free full text](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/15002879) · DOI: [10.1117/12.459686](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.459686)

## Summary

Analyses least-squares wavefront reconstructors in terms of the singular mode space over which they operate, motivated by the insensitivity of slope sensors to waffle patterns in the Fried sampling geometry. The paper shows that, without suitable preconditioning, a large reconstructor's mode set can consist almost entirely of modes containing localized waffle-like behaviour. A technique is introduced that produces a mode space segregating waffle-like behaviour into a few high-order modes, which can then be projected out of the reconstructor matrix. The same approach can be used to remove other unwanted modes such as piston, tip and tilt. The free copy is the LLNL preprint UCRL-JC-147287 hosted on OSTI.
