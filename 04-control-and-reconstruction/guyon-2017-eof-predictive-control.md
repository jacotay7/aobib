# Adaptive Optics Predictive Control with Empirical Orthogonal Functions (EOFs)

**Guyon & Males (2017)** · *arXiv:1707.00570* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.00570)

## Summary

Proposes an optimal linear predictive control approach for adaptive optics based on the Empirical Orthogonal Functions (EOF) framework used in atmospheric prediction. The predictor can be implemented as a linear pattern-matching algorithm that decomposes the most recent wavefront-sensor measurements into a sum of previously encountered patterns and uses the coefficients to predict the future state. The approach is described as robust to evolving conditions, unknown spatio-temporal correlations and non-periodic transients, and allows additional sensors such as accelerometers to contribute. Simulations show filter convergence within about a minute on a 1 kHz system and reduced, spatially and temporally decorrelated residual speckle halo for high-contrast imaging.
