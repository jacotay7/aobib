# Real-time adaptive optics with pyramid wavefront sensors: part II. Accurate wavefront reconstruction using iterative methods

**Hutterer et al. (2019)** · *Inverse Problems 35, 045008* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.10888) · DOI: [10.1088/1361-6420/ab0900](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6420/ab0900)

## Summary

Treats fast wavefront reconstruction from pyramid wavefront sensor data for ELT adaptive optics as an inverse problem and applies conjugate gradient, steepest descent, Landweber, Landweber-Kaczmarz and steepest descent-Kaczmarz iterations based on theoretical models of the sensor. The methods are compared in end-to-end closed-loop simulations of a high-order SCAO system for METIS on the ELT. The authors show that the iterative algorithms can be implemented efficiently, reaching quality comparable to matrix-vector multiplication at lower computational cost.
