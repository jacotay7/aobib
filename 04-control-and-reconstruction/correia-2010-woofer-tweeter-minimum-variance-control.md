# Minimum-variance control for woofer-tweeter systems in adaptive optics

**Correia et al. (2010)** · *JOSA A 27, A133* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-04511099) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.27.00A133](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.27.00A133)

## Summary

Derives a minimum-variance, LQG-based controller for woofer-tweeter adaptive optics, in which a slow, large-stroke woofer and a fast, low-amplitude tweeter share part of their correction space. The analytical model builds on earlier work on a single deformable mirror with temporal dynamics. Monte Carlo simulations of an 8x8-actuator mirror (treated as infinitely fast) mounted on a slow tip/tilt platform show that temporal dynamics must be taken into account in the estimation step. Unlike the other control strategies considered, the optimal solution is always stable.
