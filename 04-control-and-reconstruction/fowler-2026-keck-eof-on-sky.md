# Ground control to major time-lag: on-sky results of data-driven predictive wavefront control at Keck Observatory

**Fowler et al. (2026)** · *Proc. SPIE 14150, 141506Z* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20838) · DOI: [10.1117/12.3103126](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3103126)

## Summary

Presents on-sky results of an EOF predictive controller built directly into the Keck II real-time controller. Engineering tests show a 20% improvement over a classic integrator in Shack-Hartmann wavefront-sensor residuals. Parameter studies show clear improvements when varying predictive filter hyper-parameters, while within a reasonable regime the predictor does not perform notably worse than an integrator. NIRC2 Brackett-gamma imaging shows comparable Strehl ratio and coronagraph-free contrast for integrator and predictor, and principal-component analysis indicates a modest improvement. The work is positioned as a pathfinder for predictive control on extremely large telescopes.
