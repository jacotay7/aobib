# Robustness of prediction for extreme adaptive optics systems under various observing conditions: An analysis using VLT/SPHERE adaptive optics data

**van Kooten et al. (2020)** · *A&A 636, A81* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.10225) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201937076](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201937076)

## Summary

Estimates the potential on-sky benefit of linear data-driven prediction for VLT/SPHERE by applying a linear minimum mean square error predictor to 27 AO telemetry data sets taken over many nights. Prediction always reduces temporal wavefront phase variance relative to the SPHERE residuals, with an average improvement factor of 5.1, or 2.0 relative to an idealized SPHERE. The predictor also narrows the spread of residual temporal variance across conditions. Including spatial information gives no benefit, and a purely temporal predictor is found best suited to SPHERE.
