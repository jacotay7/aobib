# Local ensemble transform Kalman filter, a fast non-stationary control law for adaptive optics on ELTs: theoretical aspects and first simulation results

**Gray et al. (2014)** · *Optics Express 22, 20894* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.7069) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.22.020894](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.22.020894)

## Summary

Proposes a control law for ELT adaptive optics based on the LQG approach with a localized ensemble Kalman filter (the local ensemble transform Kalman filter, LETKF) adapted from data assimilation in geophysics. The method can handle non-stationary behaviour and approaches the performance of residual-phase-variance-optimal control. Its localized structure reduces the computational burden and is intrinsically parallel, suiting implementation on extremely large telescopes. Theoretical aspects and first simulation results are presented.
