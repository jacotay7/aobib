# Upgrading SPHERE with the second stage AO system SAXO+: frequency-based data-driven controller for adaptive optics

**Dinis et al. (2024)** · *Proc. SPIE 13097* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05123) · DOI: [10.1117/12.3020179](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3020179)

## Summary

Introduces a frequency-based data-driven controller for adaptive optics that uses the disturbance power spectral density in an optimization subject to stability constraints. Disturbance rejection, command amplitude constraints and system transfer functions are handled through convex optimization, giving an optimal controller in infinite impulse response filter form. Within the SAXO+ project, the controller is evaluated under varied atmospheric conditions in both standard and disentangled cascade AO schemes. Validation uses the COMPASS simulation tool.
