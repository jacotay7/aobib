# Data-driven subspace predictive control of adaptive optics for high-contrast imaging

**Haffert et al. (2021)** · *JATIS 7, 029001* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.07566) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.7.2.029001](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.7.2.029001)

## Summary

Proposes a linear data-driven integral predictive controller based on subspace methods and updated in real time, to reduce servo-lag error in high-contrast imaging. The controller uses only the measured wavefront errors and changes in deformable-mirror commands, allowing closed-loop operation without pseudo-open-loop reconstruction and making it usable with non-linear sensors such as the pyramid wavefront sensor. Simulations show near-optimal control for stationary and non-stationary disturbances and gains of several orders of magnitude in raw contrast. The algorithm was demonstrated in the laboratory with MagAO-X, with more than two orders of magnitude contrast gain.
