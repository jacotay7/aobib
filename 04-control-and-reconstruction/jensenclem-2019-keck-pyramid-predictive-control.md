# Demonstrating predictive wavefront control with the Keck II near-infrared pyramid wavefront sensor

**Jensen-Clem et al. (2019)** · *Proc. SPIE 11117* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.05302) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2529687](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2529687)

## Summary

Presents initial tests of EOF-based predictive wavefront control for the near-infrared pyramid wavefront sensor installed in the Keck II AO system. Expected RMS wavefront error and contrast benefits are shown with simulations and by applying the predictor to on-sky telemetry saved during sensor commissioning, including how performance depends on the temporal and spatial scales used to compute the filter. The implementation of EOF prediction within the pyramid sensor's real-time controller is described. Daytime tests with pre-computed phase screens on the deformable mirror demonstrate real-time predictive control.
