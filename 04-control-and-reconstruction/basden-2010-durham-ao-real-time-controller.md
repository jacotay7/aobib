# Durham adaptive optics real-time controller

**Basden et al. (2010)** · *Applied Optics 49, 6354* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.3209) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.49.006354](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.49.006354)

## Summary

Describes the Durham adaptive optics real-time controller (DARC), which evolved from a proof-of-concept generic AO control system into a CPU-based real-time control system built mainly on commercial off-the-shelf hardware, with optional acceleration by FPGAs and GPUs. The paper explains the design concepts and reports performance, including latency and jitter below 10 microseconds for small AO systems. The authors state that it is powerful enough to serve as the real-time controller for all then-planned 8 m class telescope AO systems.
