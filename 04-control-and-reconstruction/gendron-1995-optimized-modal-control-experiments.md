# Astronomical adaptive optics. II. Experimental results of an optimized modal control

**Gendron & Léna (1995)** · *A&AS 111, 153* · [Free full text](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1995A%26AS..111..153G)

## Summary

Experimentally verifies the modal control optimization proposed in Paper I, in which each spatial mode receives a correction bandwidth depending on its signal-to-noise ratio and correlation time. The analysis uses wavefront-sensor data from the ComeOnPlus adaptive optics system on the ESO 3.6 m telescope at La Silla. Algorithms for processing the sensor data are described and their performance and robustness discussed. The efficiency of the AO system is derived as a function of reference-source magnitude and atmospheric conditions.
