# Local, hierarchic, and iterative reconstructors for adaptive optics

**MacMartin (2003)** · *JOSA A 20, 1084* · [Free full text](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/1dnrr-jtv64) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.20.001084](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.20.001084)

## Summary

Optimal reconstruction from relative (slope) measurements requires every sensor to influence every actuator, so the computational cost grows as n squared for n actuators, which becomes prohibitive for AO systems with thousands of degrees of freedom. The paper analyses a local reconstructor in which each actuator uses only nearby sensors and quantifies the resulting loss of performance on global modes. Two remedies are studied: a hierarchic scheme that combines local and global estimators, scaling roughly as n^(4/3) for two layers and approaching linear scaling with more layers, and an iterative local scheme that reuses prior estimates, acting as a temporal low-pass filter on global information and scaling as n^(3/2). The algorithms are evaluated in simulation with data from the Palomar Observatory AO system, and the analysis is noted to apply also to active optics.
