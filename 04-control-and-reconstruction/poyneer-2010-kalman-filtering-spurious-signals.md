# Kalman filtering to suppress spurious signals in adaptive optics control

**Poyneer & Véran (2010)** · *JOSA A 27, A223* · [Free full text](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1010849) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.27.00A223](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.27.00A223)

## Summary

Formulates a Kalman filter with a state-space model that suppresses temporally non-white (coloured) noise in AO control, improving the residual error relative to assuming white noise. Effectiveness is demonstrated for the estimated tip-tilt environment of the Gemini Planet Imager, which includes both common-path and non-common-path vibrations. The same framework is used to suppress spatial aliasing during frozen-flow predictive control in a low-order AO system without a spatially filtered wavefront sensor. Experimental measurements from Gemini's Altair system revealing these aliased components are presented. The free copy is LLNL report LLNL-JRNL-426892 on OSTI.
