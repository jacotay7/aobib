# Commande optimale en Optique Adaptative Classique et Multiconjuguée

**Le Roux (2003)** · *PhD thesis, Université de Nice Sophia-Antipolis* · [Free full text](https://theses.hal.science/tel-00004690)

## Summary

Thesis (in French) proposing an optimal control law, in the sense of minimum residual phase variance, for classical and multi-conjugate adaptive optics. The approach estimates the turbulent phase with a Kalman filter followed by state-feedback control in a state-space formalism. It incorporates prior knowledge of measurement noise, the turbulence altitude profile, Kolmogorov statistics and the temporal evolution of the turbulence. The gain over a traditional approach is quantified with numerical simulations in classical and multi-conjugate AO, and the robustness of the optimal approach is discussed. This work underlies the JOSA A paper by Le Roux et al. (2004).
