# Beyond FRiM, ASAP: a family of sparse approximation for covariance matrices and preconditioners

**Thiébaut et al. (2022)** · *Proc. SPIE 12185, 121852Q* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.17721) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2630192](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2630192)

## Summary

Shows that the fractal operator used in FRiM belongs to a broader family of sparse operators, called ASAP, defined by an ordered selection of nearest neighbours. The authors propose a fast algorithm to build ASAP operators that mimic the fractal structure of FRiM for pupils of any size and geometry, and to learn their sparse coefficients from empirical data. They show empirically that ASAP approximates correlated statistics well and benefits phase-restoration problems, providing sparse priors and preconditioners for fast minimum-variance reconstruction.
