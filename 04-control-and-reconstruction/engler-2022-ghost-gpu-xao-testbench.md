# The GPU-based High-order adaptive OpticS Testbench

**Engler et al. (2022)** · *Proc. SPIE 12185, 1218558* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.05408) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2630595](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2630595)

## Summary

Describes GHOST, a two-stage extreme AO testbench at ESO built to evaluate new control methods, such as machine learning and predictive control, for instruments like the ELT Planetary Camera and Spectrograph. The first-stage correction is simulated and its residual wavefront error is injected into the bench with a spatial light modulator. The second stage uses a 492-actuator Boston Micromachines deformable mirror and a pyramid wavefront sensor. The bench is also used for other studies, such as flip-flop modulation of the pyramid wavefront sensor.
