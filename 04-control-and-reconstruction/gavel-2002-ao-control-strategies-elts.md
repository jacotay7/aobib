# Adaptive optics control strategies for extremely large telescopes

**Gavel (2002)** · *Proc. SPIE 4494, 215* · [Free full text](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/15002890) · DOI: [10.1117/12.454794](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.454794)

## Summary

Presents scaling laws and strawman error budgets for adaptive optics on 30-100 m class telescopes and discusses implications for component technology and computational architecture. Developments at the NSF Center for Adaptive Optics on high-actuator-count deformable mirrors, wavefront sensors and guide-star lasers are reviewed. The paper stresses that existing wavefront reconstruction algorithms scale in a computationally intractable way for ELTs and outlines the characteristics of new algorithmic approaches that show promise for scaling to ELT AO. The free copy is the LLNL preprint (UCRL-JC-144864) on OSTI.
