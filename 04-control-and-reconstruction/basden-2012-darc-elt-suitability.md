# The Durham adaptive optics real-time controller: capability and Extremely Large Telescope suitability

**Basden & Myers (2012)** · *MNRAS 424, 1483* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.4532) · DOI: [10.1111/j.1365-2966.2012.21342.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2966.2012.21342.x)

## Summary

Reports new features and performance improvements of DARC, a generic high-performance real-time control system for astronomical adaptive optics. The paper describes ways to optimize the controller for specific AO systems and hardware implementations. New measurements show that it could be used with any existing AO system and that, on modern hardware, its performance is sufficient for most ELT adaptive optics systems.
