# Battle of the Predictive Wavefront Controls: Comparing Data and Model-Driven Predictive Control for High Contrast Imaging

**Fowler et al. (2022)** · *Proc. SPIE 12185, 1218582* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.00984) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2629521](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2629521)

## Summary

Compares two predictive control methods side by side: data-driven prediction with empirical orthogonal functions and the physically motivated Predictive Fourier Control. Both are applied post facto to simulated atmospheres and on-sky telemetry to identify circumstances in which their performance differs. Tests vary wind speed, Cn2 profile and time lag. Plans for testing both algorithms on the Santa Cruz Extreme AO Laboratory (SEAL) testbed are discussed.
