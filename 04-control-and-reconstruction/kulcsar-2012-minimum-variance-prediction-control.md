# Minimum variance prediction and control for adaptive optics

**Kulcsár et al. (2012)** · *Automatica 48, 1939* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-04511134) · DOI: [10.1016/j.automatica.2012.03.030](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.automatica.2012.03.030)

## Summary

Revisits minimum-variance control design for astronomical adaptive optics in a state-space framework and surveys the associated modelling and control issues of this multivariable disturbance-rejection problem. Under a linear time-invariant framework and mild assumptions, the optimal minimum-variance controller is shown to be a discrete-time LQG controller, both for instantaneous deformable mirrors and for a general class of mirror dynamics. The approach is extended to wide-field AO configurations with several mirrors and/or sensors, and integral-action control is compared with LQG. Laboratory wide-field AO results showing improved performance are presented, together with open theoretical and applied issues.
