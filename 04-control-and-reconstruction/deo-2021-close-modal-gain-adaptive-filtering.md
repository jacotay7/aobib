# A correlation-locking adaptive filtering technique for minimum variance integral control in adaptive optics

**Deo et al. (2021)** · *A&A 650, A41* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.09921) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202040216](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202040216)

## Summary

Proposes CLOSE (Correlation-Locking Optimization SchEme), a real-time adaptive filtering technique that tunes the modal gains of integrator-controlled AO systems using the temporal autocorrelation of modal signals in the loop telemetry. The supervisory loop is configured by a few scalar parameters and drives the modal gains towards transfer functions that achieve minimum-variance control over the range of noise and seeing conditions. Because it operates through multiplicative gain increments, CLOSE also compensates the optical-gain sensitivity loss of pyramid wavefront sensors. End-to-end simulations of the MICADO single-conjugate AO system demonstrate the method, including robustness to transients affecting pyramid sensitivity.
