# First on-sky SCAO validation of full LQG control with vibration mitigation on the CANARY pathfinder

**Sivo et al. (2014)** · *Optics Express 22, 23565* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-01739389) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.22.023565](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.22.023565)

## Summary

Presents the first on-sky results of Kalman-filter-based LQG control with vibration mitigation, obtained in single-conjugate mode on the CANARY instrument at the 4.2 m William Herschel Telescope. The results show a clear performance improvement of full LQG control over standard integrator control. The additional improvement from vibration filtering with a tip-tilt model identified from on-sky data is assessed. The results support the control strategy adopted for SPHERE at the VLT.
