# Super-resolution wavefront reconstruction

**Oberti et al. (2022)** · *A&A 667, A48* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.12052) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202243954](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202243954)

## Summary

Applies super-resolution (SR) concepts to two-dimensional wavefront reconstruction, showing that combining several wavefront-sensor samplings that are shifted relative to each other gives access to more modes than a single sensor provides. The paper gives direct-space and Fourier-optics descriptions of wavefront sensing within a periodic non-uniform sampling framework and analyses the SR model and sensitivity function for a Shack-Hartmann sensor. Numerical simulations of multi-WFS Shack-Hartmann systems confirm the larger number of reconstructable modes, with noise propagation not degraded. The concept is also extended to a single pyramid sensor, and the authors note that SR decouples subaperture size from the desired wavefront sampling.
