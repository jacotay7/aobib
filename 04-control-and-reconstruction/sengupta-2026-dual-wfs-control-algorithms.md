# Control algorithms for dual-wavefront sensor single-conjugate adaptive optics

**Sengupta et al. (2026)** · *PASP 138, 025003* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.14531) · DOI: [10.1088/1538-3873/ae3c40](https://doi.org/10.1088/1538-3873/ae3c40)

## Summary

Presents control algorithms that let AO systems incorporate focal-plane wavefront sensors within their existing control setup, rather than adding a second control stage, so that non-common-path aberrations can be corrected while science images are recorded. The paper shows that transfer of non-common-path aberrations between sensor arms can be mitigated through temporal filtering. Controller stability and performance are validated in the frequency and time domains, and the optimality of the chosen controllers is discussed.
