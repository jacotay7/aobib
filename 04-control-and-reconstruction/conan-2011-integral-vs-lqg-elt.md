# Are integral controllers adapted to the new era of ELT adaptive optics?

**Conan et al. (2011)** · *Proc. AO4ELT2 (Victoria, 2011)* · [Free full text](https://ao4elt2.lesia.obspm.fr/sites/ao4elt2/IMG/pdf/111conan.pdf)

## Summary

Examines whether integral controllers remain adequate for ELT adaptive optics, focusing on temporal aspects. Building on the demonstrated performance of LQG controllers for rejecting windshake and vibrations, the paper evaluates integrator versus LQG control on turbulent modes in both VLT and ELT configurations. It asks how the two compare for a given sampling frequency and noise level, whether LQG could relax sampling-frequency and noise specifications, and how open-loop versus closed-loop strategies and large numbers of degrees of freedom affect these conclusions.
