# Exploiting the spatiotemporal correlation in adaptive optics using data-driven H2-optimal control

**Hinnen et al. (2007)** · *JOSA A 24, 1714* · [Free full text](https://resolver.tudelft.nl/uuid:9d6b2cd6-7936-4afc-ab55-62ca84be31d3) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.24.001714](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.24.001714)

## Summary

The paper demonstrates on a laboratory setup a data-driven H2-optimal controller that exploits the spatiotemporal correlation of the wavefront instead of the usual static reconstructor followed by decoupled integrators. Analysing the dynamics of the wavefront sensor, the authors show that when the corrector can be treated as static the transfer from control input to sensor output reduces to a two-tap impulse response plus an integer delay. Using this structure, the relevant disturbance-model parameters are identified directly from measured data, and the optimal controller is obtained analytically from the minimum-phase spectral factor of the identified atmospheric disturbance model. Laboratory comparisons with the standard AO control law, together with an analysis of the dominant error sources, indicate that the optimal controller can reduce the temporal (servo-lag) error.
