# An Analysis of Fundamental Waffle Mode in Early AEOS Adaptive Optics Images

**Makidon et al. (2005)** · *PASP 117, 831* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0505195) · DOI: [10.1086/431436](https://doi.org/10.1086/431436)

## Summary

Examines how a waffle-mode wavefront error, which is not sensed by a Fried-geometry Shack-Hartmann sensor, affects the adaptive optics point-spread function on the 3.6 m AEOS telescope. The authors model the AEOS AO system to simulate a PSF matching the observed I-band PSF and find that early AEOS images contained several times more waffle error than expected. They show that improved wavefront reconstruction techniques noticeably improve AEOS AO performance. The impact of the improved reconstructors on H-band imaging with the Lyot Project coronagraph is also estimated.
