# Optimal modal Fourier-transform wavefront control

**Poyneer & Véran (2005)** · *JOSA A 22, 1515* · [Free full text](https://exaoc.ucolick.org/documents/Papers/XAOPI%20papers/PoyneerOptFourierControl_preprint04.pdf) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.22.001515](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.22.001515)

## Summary

Optimal modal Fourier-transform wavefront control (OFC) combines the speed of Fourier transform reconstruction (FTR) with real-time optimization of modal gains. The modal basis is the real Fourier basis, which allows direct control of specific regions of the PSF; the paper formulates FTR as modal control, shows how to measure custom filters, and argues that because the Fourier basis is a tight frame it can be used for modal control on a circular aperture even though it is not orthonormal there. Since the modal coefficients are available during reconstruction, the overhead of gain optimization is small. Simulations show improved performance at low signal-to-noise ratio compared with non-adaptive control, and the scheme is estimated to be implementable with off-the-shelf hardware for a 2.5 kHz, 64 x 64 AO system. The free link is the LLNL preprint (UCRL-JRNL-207495) submitted to JOSA A, hosted on the UC Santa Cruz Extreme AO Center document server.
