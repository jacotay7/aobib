# SPHERE eXtreme AO control scheme: final performance assessment and on sky validation of the first auto-tuned LQG based operational system

**Petit et al. (2014)** · *Proc. SPIE 9148, 91480O* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-01069649) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2052847](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2052847)

## Summary

Describes the control scheme of SAXO, the extreme AO system of SPHERE, which must combine very high correction and image stabilization with robust autonomous operation. The deformable mirror is controlled by an Optimized Modal Gain Integrator, while the tip-tilt mirror uses an LQG control law that estimates and predicts turbulent angle of arrival and vibrations. A dedicated scheme continuously identifies and re-tunes the LQG models to match turbulence and vibration characteristics, making SPHERE the first operational system implementing LQG with automatic model adjustment. Final performance assessment and first on-sky validation are reported.
