# An Approximation of the Riccati Equation in Large-Scale Systems With Application to Adaptive Optics

**Massioni et al. (2015)** · *IEEE Trans. Control Syst. Technol. 23, 479* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-01075192) · DOI: [10.1109/TCST.2014.2336591](https://doi.org/10.1109/TCST.2014.2336591)

## Summary

Addresses the computational bottleneck of solving the discrete-time algebraic Riccati equation for Kalman filters and linear quadratic regulators when the number of states is very large, as in ELT adaptive optics where tens of thousands of states must be estimated. The authors show that for a special class of state-space systems the Riccati equation can be simplified by an approximation leading to a closed-form solution. This class includes the first- and second-order autoregressive (AR1, AR2) turbulence models widely used in AO. The accuracy and applicability of the approximation are verified a posteriori.
