# Tip–tilt disturbance model identification for Kalman-based control scheme: application to XAO and ELT systems

**Meimon et al. (2010)** · *JOSA A 27, A122* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/47661763_Tip-tilt_disturbance_model_identification_for_Kalman-based_control_scheme_application_to_XAO_and_ELT_systems) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.27.00A122](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.27.00A122)

## Summary

Addresses correction of tip-tilt disturbances to a small fraction of the diffraction-limited spot, which is critical for very and extremely large telescopes affected by mechanical vibration peaks and wind shake. LQG control gives optimal tip-tilt correction if the temporal model of the disturbance is known. The authors propose an unsupervised identification procedure for that model that needs neither an auxiliary system nor opening the loop. It is validated on synthetic data and on experimental data.
