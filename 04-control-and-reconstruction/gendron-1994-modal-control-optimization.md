# Astronomical adaptive optics. I. Modal control optimization

**Gendron & Léna (1994)** · *A&A 291, 337* · [Free full text](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1994A%26A...291..337G)

## Summary

Addresses optimization of an adaptive optics system for low photon flux, showing that final image quality can be improved by adapting the spatial and temporal correction to each observation. The wavefront is treated as a weighted sum of spatial modes, each with its own signal-to-noise ratio and correlation time, which leads to assigning a specific, optimized correction bandwidth (loop gain) to each mode. The paper proposes a practical scheme in which all quantities needed for the optimization are collected by the AO system itself in closed loop, making the method independent of theoretical assumptions about the turbulence.
