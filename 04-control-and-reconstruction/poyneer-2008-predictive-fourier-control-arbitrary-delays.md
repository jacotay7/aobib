# Predictive wavefront control for adaptive optics with arbitrary control loop delays

**Poyneer & Véran (2008)** · *JOSA A 25, 1486* · [Free full text](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/944297) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.25.001486](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.25.001486)

## Summary

Modifies the closed-loop state-space model for AO control to allow total loop delays that are non-integer multiples of the frame period. New forms of the Predictive Fourier Control Kalman filters are derived for arbitrary delays and are shown to be linear combinations of the whole-frame-delay terms, so that the controller structure is independent of the delay. System stability margins and residual error variance transition smoothly between integer-frame delays. The free copy is LLNL preprint UCRL-JRNL-236046 on OSTI.
