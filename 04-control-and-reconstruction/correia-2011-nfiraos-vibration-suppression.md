# Vibration suppression algorithms for NFIRAOS on TMT

**Correia et al. (2011)** · *Proc. AO4ELT2 (Victoria, 2011)* · [Free full text](https://ao4elt2.lesia.obspm.fr/sites/ao4elt2/IMG/pdf/068correia.pdf)

## Summary

Applies a multi-rate vibration suppression algorithm to NFIRAOS, the first-light multi-conjugate AO facility for the Thirty Meter Telescope. Numerical simulations consider vibration peaks between 2 and 225 Hz with variable widths based on Keck telemetry. The algorithm is compared with other vibration-suppression approaches for control-loop frame rates of 20-800 Hz, as required to sense natural guide stars over a wide range of magnitudes.
