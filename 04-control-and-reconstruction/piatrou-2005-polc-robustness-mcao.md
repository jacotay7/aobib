# Robustness study of the pseudo open-loop controller for multiconjugate adaptive optics

**Piatrou & Gilles (2005)** · *Applied Optics 44, 1003* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/7981917_Robustness_study_of_the_pseudo_open-loop_controller_for_multiconjugate_adaptive_optics) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.44.001003](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.44.001003)

## Summary

Studies how robust pseudo open-loop control (POLC) is to system errors, using the Gemini South 8 m multi-conjugate AO system as the example. The model of the AO system under POLC is extended to include misalignments, noise and calibration errors in the deformable mirrors and wavefront sensors, and compared with conventional least-squares control. Pole-placement analysis of the transfer functions and Monte Carlo simulations show that POLC remains stable and robust against very large system errors and outperforms least-squares control in this respect. Approximate stability margins, Strehl ratios and rms residuals over a 1 arcmin field are given for different types and levels of error.
