# Laboratory demonstration of the prediction of wind-blown turbulence by adaptive optics at 8 kHz with use of LQG control

**Poyneer et al. (2023)** · *Applied Optics 62, 1871* · [Free full text](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1973734) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.474730](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.474730)

## Summary

Reports laboratory results from LLAMAS, a low-latency adaptive optics testbed with 21 subapertures across the pupil designed to push achievable latencies and frame rates. A reformulated version of the LQG method Predictive Fourier Control is implemented and computes all modes in 30 microseconds. Wind-blown turbulence is produced with a turbulator mixing hot and ambient air. Closed-loop telemetry shows that wind-predictive LQG removes the characteristic butterfly pattern and reduces temporal error power by up to a factor of three for mid-spatial-frequency modes compared with an integral controller, with Strehl changes consistent with the error budget. The free copy is the accepted manuscript (LLNL-JRNL-839098) on OSTI.
