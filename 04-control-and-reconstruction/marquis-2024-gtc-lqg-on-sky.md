# First on-sky tests of LQG control for a 10m-class telescope: prelude on the Gran Telescopio Canarias adaptive optics system

**Marquis et al. (2024)** · *Proc. SPIE 13097, 130977Y* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-04734161) · DOI: [10.1117/12.3020099](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3020099)

## Summary

Presents first on-sky results of a full linear quadratic Gaussian (LQG) regulator on a 10-m class telescope, the Gran Telescopio Canarias AO system developed by the IAC. The paper describes the GTCAO system and the construction of the data-driven LQG regulator, implemented in DARC, the GTCAO real-time controller. Performance is compared with the baseline integrator using point-spread functions from the science camera and residual wavefront-sensor slopes. The on-sky results are reported as consistent with earlier on-bench experiments.
