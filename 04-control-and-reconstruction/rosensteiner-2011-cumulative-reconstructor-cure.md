# Cumulative Reconstructor: fast wavefront reconstruction algorithm for Extremely Large Telescopes

**Rosensteiner (2011)** · *JOSA A 28, 2132* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/51699441_Cumulative_Reconstructor_fast_wavefront_reconstruction_algorithm_for_Extremely_Large_Telescopes) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.28.002132](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.28.002132)

## Summary

Develops the Cumulative Reconstructor (CuRe), a direct method for reconstructing a wavefront from Shack-Hartmann measurements by cumulative summation of slopes along lines. The paper adapts the algorithm to realistic telescope geometries and discusses the transition from a modified Hudgin geometry to the Fried geometry. It analyses noise propagation and the computational complexity of the method. Numerical tests show it is very fast and accurate, which the author presents as making it suitable for adaptive optics on extremely large telescopes.
