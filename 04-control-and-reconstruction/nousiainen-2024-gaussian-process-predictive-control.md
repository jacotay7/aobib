# The power of prediction: spatiotemporal Gaussian process modeling for predictive control in slope-based wavefront sensing

**Nousiainen et al. (2024)** · *JATIS 10, 039001* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.18275) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.10.3.039001](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.10.3.039001)

## Summary

Studies the limits of predictive AO control under different imaging conditions using spatiotemporal Gaussian process models, which yield a predictive reconstructor that is optimal in the least-squares sense given past WFS data and atmospheric knowledge. For a Shack-Hartmann-based extreme AO instrument, perfect knowledge of frozen-flow evolution reduces residual phase variance by up to a factor of 3.5 relative to non-predictive reconstruction, and knowing only effective wind speed gives a factor of 2.3. Experimental utility is computed to assess how many past frames a predictor should use. An optimized selection of n past frames gives a further 10-15% RMS improvement over using the n most recent frames.
