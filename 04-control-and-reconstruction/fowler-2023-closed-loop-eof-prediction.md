# Closed-Loop Until Further Notice: Comparing Predictive Control Methods in Closed-Loop

**Fowler et al. (2023)** · *arXiv:2310.02514* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.02514)

## Summary

Notes that predictive control gives 5-10x RMS error reductions over integral control in simulation but less than 2x on sky, and attributes part of this gap to pseudo-open-loop (POL) reconstruction, which relies on assumptions about the deformable-mirror response and measurement accuracy that are hard to verify. The paper explores a closed-loop formulation of empirical orthogonal function (EOF) prediction that avoids POL reconstruction. Open- and closed-loop EOF methods are compared in simulation for perfect systems and for systems with an inaccurate model of the DM response.
