# Impact of time-variant turbulence behavior on prediction for adaptive optics systems

**van Kooten et al. (2019)** · *JOSA A 36, 731* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.06541) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.36.000731](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.36.000731)

## Summary

Studies how time-varying atmospheric turbulence affects wavefront prediction aimed at reducing the time-delay error of extreme AO. A stochastic model for wind speed is introduced to represent time-variant turbulence effects. A low-order, data-driven linear minimum mean square error predictor is tested for a near-infrared AO system under varying conditions. Varying wind can significantly degrade prediction performance relative to optimal, with the largest losses at high wind speeds.
