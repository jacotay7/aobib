# Advanced techniques for Fourier transform wavefront reconstruction

**Poyneer (2003)** · *Proc. SPIE 4839, 1023* · [Free full text](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/15002381) · DOI: [10.1117/12.459461](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.459461)

## Summary

Analyses the performance of Fourier-transform (FT) reconstructors in large adaptive optics systems with Shack-Hartmann sensors and a continuous deformable mirror. FT methods derived for point-based sensor geometries are adapted to continuous systems, and analysis and simulation show how to compensate for deformable-mirror/sensor misalignment and wavefront-sensor gain. Additional filtering methods for reducing noise are presented. All modifications are applied at the filtering stage, preserving the speed of FT reconstruction, and simulations of a large system show compensated FT methods matching or exceeding vector-matrix-multiply reconstruction. The free copy is LLNL preprint UCRL-JC-149568 on OSTI.
