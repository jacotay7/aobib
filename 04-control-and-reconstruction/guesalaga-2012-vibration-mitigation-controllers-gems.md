# Comparison of vibration mitigation controllers for adaptive optics systems

**Guesalaga et al. (2012)** · *Applied Optics 51, 4520* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/228443342_Comparison_of_vibration_mitigation_controllers_for_adaptive_optics_systems) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.51.004520](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.51.004520)

## Summary

Describes methods tested to mitigate vibrations affecting instruments on the Gemini South telescope. Spectral analysis of slope measurements from several wavefront sensors and an imager identified a persistent vibration at 55 Hz and occasional ones at 14 and 100 Hz. Kalman and H∞ controllers were designed and implemented in the tip-tilt loop of the multi-conjugate AO system, where both performed similarly and clearly better than the classical integrator at rejecting vibrations. The reduction of residual slope rms depended strongly on turbulence, wind and vibration conditions, from almost none in high wind to a factor of 5 with low wind and strong vibrations.
