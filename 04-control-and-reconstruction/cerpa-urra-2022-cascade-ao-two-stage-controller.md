# Cascade adaptive optics: contrast performance analysis of a two-stage controller by numerical simulations

**Cerpa-Urra et al. (2022)** · *JATIS 8, 019001* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.11690) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.8.1.019001](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.8.1.019001)

## Summary

Develops a dynamical model of a cascade adaptive optics system in which a second AO stage with its own wavefront sensor, deformable mirror and real-time controller is added to an existing extreme AO system, each stage controlled by a standard integrator. The control analysis shows how part of the disturbance is transferred from low to high temporal frequencies, with a detrimental effect of second-stage integrator overshoot, and suggests mitigations. Simulations of a Shack-Hartmann first stage and a smaller, faster second stage show an order-of-magnitude contrast improvement and a larger reduction in speckle decorrelation time. The authors present cascade AO as a relatively simple upgrade path for existing extreme AO systems.
