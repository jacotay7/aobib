# Optimal control law for classical and multiconjugate adaptive optics

**Le Roux et al. (2004)** · *JOSA A 21, 1261* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/8449889_Optimal_control_law_for_classical_and_multiconjugate_adaptive_optics) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.21.001261](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.21.001261)

## Summary

Proposes an optimal closed-loop control law for adaptive optics that uses both the spatial and the temporal statistics of turbulence. Prior knowledge of the turbulence and of wavefront-sensing noise is written as a state-space model, and the optimal phase estimate is obtained with a Kalman filter; the paper gives the system equations, the assumptions and the derivation of the control law. Its benefit is shown in numerical simulations of multi-conjugate AO on an 8 m class telescope in the near infrared. The authors also discuss classical single-conjugate AO, where they suggest the approach could matter most for future extreme AO systems. It is one of the founding papers of LQG control for AO.
