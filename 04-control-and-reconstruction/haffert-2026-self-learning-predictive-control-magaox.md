# On-sky demonstration of self-learning predictive control with MagAO-X

**Haffert et al. (2026)** · *Proc. SPIE 14150 (Adaptive Optics Systems X), 1415078* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24339)

## Summary

Describes a self-learning model predictive controller based on subspace predictive control (SPC), implemented with an online QR-decomposition-based recursive least-squares approach. The controller targets servo-lag residuals in low-order modes, which degrade coronagraph performance at the inner working angle. It has been used on sky for self-learning control of vibrations on the MagAO-X instrument, giving an average Strehl increase of 15 percent and reducing jitter to 0.9 mas. Implementation details and on-sky performance are discussed.
