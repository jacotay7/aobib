# Toward Strehl-optimizing adaptive optics controllers

**Gavel & Wiberg (2003)** · *Proc. SPIE 4839, 890* · [Free full text](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/15002235) · DOI: [10.1117/12.459684](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.459684)

## Summary

Notes that most implemented AO reconstructors and closed-loop control laws ignore the temporal correlation of Kolmogorov wavefronts and the modified statistics of the residual in closed loop. The paper presents a general framework for designing a dynamic controller/reconstructor whose objective is to maximize the mean closed-loop Strehl ratio over time. The design uses all previous data and exploits the spatio-temporal statistics of the turbulence and measurement noise, generalizing earlier predictive controllers that used one or two previous time steps. The free copy is LLNL preprint UCRL-JC-147283 on OSTI.
