# Advances in control of a pyramid single conjugate adaptive optics system

**Agapito et al. (2021)** · *MNRAS 508, 1745* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.06144) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stab2665](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stab2665)

## Summary

Reports control improvements for the SOUL pyramid-sensor single-conjugate AO system at the Large Binocular Telescope. The first is an improved tip/tilt temporal controller that recovers nominal correction in the presence of high-temporal-frequency resonances. The second is a modal gain optimization scheme, tested on sky for the first time on this system. The authors note the relevance of these developments for the pyramid-based first-light AO systems of the ELTs.
