# SAXO+, the second-stage adaptive optics for SPHERE: a review of six selected control algorithms and the results achieved to date

**Tallon et al. (2026)** · *Proc. SPIE 14150, 1415012* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-05729377) · DOI: [10.1117/12.3106037](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3106037)

## Summary

Reviews the control algorithms considered for SAXO+, the second-stage adaptive optics planned in cascade after SPHERE's extreme AO system, which uses a modulated pyramid wavefront sensor and a loop more than twice as fast as the current system. The baseline is an optimal modal gain integrator, and five alternatives are investigated: an LQG estimator (LQGSPH), a data-driven method (DD4AO), an iterative minimum-variance predictive reconstructor (TAO-SP), and two neural-network approaches (PO4AO and DSRL). The algorithms are compared using the same COMPASS simulator, with on-sky comparisons planned. The paper reports the current status of this comparison and lessons learned.
