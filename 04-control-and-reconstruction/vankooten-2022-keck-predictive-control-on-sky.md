# Predictive wavefront control on Keck II adaptive optics bench: on-sky coronagraphic results

**van Kooten et al. (2022)** · *JATIS 8, 029006* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.14164) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.8.2.029006](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.8.2.029006)

## Summary

Reports results of a predictive wavefront controller, which minimizes the mean-square wavefront error over the loop delay, implemented on the Keck II AO bench. Filter parameters are tuned using daytime laboratory and on-sky tests. On three nights the predictor reduced residual mean-square wavefront error compared with the leaky integrator used at Keck. Contrast improvements are reported for the first time with the NIRC2 L-band vortex coronagraph, with gains of up to 2 at 3 lambda/D and up to 3 at 3-7 lambda/D.
