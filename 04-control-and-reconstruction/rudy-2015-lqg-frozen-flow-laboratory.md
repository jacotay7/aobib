# A laboratory demonstration of an LQG technique for correcting frozen flow turbulence in adaptive optics systems

**Rudy et al. (2015)** · *arXiv:1504.03686 (LLNL-JRNL-667747)* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.03686)

## Summary

Presents laboratory verification of Predictive Fourier Control, an LQG (Kalman filter) controller that identifies correlated atmospheric motions due to layers of frozen-flow turbulence and predictively removes their effects in real time. In the laboratory, RMS residual wavefront error was reduced from 35.0 nm to 11.2 nm, about a factor of 3. The far-field Strehl ratio improved from 0.479 to 0.520.
