# First laboratory validation of vibration filtering with LQG control law for Adaptive Optics

**Petit et al. (2008)** · *Optics Express 16, 87* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-04511060) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.16.000087](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.16.000087)

## Summary

Presents the first experimental validation of vibration filtering with a linear quadratic Gaussian (LQG) control law in adaptive optics. A quasi-pure mechanical vibration is generated on a classical AO bench and filtered by the control law, improving Strehl ratio and image stability. The authors note that vibration filtering can be applied to any AO system and is of particular interest for extreme AO, for instance the SPHERE AO design.
