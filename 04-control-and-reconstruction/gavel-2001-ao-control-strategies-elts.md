# Adaptive optics control strategies for extremely large telescopes

**Gavel (2001)** · *SPIE conference, San Diego 2001 (LLNL preprint UCRL-JC-144864)* · [Free full text](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/15002890)

## Summary

Presents scaling laws and strawman error budgets for adaptive optics on 30-100 m class telescopes and discusses implications for component technology and computational architecture. Developments at the NSF Center for Adaptive Optics on high-actuator-count deformable mirrors, wavefront sensors and guide-star lasers are reviewed. The paper stresses that existing wavefront reconstruction algorithms scale in a computationally intractable way for ELTs and outlines the characteristics of new algorithmic approaches that show promise for scaling to ELT AO. The free copy is the LLNL preprint on OSTI.
