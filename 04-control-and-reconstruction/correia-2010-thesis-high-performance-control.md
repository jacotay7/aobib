# Conception de lois de commande à hautes performances pour l'optique adaptative des grands / très grands télescopes

**Correia (2010)** · *PhD thesis, Université Paris 13* · [Free full text](https://theses.hal.science/tel-00516866)

## Summary

Thesis (in French) on high-performance control laws for adaptive optics on large and extremely large telescopes with many degrees of freedom and real-time constraints. Zonal Fourier methods are studied for static wavefront reconstruction, and a strategy combining Fourier and iterative methods is developed to adapt LQG optimal control to large systems. The thesis then treats the temporal dynamics of large deformable mirrors, showing that for linear dynamics the minimum-variance controller is the solution of a discrete-time LQG problem. The approach is applied to tip-tilt mirror control for large telescopes and used to quantify the degradation of suboptimal controllers.
