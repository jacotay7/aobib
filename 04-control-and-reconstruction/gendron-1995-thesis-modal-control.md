# Optimisation de la commande modale en optique adaptative : applications à l'astronomie

**Gendron (1995)** · *PhD thesis, Université Denis Diderot (Paris 7)* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/tel-01418424)

## Summary

Thesis (in French) presenting a modal analysis of the main limitations of astronomical adaptive optics, namely measurement noise at low photon flux and anisoplanatism when the reference source is off-axis. The perturbed wavefront is decomposed onto spatial modes whose temporal characteristics, signal-to-noise ratio and angular correlation vary with mode order. The thesis shows how to exploit these properties by tuning the internal control parameters of the system, allowing the number of corrected degrees of freedom and the temporal correction to adapt to each observing condition. The approach is validated on the ComeOnPlus astronomical AO system.
