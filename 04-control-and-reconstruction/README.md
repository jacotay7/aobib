# 04 · Control, Reconstruction & Real-Time Computing

This folder covers how wavefront-sensor measurements are turned into deformable-mirror commands. That includes the estimation problem (least-squares, minimum-variance/MMSE and MAP reconstructors, regularization, unsensed modes such as waffle), and the fast algorithms that make reconstruction tractable for systems with thousands of actuators: Fourier-transform reconstructors, FRiM/ASAP sparse priors, and iterative and wavelet solvers. It also covers temporal control: integrators with optimized modal gains, LQG/Kalman and minimum-variance controllers, predictive and data-driven controllers (Predictive Fourier Control, EOF, subspace methods), and rejection of telescope vibrations and windshake. The last part is computing: real-time computer hardware and software (CPU, GPU, FPGA and many-core platforms; DARC, cacao and other RTC frameworks).

Wide-field tomographic reconstruction belongs in [05](../05-wide-field-and-tomographic-ao/README.md), interaction-matrix calibration and telemetry-based identification in [10](../10-calibration-and-system-identification/README.md), and neural-network or reinforcement-learning controllers in [11](../11-machine-learning-and-ai/README.md). Many foundational reconstruction and control papers (Fried, Hudgin, Wallner, Ellerbroek's sparse MV reconstructors, CuRe, the original Predictive Fourier Control, Le Roux et al. 2004) have no legal free full text. They are listed in the paywalled list instead of here. Where possible, this folder links free preprints (OSTI, HAL, arXiv) or theses that cover the same work.

## Subtopics

- **Reconstruction theory & estimation**: least-squares vs. Wiener/MMSE reconstruction, waffle and unsensed modes, aliasing, super-resolution
- **Fast & iterative reconstructors**: FTR, FRiM/ASAP, conjugate-gradient and Kaczmarz solvers, wavelet (FEWHA) reconstructors
- **Servo control & modal gain optimization**: optimized modal gain integrators, on-line gain tuning
- **LQG, Kalman & minimum-variance control**: state-space formulations, mirror dynamics, large-scale Kalman filtering
- **Predictive & data-driven control**: Predictive Fourier Control, EOF, subspace predictive control, on-sky tests
- **Vibration & windshake rejection**: LQG vibration filtering, vibration characterization, accelerometers
- **Control architectures (POLC, woofer-tweeter, cascade)**: pseudo-open-loop implementation, cascade/two-stage AO, multi-WFS control
- **Real-time computers & RTC software**: DARC, cacao, FPGA/many-core/GPU platforms, Python/Julia RTCs
- **Overviews & controller comparisons**: surveys and integrator-vs-LQG/algorithm comparisons
