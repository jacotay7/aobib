# On-sky vibration environment for the Gemini Planet Imager and mitigation effort

**Hartung et al. (2014)** · *Proc. SPIE 9148, 91480N* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.7893) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2057200](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2057200)

## Summary

Gives an overview of investigations into the vibration environment of the Gemini Planet Imager after its first light at Gemini South in November 2013. GPI's coronagraphic performance is sensitive to residual tip-tilt and wavefront error, with a design goal of 5 mas RMS tip/tilt. Data from the GPI AO module and from accelerometers placed at several locations on the telescope structure are used to understand vibration sources and magnitudes. Mitigation options include passive or active damping and Kalman filtering of specific frequencies in the AO loop, and the status and results of mitigation efforts are reported.
