# Vibration control of ELTs

**Pott et al. (2011)** · *Proc. AO4ELT2 (Victoria, 2011)* · [Free full text](https://ao4elt2.lesia.obspm.fr/sites/ao4elt2/IMG/pdf/081pott.pdf)

## Summary

Summarizes the vibration-control strategies developed at MPIA for LINC-NIRVANA at the Large Binocular Telescope, viewed as a pathfinder for ELT AO imagers. The strategies include accelerometer-based real-time vibration measurements, feedforward and feedback optical path control, predictive filtering, resonance-insensitive active control of actuators and a dynamical model of the full telescope. The experience is intended to feed the structural AO design of the MICADO imager for the ELT.
