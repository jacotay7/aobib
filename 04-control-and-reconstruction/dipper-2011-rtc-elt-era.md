# AO real-time control systems for the ELT era

**Dipper et al. (2011)** · *Proc. AO4ELT2 (Victoria, 2011)* · [Free full text](https://ao4elt2.lesia.obspm.fr/sites/ao4elt2/IMG/pdf/040dipper.pdf)

## Summary

Reviews designs investigated at Durham for future AO real-time computers, whose processing requirements may not be met simply by next-generation CPU systems. Options in which CPUs are accelerated or replaced by GPU- and FPGA-based hardware are compared, addressing which technologies suit which applications. Plans for laboratory and on-sky testing with the CANARY demonstrator at the William Herschel Telescope are presented, together with initial results and a real-time control design for the proposed EAGLE instrument for the E-ELT.
