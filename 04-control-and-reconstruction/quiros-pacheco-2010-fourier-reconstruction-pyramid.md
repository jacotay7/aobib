# Fourier transform-wavefront reconstruction for the pyramid wavefront sensor

**Quirós-Pacheco et al. (2010)** · *Proc. AO4ELT1 (Paris, 2009), 07005* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-03184155) · DOI: [10.1051/ao4elt/201007005](https://doi.org/10.1051/ao4elt/201007005)

## Summary

Investigates the application of Fourier-transform reconstruction techniques to the pyramid wavefront sensor. A preliminary study based on end-to-end simulations of an adaptive optics system with about 40x40 subapertures and actuators is presented. The performance of the Fourier-transform reconstructor is found to be of the same order as that of a conventional matrix-vector-multiply reconstructor, suggesting FTR as a computationally efficient option for pyramid-based systems.
