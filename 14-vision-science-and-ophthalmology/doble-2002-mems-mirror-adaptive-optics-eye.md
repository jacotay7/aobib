# Use of a microelectromechanical mirror for adaptive optics in the human eye

**Doble et al. (2002)** · *Optics Letters 27, 1537* · [Free full text](https://aria.cvs.rochester.edu/papers/doble_optlet2002.pdf) · DOI: [10.1364/OL.27.001537](https://doi.org/10.1364/OL.27.001537)

## Summary

Presents what the authors describe as the first closed-loop results with a surface-micromachined microelectromechanical (MEMS) deformable mirror in a vision AO system. The motivation is the cost and size of conventional deformable mirrors, whose large apertures require high pupil magnification. For a 4.6 mm pupil the MEMS mirror performed comparably to a Xinetics mirror. For a 6.0 mm pupil the residual rms wavefront error dropped from 0.36 to 0.12 micrometres and individual photoreceptors were resolved 1 degree from the fovea.
