# Adaptive optics scanning laser ophthalmoscope with integrated wide-field retinal imaging and tracking

**Ferguson et al. (2010)** · *JOSA A 27, A265* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3071649/) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.27.00A265](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.27.00A265)

## Summary

Describes an AOSLO that integrates a wide-field line-scanning ophthalmoscope and a closed-loop optical retinal tracker, so that AOSLO rasters are stabilized by the tracking mirrors. A large-spherical-mirror optical interface and a large-stroke deformable mirror allow the AO field to be steered and corrected anywhere within a field of more than 25 degrees. In most subjects, across a range of refractive errors, image contrast was measurable near the diffraction limit. Hardware tracking was usually better than 10 micrometres rms, not yet single-cone precision, but improved image quality and the number of frames usable by software registration, and simplified montage acquisition.
