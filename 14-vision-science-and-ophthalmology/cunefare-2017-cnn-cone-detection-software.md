# Open source software for automatic detection of cone photoreceptors in adaptive optics ophthalmoscopy using convolutional neural networks

**Cunefare et al. (2017)** · *Scientific Reports 7, 6620* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5529414/) · DOI: [10.1038/s41598-017-07103-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-07103-0)

## Summary

Presents a convolutional-neural-network method for automatically detecting cone photoreceptors in AOSLO images, intended to replace time-consuming manual grading and rule-based algorithms that may not transfer between modalities. The network was trained and validated on separate data sets of confocal and split-detector AOSLO images. Its performance closely matched manual grading and was comparable to earlier automated methods tuned for specific applications, without modality-specific modifications. The software and the training and testing data sets were released openly.
