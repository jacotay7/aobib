# Cone photoreceptor classification in the living human eye from photostimulation-induced phase dynamics

**Zhang et al. (2019)** · *PNAS 116, 7951* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6475411/) · DOI: [10.1073/pnas.1816360116](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1816360116)

## Summary

Uses phase-sensitive AO-OCT to measure photostimulation-induced optical phase changes in cone outer segments and shows that these changes distinguish S, M and L cones. Images were acquired at high speed, sampling phase dynamics at up to 3 kHz, and phase changes were localized to the outer segment where photoactivation occurs. The phase responses span timescales from milliseconds to seconds, exposing the phototransduction cascade and downstream effects. Cones were classified in subjects with normal colour vision, in a deuteranope, and at several macular locations, with higher accuracy and speed than retinal densitometry.
