# Review of adaptive optics OCT (AO-OCT): principles and applications for retinal imaging [Invited]

**Pircher & Zawadzki (2017)** · *Biomedical Optics Express 8, 2536* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5480497/) · DOI: [10.1364/BOE.8.002536](https://doi.org/10.1364/BOE.8.002536)

## Summary

Tutorial-style review of AO-OCT for retinal imaging. It explains why the eye's limited numerical aperture restricts axial sectioning in AO fundus cameras and AOSLO to tens of micrometres, and how combining AO with OCT gives 2-5 micrometre axial sectioning and near-isotropic volumetric resolution. The article summarizes technical aspects of AO-OCT, reviews implementations from different groups and some clinical applications, and covers newer developments including computational AO-OCT and wavefront-sensorless AO-OCT.
