# A review of adaptive optics optical coherence tomography: technical advances, scientific applications, and the future

**Jonnal et al. (2016)** · *IOVS 57, OCT51* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4968917/) · DOI: [10.1167/iovs.16-19103](https://doi.org/10.1167/iovs.16-19103)

## Summary

Review of roughly 15 years of work combining the transverse resolution of AO with the axial resolution of OCT. The authors survey antecedent work, milestones in AO-OCT technology, improvements in resolution, speed, acquisition and post-processing, and the discoveries about retinal anatomy, function and optical properties that followed. They discuss research areas into which AO-OCT may expand and nascent applications. The review positions AO-OCT as a companion to clinical OCT for interpreting clinical findings at the cellular scale.
