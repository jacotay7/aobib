# Adaptive optics with a magnetic deformable mirror: applications in the human eye

**Fernández et al. (2006)** · *Optics Express 14, 8900* · [Free full text](https://opg.optica.org/oe/fulltext.cfm?uri=oe-14-20-8900) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.14.008900](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.14.008900)

## Summary

Characterizes a 52-actuator magnetic deformable mirror (MIRAO 52, Imagine Eyes) for ophthalmic use. The influence functions showed a linear response to applied voltage, and the mirror reproduced Zernike polynomials up to fifth order with ranges covering those of normal and highly aberrated eyes, including keratoconic data. Combined with an achromatizing lens, the mirror corrected monochromatic and chromatic aberrations for broadband light (130 nm FWHM at 800 nm) in five subjects, including a myopic eye with a near-pathological cornea. Benefits of simultaneous monochromatic and chromatic correction are discussed for retinal imaging and for vision testing.
