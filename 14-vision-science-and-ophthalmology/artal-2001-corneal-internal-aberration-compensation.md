# Compensation of corneal aberrations by the internal optics in the human eye

**Artal et al. (2001)** · *Journal of Vision 1, 1* · [Free full text](https://jov.arvojournals.org/article.aspx?doi=10.1167/1.1.1) · DOI: [10.1167/1.1.1](https://doi.org/10.1167/1.1.1)

## Summary

The study compared wavefront aberrations of the anterior corneal surface, the complete eye and the internal ocular optics (dominated by the crystalline lens), measured independently in young subjects. The aberrations of both the cornea and the internal optics were larger than those of the whole eye. The authors conclude that the anterior cornea and the internal optics partially compensate each other's aberrations, improving the retinal image. They note implications for refractive surgery, including the limitations of corneal topography alone as a guide and the value of whole-eye wavefront sensing.
