# Dynamics of the eye's wave aberration

**Hofer et al. (2001)** · *JOSA A 18, 497* · [Free full text](https://aria.cvs.rochester.edu/papers/Hofer_JOSA01.pdf) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.18.000497](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.18.000497)

## Summary

A real-time Hartmann-Shack wavefront sensor sampling at up to 60 Hz, using a low-coherence source and a scanning system to reduce speckle in short exposures, was built to measure the temporal behaviour of the eye's aberrations. Temporal power spectra were computed for wavefront rms, Seidel aberrations and 32 Zernike coefficients in three subjects with natural and paralyzed accommodation. Fluctuations were present in all aberrations, not only defocus, and the spectra of higher-order terms fell at roughly 4 dB per octave. The authors estimate that a perfect AO system with a 1-2 Hz closed-loop bandwidth would suffice for diffraction-limited imaging through a dilated pupil, if the fluctuations are of corneal or lenticular origin.
