# Wavefront sensorless adaptive optics ophthalmoscopy in the human eye

**Hofer et al. (2011)** · *Optics Express 19, 14160* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3178895/) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.19.014160](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.19.014160)

## Summary

Demonstrates real-time (25 Hz) wavefront-sensorless AO imaging of the living human eye in a confocal AOSLO. A stochastic parallel gradient descent algorithm directly maximized the mean intensity of retinal image frames, and results were compared with wavefront-sensor-based control in the same instrument. The motivation includes wavefront sensor noise and fidelity limits and interference of the sensor beacon with visual experiments. Through natural pupils both methods gave comparable intensities; through dilated pupils sensor-based control generally gave higher intensity, while sensorless control gave higher average image contrast.
