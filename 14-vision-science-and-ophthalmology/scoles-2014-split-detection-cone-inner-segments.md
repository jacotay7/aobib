# In vivo imaging of human cone photoreceptor inner segments

**Scoles et al. (2014)** · *IOVS 55, 4244* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4095721/) · DOI: [10.1167/iovs.14-14542](https://doi.org/10.1167/iovs.14-14542)

## Summary

Introduces non-confocal split-detector AOSLO, a differential-phase detection scheme in which light outside the confocal aperture is split into two halves and their normalized difference is computed; it reveals cone inner segments whether or not a waveguiding outer segment is present. The method was tested in two healthy subjects and four with achromatopsia, and inner-segment diameters measured in vivo agreed with ex vivo histology from five donor eyes. Many foveal and parafoveal cones with apparently intact inner segments were identified in achromatopsia, where confocal imaging shows little. The authors propose the technique for selecting patients for gene therapy trials and for other degenerations in which outer segments are lost first.
