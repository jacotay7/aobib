# In vivo fluorescence imaging of primate retinal ganglion cells and retinal pigment epithelial cells

**Gray et al. (2006)** · *Optics Express 14, 7144* · [Free full text](https://opg.optica.org/oe/fulltext.cfm?uri=oe-14-16-7144) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.14.007144](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.14.007144)

## Summary

Describes a fluorescence AO scanning laser ophthalmoscope combining confocal detection, adaptive optics and multispectral imaging for the living primate retina. Single ganglion cells and their axons were imaged after retrograde transport of fluorescent dye injected into the monkey lateral geniculate nucleus. A dual-channel method uses a simultaneously recorded reflectance channel to register frames, so that very weak fluorescence can be averaged despite eye motion. With this approach the smallest capillaries were resolved in fluorescein angiography and the RPE cell mosaic was imaged via lipofuscin autofluorescence.
