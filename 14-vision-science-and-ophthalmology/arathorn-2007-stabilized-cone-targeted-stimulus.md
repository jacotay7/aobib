# Retinally stabilized cone-targeted stimulus delivery

**Arathorn et al. (2007)** · *Optics Express 15, 13731* · [Free full text](https://opg.optica.org/oe/fulltext.cfm?uri=oe-15-21-13731) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.15.013731](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.15.013731)

## Summary

Demonstrates projection of aberration-corrected stimuli onto chosen retinal locations in an AOSLO, using real-time retinal image motion signals to drive high-speed modulation of the scanning laser. In three subjects with good fixation, stimulus placement accuracy averaged 0.26 arcmin (about 1.3 micrometres), smaller than foveal cone spacing. The work also demonstrates real-time correction of AOSLO image distortions with intraframe accuracy of about 7 arcsec. This capability underlies later single-cone psychophysics experiments.
