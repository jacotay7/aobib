# Adaptive optics visual simulators: a review of recent optical designs and applications [Invited]

**Marcos et al. (2022)** · *Biomedical Optics Express 13, 6508* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9774875/) · DOI: [10.1364/BOE.473458](https://doi.org/10.1364/BOE.473458)

## Summary

Review of adaptive optics visual simulators, instruments that control the eye's aberrations to study visual performance or to test optical corrections before they are manufactured. It describes simulators developed in laboratories around the world, their optical designs and active elements, and key applications. Applications covered include the spatial limits of vision, interactions of monochromatic and chromatic aberrations, neural adaptation to blur, and non-invasive testing of corrections for presbyopia, myopia and corneal irregularities. The authors discuss current trends and challenges, including clinical adoption.
