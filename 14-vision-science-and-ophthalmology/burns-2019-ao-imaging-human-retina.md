# Adaptive optics imaging of the human retina

**Burns et al. (2019)** · *Prog. Retin. Eye Res. 68, 1* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6347528/) · DOI: [10.1016/j.preteyeres.2018.08.002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.preteyeres.2018.08.002)

## Summary

Review of human adaptive optics retinal imaging with an emphasis on the adaptive optics scanning laser ophthalmoscope (AOSLO). It covers the background and general approaches to AO retinal imaging and the technology involved, including the combination of AO with OCT, fluorescence and reflectance imaging. The authors discuss imaging modes that exploit differences in the scattering properties of retinal tissue, which they note are sensitive to disease. The second half concentrates on using AO imaging to study retinal structure and function, including photoreceptors, vasculature and inner retina, and on prospects for clinical trials.
