# Adaptive optics scanning laser ophthalmoscopy

**Roorda et al. (2002)** · *Optics Express 10, 405* · [Free full text](https://opg.optica.org/oe/fulltext.cfm?uri=oe-10-9-405) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.10.000405](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.10.000405)

## Summary

Presents the first scanning laser ophthalmoscope equipped with adaptive optics to measure and correct the eye's higher-order aberrations. Correcting aberrations increased both lateral and axial resolution, so that the confocal instrument could optically section retinal tissue in vivo. The system was used to image cone photoreceptors, nerve fibre bundles and the flow of white blood cells in retinal capillaries.
