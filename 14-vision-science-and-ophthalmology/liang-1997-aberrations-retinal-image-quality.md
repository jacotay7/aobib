# Aberrations and retinal image quality of the normal human eye

**Liang & Williams (1997)** · *JOSA A 14, 2873* · [Free full text](https://aria.cvs.rochester.edu/papers/liang1997aberrations.pdf) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.14.002873](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.14.002873)

## Summary

The authors built a Hartmann-Shack wavefront sensor to measure both the classical and the irregular (higher-order) aberrations of the eye and showed that its measurements are repeatable. The modulation transfer function computed from the sensor was compared with double-pass and interferometric measurements on the same observers and found to be in fair agreement. Irregular aberrations beyond defocus, astigmatism, coma and spherical aberration had little effect on image quality for a 3 mm pupil but a substantial effect for a 7.3 mm pupil. Aberration patterns varied between subjects but were correlated between left and right eyes.
