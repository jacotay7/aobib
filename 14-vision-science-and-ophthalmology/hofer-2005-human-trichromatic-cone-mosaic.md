# Organization of the human trichromatic cone mosaic

**Hofer et al. (2005)** · *Journal of Neuroscience 25, 9669* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6725723/) · DOI: [10.1523/JNEUROSCI.2414-05.2005](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.2414-05.2005)

## Summary

Uses adaptive optics imaging with retinal densitometry to map S, M and L cones in eight human foveal mosaics. L:M ratios among males with normal colour vision ranged from 1.1:1 to 16.5:1, and a protan carrier had a ratio of 0.37:1, while S-cone densities were nearly identical across subjects. L:M estimates correlated with flicker-photometric ERG measurements in the same eyes. Arrangements of L and M cones were highly disordered, but three subjects showed departures from a strictly random assignment, including local clumping of like cones.
