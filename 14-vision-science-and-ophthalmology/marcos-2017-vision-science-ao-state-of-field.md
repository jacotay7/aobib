# Vision science and adaptive optics, the state of the field

**Marcos et al. (2017)** · *Vision Research 132, 3* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5437977/) · DOI: [10.1016/j.visres.2017.01.006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.visres.2017.01.006)

## Summary

Multi-author survey prepared by the editors of a Vision Research feature issue, structured as answers from many groups to a set of questions about adaptive optics in vision science. The first part covers AO for psychophysical measurement of visual function and for improving the optics of the eye. The second part addresses AO retinal imaging and its role in basic and applied research. The third part examines how AO is used in studies of the neurophysiology of the visual system.
