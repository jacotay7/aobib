# High-speed volumetric imaging of cone photoreceptors with adaptive optics spectral-domain optical coherence tomography

**Zhang et al. (2006)** · *Optics Express 14, 4380* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2605071/) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.14.004380](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.14.004380)

## Summary

Reports what the authors describe as the first observations of the three-dimensional morphology of cone photoreceptors in the living human retina. The AO spectral-domain OCT camera used a Shack-Hartmann sensor and a bimorph mirror (closed loop at 12 Hz) placed between the scanners and the eye, and acquired 75,000 A-scans per second. Small volumes were acquired quickly to limit motion artefacts. Regularly spaced bright spots at the inner/outer segment junction and at the posterior tips of the outer segments were correlated with each other and with expected cone spacing, but not with the RPE.
