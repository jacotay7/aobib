# Imaging single cells in the living retina

**Williams (2011)** · *Vision Research 51, 1379* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3189497/) · DOI: [10.1016/j.visres.2011.05.002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.visres.2011.05.002)

## Summary

Retrospective review of how retinal imaging moved from a macroscopic view of the fundus to resolving individual cells in the living eye over roughly 25 years. It describes the contributions of confocal scanning laser ophthalmoscopy, optical coherence tomography and adaptive optics fundus imaging. The review summarizes studies these tools enabled, including imaging of the trichromatic cone mosaic, rods, retinal pigment epithelium, ganglion cells and blood flow, and discusses directions for cellular-level imaging of retinal function.
