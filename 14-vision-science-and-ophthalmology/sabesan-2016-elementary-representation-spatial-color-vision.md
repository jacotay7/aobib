# The elementary representation of spatial and color vision in the human retina

**Sabesan et al. (2016)** · *Sci. Adv. 2, e1600797* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5023317/) · DOI: [10.1126/sciadv.1600797](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.1600797)

## Summary

Records the colour sensations produced by stimulating individual spectrally identified L, M and S cones near the fovea with small spots of light delivered through an AOSLO. Two populations emerged: a smaller group of cones mainly associated with chromatic sensations and a larger group linked to achromatic percepts. Red and green sensations were driven mainly by L and M cones respectively, though both types also produced achromatic percepts, and responses for a given cone were consistent over months. Cones surrounded by a spectrally opponent neighbourhood were not more likely to signal colour. The authors interpret the results as consistent with separate high-resolution achromatic and lower-resolution chromatic pathways from the first synapse.
