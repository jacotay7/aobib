# Adaptive-optics imaging of human cone photoreceptor distribution

**Chui et al. (2008)** · *JOSA A 25, 3021* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2738986/) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.25.003021](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.25.003021)

## Summary

An AOSLO was used to image the cone mosaic over the central 10-12 degrees in four normal subjects, and montages were processed to locate individual cones. Cone density ranged from about 40,000 cones/mm2 at 1 degree to about 10,000 cones/mm2 at 7 degrees, while the smallest foveal cones were not resolved. All subjects showed a slight cone streak, and packing regularity decreased from fovea to periphery. Densities varied between subjects and anisotropy varied locally; montages and cone coordinates were released for download.
