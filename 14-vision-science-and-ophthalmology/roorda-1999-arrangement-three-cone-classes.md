# The arrangement of the three cone classes in the living human eye

**Roorda & Williams (1999)** · *Nature 397, 520* · [Free full text](https://aria.cvs.rochester.edu/papers/Roorda99nature.pdf) · DOI: [10.1038/17383](https://doi.org/10.1038/17383)

## Summary

Combines adaptive optics flood-illumination imaging with retinal densitometry (imaging before and after selective photopigment bleaching) to classify individual short-, medium- and long-wavelength-sensitive cones in the living human eye. The authors present what they describe as the first images of the arrangement of S, M and L cones in vivo. The L:M cone ratio differed markedly between two male subjects with normal colour vision, and both mosaics contained large patches lacking either M or L cones. The authors discuss the consequences of this arrangement for recovering chromatic and luminance detail.
