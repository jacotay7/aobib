# Adaptive-optics optical coherence tomography for high-resolution and high-speed 3D retinal in vivo imaging

**Zawadzki et al. (2005)** · *Optics Express 13, 8532* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2605068/) · DOI: [10.1364/OPEX.13.008532](https://doi.org/10.1364/OPEX.13.008532)

## Summary

Combines Fourier-domain OCT with a closed-loop AO system using a Hartmann-Shack wavefront sensor and a bimorph deformable mirror. AO correction of the eye's aberrations gave about 4 micrometres lateral resolution while retaining the roughly 6 micrometres axial resolution of OCT, for a 3D resolution of about 4 x 4 x 6 micrometres. The instrument produced volumetric images of retinal structures in vivo, including microscopic blood vessels and the cone photoreceptor mosaic. It is one of the early Fourier-domain AO-OCT systems.
