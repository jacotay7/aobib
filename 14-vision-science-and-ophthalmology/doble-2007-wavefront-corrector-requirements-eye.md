# Requirements for discrete actuator and segmented wavefront correctors for aberration compensation in two large populations of human eyes

**Doble et al. (2007)** · *Applied Optics 46, 4501* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2654185/) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.46.004501](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.46.004501)

## Summary

Models the performance of discrete-actuator, segmented piston-only and segmented piston/tip/tilt wavefront correctors using ocular aberrations measured in two large populations of normal eyes. Wavefront errors reached 53 micrometres depending on pupil diameter (2-7.5 mm) and refractive state. The number of actuators required for diffraction-limited imaging was computed for three pupil sizes, three second-order aberration states and four imaging wavelengths between 0.4 and 1.0 micrometres. Requirements ranged from a few actuators across the pupil for continuous-facesheet correctors to more than 100 for piston-only segmented devices.
