# Adaptive-optics ultrahigh-resolution optical coherence tomography

**Hermann et al. (2004)** · *Optics Letters 29, 2142* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/8254462_Adaptive-optics_ultrahigh-resolution_optical_coherence_tomography) · DOI: [10.1364/OL.29.002142](https://doi.org/10.1364/OL.29.002142)

## Summary

Reports the first combination, to the authors' knowledge, of ultrahigh-resolution optical coherence tomography with adaptive optics for in vivo retinal imaging, giving about 3 μm axial and 5–10 μm transverse resolution. A compact closed-loop AO system, with a 30 Hz Hartmann-Shack wavefront sensor and a 37-actuator membrane deformable mirror, is coupled to an OCT system based on a commercial instrument and a broadband Ti:sapphire laser. Closed-loop correction of ocular and system aberrations leaves about 0.1 μm rms of residual wavefront error over a 3.68 mm pupil. Compared with earlier ultrahigh-resolution OCT, transverse resolution improves two to three times, and the signal-to-noise ratio improves by up to 9 dB.
