# Reflective afocal broadband adaptive optics scanning ophthalmoscope

**Dubra & Sulai (2011)** · *Biomedical Optics Express 2, 1757* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3114240/) · DOI: [10.1364/BOE.2.001757](https://doi.org/10.1364/BOE.2.001757)

## Summary

Describes the optical design of a broadband AO scanning ophthalmoscope built from four afocal telescopes made of pairs of off-axis spherical mirrors folded out of plane. The non-planar folding simultaneously reduces pupil-plane and image-plane astigmatism, which lowers residual wavefront rms and scan-induced beam wander and gives diffraction-limited performance over a 3 diopter vergence range. This range allows any combination of sources in the 450-850 nm band to image different retinal layers simultaneously. The foveal cone mosaic was resolved with two superluminescent diodes at 680 and 796 nm.
