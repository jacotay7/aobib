# Imaging single-cell blood flow in the smallest to largest vessels in the living retina

**Joseph et al. (2019)** · *eLife 8, e45077* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6516827/) · DOI: [10.7554/eLife.45077](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.45077)

## Summary

Uses an AOSLO in which repeated one-dimensional line scans across a vessel (15 kHz line rate) produce space-time images of individual moving blood cells, to measure single-cell blood flow in the retina of the C57BL/6J mouse without surgery or contrast dye. Flow was measured over four orders of magnitude (0.0002-1.55 microlitres per minute) across vessel diameters from 3.2 to 45.8 micrometres. The paper describes the ultrafast imaging approach, analysis pipeline and automated measurement of millions of blood cell speeds.
