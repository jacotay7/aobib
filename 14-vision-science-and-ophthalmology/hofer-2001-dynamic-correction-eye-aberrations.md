# Improvement in retinal image quality with dynamic correction of the eye's aberrations

**Hofer et al. (2001)** · *Optics Express 8, 631* · [Free full text](https://opg.optica.org/oe/fulltext.cfm?uri=oe-8-11-631) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.8.000631](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.8.000631)

## Summary

Describes a closed-loop ocular adaptive optics system sampling the wave aberration at up to 30 Hz and reports how much dynamic correction improves retinal image quality. Correction converged in 0.25-0.5 s, with residual rms wavefront errors as low as 0.1 micrometres over a 6.8 mm pupil. The system corrected fluctuations in Zernike modes up to fifth order with temporal components up to about 0.8 Hz, in agreement with theoretical predictions. Compared with static correction of higher-order aberrations, dynamic correction increased the point-spread-function Strehl ratio nearly threefold and the contrast of cone photoreceptor images by 33%.
