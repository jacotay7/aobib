# Automated identification of cone photoreceptors in adaptive optics retinal images

**Li & Roorda (2007)** · *JOSA A 24, 1358* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/6399936_Automated_identification_of_cone_photoreceptors_in_adaptive_optics_retinal_images) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.24.001358](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.24.001358)

## Summary

Presents an automated algorithm for locating individual cone photoreceptors in adaptive optics retinal images, replacing slow manual labelling; its design uses the optical fibre-like properties of cones. On 2153 manually labelled cones from six images, the method correctly identified 94.1%, with agreement between 92.7% and 96.2% per image and disagreement on 1.2% to 9.1% of cones. Voronoi analysis of large retinal montages confirmed the generally hexagonal packing of cones and the variation of cone density across the retina. The authors conclude that automated cone identification is reliable and practical.
