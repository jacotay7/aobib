# In vivo imaging of the photoreceptor mosaic in retinal dystrophies and correlations with visual function

**Choi et al. (2006)** · *IOVS 47, 2080* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2583223/) · DOI: [10.1167/iovs.05-0997](https://doi.org/10.1167/iovs.05-0997)

## Summary

Uses the UC Davis AO flood-illumination fundus camera to image the cone mosaic in five patients with various retinal dystrophies and three controls, and compares cone density with visual fields, multifocal ERG and contrast sensitivity measured at the same locations. Diseased retinas showed extensive dark regions between groups of photoreceptors where no cones were visible, which were absent in healthy eyes. Functional losses correlated significantly with reduced cone density. The authors propose AO imaging for tracking disease progression and for cases where functional tests are ambiguous.
