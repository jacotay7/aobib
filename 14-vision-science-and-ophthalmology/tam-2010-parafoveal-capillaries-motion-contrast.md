# Noninvasive visualization and analysis of parafoveal capillaries in humans

**Tam et al. (2010)** · *IOVS 51, 1691* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2868418/) · DOI: [10.1167/iovs.09-4483](https://doi.org/10.1167/iovs.09-4483)

## Summary

Demonstrates non-invasive mapping of the human parafoveal capillary network from AOSLO videos, using processing that enhances motion contrast from flowing blood cells instead of an injected dye. Montages of parafoveal capillaries were built, and the capillary network and foveal avascular zone (FAZ) were extracted with image-analysis algorithms. The average FAZ area was 0.323 mm2 (effective diameter 633 micrometres), and capillary density next to the FAZ did not vary with direction. A comparison with fluorescein angiography was made in one subject.
