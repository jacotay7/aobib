# Mapping the perceptual grain of the human retina

**Harmening et al. (2014)** · *Journal of Neuroscience 34, 5667* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3988416/) · DOI: [10.1523/JNEUROSCI.5191-13.2014](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.5191-13.2014)

## Summary

Combines AO microstimulation with high-speed eye tracking to probe visual function at the level of individual cone photoreceptors in living eyes. Psychometric functions were measured for cone-sized stimuli targeted to single cones. Sensitivity varied across the cone mosaic in a way consistent with a simple model of cone light capture. The authors conclude that, once ocular blur is removed, individual photoreceptors can shape perception and the exact placement of stimuli on cones sets initial retinal limits on spatial vision.
