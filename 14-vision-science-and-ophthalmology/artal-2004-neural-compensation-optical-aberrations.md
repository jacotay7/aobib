# Neural compensation for the eye's optical aberrations

**Artal et al. (2004)** · *Journal of Vision 4, 4* · [Free full text](https://jov.arvojournals.org/article.aspx?doi=10.1167/4.4.4) · DOI: [10.1167/4.4.4](https://doi.org/10.1167/4.4.4)

## Summary

Tests whether the visual system compensates for the eye's own aberrations by using adaptive optics to manipulate them. Subjects viewed a stimulus through an AO system that either recreated their own aberrations or a rotated version of the same aberrations. For all five subjects, the stimulus appeared sharper with their own aberrations than with the rotated version. The authors interpret this as evidence that the neural visual system is adapted to the eye's particular aberrations, and note possible consequences for customized refractive correction.
