# The relationship between visual resolution and cone spacing in the human fovea

**Rossi & Roorda (2010)** · *Nat. Neurosci. 13, 156* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2822659/) · DOI: [10.1038/nn.2465](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.2465)

## Summary

Combines AOSLO imaging of the cone mosaic with psychophysical resolution measurements made through the same AO-corrected optics at a range of locations across the fovea. Visual resolution matched the limit set by cone spacing only at the foveal centre. Just outside the centre, resolution was worse than cone spacing predicts and better matched the sampling limit of midget retinal ganglion cells.
