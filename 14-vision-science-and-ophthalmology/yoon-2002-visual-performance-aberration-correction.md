# Visual performance after correcting the monochromatic and chromatic aberrations of the eye

**Yoon & Williams (2002)** · *JOSA A 19, 266* · [Free full text](https://aria.cvs.rochester.edu/papers/yoon_josa2002.pdf) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.19.000266](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.19.000266)

## Summary

Measures the visual benefit of correcting the eye's higher-order monochromatic aberrations with adaptive optics, in white and monochromatic light. In white light, contrast sensitivity and visual acuity with most monochromatic aberrations corrected by a deformable mirror were somewhat higher than with defocus and astigmatism correction alone. Correcting monochromatic aberrations while avoiding chromatic aberration (monochromatic viewing) gave a larger improvement. The measured gains were in reasonable agreement with predictions from the eye's optical modulation transfer function.
