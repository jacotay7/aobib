# Phase diversity: a technique for wave-front sensing and for diffraction-limited imaging

**Mugnier et al. (2006)** · *Advances in Imaging and Electron Physics 141, 1-76* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-00408562) · DOI: [10.1016/S1076-5670(05)41001-0](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1076-5670(05)41001-0)

## Summary

A 76-page review chapter on phase diversity, a focal-plane technique in which the aberrations of an optical system, and optionally the observed object, are estimated from two or more images that differ by a known additional aberration such as defocus. The chapter presents the principle of the method and the associated estimation approaches, and discusses its use both as a wavefront sensor (for example for calibrating static aberrations of AO systems) and as an imaging technique for restoring diffraction-limited images. The full text is available from the HAL repository.
