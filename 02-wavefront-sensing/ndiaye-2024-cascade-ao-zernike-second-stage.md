# Cascade adaptive optics with a second stage based on a Zernike wavefront sensor for exoplanet observations: Experimental validation on the ESO/GHOST testbed

**N'Diaye et al. (2024)** · *A&A 692, A157* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.11946) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202451449](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202451449)

## Summary

Proposes a second AO stage for extreme AO systems based on a Zernike wavefront sensor, exploiting its high sensitivity for the small residuals left by a first stage. The scheme was implemented with an integrator controller on ESO's GHOST testbed in monochromatic light. Under median seeing and wind speed, the second stage reduced atmospheric residuals by a factor of 6 and doubled wavefront stability, and with non-common path aberrations it gave a factor-of-2 contrast gain at short separations behind a Lyot coronagraph.
