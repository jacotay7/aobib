# The coronagraphic Modal Wavefront Sensor: a hybrid focal-plane sensor for the high-contrast imaging of circumstellar environments

**Wilby et al. (2017)** · *A&A 597, A112* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.04235) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201628628](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201628628)

## Summary

Introduces the coronagraphic Modal Wavefront Sensor (cMWS), a pupil-plane optic combining the apodizing phase plate coronagraph with a holographic modal wavefront sensor, giving simultaneous coronagraphic imaging and focal-plane wavefront sensing with the science PSF. Closed-loop simulations show recovery of diffraction-limited coronagraph performance over an effective dynamic range of +/-2.5 rad RMS in 2-10 iterations. On-sky tests at the William Herschel Telescope retrieved injected aberrations to 10 nm RMS and measured atmospheric wavefront variance at 50 Hz across an uncorrected subaperture.
