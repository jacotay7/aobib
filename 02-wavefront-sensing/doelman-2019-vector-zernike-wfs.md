# Simultaneous phase and amplitude aberration sensing with a liquid-crystal vector-Zernike phase mask

**Doelman et al. (2019)** · *Optics Letters 44, 17* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.08805) · DOI: [10.1364/OL.44.000017](https://doi.org/10.1364/OL.44.000017)

## Summary

Presents the vector-Zernike wavefront sensor, a patterned liquid-crystal mask that applies +/- pi/2 phase to the PSF core with opposite sign for the two circular polarisations via the achromatic geometric phase. After splitting circular polarisation, the two pupil intensity images are used to reconstruct both phase and amplitude of the incoming wavefront. Complex wavefront reconstruction is demonstrated with monochromatic laboratory measurements, and simulations show high accuracy and sensitivity over bandwidths up to 100%.
