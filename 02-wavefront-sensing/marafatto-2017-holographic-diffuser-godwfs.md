# A holographic diffuser generalised optical differentiation wavefront sensor

**Marafatto et al. (2017)** · *Proc. AO4ELT5 (Tenerife, 2017)* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.02261) · DOI: [10.26698/AO4ELT5.0140](https://doi.org/10.26698/AO4ELT5.0140)

## Summary

Proposes a practical implementation of a generalised optical differentiation wavefront sensor, a class designed to decouple dynamic range from sensitivity. A holographic diffuser diffracts part of the light into a ring around the tip of a pyramid, so that undiffracted light provides the high-sensitivity regime while the diffused light acts as a static modulation giving signal in strong turbulence. The zeroth-order efficiency of the diffuser sets the sensitivity and the diffusing angle sets the dynamic range. Interchangeable diffusers could adapt the sensor to changing seeing.
