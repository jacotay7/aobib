# Adaptive optics with programmable Fourier-based wavefront sensors: a spatial light modulator approach to the LOOPS testbed

**Janin-Potiron et al. (2019)** · *JATIS 5, 039001* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.06599) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.5.3.039001](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.5.3.039001)

## Summary

Describes the upgrade of the LOOPS adaptive optics testbed at the Laboratoire d'Astrophysique de Marseille with a spatial light modulator placed in a focal plane to emulate Fourier-filtering masks. This allows novel sensors such as n-faced pyramids (tending to an axicon) and flattened pyramids to be tested without manufacturing bulk optics. The paper presents the optical design, the generation of phase masks on the SLM and its implications, and first closed-loop results in static and dynamic modes with different emulated wavefront sensors.
