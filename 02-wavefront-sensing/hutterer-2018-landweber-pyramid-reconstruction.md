# Nonlinear wavefront reconstruction methods for pyramid sensors using Landweber and Landweber-Kaczmarz iteration

**Hutterer & Ramlau (2018)** · *Applied Optics 57, 8790* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.07673) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.57.008790](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.57.008790)

## Summary

Notes that most pyramid sensor reconstructors rely on a linearised model, which can be inadequate when phase errors such as non-common path aberrations are present. Two nonlinear reconstruction methods based on Landweber and Landweber-Kaczmarz iterations are introduced to solve the nonlinear inverse problem. The authors report high-quality wavefront estimates, particularly for the non-modulated sensor, with computational cost kept feasible for large-scale AO systems.
