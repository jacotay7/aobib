# Peak-locking centroid bias in Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensing

**Anugu et al. (2018)** · *MNRAS 476, 300* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.06836) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/sty182](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/sty182)

## Summary

Quantifies the bias (accuracy) error of Shack-Hartmann spot-position estimation due to peak-locking, quantisation and sampling, which had not been systematically studied. Four classes of subaperture images (point source, elongated laser guide star, crowded field, solar extended scene) and five correlation peak-finding algorithms (1D parabola, centre of gravity, Gaussian, 2D quadratic, pyramid) are compared over a range of signal-to-noise conditions. No algorithm is satisfactory for both bias and random error. A correction exploiting the anti-symmetric dependence of the bias on sub-pixel position reduces the bias by a factor of about 7, to below 0.02 pixel, at roughly twice the cost of standard cross-correlation.
