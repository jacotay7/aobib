# On-sky, real-time optical gain calibration on MagAO-X using incoherent speckles

**McEwen et al. (2024)** · *Proc. SPIE 13097 (Adaptive Optics Systems IX)* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.13022) · DOI: [10.1117/12.3020254](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3020254)

## Summary

Reports on-sky measurements of pyramid wavefront sensor optical gain on MagAO-X, the extreme AO system on the 6.5 m Magellan Clay telescope. The planned tracking method uses high-temporal-frequency deformable-mirror probes that create incoherent PSF copies at 10-22 lambda/D, already used routinely for coronagraph centring and calibration. These measurements are cross-checked with optical gains derived from sequential modal pokes. Tracked against DIMM seeing, the calibrations show a clear dependence of optical gain on Strehl ratio.
