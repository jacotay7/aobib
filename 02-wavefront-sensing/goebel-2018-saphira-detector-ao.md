# Overview of the SAPHIRA detector for adaptive optics applications

**Goebel et al. (2018)** · *JATIS 4, 026001* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.08419) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.4.2.026001](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.4.2.026001)

## Summary

Discusses the operation of Leonardo SAPHIRA detectors for AO wavefront sensing: 320x256, 24 micron pixel HgCdTe linear avalanche photodiode arrays sensitive from 0.8 to 2.5 microns. Global and line-by-line reset schemes, and the readout modes they enable, are described along with their benefits, drawbacks, noise sources and best-suited observing modes. Subarray readout for higher frame rates is covered, and the distinction between user-adjustable avalanche gain and fixed charge gain is clarified.
