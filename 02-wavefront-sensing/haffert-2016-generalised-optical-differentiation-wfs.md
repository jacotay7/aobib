# Generalised optical differentiation wavefront sensor: a sensitive high dynamic range wavefront sensor

**Haffert (2016)** · *Optics Express 24, 18986* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.24.018986) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.24.018986](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.24.018986)

## Summary

Develops the generalised optical differentiation wavefront sensor (g-ODWFS), aimed at combining large dynamic range with high sensitivity. Based on optical differentiation with focal-plane amplitude filters, the theory of linear optical differentiation is extended to nonlinear filters. Numerical simulations and laboratory experiments are used to study its properties, leading to a filter design that decouples dynamic range from sensitivity. The sensor is proposed for AO systems that must measure a large range of phase aberrations with high precision.
