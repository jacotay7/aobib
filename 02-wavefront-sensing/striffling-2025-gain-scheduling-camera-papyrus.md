# Optical gains measurement with a gain scheduling camera: on-sky demonstration with PAPYRUS and perspectives

**Striffling et al. (2025)** · *A&A 703, A253* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.15471) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202555909](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202555909)

## Summary

Validates on sky the gain scheduling camera, a focal-plane detector associated with a Fourier-filtering wavefront sensor to estimate in real time the modal optical gains of the sensor. A gain scheduling camera was implemented alongside the pyramid wavefront sensor of the PAPYRUS AO bench and results were compared with a high-fidelity numerical twin. Optical gains were estimated at 100 Hz, limited by the bench hardware, in both laboratory and on-sky tests, with good agreement with simulations. The authors conclude the approach is suitable for frame-by-frame optical-gain tracking in future extreme AO systems.
