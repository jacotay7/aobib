# Efficient, nonlinear phase estimation with the nonmodulated pyramid wavefront sensor

**Frazin (2018)** · *JOSA A 35, 594* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.01011) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.35.000594](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.35.000594)

## Summary

Argues that nonlinear estimation based on Newton's method can mitigate the strong nonlinearity of the non-modulated pyramid wavefront sensor. All optical modelling is precomputed, avoiding real-time beam-propagation simulations, and the remaining computations are suited to parallel hardware. A singular value analysis shows that reducing the four pupil images to two slope maps discards information, and estimators using raw pixel values, including pixels outside the geometric pupils, are advocated. Simulations indicate nonlinear estimation is effective for Strehl ratios above about 0.3.
