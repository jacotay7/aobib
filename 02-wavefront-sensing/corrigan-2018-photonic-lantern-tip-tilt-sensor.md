# Demonstration of a photonic lantern low order wavefront sensor using an adaptive optics testbed

**Corrigan et al. (2018)** · *Proc. SPIE 10703, 107035H* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.08131) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2311336](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2311336)

## Summary

Demonstrates a five-core photonic lantern used to measure tip/tilt at the telescope focal plane while simultaneously feeding an instrument, replacing a single-mode fibre to stabilise the instrument input against non-common path tip/tilt. Two configurations are studied: outer cores for tip/tilt with the central core feeding the instrument, and all cores used for sensing as in a spectrograph feed. Simulations show a linear tip/tilt range of +/-55 mas, with throughput comparable to an on-axis single-mode fibre at the 1.55 micron design wavelength in the first configuration.
