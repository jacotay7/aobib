# Curvature wavefront sensing for the Large Synoptic Survey Telescope

**Xin et al. (2015)** · *Applied Optics 54, 9045* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.04839) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.54.009045](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.54.009045)

## Summary

Describes the curvature wavefront sensing used by the LSST (Rubin Observatory) active optics system, based on four split detectors at the focal-plane corners placed 1 mm either side of focus. Extensions to published curvature algorithms are developed to handle the large central obscuration, the fast f/1.23 beam, off-axis pupil distortion, vignetting, the split-sensor geometry and the different stars used for intra- and extra-focal images. Simulations demonstrate convergence, linearity and negligible noise relative to atmospheric effects. The extensions are applicable to other wide-field telescopes with off-axis curvature sensing.
