# The asymmetric pupil Fourier wavefront sensor

**Martinache (2013)** · *PASP 125, 422* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.6678) · DOI: [10.1086/670670](https://doi.org/10.1086/670670)

## Summary

Introduces a wavefront sensing approach based on Fourier analysis of a single conventional focal-plane image. In the high-Strehl regime the phase measured in the Fourier plane is linearly related to pupil wavefront errors, building on the kernel-phase concept. The method requires small wavefront errors (about one radian or less) and an asymmetric pupil, which can be introduced with a mask. Simulations show the Strehl ratio improving from 50% to over 90% in a few iterations with good photon-noise sensitivity.
