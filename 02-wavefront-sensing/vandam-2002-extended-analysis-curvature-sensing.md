# Extended analysis of curvature sensing

**van Dam & Lane (2002)** · *JOSA A 19, 1390* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/11281097_Extended_analysis_of_curvature_sensing) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.19.001390](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.19.001390)

## Summary

Analyses the performance limits of curvature wavefront sensors, which in practice are constrained by non-linear behaviour. The authors characterize the sensor by solving the irradiance transport equation together with the accompanying wavefront transport equation. They show how non-linear geometric terms limit measurement accuracy and how diffraction limits spatial resolution. The effect of photon noise on the sensor is also quantified.
