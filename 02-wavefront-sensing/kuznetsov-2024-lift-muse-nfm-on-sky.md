# Striving towards robust phase diversity on-sky: implementing LIFT for VLT/MUSE-NFM

**Kuznetsov et al. (2024)** · *A&A 687, A221* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.08529) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202449860](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202449860)

## Summary

Reports the first on-sky validation of the linearized focal-plane technique (LIFT) on VLT/UT4, enabled by the IRLOS upgrade for MUSE narrow-field mode that replaced the 2x2 Shack-Hartmann with a full-pupil mode. Strong polychromatic non-common path aberrations made the PSF complex and hampered tip-tilt and focus retrieval. A two-stage approach is proposed in which NCPAs are first estimated directly from on-sky focal-plane PSFs and then used by LIFT. This significantly improved defocus estimation even at low flux, demonstrating LIFT as a slow truth focus sensor.
