# A telescope-ready approach for modal compensation of pyramid wavefront sensor optical gain

**Deo et al. (2019)** · *A&A 629, A107* · [Free full text](https://www.aanda.org/articles/aa/pdf/2019/09/aa35847-19.pdf) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201935847](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201935847)

## Summary

Investigates the optical gain phenomenon of the pyramid wavefront sensor, a reduction and modification of the sensor response induced by the residual wavefront, which is significant for ELT-scale AO in standard conditions. Through extensive numerical analysis, a formalism is proposed to measure and minimise the first-order nonlinearity error via modal compensation of the calibrated reconstructor, raising for example the H-band Strehl ratio from 16 to 30% for a 16th magnitude star in r0 = 13 cm seeing. A complete algorithm is designed that dithers a few deformable-mirror modes to retrieve the optimal gains and update the command matrix automatically. End-to-end simulations of an ELT single-conjugate AO system demonstrate the self-updating technique with minimal interference with the science instrument.
