# Adaptive optics for high-contrast imaging: pyramid sensor versus spatially filtered Shack-Hartmann sensor

**Vérinaud et al. (2005)** · *MNRAS 357, L26* · [Free full text](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/2005MNRAS.357L..26V) · DOI: [10.1111/j.1745-3933.2005.08638.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1745-3933.2005.08638.x)

## Summary

Compares pyramid-based and spatially filtered Shack-Hartmann-based high-order AO for 8 m-class telescopes and ELTs. End-to-end simulations of an 8 m system show how each sensor handles aliasing, which can dominate the error budget at high flux. The field dependence of the photon-noise sensitivity gain of the pyramid relative to the Shack-Hartmann is then studied. An analytical model is used to investigate visible-light correction on ELTs for the detection of Earth-like exoplanets.
