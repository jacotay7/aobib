# Characterizing the performance of two C-RED ONE cameras for implementation in RISTRETTO and SAXO+ projects

**Shinde et al. (2024)** · *Proc. SPIE 13097, 1309744* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.04247) · DOI: [10.1117/12.3020363](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3020363)

## Summary

Characterises two First Light Imaging C-RED One cameras, based on SAPHIRA HgCdTe e-APD arrays (320x256, 24 micron pixels, 0.8-2.5 micron) with sub-electron read noise and subarray readout at up to tens of kHz, purchased for the RISTRETTO spectrograph and the SAXO+ upgrade of VLT/SPHERE. Measurements cover read noise, bias and their temporal variability, conversion gain and avalanche-gain calibration, and the evolution of these parameters over time, with a comparison between the two cameras.
