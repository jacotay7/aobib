# The bright pyramid wavefront sensor

**Gerard et al. (2021)** · *Proc. SPIE 11823, 118231B* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.13105) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2594795](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2594795)

## Summary

Introduces the bright pyramid wavefront sensor (bPWFS), a design intended to improve both linearity and measurement error relative to the non-modulated pyramid sensor, rather than trading one against the other. The authors discuss operation with tip-tilt modulation for closing on open-loop turbulence, and non-modulated use for reaching deeper closed-loop residuals, for example as a second-stage AO sensor. An AO error-budget analysis of the non-modulated bPWFS is presented together with supporting testbed results from the Marseille Astrophysics Laboratory.
