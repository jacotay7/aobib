# Phase retrieval algorithms: a comparison

**Fienup (1982)** · *Applied Optics 21, 2758* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/43160608_Phase_retrieval_algorithms_a_comparison) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.21.002758](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.21.002758)

## Summary

Compares iterative phase-retrieval algorithms with gradient-search methods for two problems: recovering phase from two intensity measurements (as in electron microscopy or wavefront sensing) and from a single intensity measurement plus a non-negativity constraint (as in astronomy), with emphasis on the latter. The paper shows that the error-reduction algorithm for the single-measurement problem and the Gerchberg-Saxton algorithm for the two-measurement problem converge, and that error reduction is closely related to steepest descent. Other algorithms, including the input-output family and the conjugate-gradient method, are shown in examples to converge much faster in practice. The hybrid input-output algorithm described here became a standard reference method for iterative phase retrieval, which underlies many focal-plane wavefront-sensing and phasing techniques.
