# A pyramid wavefront sensor with no dynamic modulation

**Ragazzoni et al. (2002)** · *Optics Communications 208, 51* · [Free full text](http://www.eso.org/~evernet/optcommunication.pdf) · DOI: [10.1016/S0030-4018(02)01580-8](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0030-4018(02)01580-8)

## Summary

Addresses how to give the pyramid wavefront sensor a large dynamic range, which until then required moving parts: vibrating the pyramid or oscillating a tip-tilt mirror in a plane conjugated to the telescope exit pupil. The authors propose obtaining the same effect with no moving parts by placing a light-diffusing plate in an intermediate pupil plane. They present some practical implementations of the concept and discuss its relevance to multi-conjugate adaptive optics.
