# Estimation of centroid positions with a matched-filter algorithm: relevance for aberrometry of the eye

**Leroux & Dainty (2010)** · *Optics Express 18, 1197* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.18.001197) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.18.001197](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.18.001197)

## Summary

Addresses centroid bias in Shack-Hartmann aberrometers of the eye caused by background light scattered from retinal layers, which pulls centre-of-gravity estimates towards the window centre. A matched-filter centroiding algorithm is implemented and its robustness to background light is recalled. Using data from five human eyes, a simple and fast centroiding algorithm is parameterised to reduce the difference with the matched-filter result to a small mean residual wavefront error.
