# LIFT: a focal-plane wavefront sensor for real-time low-order sensing on faint sources

**Meimon et al. (2010)** · *Optics Letters 35, 3036* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/46288944_LIFT_a_focal-plane_wavefront_sensor_for_real-time_low-order_sensing_on_faint_sources) · DOI: [10.1364/OL.35.003036](https://doi.org/10.1364/OL.35.003036)

## Summary

Proposes the linearized focal-plane technique (LIFT), a wavefront sensor that estimates low-order aberrations from a single focal-plane image formed with a known astigmatic diversity, aimed at real-time sensing on faint natural guide stars. LIFT is compared with classical sensors used in this role: the quad-cell, pyramid and Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensors. The number of modes estimated can be adjusted in software without hardware changes or loss of low-order performance. The authors derive an analytical noise-propagation model and validate it with end-to-end simulations.
