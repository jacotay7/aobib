# Performance comparison of the Shack-Hartmann and pyramid wavefront sensors with a laser guide star for 40 m telescopes

**Oyarzún et al. (2024)** · *A&A 691, A205* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.04863) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202451670](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202451670)

## Summary

Compares the noise performance of the Shack-Hartmann and pyramid wavefront sensors used with laser guide stars on 40 m-class telescopes. A noise model developed for the pyramid is extended to the Shack-Hartmann by expressing centroiding as a matrix-vector multiplication, and validated against end-to-end simulations for 8 m and 16 m telescopes. For a single wavefront sensor, the pyramid outperforms the Shack-Hartmann for equal numbers of subapertures, with a limiting magnitude about one magnitude fainter at 40 m. In a tomographic configuration using generalised least squares, the Shack-Hartmann gains about 0.3 mag, and the two become nearly identical when the pyramid uses sub-electron read-noise detectors.
