# On-sky demonstration of second-stage wavefront control with a photonic lantern

**Sengupta et al. (2025)** · *AJ 171, 65 (2026)* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.20560) · DOI: [10.3847/1538-3881/ae2617](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-3881/ae2617)

## Summary

Demonstrates on sky closed-loop control of residual wavefront errors using a non-dispersed photonic lantern as a second-stage focal-plane wavefront sensor after first-stage AO correction on the Shane 3 m telescope at Lick Observatory. In this two-stage architecture the first-stage sensor handles atmospheric turbulence while the lantern senses non-common path and other aberrations unseen by the first stage. The results indicate that photonic lanterns can be retrofitted to existing AO systems for second-stage sensing.
