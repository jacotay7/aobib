# Using the generalised-optical differentiation wavefront sensor for laser guide star wavefront sensing

**Haffert et al. (2020)** · *Proc. SPIE 11448, 114481T* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.02336) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2562610](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2562610)

## Summary

Proposes the generalised optical differentiation wavefront sensor for laser guide star wavefront sensing, as an alternative to the Shack-Hartmann, whose direct sampling of the image is a disadvantage for extended sources. The g-ODWFS uses only four pixels per subaperture, has little aliasing noise and hence no spurious low-order errors, and requires no centroid-gain calibration. Simulations comparing it with the Shack-Hartmann are presented.
