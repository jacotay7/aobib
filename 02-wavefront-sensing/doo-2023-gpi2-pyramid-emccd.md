# GPI 2.0: performance evaluation of the wavefront sensor's EMCCD

**Do Ó et al. (2023)** · *arXiv preprint 2310.06276* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06276)

## Summary

Characterises the custom HNu 240 EMCCD from Nuvu Cameras built for the pyramid wavefront sensor of the GPI 2.0 upgrade, which replaces GPI's Shack-Hartmann sensor. The detector offers dark current below 0.01 e-/pix/frame, read noise of about 0.1 e- at an EM gain of 5000, quantum efficiency around 90% at 600-800 nm, and full-frame rates up to 3000 fps. Test results are presented on read noise, pixel-to-pixel variability, bias, linearity and EM-gain calibration, all carried out before integration into the sensor.
