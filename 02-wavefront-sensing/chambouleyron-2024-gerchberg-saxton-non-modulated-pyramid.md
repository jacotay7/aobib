# Using the Gerchberg-Saxton algorithm to reconstruct nonmodulated pyramid wavefront sensor measurements

**Chambouleyron et al. (2024)** · *A&A 681, A48* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.14283) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202347220](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202347220)

## Summary

Proposes inverting non-modulated pyramid wavefront sensor signals using light-propagation reciprocity and the Gerchberg-Saxton iterative algorithm. The reconstructor is studied in simulation and then tested on the Santa Cruz Extreme AO Laboratory (SEAL) testbed. It greatly increases the dynamic range of reconstruction, approaching that of a modulated pyramid sensor, at the cost of a heavy iterative computation that may limit real-time use.
