# Real-time adaptive optics with pyramid wavefront sensors: part I. A theoretical analysis of the pyramid sensor model

**Hutterer et al. (2019)** · *Inverse Problems 35, 045007* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.00682) · DOI: [10.1088/1361-6420/ab0656](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6420/ab0656)

## Summary

Presents a mathematical analysis of the forward operators of the pyramid wavefront sensor, intended as a basis for fast and stable reconstruction algorithms. Detailed models are derived for both modulated and non-modulated operation, together with models of the simpler roof wavefront sensor and various approximations. Adjoint operators are computed, providing the ingredients for iterative inverse-problem methods such as gradient-based algorithms, Landweber iteration and Kaczmarz methods.
