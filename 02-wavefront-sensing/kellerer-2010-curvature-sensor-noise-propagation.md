# Curvature sensors: noise and its propagation

**Kellerer (2010)** · *JOSA A 27, A29* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1006.2413) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.27.000A29](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.27.000A29)

## Summary

Analyses the signal of a curvature wavefront sensor. The minimum number of sensing elements needed at the pupil edge is derived as a function of the total number of elements. The statistics of the sensor signal are characterised (mean, variance, kurtosis, skewness), showing that while a Gaussian approximation holds down to fairly low photon numbers, much higher photon numbers are needed for meaningful measurements of small wavefront distortions. Closed expressions are given for the error-propagation factor and the photon-noise-induced Strehl loss.
