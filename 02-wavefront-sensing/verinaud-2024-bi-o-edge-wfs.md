# The Bi-O edge wavefront sensor: how Foucault-knife-edge variants can boost extreme adaptive optics

**Vérinaud et al. (2024)** · *A&A 682, A27* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.07485) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202346660](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202346660)

## Summary

Introduces the Bi-Orthogonal Foucault-knife-edge sensors (Bi-O-edge), in which a beam-splitter produces two foci each sensed by a knife edge oriented orthogonally to the other. Two implementations are described: a sharp edge with tip-tilt modulation, and a 'grey' edge with smooth gradual transmission. Comparison with the four-sided pyramid sensor gives insight into the nature of the measurements. The sensitivity gain over the pyramid depends on the number of controlled modes and on modulation; for the sharp Bi-O-edge the reduction in propagated photon-noise variance approaches a factor of 2 for many modes and small modulation.
