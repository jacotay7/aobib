# Real-time experimental demonstrations of a photonic lantern wavefront sensor

**Lin et al. (2023)** · *ApJL 959, L34* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.13381) · DOI: [10.3847/2041-8213/ad12a4](https://doi.org/10.3847/2041-8213/ad12a4)

## Summary

Reports a real-time experimental demonstration of the photonic lantern wavefront sensor on the Subaru/SCExAO testbed, as a focal-plane second-stage sensor addressing non-common-path aberrations and petal modes. The system is stable to roughly 400 nm of low-order Zernike error and can correct petaling modes. With about 30 nm RMS of injected time-varying low-order error, roughly 10x rejection is achieved on 1 s timescales. The authors note that refinements to the control law and lantern fabrication should enable finer control.
