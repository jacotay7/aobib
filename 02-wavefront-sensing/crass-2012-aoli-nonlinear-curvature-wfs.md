# The AOLI low-order non-linear curvature wavefront sensor: a method for high sensitivity wavefront reconstruction

**Crass et al. (2012)** · *Proc. SPIE 8447, 84470T* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.0991) · DOI: [10.1117/12.925714](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.925714)

## Summary

Presents the AO system designed for the Adaptive Optics Lucky Imager (AOLI), comprising a large-stroke deformable mirror, a fixed-component nonlinear curvature wavefront sensor and photon-counting EMCCD detectors. The sensor optics provide four reference images on two photon-counting CCDs. Simulations of its optical characteristics are discussed for low- and high-order AO, along with the development of fast reconstruction algorithms and simulated sensitivity.
