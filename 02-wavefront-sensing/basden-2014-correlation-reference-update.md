# Real-time correlation reference update for astronomical adaptive optics

**Basden (2014)** · *MNRAS 439, 968* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.2635) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stu027](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stu027)

## Summary

Presents a technique for updating the reference images used in correlation-based Shack-Hartmann slope estimation while the AO loop is closed, motivated by elongated laser guide star spots. The update is shown not to affect the resulting PSFs, and an on-sky demonstration is reported. Different methods of obtaining reference images are compared over a wide range of system parameters. Correlation is found to outperform the centre-of-gravity algorithm and to be well suited to open-loop multi-object AO.
