# General formalism for Fourier-based wave front sensing

**Fauvarque et al. (2016)** · *Optica 3, 1440* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.02969) · DOI: [10.1364/OPTICA.3.001440](https://doi.org/10.1364/OPTICA.3.001440)

## Summary

Introduces a general formalism for Fourier-based wavefront sensors in which the focal-plane filtering mask is treated as a free parameter, unifying the pyramid and Zernike wavefront sensors as members of broader sensor classes (e.g. variable apex angle or mask depth). A phase-linear quantity called the meta-intensity is defined, allowing the linear behaviour of a sensor to be separated analytically from non-linear contributions. This yields formal definitions of sensitivity and linearity range, and an 'SD factor' quantifying the trade-off between them. The formalism is extended to modulation and polychromatic light and applied to both sensor classes, recovering known results and showing the influence of the optical parameters.
