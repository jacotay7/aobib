# Spatial frequency response and sensitivity of the non-linear curvature wavefront sensor

**Letchev et al. (2022)** · *Proc. SPIE 12185, 121858H* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.00071) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2632449](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2632449)

## Summary

Explores the design parameter space of the nonlinear curvature wavefront sensor, whose sensitivity has been predicted to be up to ten times that of a Shack-Hartmann. Using simulations under Kolmogorov turbulence, the effects of measurement-plane positions and detector sampling are studied in terms of residual wavefront error and number of iterations to convergence. Alternatives to the nominal symmetric four-plane design, including three- and five-plane configurations, are evaluated, and a preliminary study of broadband illumination is included.
