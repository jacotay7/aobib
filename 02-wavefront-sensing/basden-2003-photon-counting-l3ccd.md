# Photon counting strategies with low-light-level CCDs

**Basden et al. (2003)** · *MNRAS 345, 985* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0307305) · DOI: [10.1046/j.1365-8711.2003.07020.x](https://doi.org/10.1046/j.1365-8711.2003.07020.x)

## Summary

Examines how to process the output of low-light-level CCDs (L3CCDs or EMCCDs), whose on-chip gain gives sub-electron effective read noise but introduces stochastic multiplication noise. A transfer function mapping output levels to estimated photon counts is optimised using several thresholding strategies, and their effect on photometric accuracy and signal-to-noise is quantified. The multiplication noise can be eliminated at the lowest light levels, with diminishing improvement up to about 20 photons per pixel. Operating at high frame rates to keep flux per frame low maximises the benefit.
