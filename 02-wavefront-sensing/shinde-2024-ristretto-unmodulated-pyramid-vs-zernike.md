# RISTRETTO: a comparative performance analysis of the unmodulated pyramid wavefront sensor and the Zernike wavefront sensor

**Shinde et al. (2024)** · *Proc. SPIE 13097* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.04255) · DOI: [10.1117/12.3017549](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3017549)

## Summary

Compares three-sided and four-sided unmodulated pyramid wavefront sensors with the Zernike wavefront sensor at visible and near-infrared wavelengths for the XAO system of RISTRETTO, a proposed high-contrast high-resolution spectrograph for the VLT targeting Proxima b at about 2 lambda/D. The requirements of raw contrast below 1e-4 at 2 lambda/D and Strehl above 70% demand high sensitivity to low-order aberrations and petal modes. The study examines how the non-linearities and limited dynamic range of these sensors, which increase with wavelength, affect their performance.
