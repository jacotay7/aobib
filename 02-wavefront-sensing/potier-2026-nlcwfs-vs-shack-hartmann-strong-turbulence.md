# Performance comparison of the nonlinear curvature and Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensors in strong turbulence

**Potier et al. (2026)** · *Optical Engineering 65, 081823* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10536) · DOI: [10.1117/1.OE.65.8.081823](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.OE.65.8.081823)

## Summary

Compares the nonlinear curvature wavefront sensor with a Shack-Hartmann sensor in strong turbulence with high spherical-wave Rytov numbers, where scintillation and branch points hamper gradient-based sensors. Open-loop and static and dynamic closed-loop simulations are presented as a function of turbulence strength and flux. In static and weak-to-moderate scintillation regimes the nlCWFS outperforms the Shack-Hartmann by exploiting amplitude-phase coupling. In dynamic closed loop at stronger scintillation, intensity nulls and branch points degrade the Gerchberg-Saxton-based nlCWFS reconstruction more than the Shack-Hartmann, motivating branch-point-tolerant algorithms.
