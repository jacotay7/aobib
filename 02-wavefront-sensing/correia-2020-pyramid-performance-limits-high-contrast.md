# Performance limits of adaptive-optics/high-contrast imagers with pyramid wavefront sensors

**Correia et al. (2020)** · *MNRAS 495, 4380* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.06011) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/staa843](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/staa843)

## Summary

Develops spatial-frequency-domain forward models of the pyramid wavefront sensor derived from Fourier diffraction theory and uses them for error breakdowns, fast reconstruction and distributed predictive control. Analytic estimates are compared with end-to-end Monte-Carlo simulations and anchored to on-sky contrast measured on existing systems. For an upgrade of a 10 m-class high-contrast imager under median Paranal conditions, the authors predict a contrast gain of 2-5x from replacing the wavefront sensor alone near the control radius, and more than 10x when combined with distributed control across most of the corrected region.
