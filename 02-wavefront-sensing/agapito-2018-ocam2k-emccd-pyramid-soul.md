# EMCCD for pyramid wavefront sensor: laboratory characterization

**Agapito et al. (2018)** · *Proc. SPIE 10703* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.14634) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2313827](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2313827)

## Summary

Presents laboratory characterisation of three custom OCAM2K EMCCD cameras from First Light Imaging, selected as the detector upgrade for the pyramid wavefront sensors of the LBT SOUL single-conjugate AO systems. The cameras showed read noise of about 0.4 e- (0.4-0.7 e- in binned modes) and low dark current. The camera gain was measured and its dependence on power cycling and frame rate identified, and the impact of these gain variations on SOUL performance was estimated to be negligible.
