# Linearity of the pyramid wavefront sensor

**Burvall et al. (2006)** · *Optics Express 14, 11925* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.14.011925) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.14.011925](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.14.011925)

## Summary

Examines the response of the circularly modulated pyramid wavefront sensor, which is closely related to the Foucault knife-edge test. Approximate geometrical-optics calculations, more exact diffraction calculations and laboratory measurements are compared. Both the sinusoidal and the approximately linear relation between wavefront derivative and sensor signal are derived rigorously from diffraction theory. Since all three approaches agree closely, the authors conclude that the simpler geometrical predictions can be used in place of the diffraction results.
