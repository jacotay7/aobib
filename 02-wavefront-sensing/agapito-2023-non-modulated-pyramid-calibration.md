# Non-modulated pyramid wavefront sensor: use in sensing and correcting atmospheric turbulence

**Agapito et al. (2023)** · *A&A 677, A168* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.01066) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202346359](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202346359)

## Summary

Studies how to operate the pyramid wavefront sensor without tip-tilt modulation, which maximises sensitivity but strongly reduces linear range. A calibration approach is developed in which the interaction matrix models the sensor response in partial correction, i.e. in the presence of residual turbulence, thereby reducing model error without extending the linearity range. Simulations show that this allows the non-modulated sensor to sense and correct atmospheric turbulence, and the authors discuss when it is preferable to the modulated case.
