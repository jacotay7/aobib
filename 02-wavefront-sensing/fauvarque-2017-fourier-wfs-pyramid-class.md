# General formalism for Fourier-based wave front sensing: application to the pyramid wave front sensors

**Fauvarque et al. (2017)** · *JATIS 3, 019001* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.03269) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.3.1.019001](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.3.1.019001)

## Summary

Applies the Fourier-filtering formalism to the 'class of pyramidal wavefront sensors': the classical four-faced pyramid, six- and eight-faced variants, the flattened pyramid, and new concepts such as the flattened cone and three-faced pyramid. Performance criteria are defined for signal footprint on the detector, flux efficiency, sensitivity, linear range and chromaticity, and their dependence on mask and modulation parameters is studied. The authors find that the number of faces changes the number of detector pixels required but not sensitivity or linearity, which are governed mainly by modulation radius and apex angle. The time spent on edges versus faces during modulation is shown to tune the sensitivity/linearity trade-off.
