# Joint optimization of wavefront sensing and reconstruction with automatic differentiation

**Landman et al. (2022)** · *Proc. SPIE 12185 (Adaptive Optics Systems VIII)* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.05904) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2629930](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2629930)

## Summary

Proposes numerically optimising Fourier-filtering wavefront sensor masks with automatic differentiation. Sensors are first optimised for sensitivity for various apertures and wavefront statistics, giving designs more sensitive than current ones and close to the theoretical limit in monochromatic light. The residual wavefront error is then minimised directly by jointly optimising differentiable models of the sensor and reconstructor, including convolutional neural network reconstructors to allow nonlinearity. The approach is general and applicable to any wavefront sensor architecture with free design parameters.
