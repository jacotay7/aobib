# Error propagation: a comparison of Shack-Hartmann and curvature sensors

**Kellerer & Kellerer (2011)** · *JOSA A 28, 801* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.4378) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.28.000801](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.28.000801)

## Summary

Calculates the ratio of the error-propagation factors of curvature and Shack-Hartmann sensors as a function of the number of sensing elements n on a circular aperture. When the sensor spacing is kept fixed and the pupil enlarged, the ratio grows as n^0.4; when more elements are fitted into the same aperture it grows as n^0.8. The authors conclude that this increase can limit the applicability of curvature sensors for large numbers of sensing elements.
