# Last performances improvement of the C-RED One camera using the 320x256 e-APD infrared Saphira detector

**Feautrier & Gach (2022)** · *Proc. SPIE 12183, 121832E* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.00377) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2630860](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2630860)

## Summary

Presents recent improvements to First Light Imaging's C-RED One camera, which uses the Leonardo SAPHIRA 320x256 e-APD infrared array in an autonomous cryogenic system with a pulse-tube cooler. Total noise was reduced to 0.6 e- at gain 50 in CDS mode at 1720 frames per second (with a 1.75 micron cut-off and room-temperature background), half the previous value, and the background was reduced threefold. Bias oscillations were removed and pulse-tube-induced focal-plane vibrations were damped to 0.3 micron RMS. Other camera characteristics, including permanent vacuum, low-latency CameraLink interface and cooling, are described.
