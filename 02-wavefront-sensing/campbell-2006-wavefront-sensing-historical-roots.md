# Wavefront sensing: from historical roots to the state-of-the-art

**Campbell & Greenaway (2006)** · *EAS Publications Series 22, 165* · [Free full text](https://waf.eps.hw.ac.uk/downloads/papers/Wavefront%20Sensing%20-%20ITHD3%20-%20Distributable%20version.pdf) · DOI: [10.1051/eas:2006131](https://doi.org/10.1051/eas:2006131)

## Summary

An overview of wavefront-sensing technologies, written as lecture notes for a school on high-contrast imaging. After a brief history of the field it studies several sensors in detail, with particular attention to the Shack-Hartmann, phase-diversity and curvature sensors. It also discusses interferometric and pyramid sensors, Schlieren imaging, algorithmic approaches such as image sharpening, hybrid Shack-Hartmann/curvature sensing and modal wavefront sensors.
