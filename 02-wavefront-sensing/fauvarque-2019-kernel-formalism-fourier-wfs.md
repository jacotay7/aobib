# Kernel formalism applied to Fourier-based wave-front sensing in presence of residual phases

**Fauvarque et al. (2019)** · *JOSA A 36, 1241* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.05440) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.36.001241](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.36.001241)

## Summary

Describes Fourier-based wavefront sensors as linear integral operators characterised by a kernel, and derives how the kernel depends on pupil geometry, filtering mask and tip/tilt modulation. Under stated assumptions the kernel becomes convolutional, so that the sensor is summarised by an impulse response from which its sensitivity versus spatial frequency follows directly. The impact of residual phases on the impulse response is analysed and the convolutional model is shown to remain valid. A section on the pyramid sensor shows how slopes maps are handled in this framework, which later underpins optical-gain estimation methods.
