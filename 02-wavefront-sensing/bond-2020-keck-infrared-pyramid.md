# Adaptive optics with an infrared pyramid wavefront sensor at Keck

**Bond et al. (2020)** · *JATIS 6, 039003* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.6.3.039003) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.6.3.039003](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.6.3.039003)

## Summary

Presents the near-infrared pyramid wavefront sensor added to the Keck II AO system to improve correction on faint red objects such as M dwarfs and embedded young stars. It was the first pyramid sensor installed on a segmented telescope and the first infrared pyramid on a 10 m-class telescope. The paper discusses the advantages of infrared wavefront sensing, the design and commissioning of the sensor, and the performance of its SAPHIRA avalanche photodiode detector. Science verification with the NIRC2 vortex coronagraph is reported, along with commissioning alongside a fibre injection unit for NIRSPEC.
