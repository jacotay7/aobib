# Modeling noise propagation in Fourier-filtering wavefront sensing, fundamental limits, and quantitative comparison

**Chambouleyron et al. (2023)** · *A&A 670, A153* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.13577) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202245351](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202245351)

## Summary

Derives a noise-propagation model valid for any Fourier-filtering wavefront sensor in the linear regime, accounting for both photon noise and detector read-out noise. The model is used to revisit the fundamental sensitivity limit of this class of sensors. A quantitative comparison is then made between the two most widely used members of the class, the Zernike and pyramid wavefront sensors, clarifying how their sensitivities differ with respect to the two noise sources.
