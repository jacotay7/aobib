# Pyramid wavefront sensor behavior in partial correction adaptive optic systems

**Esposito & Riccardi (2001)** · *A&A 369, L9* · [Free full text](https://www.aanda.org/articles/aa/pdf/2001/14/aacl202.pdf) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361:20010219](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361:20010219)

## Summary

Evaluates by simulation the limiting magnitude of a pyramid sensor in a closed-loop astronomical AO system operating in the partial-correction regime. Earlier heuristic analyses predicted a sensitivity gain over the Shack-Hartmann sensor for a point source once diffraction-limited conditions are reached at the sensing wavelength, which is rarely achieved in practice. The simulations show that the gain is retained under partial correction, with an average gain of about two magnitudes in the case considered. The authors note the relevance of this result for the sky coverage of natural guide star AO.
