# A generalised non-linear reconstructor for all Fourier-type wavefront sensors

**Laidlaw (2025)** · *Optics Express 33, 31281* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.03477) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.567084](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.567084)

## Summary

Presents an iterative reconstructor for Fourier-type wavefront sensors operating in non-linear regimes, such as strong turbulence at non-rural sites or in daytime. The method is a non-linear Landweber iteration with Nesterov acceleration and applies to any Fourier-type sensor by changing the filtering mask in the model. Several Fourier-type sensors are compared for ELT-scale instruments, with emphasis on the pyramid sensor. End-to-end simulations show improved performance over linear matrix-vector reconstruction in non-linear regimes.
