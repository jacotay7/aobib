# Fast & Furious focal-plane wavefront sensing

**Korkiakoski et al. (2014)** · *Applied Optics 53, 4565* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.1006) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.53.004565](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.53.004565)

## Summary

Presents two algorithms that use focal-plane images to control a high-resolution wavefront corrector. Fast & Furious (FF) uses a weak-aberration assumption and pupil symmetries for fast reconstruction; its extension FF-GS handles arbitrary pupils using Gerchberg-Saxton-style error reduction to determine pupil amplitudes. Simulations and experiments with a 170x170-pixel spatial light modulator raise the Strehl ratio from about 0.75 to 0.98-0.99, with residual errors of about 0.15 rad (FF) and 0.10 rad (FF-GS).
