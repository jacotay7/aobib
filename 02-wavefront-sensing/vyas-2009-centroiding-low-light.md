# Performance of centroiding algorithms at low light level conditions in adaptive optics

**Vyas et al. (2009)** · *Proc. ARTCom 2009 (IEEE)* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1001.1503) · DOI: [10.1109/ARTCom.2009.30](https://doi.org/10.1109/ARTCom.2009.30)

## Summary

Compares Shack-Hartmann centroiding algorithms at low light levels, where photon noise with Poisson statistics makes short-exposure spots non-Gaussian. Centroid estimation errors are computed with Monte Carlo simulations at different photon levels for varying spot size and spot shift. The analysis is intended to help choose sensor specifications for low-light operation.
