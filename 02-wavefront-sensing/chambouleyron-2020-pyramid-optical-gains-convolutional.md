# Pyramid wavefront sensor optical gains compensation using a convolutional model

**Chambouleyron et al. (2020)** · *A&A 644, A6* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08294) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202037836](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202037836)

## Summary

Addresses the loss of pyramid wavefront sensor sensitivity in the presence of residual wavefront errors, known as the optical gains effect, which degrades closed-loop performance and NCPA compensation. Using a convolutional model of the sensor, the authors predict its closed-loop behaviour analytically from the statistics of the residual phase. The method estimates optical gains non-invasively from AO telemetry; end-to-end simulations show that with only an estimate of r0 and basic system parameters the optical gains are recovered to better than 10%. The importance of optical-gain estimation for NCPA handling is illustrated.
