# Super-resolution wavefront reconstruction in adaptive-optics with pyramid sensors

**Correia et al. (2025)** · *arXiv preprint 2512.17469* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.17469)

## Summary

Extends the super-resolution concept, previously demonstrated with multiple Shack-Hartmann sensors in tomographic LGS systems, to a single pyramid wavefront sensor on a natural guide star. Revisiting the analytic pyramid diffraction model, the authors argue that spatial frequencies beyond the Nyquist limit set by the detector pixels can be reconstructed and that amplitude aberrations can also be measured. Super-resolution allows a higher-density deformable mirror to be controlled from fewer samples, increases resilience to misregistration and relaxes alignment requirements, at up to twice the real-time computational cost. Note: in the v2 arXiv comment the authors state that the theoretical justification given in the introduction has a significant mathematical flaw, while the numerical results stand; a similarly titled paper by the same group appeared in Proc. SPIE 12185 (2022, DOI 10.1117/12.2632753).
