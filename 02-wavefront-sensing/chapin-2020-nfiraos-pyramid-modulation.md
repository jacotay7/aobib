# Fast modulation and dithering for the NFIRAOS pyramid wavefront sensor

**Chapin et al. (2020)** · *Proc. SPIE 11451* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.06429) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2560418](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2560418)

## Summary

Describes the modulation subsystem of the natural guide star pyramid wavefront sensor for NFIRAOS, the TMT first-light AO system. A 32 mm fast steering mirror traces a circular tip/tilt pattern at up to 800 Hz, with a superimposed circular dither at a quarter of the modulation frequency to enable optical gain measurement, synchronised with the sensor detector and other wavefront sensors. A monocrystalline piezo stage was selected to meet lifetime, stroke and frequency requirements. Test-bench characterisation at room temperature and at the -30 C operating temperature, and a prototype triggering scheme, are reported.
