# Polychromatic pyramid wavefront sensor with MKID technology for high contrast imaging

**Magniez et al. (2026)** · *RAS Techniques and Instruments 5, rzaf064* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.08398) · DOI: [10.1093/rasti/rzaf064](https://doi.org/10.1093/rasti/rzaf064)

## Summary

Introduces the concept of a polychromatic pyramid wavefront sensor using Microwave Kinetic Inductance Detector (MKID) arrays, which record position, arrival time and energy of each photon. The paper defines the required technologies and techniques and develops a method to track optical gains using the wavelength information, compensating them within a reconstructor optimised to minimise noise propagation. End-to-end simulations with Keck II as reference show limiting magnitude gains of 1-2 mag and contrast improvements of 1.5-4x relative to single-band pyramids, by sensing over 800-1800 nm. Practical design and implementation issues are discussed.
