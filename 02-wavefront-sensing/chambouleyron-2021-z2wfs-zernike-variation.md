# Variation on a Zernike wavefront sensor theme: optimal use of photons

**Chambouleyron et al. (2021)** · *A&A 650, L8* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.07690) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202140870](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202140870)

## Summary

Investigates how the diameter of the phase-shifting dot of a Zernike wavefront sensor affects its sensitivity, using an analytical Fourier-filtering framework validated with end-to-end simulations. The authors show that sensitivity can be optimised by changing the dot diameter, approaching the theoretical optimum at the expense of low-spatial-frequency sensitivity. A sensor with a 2 lambda/D dot (Z2WFS) has twice the sensitivity of the classical 1.06 lambda/D design for all modes except tip-tilt, with a similar dynamic range.
