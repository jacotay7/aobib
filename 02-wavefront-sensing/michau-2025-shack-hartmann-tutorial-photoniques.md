# La mesure de surface d'onde par Shack-Hartmann (Shack-Hartmann wavefront measurement)

**Michau & Fusco (2025)** · *Photoniques 133, 38-42* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-05210245) · DOI: [10.1051/photon/2025313338](https://doi.org/10.1051/photon/2025313338)

## Summary

A short French-language tutorial on the Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensor, widely used in adaptive optics and optical metrology. It recalls the principle of a microlens array sampling the wavefront, then covers data processing to obtain local slopes and the reconstruction of the wavefront from them. Advantages and limitations of the device are highlighted.
