# Review on methods for wavefront reconstruction from pyramid wavefront sensor data

**Shatokhina et al. (2020)** · *JATIS 6, 010901* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.6.1.010901) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.6.1.010901](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.6.1.010901)

## Summary

Reviews wavefront reconstruction algorithms for the pyramid wavefront sensor in the context of ELT-scale instruments. Linear and nonlinear algorithms developed over the previous decades from several forward models are summarised, and compared in terms of underlying sensor model, computational complexity and reconstruction quality. Existing and possible solutions are also reviewed for practical effects that matter more at ELT scale, including pupil segmentation, the low wind effect, sensor nonlinearity and non-common path aberrations, and directions for further work are outlined.
