# On-sky demonstration of a vector Zernike wavefront sensor in a cascaded adaptive optics system

**Motte et al. (2026)** · *A&A (2026)* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30666) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202661472](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202661472)

## Summary

Tests whether a Zernike wavefront sensor can operate on sky as the second-stage sensor of a cascaded AO system. The OZIRIIS stage, combining a vector Zernike wavefront sensor with a 97-actuator deformable mirror at 400 Hz, was added downstream of the pyramid-based first stage of PAPYRUS at Observatoire de Haute-Provence. Control used a single ZWFS signal, while the full vector-ZWFS data were used afterwards for non-linear reconstruction and telemetry analysis. The second stage increased Strehl ratio by up to 16 percentage points, and telemetry revealed optical-gain effects at low Strehl, in agreement with simulations.
