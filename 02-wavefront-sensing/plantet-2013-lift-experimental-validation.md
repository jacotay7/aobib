# Experimental validation of LIFT for estimation of low-order modes in low-flux wavefront sensing

**Plantet et al. (2013)** · *Optics Express 21, 16337* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.21.016337) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.21.016337](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.21.016337)

## Summary

Presents the LIFT focal-plane wavefront sensor, which performs maximum-likelihood phase retrieval on a single image with a known astigmatism to estimate low-order modes (tip/tilt and focus) from faint natural guide stars in laser tomographic and multi-conjugate AO. LIFT has better sensitivity than a 2x2 Shack-Hartmann and performance similar to a non-modulated pyramid for these modes, with a simpler set-up. The paper describes the concept and data processing and reports laboratory validation of tip/tilt and focus estimation in monochromatic and broadband light, confirming the low noise sensitivity predicted by theory.
