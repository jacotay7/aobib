# Focal-plane wavefront sensing with photonic lanterns: theoretical framework

**Lin et al. (2022)** · *JOSA B 39, 2643* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.10563) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAB.466227](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAB.466227)

## Summary

Provides a mathematical framework for the photonic lantern wavefront sensor (PLWFS), a tapered waveguide that couples light into several single-mode fibres and can perform low-order focal-plane wavefront sensing while feeding spectrographs or other fibre instruments. Linear and higher-order reconstruction models are derived together with metrics for sensing performance in linear and nonlinear regimes. The framework can include additional optics such as beam-shaping optics and vortex masks and generalises to other architectures. Initial numerical verification is given for a 6-port lantern.
