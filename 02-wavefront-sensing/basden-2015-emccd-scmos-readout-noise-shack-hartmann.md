# Analysis of electron multiplying charge coupled device and scientific CMOS readout noise models for Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensor accuracy

**Basden (2015)** · *JATIS 1, 039002* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.07929) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.1.3.039002](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.1.3.039002)

## Summary

Compares read-out noise models for the two main faint-flux detector technologies used in AO, EMCCDs and scientific CMOS (sCMOS), in terms of Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensor accuracy. The EMCCD is found to be advantageous in almost all situations, and the commonly used simplified EMCCD read-out model is found adequate. The common simple sCMOS noise models are found to be optimistic, and the author recommends modelling the full probability distribution of sCMOS rms read noise in performance studies.
