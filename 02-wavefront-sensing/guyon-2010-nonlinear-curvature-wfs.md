# High sensitivity wavefront sensing with a nonlinear curvature wavefront sensor

**Guyon (2010)** · *PASP 122, 49* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/0911.1310) · DOI: [10.1086/649646](https://doi.org/10.1086/649646)

## Summary

Describes the nonlinear curvature wavefront sensor (nlCWFS), which uses defocused pupil images as in curvature sensing but reconstructs the wavefront with a nonlinear phase-retrieval scheme. By exploiting the spatial coherence of the wavefront, interference speckles encode aberrations with sensitivity set by the diffraction limit lambda/D rather than the seeing limit, approaching the fundamental sensitivity limit. Closed-loop simulations show a 100 nm RMS wavefront error on an 8 m telescope with an mV = 13 guide star. The technique is presented as suited to high-precision AO on bright stars, such as exoplanet and disk imaging.
