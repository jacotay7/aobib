# Comparison of centroid computation algorithms in a Shack-Hartmann sensor

**Thomas et al. (2006)** · *MNRAS 371, 323* · [Free full text](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/2006MNRAS.371..323T) · DOI: [10.1111/j.1365-2966.2006.10661.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2966.2006.10661.x)

## Summary

Combines analytical theory with extensive numerical simulations to compare Shack-Hartmann centroiding algorithms: thresholding, weighted centroid, correlation and quad cell. Optimal parameters for each method are derived as functions of photon flux, read-out noise and turbulence strength. At very low flux the quad cell and weighted centroid give the best results, the latter remaining linear if the weight follows spot displacements; both work down to about 10 photons per subaperture with 3 electrons read noise. At high flux, errors are dominated by non-linearity, spot truncation and pixel sampling, and optimised centre-of-gravity and correlation methods become equivalent and outperform the quad cell.
