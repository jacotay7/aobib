# Development of a non-linear curvature wavefront sensor for the Subaru Telescope's AO3k system

**Ahn et al. (2023)** · *Proc. AO4ELT7 (Avignon, 2023)* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-04429016) · DOI: [10.13009/AO4ELT7-2023-075](https://doi.org/10.13009/AO4ELT7-2023-075)

## Summary

Presents the nonlinear curvature wavefront sensor developed as a new visible sensor for AO3k, the upgrade of Subaru's AO188 system, including its optical and optomechanical design. The sensor has two features: a dual-stroke linear mode giving good sensitivity to both low- and high-order aberrations, and nonlinear wavefront reconstruction providing a large dynamic range. Laboratory closed-loop results with linear and nonlinear reconstruction algorithms are reported.
