# An all-photonic focal-plane wavefront sensor

**Norris et al. (2020)** · *Nature Communications 11, 5335* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-19117-w) · DOI: [10.1038/s41467-020-19117-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-19117-w)

## Summary

Presents a wavefront sensor based on a photonic lantern mode converter combined with deep learning, placed in the same focal plane as the science image and suited to single-mode fibre injection. Measuring the intensities of an array of single-mode outputs allows both phase and amplitude of the incident wavefront to be reconstructed. The concept is demonstrated in simulation and in the laboratory, recovering Zernike wavefront errors from focal-plane measurements to 5.1e-3 pi radians RMS error.
