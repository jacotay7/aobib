# The 1.6 m Off-Axis New Solar Telescope (NST) in Big Bear

**Goode & Cao (2012)** · *Second ATST-EAST Meeting, ASP Conf. Ser. 463, 357* · [Free full text](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/2012ASPC..463..357G)

## Summary

Overview of the 1.6-m off-axis New Solar Telescope (later renamed the Goode Solar Telescope) at Big Bear Solar Observatory, the first facility-class solar telescope built in the US in a generation and an off-axis design of the kind planned for ATST/DKIST. The paper reports that the telescope is in regular operation with adaptive optics feeding photometric and near-infrared polarimetric instruments and an imaging spectrograph, and shows examples of its high-resolution capabilities. It also outlines plans and motivation for the next generation of NST instrumentation.
