# Multi-frame blind deconvolution with linear equality constraints

**Löfdahl (2002)** · *Proc. SPIE 4792, 146* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0209004) · DOI: [10.1117/12.451791](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.451791)

## Summary

Formulates phase-diverse speckle as multi-frame blind deconvolution (MFBD) with a set of linear equality constraints on the wavefront expansion coefficients. In this MFBD-LEC formulation, different data-collection schemes are specified as constraint data rather than in the code, and new expressions for the gradient of the wavefront parameters do not have to be derived for each type of data set. The idea is first presented for phase diversity, phase-diverse speckle and Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensing and then generalised. The formulation was later used as the basis of the multi-object MFBD (MOMFBD) method of van Noort et al. (2005).
