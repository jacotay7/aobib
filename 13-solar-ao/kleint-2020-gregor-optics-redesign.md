# GREGOR: Optics Redesign and Updates from 2018-2020

**Kleint et al. (2020)** · *A&A 641, A27* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.11875) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202038208](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202038208)

## Summary

Describes the 2018-2020 upgrade of the 1.5 m GREGOR solar telescope on Tenerife, covering optics, alignment, instrumentation, vibration reduction, control systems, building and management. All powered mirrors except the primary were exchanged, and the AO relay optics and instrument layout were redesigned. The AO system was also updated to automate tasks previously performed manually. Since 2020 the telescope has observed with diffraction-limited performance.
