# The wavefront sensing making-of for THEMIS solar telescope

**Tallon et al. (2019)** · *Proc. AO4ELT6 (Québec, 2019)* · [Free full text](https://ao4elt6.copl.ulaval.ca/proceedings/401-ALSf-12.pdf)

## Summary

Describes the wavefront-sensing algorithms of the single-deformable-mirror AO system being installed on the 90 cm THEMIS solar telescope, which is required to run unsupervised in any seeing condition, including sensing on low-contrast granulation. In poor conditions the Shack-Hartmann subaperture images are blurred in a spatially random, rapidly changing way. The authors use an inverse-problem approach in which the sensor estimates the wavefront gradients together with their errors and covariances, allowing the reconstruction and temporal control to adapt to the changing conditions. The implementation is constrained to run on a low-cost multi-core CPU.
