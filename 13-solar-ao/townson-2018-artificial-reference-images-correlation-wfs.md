# Generating Artificial Reference Images for Open Loop Correlation Wavefront Sensors

**Townson et al. (2018)** · *MNRAS 479, 1595* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.03233) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/sty1362](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/sty1362)

## Summary

Describes a method for synthesising reference images for correlation Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensors that cover a larger field of view than an individual subaperture, built only from the wavefront-sensor images themselves. These supersized references allow larger relative shifts between subapertures to be measured, as occurs in open-loop wavefront sensing. For solar AO, the measurable shift range grows from 12% to 50% of the subaperture field of view, and for elongated laser-guide-star spots from 12% to 25%. The technique is presented as a software-only upgrade for existing systems.
