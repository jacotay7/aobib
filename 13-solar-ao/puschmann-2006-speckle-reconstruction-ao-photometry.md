# Speckle reconstruction of photometric data observed with adaptive optics

**Puschmann & Sailer (2006)** · *A&A 454, 1011* · [Free full text](https://www.aanda.org/10.1051/0004-6361:20053918) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361:20053918](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361:20053918)

## Summary

Combines adaptive optics with post-facto speckle reconstruction for active-region observations taken with the Swedish 1-m Solar Telescope. Two modified versions of the Göttingen speckle-masking code are presented and compared with the conventional method: one using field-dependent speckle transfer functions, and one using simulated AO-corrected speckle transfer functions. The comparison shows that the field dependence of the AO correction and the deformable-mirror fitting error must be taken into account, and that the method with simulated AO-corrected transfer functions gives the most uniform image quality over the field. Small differences between the transfer functions used can change the estimated intensities by up to 22%.
