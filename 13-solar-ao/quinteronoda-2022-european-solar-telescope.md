# The European Solar Telescope

**Quintero Noda et al. (2022)** · *A&A 666, A21* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.10905) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202243867](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202243867)

## Summary

Presents the European Solar Telescope (EST), a planned 4.2 m open-configuration solar telescope for visible and near-infrared observations aimed at the magnetic connectivity of the solar atmosphere from the deep photosphere to the upper chromosphere. The design builds on experience from SST, VTT, GREGOR, THEMIS and DOT. Its listed technological features include the first adaptive secondary mirror for a solar telescope, a multi-conjugate AO system whose deformable mirrors are integrated into the telescope optical design, a polarimetrically compensated layout, and a suite of mostly etalon-based instruments. The paper describes the science cases and the telescope and instrument concepts.
