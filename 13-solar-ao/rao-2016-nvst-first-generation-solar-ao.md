# First generation solar adaptive optics system for 1-m New Vacuum Solar Telescope at Fuxian Solar Observatory

**Rao et al. (2016)** · *Res. Astron. Astrophys. 16, 023* · [Free full text](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/1674-4527/16/2/023) · DOI: [10.1088/1674-4527/16/2/023](https://doi.org/10.1088/1674-4527/16/2/023)

## Summary

Describes the first-generation solar adaptive optics system of the 1-m New Vacuum Solar Telescope at Fuxian Solar Observatory. It has a fine tracking loop with a tip-tilt mirror and correlation tracker, and a high-order loop with a 37-element deformable mirror, a correlating Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensor based on the absolute-difference algorithm, and a real-time controller. Compared with the earlier 37-element system on the 26-cm Solar Fine Structure Telescope, the subaperture layout was changed from square to hexagonal and a faster camera and controller were added. The sensor runs at 2100 Hz with a correction bandwidth above 100 Hz, and after correction the mean residual image motion and RMS wavefront error are 0.06 arcsec and 45 nm.
