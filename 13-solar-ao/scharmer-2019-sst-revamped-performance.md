# Is the sky the limit? Performance of the revamped Swedish 1-m Solar Telescope and its blue- and red-beam reimaging systems

**Scharmer et al. (2019)** · *A&A 626, A55* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.05588) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201935735](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201935735)

## Summary

Reports on the performance of the Swedish 1-m Solar Telescope after its upgrade with an 85-electrode adaptive (monomorph) mirror and a new tip-tilt mirror. The authors show that for data compensated by AO and/or post-processing, the rms granulation contrast is proportional to the Strehl ratio computed from the residual small-scale wavefront error, and argue that the wings of the high-order compensated PSF extend to no more than about 2 arcsec. Simultaneous seeing and granulation-contrast measurements are reported for wavelengths from 395 nm to 853.6 nm on the CRISP and CHROMIS beams, with maximum 2-s average contrasts of 11.8% at 525 nm and about 16% at 395 nm after compensating only for the diffraction-limited PSF.
