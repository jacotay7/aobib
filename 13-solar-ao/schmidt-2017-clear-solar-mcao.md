# Clear widens the field for observations of the Sun with multi-conjugate adaptive optics

**Schmidt et al. (2017)** · *A&A 597, L8* · [Free full text](https://www.aanda.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201629970) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201629970](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201629970)

## Summary

Reports on-sky results of Clear, the MCAO pathfinder at the 1.6 m Goode Solar Telescope at Big Bear Solar Observatory, which uses three deformable mirrors for wide-field correction. Granulation and sunspot images taken in July 2016 show that MCAO correction was most effective over a field about three times wider than that corrected by classical AO, and the full 53 arcsec field of view benefited from the correction. The authors also note that correction of only the ground layer is suited for wide-field seeing improvement.
