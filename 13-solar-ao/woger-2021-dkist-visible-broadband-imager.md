# The Daniel K. Inouye Solar Telescope (DKIST)/Visible Broadband Imager (VBI)

**Wöger et al. (2021)** · *Solar Physics 296, 145* · [Free full text](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11207-021-01881-7) · DOI: [10.1007/s11207-021-01881-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11207-021-01881-7)

## Summary

Describes the Visible Broadband Imager, a first-light instrument of the 4-m Daniel K. Inouye Solar Telescope that images the photosphere and chromosphere at high spatial resolution and cadence. The instrument is designed to deliver diffraction-limited images at several wavelengths by combining the DKIST adaptive optics with post-facto speckle image reconstruction that removes residual atmospheric effects. The paper covers the instrument design, operation and data processing. First VBI images show that the DKIST optical system enables diffraction-limited imaging across a large field of view at several layers of the solar atmosphere.
