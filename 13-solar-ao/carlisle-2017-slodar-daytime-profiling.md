# Use of SLODAR for daytime turbulence profiling

**Carlisle et al. (2017)** · *Proc. AO4ELT5 (Tenerife, 2017), 0103* · [Free full text](https://meetings.iac.es/AO4ELT5/media/proceedings/proceeding-103.pdf) · DOI: [10.26698/AO4ELT5.0103](https://doi.org/10.26698/AO4ELT5.0103)

## Summary

Adapts the SLODAR turbulence-profiling technique to solar telescopes with Shack-Hartmann sensors observing granulation, starting from the multi-direction SLODAR variant developed for the Gemini South MCAO system. Computer simulations are used to assess the accuracy and reliability of the method with extended objects, with emphasis on its reduced sensitivity with height, which is important in the solar case. The aim is to validate solar SLODAR and choose the best design for deployment at Clear (the MCAO system at Big Bear) and at DKIST.
