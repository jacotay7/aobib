# AO-308: the high-order adaptive optics system at Big Bear Solar Observatory

**Shumko et al. (2014)** · *Proc. SPIE 9148 (Adaptive Optics Systems IV), 914835* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/269320473_AO-308_the_high-order_adaptive_optics_system_at_Big_Bear_Solar_Observatory) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2056731](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2056731)

## Summary

Presents AO-308, the high-order adaptive optics system of the Big Bear Solar Observatory's 1.6 m telescope, built in collaboration with the National Solar Observatory. It uses a 357-actuator Xinetics deformable mirror and a 308-subaperture wavefront sensor read by a Phantom V7.3 camera at 2000 Hz, with image processing on digital signal processors. The system was used successfully during the 2013 observing season, correcting up to 310 modes and delivering diffraction-limited images at all wavelengths of interest.
