# Speckle image reconstruction of the adaptive optics solar images

**Zhong et al. (2014)** · *Optics Express 22, 29249* · [Free full text](https://opg.optica.org/oe/fulltext.cfm?uri=oe-22-23-29249) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.22.029249](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.22.029249)

## Summary

Restores AO-corrected solar images with speckle reconstruction in which the speckle transfer function is modelled as an annular distribution following the angular dependence of AO compensation, with the transfer function in each annulus obtained from a Fried parameter estimated by the spectral ratio method. Reconstructions of images from a 37-element solar AO system show improved image quality. The photometric accuracy of the reconstruction is found to depend on distance from the AO lock point, which simulations attribute mainly to the mismatch between the calculated and true AO speckle transfer functions.
