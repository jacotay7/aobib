# 13 · Solar Adaptive Optics

Solar adaptive optics corrects daytime seeing at ground-based solar telescopes. Several things make it different from night-time AO. There is no point source: wavefront sensing is done with correlating (extended-scene) Shack-Hartmann sensors, which cross-correlate subaperture images of low-contrast granulation, sunspots or pores against a reference image. Observations are mostly at visible wavelengths. Daytime turbulence is strong, fast and concentrated near the ground. The corrected field is only a few arcseconds wide, while solar targets such as active regions span an arcminute or more. Since the late 1990s, single-conjugate solar AO has been routine at the DST, SST, VTT, GREGOR, GST/BBSO and NVST. It is a core part of the 4 m DKIST and the planned EST, and multi-conjugate and ground-layer AO are being developed to widen the corrected field.

This folder covers reviews; image-shift (correlation) algorithms and alternative sensors for extended scenes; solar AO systems, including low-order correlating wavefront sensors on balloon-borne telescopes; solar MCAO and GLAO pathfinders and simulations; AO for DKIST and EST; and daytime turbulence profiling for MCAO design and site testing (S-DIMM+, SHABAR, wide-field Shack-Hartmann, solar SLODAR). It also covers post-facto image restoration of AO-corrected solar data (phase diversity, speckle interferometry, MFBD and MOMFBD), since nearly all high-resolution solar data combine AO with restoration. Several reference papers on classical solar AO systems are paywalled (mostly SPIE proceedings) and are listed in [PAYWALLED.md](../PAYWALLED.md) rather than here.

## Subtopics

- **Reviews & overviews**: reviews of solar AO, and image-quality metrics for AO-equipped solar telescopes
- **Extended-scene / correlating Shack-Hartmann WFS**: shift-estimation algorithms, reference images, field-of-view effects, interferometric alternatives, THEMIS sensing
- **Solar AO systems (single-conjugate & balloon-borne)**: SST, NVST, GREGOR, off-limb AO at the DST, and the Sunrise correlating wavefront sensors
- **Solar MCAO & GLAO**: VTT and Clear (GST) MCAO, NVST GLAO, layer-oriented concepts, simulation tools
- **Large-aperture facilities: DKIST & EST**: observatory overviews and their AO/MCAO designs
- **Daytime turbulence profiling & site testing**: S-DIMM+, wide-field SH profiles, solar SLODAR, SHABAR/S-DIMM site surveys
- **Image restoration with solar AO (speckle, MFBD, MOMFBD)**: phase diversity, MFBD/MOMFBD, speckle with AO-corrected transfer functions, deep-learning acceleration
