# Blur: Solar Multi-Conjugate Adaptive Optics Simulations

**Marino & Schmidt (2017)** · *Proc. AO4ELT5 (Tenerife, 2017), 0104* · [Free full text](https://meetings.iac.es/AO4ELT5/media/proceedings/proceeding-104.pdf) · DOI: [10.26698/AO4ELT5.0104](https://doi.org/10.26698/AO4ELT5.0104)

## Summary

Presents Blur, an end-to-end simulation package for solar AO and MCAO built for fast and accurate MCAO simulations with extended solar scenes. Blur is coupled with KAOS, the solar AO/MCAO control software, to simulate Clear, the pathfinder MCAO system at the 1.6 m Goode Solar Telescope. The paper uses these simulations to evaluate and predict the MCAO performance under different conditions.
