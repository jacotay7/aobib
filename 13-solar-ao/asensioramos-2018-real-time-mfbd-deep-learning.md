# Real-time multiframe blind deconvolution of solar images

**Asensio Ramos et al. (2018)** · *A&A 620, A73* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.07150) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201833648](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201833648)

## Summary

Uses deep neural networks to accelerate multi-frame blind deconvolution of AO-corrected solar images, which otherwise requires time-consuming iterative restoration. Two architectures are trained to reproduce MFBD-quality corrections, reaching a peak rate of about 100 images per second while suppressing noise and preserving the photometric properties of the images. Because photometry is preserved, polarimetric signals can be obtained with standard modulation without significant artefacts. The authors anticipate on-site real-time correction of solar images.
