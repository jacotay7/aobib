# First light of solar multi-conjugate adaptive optics at the 1-m New Vacuum Solar Telescope

**Rao et al. (2018)** · *Sci. China Phys. Mech. Astron. 61, 089621* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/324493831_First_light_of_solar_multi-conjugate_adaptive_optics_at_the_1-m_new_vacuum_solar_telescope) · DOI: [10.1007/s11433-017-9178-6](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11433-017-9178-6)

## Summary

Reports the first-light results of the solar multi-conjugate adaptive optics system built for the 1-m New Vacuum Solar Telescope at Fuxian Lake in China. The paper describes the system, in which deformable mirrors conjugated to different altitudes extend the corrected field of view beyond that of single-conjugate solar AO, and presents its first on-sky results on the Sun. It reports an on-sky demonstration of solar MCAO at the NVST.
