# Solar Site Survey for the Advanced Technology Solar Telescope. I. Analysis of the Seeing Data

**Socas-Navarro et al. (2005)** · *PASP 117, 1296* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0508690) · DOI: [10.1086/496939](https://doi.org/10.1086/496939)

## Summary

Describes the seeing-data analysis of the site survey for the Advanced Technology Solar Telescope (now DKIST), in which six lake, island and continental sites were probed for more than two years. Because daytime seeing can differ strongly between the ground and the height of a large telescope aperture, two independent inversion codes were developed to retrieve the height stratification of turbulence from simultaneous scintillometer-array (SHABAR) and solar differential image motion monitor data. Applied to a test subset from May 2003, both codes retrieve similar stratifications, and comparisons with in situ measurements support the inversions. The sample data show lake sites dominated by high-altitude seeing and the other sites by near-ground turbulence.
