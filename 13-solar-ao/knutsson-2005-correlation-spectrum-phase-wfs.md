# Extended object wavefront sensing based on the correlation spectrum phase

**Knutsson et al. (2005)** · *Optics Express 13, 9527* · [Free full text](https://opg.optica.org/oe/fulltext.cfm?uri=oe-13-23-9527) · DOI: [10.1364/OPEX.13.009527](https://doi.org/10.1364/OPEX.13.009527)

## Summary

Investigates a Fourier-based algorithm that estimates the subpixel shift between two noisy images from the phase of their correlation spectrum, for use in Shack-Hartmann adaptive optics on extended objects such as the solar surface. The precision of the shift estimate is analysed analytically and with Monte Carlo simulations as a function of image contrast, photon counts, readout noise, sampling format, zero-padding and field of view, with good agreement between the two. Compared with the conventional correlation approach to extended-object wavefront sensing, the method reduces computational cost at a small loss in precision.
