# Solar Adaptive Optics

**Rimmele & Marino (2011)** · *Living Reviews in Solar Physics 8, 2* · [Free full text](https://link.springer.com/article/10.12942/lrsp-2011-2) · DOI: [10.12942/lrsp-2011-2](https://doi.org/10.12942/lrsp-2011-2)

## Summary

Review of adaptive optics at ground-based solar telescopes. It explains why solar AO is feasible at visible wavelengths (bright source, modest apertures) and why it requires correlating Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensing on extended, low-contrast scenes such as granulation. The review describes the techniques used in solar AO systems, summarizes the systems deployed at the major solar telescopes and the scientific results they enabled, and discusses requirements for the new generation of large-aperture solar telescopes. It concludes with an outlook on multi-conjugate AO (MCAO) and ground-layer AO (GLAO) for wide-field solar observations.
