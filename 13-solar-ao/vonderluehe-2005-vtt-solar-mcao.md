# Multi-conjugate solar adaptive optics at the Vacuum Tower Telescope on Tenerife

**von der Lühe et al. (2005)** · *Comptes Rendus Physique 6, 1139* · [Free full text](https://comptes-rendus.academie-sciences.fr/physique/articles/10.1016/j.crhy.2005.09.007/) · DOI: [10.1016/j.crhy.2005.09.007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.crhy.2005.09.007)

## Summary

Describes the breadboard MCAO system operated at the German Vacuum Tower Telescope on Tenerife as a testbed for solar MCAO. The authors developed methods to estimate solar AO performance quantitatively from short-exposure science images and applied them to several data sets of the photosphere. With MCAO switched on, image quality is substantially improved over a 30 arcsec field of view, compared with about 10 arcsec for conventional solar AO. The paper also shows how irradiance fluctuations in the MCAO-compensated focus can be suppressed.
