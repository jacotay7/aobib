# The AO and MCAO for the 4m European Solar Telescope

**Berkefeld et al. (2017)** · *Proc. AO4ELT5 (Tenerife, 2017), 0173* · [Free full text](https://meetings.iac.es/AO4ELT5/media/proceedings/proceeding-173.pdf) · DOI: [10.26698/AO4ELT5.0173](https://doi.org/10.26698/AO4ELT5.0173)

## Summary

Overview of the AO and MCAO design for the planned 4 m European Solar Telescope, where the MCAO is integrated into the telescope optics so that the full light path contains 14 mirrors, including five deformable mirrors and a separate tip-tilt mirror. The paper lists the image-quality requirements (e.g. 30% Strehl at r0 = 7 cm and 60% at r0 = 20 cm over a one-arcminute field) and explains the two candidate wavefront-sensor configurations. It also discusses the effect of pupil segmentation by the telescope spiders.
