# The Daniel K. Inouye Solar Telescope – Observatory Overview

**Rimmele et al. (2020)** · *Solar Physics 295, 172* · [Free full text](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11207-020-01736-7) · DOI: [10.1007/s11207-020-01736-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11207-020-01736-7)

## Summary

Overview of the 4 m Daniel K. Inouye Solar Telescope (DKIST), its instruments and support facilities. DKIST uses a fast (f/2) off-axis Gregorian design with a maximum field of view of 5 arcmin and a thermal-control system that removes most of the 13 kW collected by the primary at prime focus to avoid self-induced seeing. A high-order adaptive-optics system with 1600 actuators corrects atmospheric seeing to enable diffraction-limited imaging and spectroscopy for the five first-light instruments, four of which are polarimeters. The paper also summarises the science drivers, including measurement of chromospheric and coronal magnetic fields.
