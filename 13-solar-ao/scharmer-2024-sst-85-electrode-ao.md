# The 85-electrode adaptive optics system of the Swedish 1-m Solar Telescope

**Scharmer et al. (2024)** · *A&A 685, A32* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.13690) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201936005](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201936005)

## Summary

Describes the concepts, design, implementation and calibration of the 85-electrode adaptive optics system installed at the Swedish 1-m Solar Telescope in 2013. The system combines a monomorph deformable mirror with a correlating Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensor, and uses a second, higher-resolution Shack-Hartmann microlens array to characterize and calibrate the mirror and to support modal control. Cross-correlations over the large wavefront-sensor field of view and the real-time control at 2 kHz run on a standard Intel PC workstation, continuing the workstation-based approach used for SST correlation trackers and AO since 1995. The paper documents critical aspects of design, calibration and software, and reports high Strehl ratios (inferred from granulation contrast) at wavelengths shorter than 400 nm.
