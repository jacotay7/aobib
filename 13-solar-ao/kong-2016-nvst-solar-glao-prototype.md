# Prototype of solar ground layer adaptive optics at the 1 m New Vacuum Solar Telescope

**Kong et al. (2016)** · *Chinese Optics Letters 14, 100102* · [Free full text](https://www.researching.cn/ArticlePdf/m00005/2016/14/10/COL201614100102.pdf) · DOI: [10.3788/COL201614.100102](https://doi.org/10.3788/COL201614.100102)

## Summary

Describes a prototype solar ground-layer AO system at the 1 m New Vacuum Solar Telescope. It uses a multi-direction correlating Shack-Hartmann sensor with 30 effective subapertures and about 1 arcmin field of view per subaperture, a 151-actuator deformable mirror conjugated to the pupil, and an FPGA/multi-core-DSP real-time controller. The system saw first light on 12 January 2016. On-sky results show improved solar images over the whole field of view of more than 1 arcmin when the GLAO correction is applied.
