# High-order aberration compensation with Multi-frame Blind Deconvolution and Phase Diversity image restoration techniques

**Scharmer et al. (2010)** · *A&A 521, A68* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1007.1236) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201014800](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201014800)

## Summary

SST images restored with MFBD and joint phase-diverse speckle show improved resolution but granulation contrasts that are too low, indicating additional stray light. The authors reformulate the restoration so that the average effect of hundreds of uncorrected high-order modes is compensated using Kolmogorov statistics, which requires simultaneous measurements of r0, and extend this to conventional stray light. Tests with simulations and real data show that only part of the contrast reduction in SST images is due to uncompensated high-order aberrations; the remainder is attributed to stray light from the atmosphere, telescope and re-imaging optics and to high-altitude seeing effects.
