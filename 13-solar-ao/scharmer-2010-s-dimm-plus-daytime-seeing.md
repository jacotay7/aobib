# S-DIMM+ height characterization of day-time seeing using solar granulation

**Scharmer & van Werkhoven (2010)** · *A&A 513, A25* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1002.3151) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/200913791](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/200913791)

## Summary

Introduces S-DIMM+, a method for measuring the height distribution of daytime seeing from Shack-Hartmann data recorded over a large field of view on solar granulation. The method uses only differential image displacements from individual exposures, so it is insensitive to residual tip-tilt errors. The authors conclude that it gives good measurements when the ground-layer r0 exceeds about 7.5 cm, and propose it for site characterisation and MCAO development for the European Solar Telescope. They identify the large field of view used for wavefront sensing as a major limitation, leading to large uncertainties in r0 at 30 km distance.
