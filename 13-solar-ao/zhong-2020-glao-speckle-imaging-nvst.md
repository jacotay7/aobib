# Wide field-of-view, high-resolution Solar observation in combination with ground layer adaptive optics and speckle imaging

**Zhong et al. (2020)** · *A&A 637, A99* · [Free full text](https://www.aanda.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201935109) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201935109](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201935109)

## Summary

Addresses photometrically accurate speckle reconstruction of wide-field solar images taken with ground-layer adaptive optics. The speckle transfer function model of Wöger and von der Lühe, developed for classical AO, is adapted to GLAO by deriving its inputs, the correction efficiency and an equivalent turbulence height, from the telemetry of the GLAO system rather than assuming a generic layer height. Simulations examine how the turbulence profile and the extent of the guide regions affect photometric accuracy. The method is applied to GLAO observations with the 1-m New Vacuum Solar Telescope.
