# Multi-conjugate AO for the European Solar Telescope

**Montilla et al. (2012)** · *Proc. SPIE 8447 (Adaptive Optics Systems III), 84475H* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/258719086_Multi-conjugate_AO_for_the_European_Solar_Telescope) · DOI: [10.1117/12.925744](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.925744)

## Summary

Studies the built-in multi-conjugate adaptive optics system of the planned 4 m European Solar Telescope. The design pairs a narrow-field high-order sensor for the ground layer with a wide-field low-order sensor for the high-altitude mirrors. Because solar AO must work from zenith to near the horizon, in daytime seeing and at visible wavelengths, the design uses four high-altitude deformable mirrors, so fast reconstruction is needed for the many degrees of freedom. The authors evaluate the Fractal Iterative Method (FrIM) and the Fourier Transform Reconstructor for this case with ESO's end-to-end simulator OCTOPUS.
