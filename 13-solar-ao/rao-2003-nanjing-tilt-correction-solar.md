# A Tilt-correction Adaptive Optical System for the Solar Telescope of Nanjing University

**Rao et al. (2003)** · *Chin. J. Astron. Astrophys. 3, 576* · [Free full text](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/2003ChJAA...3..576R) · DOI: [10.1088/1009-9271/3/6/576](https://doi.org/10.1088/1009-9271/3/6/576)

## Summary

Describes a tilt-correction adaptive optical system installed on the 430-mm Solar Telescope of Nanjing University, an early solar AO system developed by the Institute of Optics and Electronics (CAS). It consists of a tip-tilt mirror, a correlation tracker that measures image motion with an absolute-difference algorithm, and an imaging CCD camera, running at a sampling frequency of 419 Hz. The paper presents the system configuration, a performance analysis and observational results, reporting a residual jitter of 0.14 arcsec. The error-rejection bandwidth can be tuned between 5 and 28 Hz according to beacon size and turbulence strength.
