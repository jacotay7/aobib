# Adaptive Optics at the Big Bear Solar Observatory: Instrument Description and First Observations

**Denker et al. (2007)** · *PASP 119, 170* · [Free full text](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1086/512493) · DOI: [10.1086/512493](https://doi.org/10.1086/512493)

## Summary

Describes the high-order adaptive optics system installed at the Big Bear Solar Observatory in January 2004, built in collaboration with the National Solar Observatory. The hardware closely follows the AO system of the Dunn Solar Telescope, with a 97-actuator deformable mirror, a 76-subaperture Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensor and an off-the-shelf digital signal processor system, while the optical design was adapted to the 65-cm vacuum reflector and its post-focus instruments. The paper presents the optical layout, demonstrates AO performance on the Sun, and uses image restoration to illustrate the image quality achievable with the system. It documents the AO system that preceded the high-order AO of the 1.6-m Goode Solar Telescope at the same site.
