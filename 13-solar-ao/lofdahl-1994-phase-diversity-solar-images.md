# Wavefront sensing and image restoration from focused and defocused solar images

**Löfdahl & Scharmer (1994)** · *A&AS 107, 243* · [Free full text](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1994A%26AS..107..243L)

## Summary

Implements a least-squares phase-diversity technique that recovers wavefront aberrations and restored images from simultaneously recorded focused and defocused solar images, processing small subfields to handle anisoplanatism and fitting the image-alignment parameters together with the aberrations. Simulations indicate that wavefronts of up to 1/2 wave rms and 15-21 Zernike coefficients can be recovered from small granulation subfields. Applied to granulation sequences from the Swedish Vacuum Solar Telescope on La Palma, consistency tests between subfields and sequences confirm the method on real data and identify astigmatism and coma as the main telescope aberrations. The authors present the technique as a complement to, or for some applications a substitute for, adaptive optics for solar telescopes.
