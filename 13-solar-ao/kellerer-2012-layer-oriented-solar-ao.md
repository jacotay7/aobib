# Layer-oriented adaptive optics for solar telescopes

**Kellerer (2012)** · *Applied Optics 51, 5743* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.2801) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.51.005743](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.51.005743)

## Summary

Argues that star-oriented solar MCAO becomes difficult to extend beyond fields of about 60-80 arcsec, and proposes a layer-oriented approach for solar telescopes. Wide-field Shack-Hartmann sensors are conjugated to the strongest turbulent layers; averaging the wavefront over a wide field attenuates the signal from distant turbulence, so the tomographic separation is done optically. Each layer is then controlled by an independent loop that only needs to account for local turbulence, allowing larger subapertures and lower loop rates. The author argues that, unlike the star-oriented scheme, the layer-oriented approach benefits from larger fields.
