# Phase sensor for solar adaptive-optics

**Kellerer (2011)** · *A&A 530, A79* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1104.2560) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201116835](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201116835)

## Summary

Proposes an alternative to correlating Shack-Hartmann sensing for solar AO, whose spatial and temporal resolution is limited by the computing load of correlating subaperture images at daytime turbulence speeds. The proposed sensor sends the wavefront through a modified Mach-Zehnder interferometer in which a small central part of the wavefront serves as reference and interferes with the rest. The contrast of two simultaneously measured interference patterns gives a direct estimate of the wavefront phase without further computation. The paper identifies precise initial alignment as the critical point in implementing the scheme.
