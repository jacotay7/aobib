# Solar Multi-Conjugate Adaptive Optics at the Dunn Solar Telescope

**Rimmele et al. (2010)** · *Proc. AO4ELT1 (Paris, 2009), 08002* · [Free full text](https://ao4elt.edpsciences.org/articles/ao4elt/pdf/2010/01/ao4elt_08002.pdf) · DOI: [10.1051/ao4elt/201008002](https://doi.org/10.1051/ao4elt/201008002)

## Summary

Describes the dedicated multi-conjugate adaptive optics bench implemented at the Dunn Solar Telescope to develop well-characterized, operational solar MCAO for the next generation of large solar telescopes. Solar observations need correction over a field much larger than the isoplanatic patch, and solar structure conveniently provides 'multiple guide stars' in any configuration. The system uses two deformable mirrors, one conjugated to the telescope entrance pupil and one to an upper-atmosphere layer that can be placed between 2 and 10 km altitude. It has been locked stably both on artificial objects (slides with turbulence screens in front of the mirrors) and on solar structure, and the paper presents preliminary results and future plans. It is a separate, open-access paper from the same group's SPIE paper of the same title.
