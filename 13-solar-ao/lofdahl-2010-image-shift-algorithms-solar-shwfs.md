# Evaluation of image-shift measurement algorithms for solar Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensors

**Löfdahl (2010)** · *A&A 524, A90* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1009.3401) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201015331](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201015331)

## Summary

Solar Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensors measure local wavefront tilts as relative image shifts between subaperture images of an extended scene. This paper evaluates the accuracy of several shift-measurement methods (correlation-type matching functions combined with different subpixel interpolation schemes) and the effects of noise, bias mismatch and blurring, using artificial data with known shifts and simulated seeing for different r0. The best results are obtained with the square difference function and the absolute difference function squared, combined with two-dimensional quadratic interpolation. The paper also shows that these methods measure Z-tilts rather than G-tilts.
