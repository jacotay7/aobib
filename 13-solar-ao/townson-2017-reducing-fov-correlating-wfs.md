# Reducing the Field of View in Correlating Wavefront Sensors for Solar Adaptive Optics

**Townson & Saunter (2017)** · *Proc. AO4ELT5 (Tenerife, 2017), 0156* · [Free full text](https://meetings.iac.es/AO4ELT5/media/proceedings/proceeding-156.pdf) · DOI: [10.26698/AO4ELT5.0156](https://doi.org/10.26698/AO4ELT5.0156)

## Summary

Extended-field Shack-Hartmann sensors used in solar AO lose sensitivity to high-altitude turbulence because each subaperture averages over a meta-pupil that grows with altitude. This paper discusses how centroiding in a correlating sensor relates to this effect and proposes reducing the subaperture field of view while keeping a larger field of view in the reference image, so that accurate shifts can still be obtained from smaller subaperture images. The method is evaluated with end-to-end DASP simulations using a two-layer turbulence profile (ground and 10 km).
