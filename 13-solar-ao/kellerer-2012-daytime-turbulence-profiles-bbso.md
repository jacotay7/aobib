# Profiles of the daytime atmospheric turbulence above Big Bear solar observatory

**Kellerer et al. (2012)** · *A&A 542, A2* · [Free full text](https://www.aanda.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201218844) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201218844](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201218844)

## Summary

Measures daytime turbulence profiles above Big Bear Solar Observatory with a wide-field wavefront sensor installed behind the 1.6 m New Solar Telescope, reconstructing the altitude distribution of turbulence from the variation of wavefront distortions with viewing direction. The profiles show a ground layer below 500 m containing about 55-65% of the turbulence, a boundary layer between 1 and 7 km with 30-40%, and about 5% near the tropopause, which lies above 12 km in summer and between 8 and 12 km in winter. For MCAO at the site, the authors suggest correcting the ground layer, the centre of the boundary layer at about 3 km and eventually the upper boundary layer near 6 km.
