# Improved shift estimates on extended Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensor images

**Townson et al. (2015)** · *MNRAS 452, 4022* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.05011) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stv1503](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stv1503)

## Summary

Studies the accuracy of locating the cross-correlation peak in correlating Shack-Hartmann sensors for solar AO. The authors introduce a method for exploring the parameter space of image-shift algorithms that operates on the correlation images, so it does not require recomputing the correlations, and use it to optimise a thresholded, windowed centre-of-mass estimator. In a signal-to-noise regime typical of solar AO, this estimator improves centroid accuracy by about a factor of three compared with parabolic fitting. The method can estimate centroid errors in real time from on-sky data, allowing parameter optimisation without a separate calibration mode.
