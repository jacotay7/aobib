# The Multi-Conjugate AO system of the EST: DM height determination for best performance using real daytime statistical turbulence data

**Montilla & Montoya (2017)** · *Proc. AO4ELT5 (Tenerife, 2017), 0181* · [Free full text](https://meetings.iac.es/AO4ELT5/media/proceedings/proceeding-181.pdf) · DOI: [10.26698/AO4ELT5.0181](https://doi.org/10.26698/AO4ELT5.0181)

## Summary

Determines the conjugation heights of the four high-altitude deformable mirrors of the EST MCAO system. The design combines a narrow-field high-order wavefront sensor for the ground layer with a wide-field lower-order sensor for the altitude mirrors, using correlations on solar images with about 10 arcsec field of view, which affects the sensing of high-altitude turbulence. Performance is analysed for a broad range of measured daytime turbulence profiles from the Canary Islands observatories and for different telescope elevations, comparing static Fractal Iterative Method simulations plus an analytical error budget with end-to-end DASP-solar simulations. The authors report a homogeneous Strehl ratio above 40% over the 1 arcmin field for the full elevation range.
