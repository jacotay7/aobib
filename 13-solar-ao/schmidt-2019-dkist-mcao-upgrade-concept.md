# The concept of the MCAO upgrade for the Daniel K. Inouye Solar Telescope

**Schmidt et al. (2019)** · *Proc. AO4ELT6 (Québec, 2019)* · [Free full text](https://ao4elt6.copl.ulaval.ca/proceedings/401-VKPN-251.pdf)

## Summary

Presents the technical concept for the multi-conjugate AO system planned to replace the initial high-order single-conjugate AO of the 4 m DKIST a few years after first light. The design uses three deformable mirrors, nine correlating Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensors and a computer cluster for the control loop. The paper discusses the demands and challenges of the system and the plans for implementing its wavefront sensing and control.
