# Sunrise III: The Wavefront Correction System

**Berkefeld et al. (2026)** · *Solar Physics 301, 57* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07448) · DOI: [10.1007/s11207-026-02649-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11207-026-02649-7)

## Summary

Describes the wavefront correction and image stabilisation system (CWS) of the Sunrise III balloon-borne solar telescope and its performance during integration and the 2024 science flight. Fast image stabilisation uses a correlation tracker and a fast tip-tilt mirror. Low-order aberrations such as defocus and coma are measured with a six-element Shack-Hartmann sensor and corrected by the active secondary mirror, with automated focus and manual coma correction. The system is specified for 0.005 arcsec rms image stabilisation and 0.01-wave focus stability.
