# Solar multiconjugate adaptive optics at the Dunn Solar Telescope: preliminary results

**Langlois et al. (2004)** · *Proc. SPIE 5490 (Advancements in Adaptive Optics), 59* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/235322105_Solar_Multi-Conjugate_Adaptive_Optics_at_the_Dunn_Solar_Telescope_Preliminary_Results) · DOI: [10.1117/12.548929](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.548929)

## Summary

Reports preliminary results from the multi-conjugate adaptive optics system at the Dunn Solar Telescope (National Solar Observatory). The system uses tomography: three wavefront sensors, each locked on extended solar features such as granulation or sunspots, measure the turbulent volume to drive two deformable mirrors. Each sensor is a 5 × 5 subaperture correlating Shack-Hartmann on an 18 arcsec guide region within a 1.25 arcmin field. The paper also estimates the turbulence distribution and future MCAO performance from a separate tomographic wavefront-sensing experiment, as a step towards solar MCAO for the Dunn telescope and the planned 4 m ATST (now DKIST).
