# Multiple field-of-view MCAO for a Large Solar Telescope: LOST simulations

**Stangalini et al. (2010)** · *Proc. SPIE 7736, 77364H* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1008.0555) · DOI: [10.1117/12.856999](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.856999)

## Summary

Studies the performance of MCAO for a 4 m class solar telescope using the LOST simulation package, focusing on reducing the time-delay error that is particularly important in solar AO. Two approaches are examined: reordering the modal basis used for wavefront reconstruction according to mutual information, and forecasting the wavefront correction with different prediction methods. The authors discuss the advantages and drawbacks of each technique under different control conditions and present preliminary tests on real data.
