# Instrument Description and Performance Evaluation of a High-Order Adaptive Optics System for the 1 m New Vacuum Solar Telescope at Fuxian Solar Observatory

**Rao et al. (2016)** · *ApJ 833, 210* · [Free full text](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.3847/1538-4357/833/2/210) · DOI: [10.3847/1538-4357/833/2/210](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/833/2/210)

## Summary

Describes the high-order solar AO system of the 1 m New Vacuum Solar Telescope (Fuxian Solar Observatory), in routine operation since 2016. It combines a fine tracking loop with a high-order loop using a 151-actuator deformable mirror, a correlating Shack-Hartmann sensor with 102 subapertures (gradients from the absolute difference square algorithm) running at up to 3500 Hz, and a real-time controller built from an FPGA and multi-core DSPs. The system operates for r0 (500 nm) above 3 cm and corrects the first 65 Zernike modes; for r0 above 10 cm the Strehl ratio of the corrected TiO image of a pore exceeds 0.75. Performance is evaluated from real-time controller telemetry and observations.
