# Overcoming the effect of pupil distortion in multiconjugate adaptive optics

**van Dam et al. (2020)** · *Proc. SPIE 11448, 114480P* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.05913) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2560873](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2560873)

## Summary

In MCAO, deformable mirrors conjugated to altitude distort the pupil seen by the wavefront sensors, producing a dynamic misregistration between DM actuators and WFS subapertures. The authors argue that this is much more severe in solar MCAO than at night, because of the finer WFS and DM sampling and because solar observations are made through stronger turbulence and at low elevation. They present PropAO, an AO simulation tool built on the Python version of the PROPER library that propagates amplitude and phase through the atmosphere and MCAO system with Fresnel propagation. PropAO reproduces the pupil-distortion problem and is used to test non-linear reconstruction strategies for the European Solar Telescope.
