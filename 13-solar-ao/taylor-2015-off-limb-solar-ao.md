# Performance Testing of an Off-Limb Solar Adaptive Optics System

**Taylor et al. (2015)** · *Solar Physics 290, 1871* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.01826) · DOI: [10.1007/s11207-015-0697-9](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11207-015-0697-9)

## Summary

Solar prominences above the limb lack the granulation contrast used by standard solar AO, which limits the resolution of long-exposure near-infrared spectropolarimetry of these structures. This paper presents an AO system designed to lock directly onto prominence structure away from the solar limb. The measured performance of the off-limb AO system is reported, together with its expected performance at the Ca II 8542 Å science wavelength.
