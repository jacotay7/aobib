# Image Quality Specification for Solar Telescopes

**Subramanian & Rengaswamy (2023)** · *Solar Physics 298, 15* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.09829) · DOI: [10.1007/s11207-022-02105-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11207-022-02105-2)

## Summary

Proposes the rms contrast of solar granulation as an image-quality metric for AO-equipped ground-based solar telescopes, since the Strehl ratio cannot be measured directly on extended solar scenes. Simulations relate Strehl ratio to rms granulation contrast for a range of telescope diameters, Fried parameters and degrees of AO compensation, and the results are presented as semi-logarithmic curves. The authors compare these curves with Strehl ratios and contrasts published for several operating solar AO systems to estimate their efficiency, and suggest using the results to predict a lower bound on the granulation contrast of future telescopes.
