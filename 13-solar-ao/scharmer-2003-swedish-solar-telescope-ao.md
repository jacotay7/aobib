# Adaptive optics system for the new Swedish solar telescope

**Scharmer et al. (2003)** · *Proc. SPIE 4853 (Innovative Telescopes and Instrumentation for Solar Astrophysics), 370* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/2539760_Adaptive_optics_system_for_the_new_Swedish_solar_telescope) · DOI: [10.1117/12.460387](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.460387)

## Summary

Describes the low-order adaptive optics system of the 1-m Swedish Solar Telescope on La Palma, which began operating in 2002 and aims at its 0.1 arcsec diffraction limit in blue light, and its predecessor on the 50-cm Swedish Vacuum Solar Telescope. Both systems combine bimorph modal mirrors with correlating Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensors. All image extraction, cross-correlation and mirror control run on a single workstation or PC, using processor instructions that accelerate the correlations. The paper covers performance, maintenance and upgrades, the optimization of the electrode geometry of the new 37-electrode bimorph mirror for controlling Karhunen-Loève modes, and expected performance from closed-loop simulations.
