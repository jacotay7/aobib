# Adaptive Optics for GEO-Feeder Links: from Performance Analysis via Reciprocity Based Models to Experimental Demonstration

**Conan et al. (2019)** · *COAT-2019 Workshop (Communications and Observations through Atmospheric Turbulence)* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-03206080) · DOI: [10.34693/COAT2019-S5-003](https://doi.org/10.34693/COAT2019-S5-003)

## Summary

Uses SAOST, ONERA's reciprocity-based performance tool, to study AO for GEO feeder links: error budgets and link availability thresholds for forward and return links as a function of the number of corrected modes and the ground telescope diameter. AO correction is found to be essential for both downlink and uplink. A large telescope diameter benefits the downlink, whereas point-ahead anisoplanatism levels off uplink performance for diameters above a few tens of centimetres. The paper argues that reciprocity is a key principle for understanding AO-assisted ground-space links, and briefly presents FEEDELIO, described as the first experimental demonstration of uplink pre-compensation on a slant path representative of a GEO feeder link.
