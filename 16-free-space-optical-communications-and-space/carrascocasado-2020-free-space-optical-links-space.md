# Space Optical Links for Communication Networks

**Carrasco-Casado & Mata-Calvo (2020)** · *Springer Handbook of Optical Networks, ch. 34* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.13166) · DOI: [10.1007/978-3-030-16250-4_34](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-16250-4_34)

## Summary

Handbook chapter describing the basic principles and current trends of free-space optical communication for space links. It motivates optical links as a way to relieve the data-volume bottleneck of radio-frequency systems while reducing terminal size, weight and power. The chapter covers the atmospheric channel, space and ground terminal technologies, and optical ground stations, including adaptive optics (tip-tilt mirror, wavefront sensor and deformable mirror) to counter turbulence-limited single-mode-fiber coupling. It also surveys in-orbit demonstrations and emerging scientific and commercial lasercom projects. The arXiv preprint is titled "Free-space optical links for space communication networks".
