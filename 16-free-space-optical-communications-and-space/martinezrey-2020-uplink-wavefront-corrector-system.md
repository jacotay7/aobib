# Uplink wavefront corrector system: from paper to reality

**Martínez Rey et al. (2020)** · *Optics Express 28, 5886* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.28.005886) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.28.005886](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.28.005886)

## Summary

The Uplink Wavefront Corrector System (UWCS) is a pathfinder instrument developed by the Instituto de Astrofísica de Canarias with ESA to demonstrate adaptive optics correction of an upward-propagating laser beam, relevant both to free-space optical communication uplinks and to laser guide star launch. A Rayleigh laser beacon is propagated to the sky while a 12×12 Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensor measures atmospheric aberrations on a natural guide star, and a 97-actuator deformable mirror pre-compensates the outgoing beam. A scoring camera on the finder telescope next to the main aperture images the beacon, and the result is assessed in terms of beam power concentration. The paper describes the design, integration and testing of the UWCS at the ESA Optical Ground Station telescope at Teide Observatory, Tenerife.
