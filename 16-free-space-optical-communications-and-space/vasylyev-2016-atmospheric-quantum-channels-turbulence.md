# Atmospheric Quantum Channels with Weak and Strong Turbulence

**Vasylyev et al. (2016)** · *Physical Review Letters 117, 090501* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.01373) · DOI: [10.1103/PhysRevLett.117.090501](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.117.090501)

## Summary

Derives the probability distribution of atmospheric transmittance through a finite receiver aperture, including beam wandering, beam-shape deformation and beam broadening. The resulting model, called the elliptic-beam approximation, treats the beam at the receiver as an ellipse with randomly fluctuating centre, semi-axes and orientation. The authors state that it applies to weak, weak-to-moderate and strong turbulence and compare it with the log-normal model. The model is aimed at classical and quantum free-space communication links. It builds on the authors' earlier beam-wandering model (PRL 108, 220501, 2012).
