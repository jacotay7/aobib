# Zonal-based high-performance control in adaptive optics systems with application to astronomy and satellite tracking

**Prengère et al. (2020)** · *JOSA A 37, 1083* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-03169469) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.391484](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.391484)

## Summary

Presents a model-based AO control approach using a zonal (pixelised) representation of turbulence that encodes the frozen-flow hypothesis in a control-oriented model. A multilayer zonal model for single-conjugate AO is proposed, with edge compensation to obtain a sparser structure, and reduced models localised in the pupil with AR1 or AR2 dynamics are derived to lower computational cost. The resulting LQG regulators are evaluated with end-to-end simulations for a VLT-class SCAO system and for LEO satellite tracking, where apparent wind from telescope slewing accelerates turbulence evolution. Trade-offs between performance and complexity are discussed.
