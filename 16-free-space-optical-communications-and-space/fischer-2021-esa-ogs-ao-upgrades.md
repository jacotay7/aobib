# Adaptive optics upgrades for laser communications to the ESA optical ground station

**Fischer et al. (2021)** · *Proc. SPIE 11852 (International Conference on Space Optics — ICSO 2020), 118522A* · [Free full text](https://www.spiedigitallibrary.org/conference-proceedings-of-spie/11852/2599370/Adaptive-optics-upgrades-for-laser-communications-to-the-ESA-optical/10.1117/12.2599370.full) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2599370](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2599370)

## Summary

Presents an adaptive optics upgrade of the ESA Optical Ground Station on Tenerife for satellite-to-ground laser communication. It describes the design, fabrication and site acceptance tests of CARO (Cassegrain Adaptive Receiver Optics), an add-on AO instrument for the station's 1 m telescope built by Synopta. It also presents a baseline concept for a future bidirectional AO system in the Coudé path of the ESA OGS, with expected performance for a selected uplink application.
