# Performance of the Optical Communication Adaptive Optics Testbed

**Troy et al. (2005)** · *IPN Progress Report 42-161* · [Free full text](https://ipnpr.jpl.nasa.gov/progress_report/42-161/161A.pdf)

## Summary

The authors describe a JPL laboratory adaptive optics testbed built to study AO for optical communication receivers. It simulates night-time and daytime observing on a 1-m telescope (matching the OCTL telescope) and uses a 97-actuator deformable mirror. With laboratory-generated seeing of 2.1 arcsec at 0.5 µm, the system reaches a Strehl ratio of 21% at 1.064 µm, corresponding to 210 nm rms residual wavefront error. Performance predicted from real-time wavefront-sensor telemetry and analytical error terms agrees with the measured images. A version of the paper also appeared in Proc. SPIE 5892 (2005); the alternate link is the JPL repository copy.
