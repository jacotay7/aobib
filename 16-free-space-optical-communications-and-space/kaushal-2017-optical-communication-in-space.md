# Optical Communication in Space: Challenges and Mitigation Techniques

**Kaushal & Kaddoum (2017)** · *IEEE Communications Surveys & Tutorials 19, 57-96* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.10630) · DOI: [10.1109/COMST.2016.2603518](https://doi.org/10.1109/COMST.2016.2603518)

## Summary

Survey of free-space optical (FSO) communication for ground-to-satellite, satellite-to-ground and inter-satellite links. The first part reviews channel impairments, including absorption, scattering, atmospheric turbulence (scintillation, beam wander, beam spreading) and pointing errors. The second part reviews mitigation techniques at the physical layer (aperture averaging, diversity, adaptive optics, modulation and coding) and at the link, network and transport layers. It also covers orbital-angular-momentum multiplexing and the use of space-based optical backhaul links.
