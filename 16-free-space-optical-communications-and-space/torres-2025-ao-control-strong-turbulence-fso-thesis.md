# High-Performance Adaptive Optics Control in Strong Turbulence Scenarios for Free Space Optical Communications

**Torres (2025)** · *PhD thesis, Université Paris-Saclay / DLR* · [Free full text](https://theses.hal.science/tel-05634004) · DOI: [10.70675/9471b2edz2586z46c5zad17z7c8129a07794](https://doi.org/10.70675/9471b2edz2586z46c5zad17z7c8129a07794)

## Summary

Thesis on optimal AO control for free-space optical communication with emphasis on LEO links under strong, rapidly varying (daytime) turbulence. LQG regulators with zonal, frozen-flow auto-regressive turbulence models are adapted to FSOC, implemented in a Python predictive-control library and integrated into DLR's ELSiE simulator, with diffraction and scintillation included. On a horizontal test-bed link, including scintillation reduced coupling efficiency by 56.4 % for a tuned integrator and 69.8 % for LQG, which the author presents as quantitative evidence of the importance of modelling scintillation. Worst-case daytime turbulence profiles over DLR's Oberpfaffenhofen ground station are used to assess LQG performance and robustness at several elevations.
