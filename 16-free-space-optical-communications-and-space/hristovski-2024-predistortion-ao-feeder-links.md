# Pre-distortion adaptive optics for optical feeder links: simulations and performance analyses

**Hristovski et al. (2024)** · *Optics Express 32, 20976* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.521494) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.521494](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.521494)

## Summary

The paper analyses a bidirectional optical feeder link with pre-distortion (pre-compensation) adaptive optics between the next-generation optical ground station of the German Aerospace Center (DLR) at Oberpfaffenhofen and the laser communication terminal on the geostationary satellite Alphasat. End-to-end Monte Carlo simulations give performance estimates for this GEO feeder-link scenario. Pre-distortion AO reduces total uplink losses by up to 10 dB and reduces scintillation at the satellite by an order of magnitude. It also relaxes the link-budget requirement for 99.9% link availability by 20–40 dB, and using a laser guide star gives up to 8 dB of further improvement.
