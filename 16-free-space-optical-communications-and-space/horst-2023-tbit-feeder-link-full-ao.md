# Tbit/s line-rate satellite feeder links enabled by coherent modulation and full-adaptive optics

**Horst et al. (2023)** · *Light: Science & Applications 12, 153* · [Free full text](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41377-023-01201-7) · DOI: [10.1038/s41377-023-01201-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41377-023-01201-7)

## Summary

The authors, from ETH Zurich, ONERA and partners, demonstrate single-carrier transmission at Tbit/s line rates over a 53.42-km free-space path between the Jungfraujoch (3700 m) and the Zimmerwald Observatory (895 m) in Switzerland, used as a turbulent stand-in for a satellite–ground feeder link. A full adaptive optics system corrected the received wavefront, and polarization-multiplexed high-order complex modulation formats were used, giving net rates up to 0.94 Tbit/s. The authors report that the AO correction does not distort the reception of coherent modulation formats. They also introduce a four-dimensional BPSK constellation modulation and show 13.3 Gbit/s and 210 Gbit/s transmission with 4.3 and 7.8 photons per bit at a bit-error ratio of 10^-3.
