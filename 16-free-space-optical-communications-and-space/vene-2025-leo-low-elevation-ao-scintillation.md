# LEO-to-ground low elevation optical communication: optimization of an adaptive optics design robust to scintillation

**Vène et al. (2025)** · *Proc. SPIE 13355 (Free-Space Laser Communications XXXVII)* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22069) · DOI: [10.1117/12.3043460](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3043460)

## Summary

Addresses AO correction for LEO optical downlinks at low elevation, where strong scintillation degrades wavefront sensing and single-mode-fiber coupling. The authors propose a methodology for designing a scintillation-robust wavefront sensor, using the Shack-Hartmann sensor as an example. A slope estimator able to handle the large dynamic range of intensities between subapertures is presented. End-to-end simulations show that finer pupil sampling reduces wavefront measurement error, and a detailed AO error budget is used to discuss feasibility for a LEO downlink.
