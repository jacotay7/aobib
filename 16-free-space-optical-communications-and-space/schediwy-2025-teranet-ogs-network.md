# Commissioning of the TeraNet Optical Communications Ground Station Network

**Schediwy et al. (2025)** · *IAF Space Communications and Navigation Symposium 2025, 231-242* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.25090) · DOI: [10.52202/083082-0029](https://doi.org/10.52202/083082-0029)

## Summary

TeraNet is a three-node optical ground station network in Western Australia, built by the University of Western Australia to support missions from low Earth orbit to the Moon. Each node supports direct-detection, bidirectional optical links with LEO spacecraft and also develops one specific technology. TN-1 is a 70-cm station in Perth with sensitive detectors and modulation formats for lunar distances, TN-2 is a 70-cm station at the Yarragadee Geodetic Observatory with a commercial adaptive optics system for single-mode-fiber coupling, coherent communication and coherent time transfer, and TN-3 is a 43-cm mobile station on a utility vehicle for quantum communication and time transfer. The paper reports the commissioning campaign, including links with spacecraft in orbit, the performance of the TN-2 AO system, remote operation of the network and interoperability tests with other stations in Australia and New Zealand.
