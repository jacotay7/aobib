# A Framework for Iterative Phase Retrieval Technique Integration into Atmospheric Adaptive Optics—Part II: High Resolution Wavefront Control in Strong Scintillations

**Vorontsov & Polnau (2025)** · *Photonics 12, 185* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.3390/photonics12030185) · DOI: [10.3390/photonics12030185](https://doi.org/10.3390/photonics12030185)

## Summary

Second part of a study on integrating scintillation-resistant wavefront sensors based on iterative phase retrieval (described in Part I) into closed-loop atmospheric AO systems. The paper provides mathematical and numerical models, performance metrics, control algorithms and wave-optics simulation results for several system types, including free-space optical communication and remote laser power delivery. Theoretical analysis and simulations are used to show that the proposed closed-loop architectures can mitigate turbulence-induced aberrations under strong intensity scintillation, a regime where conventional wavefront sensing fails.
