# Adaptive optics benefit for quantum key distribution uplink from ground to a satellite

**Pugh et al. (2020)** · *Advanced Optical Technologies 9, 263-273* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.04193) · DOI: [10.1515/aot-2020-0017](https://doi.org/10.1515/aot-2020-0017)

## Summary

Models the effect of atmospheric turbulence on the link efficiency of a ground-to-satellite optical uplink for QKD and how much adaptive optics can recover. Both LEO and GEO uplink scenarios are examined. With a co-aligned downward beacon, AO provides a transmissivity improvement of about 3 dB, while an offset beacon such as a laser guide star allows improvements of up to 7 dB. The authors note that, in the high-loss regime, reductions in channel loss have a favourably non-linear effect on secret key rate.
