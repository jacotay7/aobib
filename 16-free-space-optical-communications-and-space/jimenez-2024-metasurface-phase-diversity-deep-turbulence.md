# Single-Shot Phase Diversity Wavefront Sensing in Deep Turbulence via Metasurface Optics

**Jimenez et al. (2024)** · *Nature Photonics 19, 1315 (2025)* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.18789) · DOI: [10.1038/s41566-025-01772-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41566-025-01772-4)

## Summary

Notes that conventional AO wavefront sensors degrade in long-range, deep-turbulence conditions relevant to free-space optical communication, while existing phase-diversity sensors are bulky and slow. The authors use a nanostructured birefringent metasurface to produce phase-diverse images in a single shot, enabling compact, low-latency phase-diversity wavefront sensing. The approach is tested in simulation and experiment for Rytov numbers from 0.2 to 0.6. In both cases the corrected beam shows an average 16-fold increase in signal.
