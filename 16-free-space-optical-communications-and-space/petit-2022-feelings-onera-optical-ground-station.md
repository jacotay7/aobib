# FEELINGS: the Onera's optical ground station for Geo Feeder links demonstration

**Petit et al. (2022)** · *IEEE ICSOS 2022* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-03828251) · DOI: [10.1109/ICSOS53063.2022.9749705](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICSOS53063.2022.9749705)

## Summary

Presents the design and status of FEELINGS, an experimental optical ground station developed by ONERA primarily for GEO feeder-link experiments, with an extension to LEO links planned. The station is intended to investigate GEO feeder-link optimisation, including AO correction of the downlink and AO pre-compensation of the uplink, and to prepare future high-capacity operational ground stations. The paper discusses how the critical scientific issues of GEO feeder links will be addressed with the facility.
