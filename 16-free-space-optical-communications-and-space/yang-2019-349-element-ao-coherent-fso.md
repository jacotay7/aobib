# Performance analysis of 349-element adaptive optics unit for a coherent free space optical communication system

**Yang et al. (2019)** · *Scientific Reports 9, 13150* · [Free full text](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-019-48338-3) · DOI: [10.1038/s41598-019-48338-3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-48338-3)

## Summary

This work from the Chinese Academy of Sciences and Jilin University follows an earlier experiment that coupled a coherent free-space optical communication system with a 97-element adaptive optics system. Here the authors evaluate the same kind of coherent receiver with a larger and faster AO system using a 349-actuator continuous-facesheet deformable mirror. Mixing efficiency and bit-error rate are computed as performance metrics for a range of Greenwood frequencies. The results show that performance improves when the larger, higher-speed AO system is used, which the authors present as the first quantitative verification of a coherent FSOC system with an AO system of this scale.
