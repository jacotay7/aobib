# First results from the adaptive optics system from LCRD's Optical Ground Station One

**Roberts et al. (2018)** · *AMOS Technologies Conference 2018* · [Free full text](https://amostech.com/TechnicalPapers/2018/Adaptive-Optics_Imaging/Roberts.pdf)

## Summary

Describes the optical system deployed at the 1 m OCTL telescope for Optical Ground Station 1 of NASA's Laser Communications Relay Demonstration (LCRD), a GEO laser-communication mission. The adaptive optics system compensates the 1.55 µm downlink for turbulence and couples it into the modem's single-mode fiber, with a requirement of Strehl ratio above 70 % at 20 degrees elevation in day and night. It uses two deformable mirrors in a woofer-tweeter arrangement and a Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensor. The paper also covers the transmit system relaying beacon and communication beams for the uplink, performance predictions, and first measured performance. An earlier SPIE description of the same system is listed as an alternative link.
