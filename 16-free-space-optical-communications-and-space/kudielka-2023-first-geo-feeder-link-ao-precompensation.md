# Successful first optical feeder link demonstration between a ground station and a GEO satellite applying adaptive optics pre-compensation ("Alpha-Up")

**Kudielka et al. (2023)** · *COAT-2023 Workshop* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-04605018) · DOI: [10.34693/COAT2023-014](https://doi.org/10.34693/COAT2023-014)

## Summary

Reports uplink pre-compensation experiments between the ESA Optical Ground Station (Tenerife) and the TDP1 laser terminal on Alphasat in GEO. A bi-directional AO system with a common downlink/uplink aperture, a 69-element deformable mirror and a narrowband filter to reject back-scattered uplink light uses the downlink wavefront to pre-compensate the uplink, correcting either tip/tilt only or about 30 modes. Under strong boundary-layer turbulence, full pre-compensation increased average received power at the satellite by a factor 1.4 to 1.7 over tip/tilt, lowered the scintillation index from 0.45 to 0.2, and improved channel transmission by about 10 dB at the 99 % level. With weak boundary-layer turbulence both schemes performed well, and the remaining variability is attributed to tropopause turbulence and anisoplanatism.
