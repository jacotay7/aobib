# Application of AdamSPGD algorithm to sensor-less adaptive optics in coherent free-space optical communication system

**Zhang et al. (2022)** · *Optics Express 30, 7477* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.451350) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.451350](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.451350)

## Summary

Wavefront-sensorless adaptive optics based on stochastic parallel gradient descent (SPGD) can compensate turbulence in coherent free-space optical communication receivers, but SPGD converges slowly and can stall in local extrema. The authors, from the Chinese Academy of Sciences and Jilin University, combine SPGD with adaptive moment estimation (Adam) into an AdamSPGD algorithm. Analysis and numerical simulations show faster convergence, better robustness and better tracking of changing turbulence, with corresponding improvements in mixing efficiency, bit-error rate and outage probability. In experiments AdamSPGD needs about 50% fewer iterations than standard SPGD.
