# Adaptive Optics Communications Performance Analysis

**Srinivasan et al. (2004)** · *IPN Progress Report 42-158* · [Free full text](https://ipnpr.jpl.nasa.gov/progress_report/42-158/158B.pdf)

## Summary

The report analyses how much adaptive optics improves a deep-space optical communication link received through atmospheric turbulence in the presence of background light. Using simulated focal-plane intensity distributions with and without AO correction, the authors compute uncoded pulse-position-modulation (PPM) bit-error probabilities for two receivers: an adaptive focal-plane detector array and a single detector whose size is adapted to the signal spot. They find that practical AO systems give gains of about 1 to 6 dB over an uncompensated receiver, depending on the PPM order and on the background level. The work was part of JPL's study of AO for daytime reception of deep-space laser links.
