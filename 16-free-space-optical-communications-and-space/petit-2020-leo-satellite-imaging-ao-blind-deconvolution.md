# LEO satellite imaging with adaptive optics and marginalized blind deconvolution

**Petit et al. (2020)** · *AMOS Technologies Conference 2020* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-03051923)

## Summary

Presents ONERA's work on adaptive optics for imaging satellites in low Earth orbit for space situational awareness. The AO system and its performance when tracking fast-moving targets through the atmosphere are briefly described. Image post-processing is addressed with marginalised blind deconvolution combined with a parsimonious PSF model, suited to observations where turbulence and AO correction evolve strongly during a pass. On-sky results are presented. An earlier COAT-2019 paper by the same group (hal-03206079) covers the same system.
