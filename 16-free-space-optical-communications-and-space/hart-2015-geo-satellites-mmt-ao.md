# Resolved observations of geostationary satellites from the 6.5 m MMT

**Hart et al. (2015)** · *AMOS Technologies Conference 2015* · [Free full text](https://amostech.com/TechnicalPapers/2015/Adaptive_Optics_Imaging/Hart.pdf)

## Summary

The authors observed several spacecraft in geostationary orbit in the J, H and Ks bands with the adaptive optics system of the 6.5-m MMT telescope in January 2015. With AO the satellites were resolved close to the diffraction limit in each band. Combining the bands gives true-colour images, and the large aperture allows accurate photometric calibration with high time resolution, even for small and faint objects. Solar panels can only be imaged well at wavelengths longer than their cut-off of about 1.5–2 µm, so the authors argue they are well resolved only by telescopes larger than about 4 m. In one case the panels were seen to span about 24 m, twice the size given in published data.
