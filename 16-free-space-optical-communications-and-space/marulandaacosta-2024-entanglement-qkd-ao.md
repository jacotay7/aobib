# Increasing the secret key rate of satellite-to-ground entanglement-based QKD assisted by adaptive optics

**Marulanda Acosta et al. (2024)** · *arXiv preprint* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.09564)

## Summary

Assesses turbulence mitigation by AO for satellite-to-ground entanglement-based QKD, a protocol that requires two free-space channels to be considered simultaneously. The focus is on daytime link operation, where turbulence is strongest, and on increasing the coupling of the received signal into optical fiber. The analysis shows that AO improves the secret key rate of entanglement-based QKD by up to a few hundred bits per second compared with the uncorrected case.
