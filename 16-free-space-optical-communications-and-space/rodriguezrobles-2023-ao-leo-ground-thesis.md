# Adaptive Optics for LEO-to-Ground Optical Communication: Modeling, Optimization, and Experiment

**Rodríguez Robles (2023)** · *PhD thesis, Aix-Marseille Université / ONERA* · [Free full text](https://theses.hal.science/tel-04746416)

## Summary

Thesis on improving AO for LEO satellite-to-ground optical downlinks coupled into single-mode fiber. It analyses the servo-lag error caused by the fast apparent wind during satellite tracking, derives a theoretical bound on the reduction achievable by turbulence prediction, and proposes a predictive controller that reduces fiber-coupling fades in simulation. The same controller is applied to ground-based imaging of LEO satellites. The thesis also presents PICOLO, a laboratory turbulence emulator for low-elevation links, characterises its scintillation against wave-propagation simulations, and reports first AO coupling tests on the bench.
