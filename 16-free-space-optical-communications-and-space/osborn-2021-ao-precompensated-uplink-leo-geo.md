# Adaptive Optics pre-compensated laser uplink to LEO and GEO

**Osborn et al. (2021)** · *Optics Express 29, 6113* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.413013) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.413013](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.413013)

## Summary

Presents Monte Carlo simulations of AO pre-compensated laser uplinks from the ground to LEO and GEO satellites, including the point-ahead angle. Several correction modes are compared with no correction and perfect correction: tip/tilt only, full AO using the downlink beam, and a laser guide star at the point-ahead angle. Performance metrics relevant to optical communication are evaluated as a function of launch aperture, wavelength, launch geometry, ground-layer turbulence strength (day/night), elevation and orbit. The results are presented as upper bounds on AO-corrected performance intended to inform the design of future optical ground stations.
