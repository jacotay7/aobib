# Optimization of High Data Rate Ground to Satellite Links Pre-compensated by Adaptive Optics

**Lognoné (2023)** · *PhD thesis, Institut Polytechnique de Paris (Télécom Paris / ONERA)* · [Free full text](https://theses.hal.science/tel-04465340)

## Summary

Thesis on ground-to-GEO optical feeder uplinks pre-compensated by AO, where point-ahead anisoplanatism leaves long and deep fades in the flux coupled on board the satellite. Using a reciprocal formalism that describes the pre-compensation phase error and coupled flux analytically, four methods are developed to optimise the pre-compensation phase at the point-ahead angle from measurements available at the ground station. All methods reduce the pre-compensation phase error and improve the coupled-flux statistics. The thesis also evaluates telecommunication performance with these methods and develops a statistical channel model of the AO pre-compensated link.
