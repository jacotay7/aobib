# Validation of pre-compensation under point-ahead-angle in a 1 km free-space propagation experiment

**Brady et al. (2019)** · *Optics Express 27, 17840* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.27.017840) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.27.017840](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.27.017840)

## Summary

Reports an experimental investigation of adaptive optical pre-compensation under large point-ahead angles, motivated by Earth-to-GEO feeder links. A 1.0 km horizontal free-space link was built with a divergent beacon beam and a focused signal beam propagating in opposite directions through real turbulence. The paper describes the setup and measurement campaign. With a median isoplanatic angle of 0.16 mrad, an increase in received optical power through pre-compensation was demonstrated for point-ahead angles from 0.13 to 0.27 mrad.
