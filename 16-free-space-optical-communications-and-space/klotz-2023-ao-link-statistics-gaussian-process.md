# Assessment of adaptive optics-corrected optical links statistics from integrated turbulence parameters through a Gaussian process metamodel

**Klotz et al. (2023)** · *Int. J. Satellite Communications and Networking 42, 38-56* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1002/sat.1497) · DOI: [10.1002/sat.1497](https://doi.org/10.1002/sat.1497)

## Summary

Addresses how to predict the statistics of the power coupled into a single-mode fiber after AO correction on a GEO-to-ground downlink without full Cn2 and wind profiles. A Gaussian-process metamodel is trained to map four integrated turbulence and wind parameters, measurable with simple site instruments, to AO-corrected received-power statistics. The authors report an estimation error below 0.7 dB on the 1 % quantile of received power. The same inputs are also used to estimate the correlation time of the received power. The method targets the selection of available stations in a ground-station network.
