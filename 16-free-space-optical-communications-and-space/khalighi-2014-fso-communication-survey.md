# Survey on free space optical communication: a communication theory perspective

**Khalighi & Uysal (2014)** · *IEEE Communications Surveys & Tutorials 16, 2231* · [Free full text](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/ielx7/9739/6963743/06844864.pdf) · DOI: [10.1109/COMST.2014.2329501](https://doi.org/10.1109/COMST.2014.2329501)

## Summary

This survey covers outdoor terrestrial free-space optical (FSO) communication links operating in the near infrared. The first part describes FSO channel models, including atmospheric attenuation, turbulence-induced fading and pointing errors, as well as transmitter and receiver structures. The second part reviews information-theoretic limits of FSO channels and system-design techniques intended to approach them, including modulation, channel coding, spatial and cooperative diversity, adaptive transmission and hybrid RF/FSO systems. The authors identify link reliability under turbulence and adverse weather as the main obstacle to wider use of FSO. Adaptive optics is mentioned only briefly; the survey is a reference for the communication-theory side of turbulence mitigation.
