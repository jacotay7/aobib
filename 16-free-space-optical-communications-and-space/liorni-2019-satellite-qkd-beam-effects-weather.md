# Satellite-based links for Quantum Key Distribution: beam effects and weather dependence

**Liorni et al. (2019)** · *New Journal of Physics 21, 093055* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.08115) · DOI: [10.1088/1367-2630/ab41a2](https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/ab41a2)

## Summary

Builds a model of the losses suffered by optical signals on satellite-to-ground and ground-to-satellite free-space links, including diffraction, beam wandering, beam broadening and deformation from turbulence, and atmospheric extinction under different weather conditions. The loss model is used to compute the transmittance statistics for downlinks and uplinks. A performance analysis of several QKD protocols, including finite-key effects, is then carried out for representative scenarios. The approach is intended for feasibility studies of satellite QKD missions.
