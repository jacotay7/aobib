# Adaptive-optics-enabled quantum communication: A technique for daytime space-to-Earth links

**Gruneisen et al. (2021)** · *Phys. Rev. Applied 16, 014067* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.07745) · DOI: [10.1103/PhysRevApplied.16.014067](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevApplied.16.014067)

## Summary

Reports a quantum communication field experiment in which the atmospheric channel was tuned to reproduce the daytime sky radiance and slant-path turbulence of space-to-Earth downlinks. Higher-order adaptive optics increased quantum channel efficiency well beyond tip/tilt correction alone, and spatial filtering at the diffraction limit rejected sky background without an ultra-narrow spectral filter. High signal-to-noise probabilities and low quantum bit error rates were obtained over a wide range of sky radiances and turbulence conditions. The benefits for satellite-based quantum key distribution in daylight are quantified.
