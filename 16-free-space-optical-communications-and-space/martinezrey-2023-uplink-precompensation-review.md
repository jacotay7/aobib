# Atmospheric Pre-Compensation of Ground-to-Space Communications with Adaptive Optics: Past, Present and Future - A Field Review

**Martínez Rey (2023)** · *Photonics 10, 858* · [Free full text](https://www.mdpi.com/2304-6732/10/7/858) · DOI: [10.3390/photonics10070858](https://doi.org/10.3390/photonics10070858)

## Summary

This review covers adaptive optics pre-compensation of ground-to-space optical uplinks. It notes that the field has long had two camps, one advocating AO for optical communication and one considering it unnecessary. The review sets out the technical challenges of correcting an uplink, surveys research and on-sky demonstrations from the early experiments to the present, and discusses future prospects, including trade-offs between performance, cost and operational constraints. The review was written at the Australian National University.
