# Ultrastable Free-Space Laser Links for a Global Network of Optical Atomic Clocks

**Gozzard et al. (2022)** · *Physical Review Letters 128, 020801* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.12909) · DOI: [10.1103/PhysRevLett.128.020801](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.128.020801)

## Summary

Demonstrates optical frequency transfer over a 2.4 km horizontal atmospheric link with turbulence comparable to that of a ground-to-space link, motivated by comparisons between ground, airborne and spaceborne optical clocks. The link reached a fractional frequency stability of 6.1e-21 after 300 s of integration. The authors also show that a clock comparison between the ground and low Earth orbit would be limited by the clocks themselves after only a few seconds of integration.
