# Phase estimation at the point-ahead angle for AO pre-compensated ground to GEO satellite telecoms

**Lognoné et al. (2023)** · *Optics Express 31, 3441* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.476328) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.476328](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.476328)

## Summary

Proposes an estimator of the off-axis pre-compensation phase for a ground-to-GEO optical link affected by point-ahead anisoplanatism, using the downlink phase and log-amplitude measurements available at the ground station. Analytical tools and a general modal formalism are developed for the reciprocal residual phase covariance of any linear estimator, and a pseudo-analytical approach is used to generate coupled-flux samples. The estimator reduces the anisoplanatic residual phase variance by 35 % to 46 %, mostly on tip and tilt, giving up to 15 dB gain on the coupled-flux cumulative distribution at probability 1e-3. The authors note that this renews interest in 60 cm class ground telescopes for GEO feeder links.
