# Intermodal quantum key distribution over an 18-km free-space channel with adaptive optics and room-temperature detectors

**Rossi et al. (2026)** · *npj Quantum Information* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16680) · DOI: [10.1038/s41534-026-01358-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41534-026-01358-0)

## Summary

Demonstrates real-time intermodal QKD at telecom wavelengths over an 18 km free-space link between a remote terminal and an urban optical ground station with a 40 cm-class telescope. An adaptive optics system with direct wavefront sensing and high-order correction enables efficient single-mode-fiber coupling at the receiver. Secure key generation at 200 bit/s is obtained with a compact state analyser using room-temperature detectors. Experimental data are also used to validate a turbulence-based model for predicting fiber-coupling efficiency, from which design guidelines are derived.
