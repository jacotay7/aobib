# Feasibility demonstration of AO pre-compensation for GEO feeder links in a relevant environment

**Montmerle Bonnefois et al. (2022)** · *Optics Express 30, 47179* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.470705) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.470705](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.470705)

## Summary

Reports AO pre-compensation experiments on a 13 km slant path in Tenerife designed to be representative of a GEO feeder uplink with point-ahead anisoplanatism. Measured coupled-flux statistics are compared with performance models and end-to-end simulations across a wide range of turbulence conditions and found to be consistent. The validation is presented as consolidating the propagation channel models used to design future GEO feeder-link ground terminals. The experimental results are then compared with expected on-sky performance, and the representativeness of slant-path and horizontal-path experiments for real GEO links is discussed.
