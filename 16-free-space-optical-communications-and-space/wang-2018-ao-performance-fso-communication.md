# Performance analysis of an adaptive optics system for free-space optics communication through atmospheric turbulence

**Wang et al. (2018)** · *Scientific Reports 8, 1124* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-19559-9) · DOI: [10.1038/s41598-018-19559-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-19559-9)

## Summary

Studies how spatial and temporal turbulence characteristics affect the benefit of AO in a free-space optical communication receiver. Using the Greenwood frequency and the ratio D/r0, the authors derive a relation between fiber coupling efficiency and AO parameters (number of corrected Zernike modes and correction bandwidth). Simulations and laboratory experiments indicate that about 35 corrected modes suffice for common conditions and that the AO bandwidth should exceed the Greenwood frequency. In moderate turbulence (D/r0 = 10, fG = 60 Hz), doubling the bandwidth relative to the Greenwood frequency reduced the measured average bit error rate by two orders of magnitude.
