# Tip tilt and focus estimation based on LGS and downlink joint measurements for ground to GEO satellite optical communication link

**Lognoné et al. (2024)** · *Optics Express 32, 37739* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.538333) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.538333](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.538333)

## Summary

Addresses the estimation of tip, tilt and focus at the point-ahead angle for AO pre-compensated GEO feeder uplinks, modes that a laser guide star alone cannot measure reliably. The authors extend their minimum-mean-square-error estimator, previously based on downlink phase and log-amplitude, to also incorporate high-order measurements from an LGS at the point-ahead angle. The associated analytical reconstructor is derived, and phase-estimation performance and coupled-flux statistics on board the satellite are evaluated assuming an idealised LGS. The combined estimator reduces tip, tilt and focus error variances by up to 70 %.
