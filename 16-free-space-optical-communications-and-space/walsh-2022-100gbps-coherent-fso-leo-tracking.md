# Demonstration of 100 Gbps coherent free-space optical communications at LEO tracking rates

**Walsh et al. (2022)** · *Scientific Reports 12, 18345* · [Free full text](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-022-22027-0) · DOI: [10.1038/s41598-022-22027-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-22027-0)

## Summary

Coherent optical links between satellites and the ground need the received light to be coupled into single-mode fiber while the terminal tracks a fast-moving target through turbulence. This University of Western Australia paper reports a coherent 1550-nm link through turbulent air between a deployable optical terminal and a retroreflector carried by a drone. Pointing errors and turbulence-induced tilt were corrected with 10 Hz machine-vision tracking nested with 200 Hz tip-tilt adaptive optics. The system kept an uninterrupted 100 Gbit/s link while tracking at angular rates up to 1.5 deg/s, comparable to a spacecraft in low Earth orbit. The authors argue that ground-to-LEO links at Tbit/s rates can be reached with suitably equipped ground stations.
