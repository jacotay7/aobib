# Statistical properties of single-mode fiber coupling of satellite-to-ground laser links partially corrected by adaptive optics

**Canuet et al. (2018)** · *JOSA A 35, 148-162* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-01904583) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.35.000148](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.35.000148)

## Summary

Presents an analytical model of the instantaneous flux coupled into a single-mode fiber after partial adaptive optics correction of a satellite-to-ground laser downlink. Expressions are given for the probability density and cumulative distribution functions of the coupled flux, and for the average fade duration and fade-duration distribution. These quantities are needed to evaluate coded transmission over correlated channels. The model is compared with end-to-end wave-optics simulations for GEO-to-ground and LEO-to-ground scenarios, and the effect of different AO performance levels on fade durations is analysed.
