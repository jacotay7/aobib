# Joint optical-digital design strategy for adaptive optics systems: application to wavelength selection for satellite imaging

**Cheyssial et al. (2026)** · *Optics Express 34, 4895* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.581902) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.581902](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.581902)

## Summary

Proposes designing an AO-assisted imaging system jointly with its image-restoration (deconvolution) stage rather than sequentially. A criterion is derived that accounts for the source properties and the performance of the whole optical/digital system. It is applied to optimise the split of wavelengths between the wavefront sensor and the imaging camera for satellite imaging. The authors also examine multiple imaging channels at different wavelengths as a way to make the system robust to variations in turbulence strength and source magnitude.
