# Building Europe's first space-based Quantum Key Distribution system - The German Aerospace Center's role in the EAGLE-1 mission

**Calistro-Rivera et al. (2024)** · *75th International Astronautical Congress (IAC 2024), Milan* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.03222)

## Summary

EAGLE-1, led by ESA and SES, is intended to be Europe's first end-to-end space-based quantum key distribution (QKD) system, with a QKD payload on a low-Earth-orbit satellite, optical ground stations, quantum operational networks and key management. The paper describes the contribution of the DLR Institute of Communications and Navigation, which designs the QKD transmitter of the payload, including its software and firmware. On the ground side, DLR's Optical Ground Station Oberpfaffenhofen (OGS-OP) is being adapted to serve as the in-orbit-test station for the mission. The ground-station developments include an upgraded adaptive optics system that corrects atmospheric distortion and improves coupling of the received quantum signal into single-mode fiber.
