# Point-to-point stabilized optical frequency transfer with active optics

**Dix-Matthews et al. (2021)** · *Nature Communications 12, 515* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.04985) · DOI: [10.1038/s41467-020-20591-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-20591-5)

## Summary

Demonstrates phase-stabilised optical frequency transfer over a 265 m horizontal free-space link between compact terminals equipped with active tip-tilt mirrors to suppress beam wander. A phase-stabilised 715 m underground fiber link between the terminals is used to measure the free-space link performance. The active optics allowed continuous, cycle-slip-free coherent transmission for periods longer than an hour. The residual phase noise was 2.7e-6 rad²/Hz at 1 Hz, and a fractional frequency stability of 1.6e-19 was reached after 40 s, below the instability of the best optical atomic clocks. Limitations from residual atmospheric noise, laser frequency noise and thermal shielding are discussed.
