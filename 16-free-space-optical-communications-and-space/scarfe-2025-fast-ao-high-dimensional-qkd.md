# Fast adaptive optics for high-dimensional quantum communications in turbulent channels

**Scarfe et al. (2025)** · *Communications Physics 8, 79* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.13041) · DOI: [10.1038/s42005-025-01986-6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42005-025-01986-6)

## Summary

Uses a high-speed adaptive optics system to correct, in real time, turbulence-induced wavefront distortions on spatial modes used for high-dimensional QKD in a turbulent free-space channel. The authors first show improved coupling efficiency of a Gaussian mode after propagation through turbulence. Process tomography shows that AO substantially reduces crosstalk between spatial modes. AO also lowers the quantum dit error rate of an orbital-angular-momentum-based high-dimensional QKD protocol, enabling secure key exchange in a channel where it would otherwise not be possible.
