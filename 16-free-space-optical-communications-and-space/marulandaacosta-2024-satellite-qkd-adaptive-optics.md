# Analysis of satellite-to-ground quantum key distribution with adaptive optics

**Marulanda Acosta et al. (2024)** · *New Journal of Physics 26, 023039* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.06747) · DOI: [10.1088/1367-2630/ad231c](https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/ad231c)

## Summary

Studies the role of AO in optimising single-mode-fiber coupling for satellite-to-ground QKD downlinks. Realistic prepare-and-measure QKD configurations with discrete- and continuous-variable encoding are considered, including finite-size effects. Measured day- and night-time turbulence datasets are used to model the coupled-signal statistics after AO correction. Secret key rates are estimated as a function of turbulence strength, satellite altitude and ground telescope diameter, and the results are used to identify configurations where advanced AO is beneficial.
