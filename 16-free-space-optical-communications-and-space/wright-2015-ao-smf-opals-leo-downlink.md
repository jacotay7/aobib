# Adaptive optics correction into single mode fiber for a low Earth orbiting space to ground optical communication link using the OPALS downlink

**Wright et al. (2015)** · *Optics Express 23, 33705* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.23.033705) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.23.033705](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.23.033705)

## Summary

Reports an adaptive optics testbed integrated into the OPALS ground station telescope at JPL's Optical Communications Telescope Laboratory for the laser communication experiment on the International Space Station. During an ISS overflight, turbulence-induced aberrations on the downlink were corrected so that the received laser signal could be coupled continuously into a single-mode fiber. A stable output Strehl ratio of about 0.6 was obtained, and a 50 Mbps encoded HD video transmission was recovered at the fiber output. The authors present it as a proof of concept for AO-assisted, fiber-coupled downlinks from fast-slewing LEO spacecraft.
