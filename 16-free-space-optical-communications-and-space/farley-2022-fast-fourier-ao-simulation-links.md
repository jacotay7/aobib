# FAST: Fourier domain adaptive optics simulation tool for bidirectional ground-space optical links through atmospheric turbulence

**Farley et al. (2022)** · *Optics Express 30, 23050* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.458659) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.458659](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.458659)

## Summary

Presents FAST, a simulation tool for AO-corrected ground-space optical links built on an analytical Fourier-domain AO model from astronomy. Using the reciprocity principle, it can simulate downlink post-compensated or uplink pre-compensated beams. Comparisons with full end-to-end wave-optics simulations show similar results while running 10 to 200 times faster. The authors present it as a way to characterise links with complex AO systems over large parameter spaces.
