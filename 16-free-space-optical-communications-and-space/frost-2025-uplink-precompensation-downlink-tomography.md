# Uplink Pre-Compensation for Ground-to-Space Optical Communications Using Downlink Tomographic Reconstruction

**Frost et al. (2025)** · *Optics Express 33, 45142* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.08866) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.576114](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.576114)

## Summary

Proposes estimating the uplink wavefront error for ground-to-satellite optical links by tomographic reconstruction from successive measurements of the downlink beam with the existing downlink wavefront sensor. As the satellite moves, the measurements sample the atmospheric volume common to the downlink beam and the ground-station field of view, from which the turbulence along the uplink path is estimated. Simulations over representative atmospheric conditions show the method performs best when the point-ahead angle is many times the isoplanatic angle. Compared with pre-compensation using the downlink phase directly, the residual mean-square wavefront error along the uplink path is reduced by up to a factor of 8.6.
