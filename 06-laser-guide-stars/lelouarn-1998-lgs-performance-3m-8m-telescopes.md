# Laser Guide Star for 3.6- and 8-m telescopes: Performance and astrophysical implications

**Le Louarn et al. (1998)** · *MNRAS 295, 756* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9710130) · DOI: [10.1046/j.1365-8711.1998.01223.x](https://doi.org/10.1046/j.1365-8711.1998.01223.x)

## Summary

The authors build an analytical model of an adaptive optics system using a sodium laser guide star and apply it to 3.6 m and 8 m class telescopes, giving Strehl ratio and PSF full width at half maximum for good and median atmospheric conditions. Sky coverage is computed for both natural and laser guide star systems in two ways: statistically from stellar densities, and by cross-correlating a science-object catalogue with the USNO catalogue to find tip-tilt stars. Results are given as the fraction of sky and number of science objects observable with Strehl ratios above 0.2 and 0.1 in K and J bands, quantifying the gain brought by LGS for the VLT era.
