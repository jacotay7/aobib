# Impact of sodium laser guide star fratricide on multi-conjugate adaptive optics systems

**Wang et al. (2010)** · *JOSA A 27, A19* · [Free full text](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/m837w-a4627) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.27.000A19](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.27.000A19)

## Summary

Light scattered from the upward-propagating sodium laser beams is partly collected by the other LGS wavefront sensors, adding background in parts of their pupils; this cross-talk is called the fratricide effect. The paper quantifies four sources of scattering, absorption and backscattering and evaluates their impact for various zenith angles and turbulence profiles for the TMT MCAO system NFIRAOS. The resulting wavefront error is of order 5-20 nm RMS provided the mean fratricide background can be calibrated and subtracted to 80% accuracy. The authors also show that momentary variations in LGS signal level, from cirrus absorption or laser power fluctuations, affect performance more than an equal variation in the fratricide level.
