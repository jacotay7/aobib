# Mesosphere Sodium Column Density and the Sodium Laser Guide Star Brightness

**Ge et al. (1997)** · *arXiv preprint astro-ph/9708275* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9708275)

## Summary

The paper reports simultaneous measurements, made in 1997 at the MMT and the CfA 60-inch telescope, of mesospheric sodium column density and the absolute flux of a sodium guide star produced by a 3 W continuous-wave laser tuned to the D2 line. Returns for both linear and circular polarization scale with the simultaneous sodium column density, and circular polarization gives about 30% more fluorescence than linear. A 1 W circularly polarized beam is estimated to produce an R = 10.3 mag guide star for a column density of 3.7e9 per cm2. Year-round absorption-line measurements of sodium column density above Tucson show smaller seasonal variation than at higher latitudes and changes of up to a factor of two within an hour.
