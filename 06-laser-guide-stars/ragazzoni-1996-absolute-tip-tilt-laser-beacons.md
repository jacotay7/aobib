# Absolute tip-tilt determination with laser beacons

**Ragazzoni (1996)** · *A&A 305, L13* · [Free full text](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1996A%26A...305L..13R)

## Summary

This letter shows that the absolute tip-tilt of the wavefront can, in principle, be inferred from two artificial sodium beacons launched from ground stations located away from the observing telescope (a bistatic configuration). Seen from the telescope, such beacons appear as elongated strips whose positions relative to background stars carry the tilt information otherwise lost in a monostatic laser guide star. The author analyses the geometry and finds the technique limited by the required field of view, making it feasible mainly for telescopes up to about 4 m in good seeing, with applicability to larger telescopes or worse seeing possible with dedicated optics.
