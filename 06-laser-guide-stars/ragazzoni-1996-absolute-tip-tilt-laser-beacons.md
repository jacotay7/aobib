# Absolute tip-tilt determination with laser beacons

**Ragazzoni (1996)** · *A&A 305, L13* · [Free full text](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1996A%26A...305L..13R)

## Summary

This letter shows that the absolute tip-tilt of the wavefront can, in principle, be inferred from two artificial sodium beacons launched from ground stations located away from the observing telescope (a bistatic configuration). Seen from the telescope, such beacons appear as elongated strips; uplink wander only translates each strip rigidly, so the departures of the observed strip from a straight line measure the downward atmospheric tilt, and two crossing strips near the science target give the absolute two-dimensional tilt that a monostatic laser guide star cannot provide. The author analyses the geometry and finds the technique limited by the required field of view, making it feasible mainly for telescopes up to about 4 m in good seeing, with applicability to larger telescopes or worse seeing possible with dedicated optics.
