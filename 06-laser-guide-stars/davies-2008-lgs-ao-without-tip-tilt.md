# Laser Guide Star Adaptive Optics without Tip-tilt

**Davies et al. (2008)** · *The Messenger 131, 7* · [Free full text](https://www.eso.org/sci/publications/messenger/archive/no.131-mar08/messenger-no131-7-10.pdf)

## Summary

Even with a laser guide star, a natural star (at the VLT brighter than about V = 17 within about 60 arcsec) is needed to correct image motion, which excludes many targets such as deep extragalactic fields. The article explores the performance that can be obtained with the VLT Laser Guide Star Facility when the tip-tilt star is dispensed with altogether, so that only the higher-order aberrations are corrected using the LGS. Using on-sky data from the SINFONI and NACO instruments, it shows that this mode still improves resolution and ensquared energy relative to seeing-limited observations, and discusses the science cases for which it is useful.
