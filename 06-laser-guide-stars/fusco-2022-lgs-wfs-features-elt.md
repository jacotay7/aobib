# Key wavefront sensors features for laser-assisted tomographic adaptive optics systems on the Extremely Large Telescope

**Fusco et al. (2022)** · *JATIS 8, 021514* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.11383) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.8.2.021514](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.8.2.021514)

## Summary

At ELT scale, laser guide star spot elongation challenges the standard design rules for LGS Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensors, which require a trade-off between the number of subapertures, subaperture field of view and pixel sampling under a fixed detector pixel budget. A larger subaperture field of view reduces spot truncation, which otherwise causes severe measurement biases, but competes with spot sampling and pupil sampling. The paper presents a sensitivity analysis of these parameters and their impact on tomographic AO performance, and introduces wavefront super-resolution, which allows reduced pupil sampling per sensor, as a route to LGS wavefront-sensor designs suited to the ELT.
