# Adaptive telescope with laser probe: isoplanatism and cone effect

**Tallon & Foy (1990)** · *A&A 235, 549* · [Free full text](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1990A%26A...235..549T)

## Summary

The paper addresses two limitations of single laser guide stars: the cone effect caused by the finite altitude of the artificial source, and the restricted isoplanatic field. The authors propose retrieving a three-dimensional map of the phase perturbations through the atmosphere by combining Shack-Hartmann measurements from several laser spots, using subpupils larger than the Fried parameter. From such a reconstruction, the wavefront for an astronomical source at infinity can be estimated, correcting the cone effect and extending correction over fields larger than the isoplanatic patch. The work is an early formulation of multi-LGS tomographic wavefront sensing.
