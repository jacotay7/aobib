# Slow focus sensor for the Keck I laser guide star adaptive optics system using focal plane wavefront sensing

**Salgueiro et al. (2026)** · *A&A 707, A263* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15746) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202557464](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202557464)

## Summary

Slow drifts of the sodium layer altitude corrupt the focus measured by laser guide star wavefront sensors, so a natural guide star is normally needed to track this slow focus error. The paper develops a focal-plane wavefront sensing approach for slow focus tracking on the Keck I LGS AO system, aiming to improve sky coverage and allow faster focus updates with reduced lag. Three algorithms - Gerchberg-Saxton, the linearized focal-plane technique (LIFT) and Gaussian fitting - are compared for H- and K-band slow focus sensing in terms of linearity, computational cost and robustness to low signal-to-noise and residuals, using simulations and bench and on-sky data from Keck in 2025. Gerchberg-Saxton was selected, and on-sky tests showed it compensating deliberately introduced focus errors even in strong turbulence.
