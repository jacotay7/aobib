# A Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensor projected on to the sky with reduced focal anisoplanatism

**Butterley et al. (2006)** · *MNRAS 368, 837* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0602238) · DOI: [10.1111/j.1365-2966.2006.10160.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2966.2006.10160.x)

## Summary

The paper presents SPLASH (Sky-Projected Laser Array Shack-Hartmann), a laser guide star wavefront-sensing technique in which an array of laser beams is projected onto the sky so that the Shack-Hartmann spot pattern is formed in the atmosphere rather than inside the instrument. A theoretical analysis and numerical simulations show that this configuration suffers considerably less focal anisoplanatism than a conventional single laser guide star. The method is presented as potentially suitable for 8 m class telescopes and possibly extremely large telescopes.
