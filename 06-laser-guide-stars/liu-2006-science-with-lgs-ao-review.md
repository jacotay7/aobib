# Astronomical science with laser guide star adaptive optics: a brief review, a current snapshot, and a bright future

**Liu (2006)** · *Proc. SPIE 6272, 62720H* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0609207) · DOI: [10.1117/12.672759](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.672759)

## Summary

The paper reviews the scientific output of laser guide star adaptive optics up to May 2006, tabulating 23 refereed science papers. It notes that the first decade of LGS AO science (1995-2004) produced about one paper per year while systems were being commissioned, followed by growth to about one per month driven largely by the new Keck II LGS system. An extensive set of Keck LGS performance measurements from the first year of a brown-dwarf near-infrared imaging survey illustrates achievable on-sky performance. The author compares strengths and weaknesses of LGS AO with the Hubble Space Telescope and lists desired improvements.
