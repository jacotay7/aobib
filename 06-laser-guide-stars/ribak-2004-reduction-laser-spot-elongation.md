# Reduction of laser spot elongation in adaptive optics

**Ribak & Ragazzoni (2004)** · *Optics Letters 29, 1351* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/8476174_Reduction_of_laser_spot_elongation_in_adaptive_optics) · DOI: [10.1364/OL.29.001351](https://doi.org/10.1364/OL.29.001351)

## Summary

Addresses the elongation of laser guide stars, which extend over a few kilometres and so appear elongated when seen from the edges of a large telescope, limiting their usefulness. The authors propose controlling the shape and length of the beacon with more elaborate launch optics: several small launch telescopes placed around the rim of the main telescope add their laser beams within the scattering layer to create a compact spot. They note that the method could also be adapted to adaptive optics for the eye.
