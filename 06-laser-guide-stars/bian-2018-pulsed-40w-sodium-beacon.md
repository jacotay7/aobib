# Investigation of return photons from sodium laser beacon excited by a 40-watt facility-class pulsed laser for adaptive optical telescope applications

**Bian et al. (2018)** · *Scientific Reports 8, 9222* · [Free full text](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-018-27576-x) · DOI: [10.1038/s41598-018-27576-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-27576-x)

## Summary

The paper reports a 40 W-class narrow-linewidth microsecond-pulsed 589 nm laser (600 Hz repetition rate, 120 microsecond pulses) and its on-sky test on the 1.8 m telescope at Lijiang Observatory. The gateable pulse format allowed Rayleigh scattering to be eliminated in LGS observations, and the laser was locked to the sodium D2a line with about 0.34 GHz linewidth. Circular polarization and D2b repumping via an electro-optic modulator were used to maximize return. A return flux of 1610 photons/cm2/s, about 47 photons/cm2/s/W of emitted power, was measured.
