# Metasurface-based toroidal lenslet array design for addressing laser guide star elongation

**Munro et al. (2024)** · *JATIS 10, 049002* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.18435) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.10.4.049002](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.10.4.049002)

## Summary

Addresses sodium laser guide star elongation in Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensing for the laser tomography AO system of the Giant Magellan Telescope, where subapertures far from the launch position see increasingly elongated spots and large detectors raise readout noise. The authors propose nano-engineered metasurface lenslets that apply a spatially varying anamorphic image-scale compression across the array. Their meta-lenslet array designs deliver about 100% of the required anamorphic size reduction for focal lengths down to 8 mm and more than 50% for focal lengths down to 2 mm. The approach aims to improve spot sampling across the whole sensor within the limits of current fabrication facilities.
