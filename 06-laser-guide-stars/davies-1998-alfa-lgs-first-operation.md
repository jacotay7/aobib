# ALFA: first operational experience of the MPE/MPIA laser guide star system for adaptive optics

**Davies et al. (1998)** · *Proc. SPIE 3353, 116* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9806351) · DOI: [10.1117/12.321747](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.321747)

## Summary

ALFA is the sodium laser guide star adaptive optics system built by MPE and MPIA at the Calar Alto 3.5 m telescope, whose laser beacon was first detected on the wavefront sensor in 1997. The paper describes the status of the launch beam and planned improvements and upgrades, reports the performance achieved when the LGS is used with the AO system, and relates practical experience from operating the system. First scientific results from ALFA are also mentioned.
