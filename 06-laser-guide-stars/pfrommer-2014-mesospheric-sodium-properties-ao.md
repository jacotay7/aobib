# High resolution mesospheric sodium properties for adaptive optics applications

**Pfrommer & Hickson (2014)** · *A&A 565, A102* · [Free full text](https://www.aanda.org/articles/aa/pdf/2014/05/aa23460-14.pdf) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201423460](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201423460)

## Summary

The performance of laser guide star AO on large telescopes is affected by variability of the sodium layer at 80-120 km altitude, whose density structure changes on timescales down to tens of milliseconds. The paper characterizes the layer's structure and dynamics using a high-resolution lidar on the 6 m Large Zenith Telescope near Vancouver, with 112 nights of useful data collected over three years. It focuses on the parameters most relevant to AO: the mean sodium altitude, the sodium layer width, and the temporal power spectrum of the centroid altitude, and provides statistics of these quantities for use in LGS AO design and simulation.
