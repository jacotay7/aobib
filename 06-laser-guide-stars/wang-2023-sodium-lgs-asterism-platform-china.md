# First sodium laser guide star asterism launching platform in China on the 1.8 m telescope at Gaomeigu Observatory

**Wang et al. (2023)** · *PASP 135, 034502* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.02847) · DOI: [10.1088/1538-3873/acbe68](https://doi.org/10.1088/1538-3873/acbe68)

## Summary

Describes a sodium laser guide star asterism launching platform built and tested on the 1.8 m telescope at Gaomeigu Observatory. Asterisms of several LGS are needed by wide-field systems such as multi-conjugate AO to probe turbulence in several directions. The platform has two functions: comparing the performance of sodium guide stars generated by different lasers at the same site, and generating LGS asterisms with adjustable geometry. Field tests in early 2021 are reported, which the authors state were the first realization of a sodium LGS asterism in China.
