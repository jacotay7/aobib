# 25 W Raman-fiber-amplifier-based 589 nm laser for laser guide star

**Feng et al. (2009)** · *Optics Express 17, 19021* · [Free full text](https://opg.optica.org/oe/fulltext.cfm?uri=oe-17-21-19021) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.17.019021](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.17.019021)

## Summary

The paper reports a 25 W continuous-wave narrow-linewidth (below 2.3 MHz) 589 nm laser source. Two narrow-linewidth Raman fiber amplifiers are coherently combined with a Mach-Zehnder interferometer scheme at over 95% efficiency, and the combined beam is frequency-doubled in an external resonant cavity with 86% efficiency. The authors present narrow-linewidth Raman fiber amplifier technology as a promising route to lasers for sodium laser guide star adaptive optics.
