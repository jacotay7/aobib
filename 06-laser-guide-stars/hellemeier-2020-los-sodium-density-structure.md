# Measuring line-of-sight sodium density structure using laser guide stars

**Hellemeier et al. (2020)** · *MNRAS 494, 2798* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.06844) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/staa808](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/staa808)

## Summary

The paper shows that the vertical sodium density profile along the line of sight can be retrieved during laser guide star operation by amplitude-modulating a continuous-wave laser with a pseudo-random binary sequence and cross-correlating the modulation with the received return signal. The method was tested at the Large Zenith Telescope with ESO's Wendelstein Raman-fibre laser and compared with simultaneous high-resolution lidar profiles and side-view imaging of the laser plume, with agreement within measurement errors. Analysis and simulations indicate that with 50% modulation the sodium centroid altitude could be measured every 5 s from a single LGS, with corresponding wavefront errors of about 50 nm for the ELT and below 30 nm for TMT and GMT.
