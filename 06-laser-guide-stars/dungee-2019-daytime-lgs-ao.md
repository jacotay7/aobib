# On the feasibility of using a laser guide star adaptive optics system in the daytime

**Dungee et al. (2019)** · *JATIS 5, 019002* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.05546) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.5.1.019002](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.5.1.019002)

## Summary

The paper investigates whether ultra-narrow-band interference filters can enable daytime operation of sodium laser guide star AO systems. Filter performance is evaluated using theoretical and vendor transmission profiles, a modeled daylight sky background, broadband daytime sky brightness measurements on Maunakea, and assumed LGS photon return and detector read noise; bandpass, out-of-band rejection and peak throughput are identified as critical parameters. End-to-end simulations of the planned TMT mid-infrared AO system (MIRAO) with five sodium LGSs, off-the-shelf filters, and a K <= 13 tip/tilt/focus natural star show daytime Strehl ratios comparable to night-time values.
