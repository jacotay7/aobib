# No Laser Guide Stars for adaptive optics in giant telescopes?

**Ragazzoni (1999)** · *A&AS 136, 205* · [Free full text](https://aas.aanda.org/articles/aas/pdf/1999/07/ds1656.pdf) · DOI: [10.1051/aas:1999207](https://doi.org/10.1051/aas:1999207)

## Summary

Because of the cone effect, diffraction-limited correction with laser guide stars on very large telescopes requires several artificial beacons, displaced from the science target by angles that scale with telescope diameter. The paper argues that beyond a certain offset angle it can be more efficient to use several off-axis natural guide stars directly instead. It concludes that next-generation giant telescopes could exploit this approach and might not require any laser guide star.
