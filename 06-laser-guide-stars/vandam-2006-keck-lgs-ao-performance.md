# The W. M. Keck Observatory Laser Guide Star Adaptive Optics System: Performance Characterization

**van Dam et al. (2006)** · *PASP 118, 310* · [Free full text](https://www2.keck.hawaii.edu/inst/ao/aodocs/MvDetal2006PASP118_310.pdf) · DOI: [10.1086/499498](https://doi.org/10.1086/499498)

## Summary

This companion paper characterizes the on-sky performance of the Keck II laser guide star adaptive optics system. It presents the control algorithms used, including the sodium-layer focus tracking with the low-bandwidth natural-star wavefront sensor and the tip-tilt control from a natural guide star. Under typical seeing the system delivers K-band Strehl ratios of 30-40% with bright tip-tilt stars, works with tip-tilt stars as faint as R = 18, and gives partial correction about one magnitude fainter. A detailed error budget, including focal anisoplanatism and tip-tilt terms, agrees with measured image quality for both bright and faint tip-tilt stars.
