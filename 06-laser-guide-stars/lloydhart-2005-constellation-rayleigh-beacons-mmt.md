# First Tests of Wavefront Sensing with a Constellation of Laser Guide Beacons

**Lloyd-Hart et al. (2005)** · *ApJ 634, 679* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0508402) · DOI: [10.1086/496960](https://doi.org/10.1086/496960)

## Summary

The paper reports the first wavefront measurements made with a constellation of laser beacons, using five 532 nm pulsed Rayleigh beacons arranged in a 2 arcmin pentagon at the 6.5 m MMT. Returns were range-gated from 20-29 km and recorded at 53 Hz with a 36-element Shack-Hartmann sensor, and beacon wavefronts were compared with simultaneous wavefronts of natural stars in or near the constellation. In 1.0 arcsec seeing, with two-thirds of the aberration from a ground layer near 380 m, subtracting the average of the five beacon wavefronts from the stellar wavefronts reduced the measured distortion by 40% rms over the 2 arcmin field. The authors discuss multiple Rayleigh beacons as an alternative to single sodium beacons on 8 m telescopes and implications for a multi-sodium-beacon system on the GMT.
