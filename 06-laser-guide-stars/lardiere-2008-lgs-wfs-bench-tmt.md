# A laser guide star wavefront sensor bench demonstrator for TMT

**Lardière et al. (2008)** · *Optics Express 16, 5527* · [Free full text](https://opg.optica.org/oe/fulltext.cfm?uri=oe-16-8-5527) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.16.005527](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.16.005527)

## Summary

Sodium laser guide stars are elongated by the thickness of the sodium layer, and temporal and spatial variability of the sodium density induces changing wavefront-measurement errors, especially for extremely large telescopes. In the framework of the Thirty Meter Telescope, the University of Victoria AO Lab built an LGS-simulator test bed to assess new centroiding algorithms for LGS Shack-Hartmann sensors. The paper presents the bench design and laboratory images with 29x29 radially elongated spots simulated for a 30 m pupil, and characterizes errors induced by sodium-layer variations such as focus and spherical aberration. The bench can serve as an LGS simulator for other projects affected by sodium-layer fluctuations.
