# Photon return on-sky test for "by-product" Rayleigh plume of sodium laser guide star

**Huang et al. (2022)** · *Optics Communications 505, 127515* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.08826) · DOI: [10.1016/j.optcom.2021.127515](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.optcom.2021.127515)

## Summary

The Rayleigh backscatter produced at low altitude when a sodium guide-star laser is launched could be used to sense low-altitude turbulence for real-time pre-correction of the laser uplink. This paper evaluates the achievable photon return of this by-product Rayleigh plume through an on-sky test and compares it with a model based on the MSISE-90 atmosphere, finding differences below 20%. For strong (r0 = 5 cm) or moderate (r0 = 10 cm) turbulence, the optimal range-gate centre is found to be 9 km, with gate depths from 3 km to 0.28 km for subaperture sizes from 3 to 10 cm at pulse rates up to 1500 Hz.
