# Measuring the absolute height and profile of the mesospheric sodium layer using a continuous wave laser

**Butler et al. (2003)** · *A&A 403, 775* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0303299) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361:20030379](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361:20030379)

## Summary

The authors develop and test a lidar-like method to measure the absolute height and profile of the mesospheric sodium layer with a continuous-wave guide-star laser that is chopped on and off, so that it can in principle run during laser guide star AO observations. Compared with side-imaging from an auxiliary telescope it needs no second telescope, depends little on atmospheric conditions and avoids triangulation problems. The experiment was carried out with the ALFA LGS system at Calar Alto, and the lidar results were validated against simultaneous auxiliary-telescope imaging. Models of a similar system for the VLT Laser Guide Star Facility predict determination of the initial AO focus to better than 200 m in altitude within about one minute.
