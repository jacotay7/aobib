# Keck II laser guide star AO system and performance with the TOPTICA/MPBC laser

**Chin et al. (2016)** · *Proc. SPIE 9909, 99090S* · [Free full text](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/t0per-cqw30) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2233138](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2233138)

## Summary

In December 2015 the Keck II LGS AO system was upgraded from its first-generation 15 W dye laser to a TOPTICA/MPBC Raman fibre amplification laser, developed through a consortium of ESO, Keck and TMT. The continuous-wave, linearly polarised 589 nm beam with optical back-pumping is designed to improve sodium coupling efficiency, and the laser requires less input power, cooling, space and manpower than earlier systems. The laser is mounted on the telescope elevation ring and projected from behind the secondary mirror using a recently implemented centre launch system to reduce the LGS spot size. The paper reports the laser's power, stability, wavelength, spot size, repumping, polarisation and return as a function of pointing relative to the geomagnetic field, compares on-sky return with the previous dye and solid-state lasers, and gives preliminary LGS AO performance after the return to science operations.
