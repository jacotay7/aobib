# Remote sensing of geomagnetic fields and atomic collisions in the mesosphere

**Pedreros Bustos et al. (2018)** · *Nature Communications 9, 3981* · [Free full text](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-018-06396-7) · DOI: [10.1038/s41467-018-06396-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-06396-7)

## Summary

The paper demonstrates remote measurement of the geomagnetic field at 85-100 km altitude by optically pumping mesospheric sodium with an intensity-modulated laser and detecting from the ground the magneto-optical resonance that occurs when the modulation matches the sodium Larmor precession frequency. The experiment, carried out at the Roque de los Muchachos Observatory on La Palma, measured a Larmor frequency of 260.4 kHz, corresponding to a field of 0.372 G, with a sensitivity of 0.28 mG per root hertz in good conditions. The observations also characterize atomic collision processes in the mesosphere, which are key inputs to models of sodium laser guide star return flux.
