# Improving sodium laser guide star brightness by polarization switching

**Fan et al. (2016)** · *Scientific Reports 6, 19859* · [Free full text](https://www.nature.com/articles/srep19859) · DOI: [10.1038/srep19859](https://doi.org/10.1038/srep19859)

## Summary

Optical pumping with circularly polarized light raises sodium guide-star brightness, but the gain is substantially reduced by Larmor precession of sodium atoms in the geomagnetic field, especially at large angles between the laser beam and the field. The paper proposes switching the laser between left and right circular polarization at the Larmor frequency (magnetically resonant pumping) with existing continuous-wave lasers. Numerical simulations for the ESO laser guide star system at Paranal show increased return flux when the angle between field and beam exceeds 60 degrees, by as much as 50% at 90 degrees, requiring only a minor addition to the delivery optics.
