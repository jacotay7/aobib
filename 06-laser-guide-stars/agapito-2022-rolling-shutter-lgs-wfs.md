# MAORY/MORFEO and rolling shutter induced aberrations in laser guide star wavefront sensing

**Agapito et al. (2022)** · *Proc. SPIE 12185* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.02661) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2629343](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2629343)

## Summary

ELT laser guide star Shack-Hartmann sensors need large-format (about 1 Mpx), low-noise, fast detectors to sample many subapertures and very elongated spots, and CMOS detectors with rolling-shutter readout are one option. The paper analyses the impact of rolling-shutter readout, in which detector rows are exposed at different times, on the LGS wavefront sensing of MORFEO (formerly MAORY) for the ELT. It focuses on aberrations induced by fast-varying signals such as LGS tilt jitter from uplink propagation, finds that the jitter-induced aberration can reach 100 nm rms for MORFEO, and discusses mitigation strategies.
