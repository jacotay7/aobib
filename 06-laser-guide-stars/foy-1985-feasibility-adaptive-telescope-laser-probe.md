# Feasibility of adaptive telescope with laser probe

**Foy & Labeyrie (1985)** · *A&A 152, L29* · [Free full text](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1985A%26A...152L..29F)

## Summary

This letter proposed using pulsed lasers to create artificial reference stars for adaptive optics, so that seeing correction would no longer depend on finding a bright natural star close to the science target. The authors discuss backscattering from atmospheric layers at 15-100 km altitude (Rayleigh/Mie scattering and resonance scattering from mesospheric sodium) with range gating to select the returning light. Photon-count estimates indicate that the backscattered flux is sufficient to drive a fast wavefront correction system. The paper also identifies the mismatch between the conical path of light from the finite-altitude spot and the cylindrical path of starlight (which the letter calls the cone effect) and suggests arrays of several laser spots to enlarge the corrected field. It is commonly cited as the first open-literature proposal of the laser guide star concept for astronomy.
