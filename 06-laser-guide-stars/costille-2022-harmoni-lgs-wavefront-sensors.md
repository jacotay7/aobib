# HARMONI at ELT: designing a laser guide star wavefront sensors for the ELT

**Costille et al. (2022)** · *Proc. SPIE 12185, 121854L* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.08451) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2627644](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2627644)

## Summary

The paper presents the final optical and mechanical design of the Laser Guide Star Sensors (LGSS) of HARMONI's laser tomography AO mode on the ELT. The LGSS comprises six independent Shack-Hartmann wavefront-sensor modules, one per laser guide star, mounted on a 600 mm rotator that stabilizes the pupil on the microlens array. The optical design accepts elongated spots up to 16 arcsec without truncation using a Sony CMOS detector, and uses freeform lenses to minimize the number of components while accommodating varying sodium-layer configurations. The rotator design and the verification strategy without access to the ELT deformable mirror are also discussed.
