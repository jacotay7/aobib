# ESO VLT Laser Guide Star Facility

**Bonaccini et al. (2001)** · *The Messenger 105, 9* · [Free full text](https://www.eso.org/sci/publications/messenger/archive/no.105-sep01/messenger-no105-9-18.pdf)

## Summary

The article reports the design and progress of the ESO Laser Guide Star Facility (LGSF) for VLT Unit Telescope 4, intended to serve the NAOS and SINFONI adaptive optics systems with a single sodium laser guide star and including provisions for multiple LGSs for later MCAO instruments. Top-level requirements include monostatic projection from a 500 mm launch telescope behind the secondary mirror, a continuous-wave sodium laser, and a return flux of at least 1e6 photons/s/m2, implying at least 6 W on air. The design uses the MPE-built PARSEC dye laser in a master-oscillator power-amplifier configuration, single-mode fibre beam transfer to the launch telescope, and a remote LGS monitoring telescope for sodium-profile measurement, and it draws on experience from the ALFA system at Calar Alto. Safety provisions for Class IV lasers and aviation regulations are also discussed.
