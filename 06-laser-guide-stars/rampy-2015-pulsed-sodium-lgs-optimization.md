# Toward optimization of pulsed sodium laser guide stars

**Rampy et al. (2015)** · *JOSA B 32, 2425* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.04982) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAB.32.002425](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAB.32.002425)

## Summary

Pulsed sodium laser guide stars allow Rayleigh blanking and avoidance of fratricide in multi-LGS systems, and Bloch-equation simulations suggest they can reach photon returns close to, or in some cases above, continuous-wave excitation. This paper studies the time-dependent behaviour of sodium fluorescence under pulsed excitation and investigates the optimal pulse format for the fiber laser guide star planned for the upgraded adaptive optics system on the Shane 3 m telescope at Lick Observatory. The general applicability of the results to other LGS systems and the potential benefit of uplink correction are discussed, and simulation predictions are compared with measurements from existing laser guide stars.
