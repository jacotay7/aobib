# The Four-Laser Guide Star Facility (4LGSF) for the ESO VLT Adaptive Optics Facility (AOF)

**Hackenberg et al. (2011)** · *Proc. AO4ELT2 (Victoria, 2011), paper 116* · [Free full text](https://ao4elt2.lesia.obspm.fr/sites/ao4elt2/IMG/pdf/116hackenberg.pdf) · DOI: [10.13009/AO4ELT2/2011.116](https://doi.org/10.13009/AO4ELT2/2011.116)

## Summary

The paper describes the design of the Four-Laser Guide Star Facility, a subsystem of the ESO Adaptive Optics Facility on VLT Unit Telescope 4 that provides four sodium laser guide stars for GALACSI/MUSE and GRAAL/HAWK-I. Its two key design choices are new industrial fibre-laser sources, with reduced volume and maintenance, higher reliability and a spectral format optimized for sodium excitation, and a modular structure in which each LGS unit combines the laser head and its launch telescope and can operate independently. At the time of writing the final design was complete and the project had entered manufacturing and integration. The authors argue that modular units avoid beam relays, allow flexible numbers of LGSs and redundancy, and provide experience for multi-LGS systems on extremely large telescopes.
