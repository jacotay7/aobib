# Tip-tilt retrieval using trilateration of a sodium laser guide star

**Barrett et al. (2026)** · *Optics Express 34, 3512* · [Free full text](https://opg.optica.org/oe/fulltext.cfm?uri=oe-34-3-3512) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.581009](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.581009)

## Summary

Tip, tilt and focus cannot be measured directly from a laser guide star, and for ground-to-satellite optical communications residual tip-tilt in the uplink pre-compensation causes beam wander and signal fades. The paper describes a method to retrieve full tip-tilt using only the LGS, by trilateration: differential arrival times of a pulsed LGS at three or more detectors placed around the laser transmitter locate the guide star and hence the uplink tip-tilt. Simulations show that with 1 ps detector timing resolution and at least 1e8 photons/s per detector, tip and tilt can be measured to about 0.45 arcsec, which for pre-compensation to a low-Earth-orbit satellite increases received power by over 2 dB compared with using downlink tip-tilt.
