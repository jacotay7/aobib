# Frequency chirped continuous-wave sodium laser guide stars: modeling and optimization

**Pedreros Bustos et al. (2020)** · *JOSA B 37, 1208* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.02717) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAB.389007](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAB.389007)

## Summary

Numerically studies a method to increase the photon return of continuous-wave sodium laser guide stars based on one-dimensional atomic cooling principles. The laser frequency is chirped upward to follow the velocity change of sodium atoms caused by photon recoil, increasing the atomic population available for excitation within the Doppler distribution. The benefit grows with the average number of excitations an atom undergoes between collisions in the mesosphere. The authors determine the parameters that maximize return flux and evaluate chirping for conditions at La Palma, finding an optimal chirp rate of 0.8 to 1.0 MHz/µs and a fluorescence increase of up to 60% with current 20 W-class guide star lasers.
