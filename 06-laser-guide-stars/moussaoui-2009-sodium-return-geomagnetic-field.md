# Dependence of sodium laser guide star photon return on the geomagnetic field

**Moussaoui et al. (2009)** · *A&A 501, 793* · [Free full text](https://www.aanda.org/articles/aa/pdf/2009/26/aa11411-08.pdf) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/200811411](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/200811411)

## Summary

The paper studies how the geomagnetic field reduces the backscattered photon return of continuous-wave sodium laser guide stars and estimates the effect at several observatory sites. Calculated populations of the sodium magnetic sublevels are combined with radiance measurements made at the Starfire Optical Range. The authors propose a semi-empirical formula for the geomagnetic dependence of the photon return under circularly polarized cw excitation, and include the effects of sodium-layer distance and atmospheric attenuation to estimate realistic return fluxes for each site.
