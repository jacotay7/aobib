# Performance of a complementary metal-oxide-semiconductor sensor for laser guide star wavefront sensing

**Ke et al. (2022)** · *JATIS 8, 021511* · [Free full text](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/09zqy-n7987) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.8.2.021511](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.8.2.021511)

## Summary

Shack-Hartmann LGS wavefront sensors for ELTs must fully sample strongly elongated spots in outer subapertures without undersampling the compact ones, which drives the need for large, fast, low-noise detectors. The paper characterises a newly released large-format CMOS sensor with 1100 x 1600 pixels of 9 micron pitch in terms of noise and linearity. It then evaluates the sensor's wavefront-sensing performance through spot-centroid measurements and illustrates how the detector could be integrated into complete LGS wavefront sensors for both the ESO ELT and the Thirty Meter Telescope.
