# Image improvement from a sodium-layer laser guide star adaptive optics system

**Max et al. (1997)** · *Science 277, 1649 (LLNL report UCRL-ID-126000)* · [Free full text](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/305943) · DOI: [10.1126/science.277.5332.1649](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.277.5332.1649)

## Summary

This LLNL report is the preprint version of the Science paper reporting astronomical image improvement from a sodium-layer laser guide star with high-order adaptive optics, on the Shane 3 m telescope at Lick Observatory. Compared with image-motion compensation alone, the LGS AO system increased the peak intensity of a point source by a factor of 2.4 and halved its full width at half maximum. Image FWHM was the same (0.3 arcsec) with laser and natural guide stars, and the Strehl ratio with the laser guide star was 65% of that achieved with a natural guide star. The authors present this as evidence that sodium LGS AO can bring ground-based telescopes toward the diffraction limit.
