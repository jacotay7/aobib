# A tomographic algorithm to determine tip-tilt information from laser guide stars

**Reeves et al. (2016)** · *MNRAS 459, 333* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.07819) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stw622](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stw622)

## Summary

Because of uplink turbulence a natural guide star is still needed for tip-tilt in laser guide star AO, preventing full sky coverage. The paper presents a method to recover partial tip-tilt information from the LGSs alone in multi-LGS tomographic systems, deriving the uplink tip-tilt geometrically and modifying the Learn and Apply tomographic reconstructor to accommodate it. Simulations show good correction of tip-tilt originating at high altitude but not of tip-tilt from low altitudes. The authors suggest combining the method with multiple far off-axis tip-tilt natural stars to improve performance and sky coverage.
