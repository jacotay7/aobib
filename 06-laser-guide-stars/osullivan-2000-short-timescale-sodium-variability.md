# Short timescale variability of the mesospheric sodium layer

**O'Sullivan et al. (2000)** · *Experimental Astronomy 10, 147* · [Free full text](https://mural.maynoothuniversity.ie/id/eprint/7102/1/COS-Short-timescale-variability.pdf) · DOI: [10.1023/A:1008126318786](https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1008126318786)

## Summary

The paper investigates short-timescale variations of the mesospheric sodium layer and their implications for laser guide star systems, using resonant backscatter of 589 nm laser light observed at Calar Alto in 1997 and 1998. Short-term variations are dominated by dense sporadic sodium layers, seen unexpectedly on four of five nights at this mid-latitude site; one 2 km wide sporadic layer reached about 2.5 times the background intensity and persisted for over five hours. Centroid-height variations of up to 400 m were observed on timescales of 1-2 minutes, which may require frequent refocusing of the beam and wavefront sensor. No variations of 5% or more in total sodium abundance were found on timescales from 100 ms upward.
