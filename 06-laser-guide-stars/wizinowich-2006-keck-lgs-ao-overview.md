# The W. M. Keck Observatory Laser Guide Star Adaptive Optics System: Overview

**Wizinowich et al. (2006)** · *PASP 118, 297* · [Free full text](https://www2.keck.hawaii.edu/realpublic/optics/aodocs/PASP06_PW.pdf) · DOI: [10.1086/499290](https://doi.org/10.1086/499290)

## Summary

The paper describes the laser guide star adaptive optics system of the Keck II telescope, which began science observations in late 2004 as the first such system on an 8-10 m class telescope. It covers the conceptual approach and technical implementation, including the LLNL-built sodium dye laser and launch, the sodium wavefront sensor, the natural-star tip-tilt sensor and low-bandwidth wavefront sensor, and operational aspects such as laser traffic control and aircraft safety. Lessons learned during commissioning are summarized, and early science capabilities are reviewed, with near-diffraction-limited near-infrared observations possible using tip-tilt stars as faint as 19th magnitude.
