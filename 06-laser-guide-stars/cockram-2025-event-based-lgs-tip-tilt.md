# Event-based detectors for laser guide star tip-tilt sensing

**Cockram & Martinez Rey (2025)** · *Optical Engineering 64, 043102* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.11436) · DOI: [10.1117/1.OE.64.4.043102](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.OE.64.4.043102)

## Summary

Investigates event-based sensors, in which each pixel asynchronously reports changes in brightness rather than recording full frames, as detectors for tip-tilt sensing with laser guide stars. The motivation is that tip-tilt cannot currently be measured from an LGS, and event-based sensors offer high temporal resolution and high dynamic range. The authors characterize the behaviour of an event-based sensor under different tip-tilt conditions and background illumination levels in the laboratory. They conclude that the high temporal resolution of the event streams makes such sensors a strong candidate for LGS tip-tilt sensing.
