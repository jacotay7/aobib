# Estimation of the laser guide star uplink tip-tilt using aperture size diversity

**Jouve et al. (2026)** · *Optics Letters 51, 5261* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12494) · DOI: [10.1364/OL.611519](https://doi.org/10.1364/OL.611519)

## Summary

The letter proposes estimating the uplink tip-tilt of a laser guide star from telemetry acquired at the laser launch telescope alone, operated monostatically as both emitter and receiver. Tip-tilt is extracted over concentric disks of different diameters within the receiving pupil; these signals mix uplink and downlink contributions in different proportions, and a linear minimum-mean-square-error estimator uses this aperture-size diversity to separate the uplink component. Simulations give residual errors of 24 mas for a single turbulent layer and 34 mas with two layers.
