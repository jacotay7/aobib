# Prototype of a laser guide star wavefront sensor for the Extremely Large Telescope

**Patti et al. (2018)** · *MNRAS 477, 539* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.07126) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/sty747](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/sty747)

## Summary

For ELT-class telescopes, sodium laser guide star wavefront sensing is complicated by the finite LGS distance, launch geometry, tip-tilt indetermination and focus anisoplanatism. The paper reports tests of a laboratory prototype LGS Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensor built during the phase-A study of MAORY (now MORFEO), the ELT multi-conjugate AO module. The fidelity of the generated LGS images was cross-checked with the MAORY simulation code, and experiments quantify the effect of noise on centroiding precision, the impact of spot truncation on wavefront-sensing accuracy, and the consequences of temporal evolution of the sodium profile and of spot under-sampling.
