# Rayleigh Laser Guide Star Systems: Application to the University of Illinois Seeing Improvement System

**Thompson & Teare (2002)** · *PASP 114, 1029* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0207074) · DOI: [10.1086/342043](https://doi.org/10.1086/342043)

## Summary

The paper describes a sustained effort to integrate a 351 nm Rayleigh laser guide star, formed at about 20 km altitude, into the UnISIS adaptive optics system on the Mt. Wilson 2.5 m telescope. It discusses why the lambda^-4 dependence of Rayleigh scattering makes short-wavelength lasers attractive for Rayleigh beacons and presents the resulting system design and on-sky return. The authors note that the ultraviolet beam is effectively invisible to aircraft pilots, simplifying FAA-related airspace safety, and argue that short-wavelength Rayleigh guide stars will find further applications.
