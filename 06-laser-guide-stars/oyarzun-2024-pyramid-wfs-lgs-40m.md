# Expected performance of the Pyramid wavefront sensor with a laser guide star for 40 m class telescopes

**Oyarzún et al. (2024)** · *A&A 686, A1* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.10177) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202348691](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202348691)

## Summary

The paper computes the expected photon-noise-limited performance of a pyramid wavefront sensor using a sodium laser guide star on a 40 m telescope, and extends the analysis to a flat two-dimensional reference object. The authors develop a new discretization of the 3D LGS and a faster light-propagation method for Fourier-filtering wavefront sensors with extended objects, and use a sensitivity model with convolutional optical-gain compensation to predict closed-loop performance. They find that the sensitivity loss due to the LGS size puts the system into a low-performance regime for expected return fluxes, with the laser beam width rather than sodium-layer thickness being the limiting factor, and conclude that knife-edge-like sensors such as the PWFS are not recommended for LGS use on 40 m telescopes.
