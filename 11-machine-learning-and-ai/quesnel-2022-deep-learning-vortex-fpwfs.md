# A deep learning approach for focal-plane wavefront sensing using vortex phase diversity

**Quesnel et al. (2022)** · *A&A 668, A36* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.00632) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202143001](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202143001)

## Summary

Combines the phase diversity naturally provided by a vortex coronagraph with deep learning to perform focal-plane wavefront sensing of non-common path aberrations without sacrificing observing time. The EfficientNet-B4 convolutional network is trained to infer phase aberrations from simulated post-coronagraphic images, for a scalar vortex coronagraph (single PSF) and a vector vortex coronagraph (two PSFs from split circular polarizations). The sign ambiguity of even modes is lifted in both cases, even at low signal-to-noise ratio, with performance similar to classical defocus-based phase diversity except at high aberration levels for the scalar case. The approach gives a 100% science duty cycle and needs no extra hardware for the scalar vortex coronagraph.
