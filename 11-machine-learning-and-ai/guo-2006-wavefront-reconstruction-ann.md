# Wavefront reconstruction with artificial neural networks

**Guo et al. (2006)** · *Optics Express 14, 6456* · [Free full text](https://opg.optica.org/oe/fulltext.cfm?uri=oe-14-14-6456) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.14.006456](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.14.006456)

## Summary

Applies artificial neural networks to reconstruct the wavefront from Shack-Hartmann spot patterns. The optimal network structure is first determined, and the networks are then trained on both noise-free and noisy spot patterns. Reconstruction results are compared with those from least-squares fitting and singular value decomposition methods.
