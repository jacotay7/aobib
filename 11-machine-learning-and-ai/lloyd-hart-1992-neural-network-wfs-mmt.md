# First results of an on-line adaptive optics system with atmospheric wavefront sensing by an artificial neural network

**Lloyd-Hart et al. (1992)** · *ApJ 390, L41* · [Free full text](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1992ApJ...390L..41L) · DOI: [10.1086/186367](https://doi.org/10.1086/186367)

## Summary

Reports the first results from an adaptive optics system operating on-line at a telescope with the wavefront aberration sensed by a trained artificial neural network. Star images at 2.2 um formed by two coherently phased apertures of the Multiple Mirror Telescope (MMT) were analyzed by the network, which derived wavefront parameters from focal-plane images in a few milliseconds. The authors report that the system was fast enough for the aberration to be nearly frozen during correction, and that the corrected image showed significant power at the 0.1 arcsec diffraction limit. The paper builds on the neural-network method for deriving wavefront aberrations from focal-plane images developed by Sandler et al. (1991), and is an early example of focal-plane wavefront sensing for segmented or multi-aperture telescopes.
