# Adaptive optics: Neural network wavefront sensing, reconstruction, and prediction

**McGuire et al. (1999)** · *Lecture Notes in Physics 522 (Scientific Applications of Neural Nets), 97* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/226693703_Adaptive_Optics_Neural_Network_Wavefront_Sensing_Reconstruction_and_Prediction) · DOI: [10.1007/BFb0104278](https://doi.org/10.1007/BFb0104278)

## Summary

A book chapter introducing adaptive optics and summarizing early applications of neural networks to it. It first reviews earlier work using multi-layer perceptrons trained with back-propagation to sense and reconstruct the wavefront. It then shows how networks can predict the wavefront ahead in time, comparing non-linear neural-network predictors with linear networks also trained by back-propagation. In the authors' simulations the linear predictors trained faster, gave lower residual phase variance and were much more tolerant to noise, though both improved on no prediction. The chapter closes with comments on how neural networks might evolve as AO becomes routine on large telescopes.
