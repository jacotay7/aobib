# Deep learning wavefront sensing

**Nishizaki et al. (2019)** · *Optics Express 27, 240* · [Free full text](https://opg.optica.org/oe/fulltext.cfm?uri=oe-27-1-240) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.27.000240](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.27.000240)

## Summary

Proposes extending the design space of wavefront sensors with machine learning, simplifying both the optical hardware and the image processing. A convolutional neural network is trained to estimate Zernike coefficients of an aberrated wavefront directly from a single intensity image. Several image-based sensing architectures are demonstrated experimentally, and the network is shown to estimate aberrations for both point sources and extended sources.
