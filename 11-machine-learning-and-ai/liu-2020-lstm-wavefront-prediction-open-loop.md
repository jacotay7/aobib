# Wavefront prediction using artificial neural networks for open-loop adaptive optics

**Liu et al. (2020)** · *MNRAS 496, 456* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.14078) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/staa1558](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/staa1558)

## Summary

Presents a nonlinear wavefront predictor based on a Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) neural network that requires no prior knowledge of the atmosphere or user input, as an alternative to parametric linear predictors that need wind-speed identification. The network predicts open-loop Shack-Hartmann slopes one frame ahead in a simulated 7x7 single-conjugate AO system at 150 Hz. The authors study the effect of the training regime, guide-star magnitude and varying wind speed and direction, and extend the approach to a two-frame latency. With the predictor, residual errors are 19.9 to 40.0 nm rms relative to a latency-free system, compared with a bandwidth error of 78.3 nm rms without prediction.
