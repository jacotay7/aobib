# Wavefront Prediction Using Artificial Neural Networks for Adaptive Optics

**Liu (2021)** · *PhD thesis, Durham University* · [Free full text](https://etheses.durham.ac.uk/id/eprint/14076/)

## Summary

Doctoral thesis on nonlinear open-loop wavefront prediction with artificial neural networks built mainly from Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) cells. The predictor is evaluated on simulated data and on on-sky 7x7 Shack-Hartmann data recorded at 150 Hz with the CANARY demonstrator on the William Herschel Telescope. The thesis reports that the network both predicts the wavefront and filters high temporal frequencies such as WFS noise, adapts to time-variant turbulence on sub-second timescales without user tuning, and can predict non-frozen-flow components such as dome seeing. It also shows that prediction can be performed on a per-subaperture basis and discusses implications for on-sky implementation.
