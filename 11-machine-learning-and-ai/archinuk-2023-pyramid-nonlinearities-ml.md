# Mitigating the nonlinearities in a pyramid wavefront sensor

**Archinuk et al. (2023)** · *JATIS 9, 049005* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.09805) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.9.4.049005](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.9.4.049005)

## Summary

Simulates wavefronts passing through a pyramid wavefront sensor under varying observing conditions to investigate whether non-linear machine-learning models can estimate wavefront errors better than a linear reconstructor. The work is motivated by natural-guide-star AO systems, where the pyramid sensor offers higher sensitivity than a Shack-Hartmann but a reduced linear range. The authors report significant improvement even with light-weight models.
