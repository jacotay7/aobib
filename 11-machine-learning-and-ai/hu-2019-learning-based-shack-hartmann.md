# Learning-based Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensor for high-order aberration detection

**Hu et al. (2019)** · *Optics Express 27, 33504* · [Free full text](https://opg.optica.org/oe/fulltext.cfm?uri=oe-27-23-33504) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.27.033504](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.27.033504)

## Summary

Presents a learning-based Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensor that estimates high-order aberrations directly from the spot pattern without image segmentation or centroid computation. Training data are generated from Zernike amplitudes measured on biological samples, and the network predicts up to 120 Zernike modes from a single pattern in about 11 ms on a personal computer. Compared with conventional modal Shack-Hartmann reconstruction, the authors report about 40% lower rms phase residual and about 27% higher Strehl ratio. The method is also validated on a 300 um thick mouse brain slice.
