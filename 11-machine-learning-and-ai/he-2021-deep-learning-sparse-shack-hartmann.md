# Deep learning wavefront sensing method for Shack-Hartmann sensors with sparse sub-apertures

**He et al. (2021)** · *Optics Express 29, 17669* · [Free full text](https://opg.optica.org/oe/fulltext.cfm?uri=oe-29-11-17669) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.427261](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.427261)

## Summary

Proposes a deep-learning method that predicts the wavefront directly from Shack-Hartmann sub-aperture images without centroid calculation. The approach reconstructs high-spatial-frequency wavefronts with fewer sub-apertures than usual, going beyond the conventional d/r0 of about 1 sampling requirement for atmospheric turbulence measurement. Transfer learning is used to reduce training time by 98.4% compared with earlier deep-learning approaches. In numerical simulations the mean residual wavefront error is 0.08 wavelengths rms.
