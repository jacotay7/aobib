# Adaptive optics based on machine learning: a review

**Guo et al. (2022)** · *Opto-Electronic Advances 5, 200082* · [Free full text](https://www.oejournal.org/oea/article/doi/10.29026/oea.2022.200082) · DOI: [10.29026/oea.2022.200082](https://doi.org/10.29026/oea.2022.200082)

## Summary

Open-access review of machine-learning methods across adaptive optics. The authors summarize recent work applying machine learning to nearly all parts of the AO chain, including wavefront sensing (with and without a dedicated sensor), wavefront reconstruction and prediction, control, and image post-processing. They review the reported performance of these 'intelligent adaptive optics' approaches and discuss their potential advantages and current deficiencies.
