# Integrating supervised and reinforcement learning for predictive control with an unmodulated pyramid wavefront sensor for adaptive optics

**Pou et al. (2024)** · *Optics Express 32, 37011* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.13610) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.530254](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.530254)

## Summary

Proposes a control approach that combines offline supervised learning, for non-linear phase reconstruction from an unmodulated pyramid wavefront sensor, with online reinforcement learning for predictive control. The high-order pyramid sensor drives both a tip-tilt stage and a high-order deformable mirror. In simulation the method outperforms conventional control, with the largest gains for faint stars and poor seeing, and is robust to changes in atmospheric conditions.
