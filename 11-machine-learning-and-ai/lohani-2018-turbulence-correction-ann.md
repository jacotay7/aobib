# Turbulence correction with artificial neural networks

**Lohani & Glasser (2018)** · *Optics Letters 43, 2611* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.07456) · DOI: [10.1364/OL.43.002611](https://doi.org/10.1364/OL.43.002611)

## Summary

Designs, in simulation, an optical feedback network based on machine learning that corrects the effects of turbulent propagation on optical modes for free-space links. The artificial neural network relies only on measuring the intensity profile of the distorted modes, and generates mode profiles at the transmitter that, after propagation through turbulence, closely match the desired target modes. The corrected mode profiles at the receiver are reported to be nearly identical to the targets, with near-zero mean-square error.
