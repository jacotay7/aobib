# Wavefront sensor-less adaptive optics using deep reinforcement learning

**Durech et al. (2021)** · *Biomedical Optics Express 12, 5423* · [Free full text](https://opg.optica.org/boe/fulltext.cfm?uri=boe-12-9-5423) · DOI: [10.1364/BOE.427970](https://doi.org/10.1364/BOE.427970)

## Summary

Presents a deep reinforcement learning approach to wavefront-sensorless AO, in which image information is used directly to guide the deformable-mirror shape instead of a Shack-Hartmann measurement. The method is implemented on a custom fluorescence confocal scanning laser microscope. Experimental results show improved performance of the reinforcement-learning approach relative to a Zernike-mode hill-climbing sensorless algorithm.
