# Short-term Turbulence Prediction for Seeing Using Machine Learning

**Medlej et al. (2026)** · *PASP 138, 074507* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24466) · DOI: [10.1088/1538-3873/ae862c](https://doi.org/10.1088/1538-3873/ae862c)

## Summary

Addresses short-term forecasting of the seeing parameter up to two hours ahead, motivated by the reactive nature of AO and by free-space optical communications. Statistical and deep-learning approaches are compared, including Gaussian processes, recurrent neural networks and LSTMs, and a normalizing-flow model for time series (FLOTS) introduced by the authors. All models are trained only on historical seeing data, with emphasis on probabilistic models that quantify predictive uncertainty. The authors report that FLOTS gives the best balance between accuracy and calibrated uncertainty.
