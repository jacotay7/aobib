# Making the unmodulated pyramid wavefront sensor smart. Closed-loop demonstration of neural network wavefront reconstruction with MagAO-X

**Landman et al. (2024)** · *A&A 684, A114* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.16325) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202348898](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202348898)

## Summary

Presents closed-loop laboratory results of a convolutional-neural-network nonlinear reconstructor for the unmodulated pyramid wavefront sensor of MagAO-X. Unmodulated operation preserves sensitivity, including to low-order and petal-piston modes, but has a very limited linear range. The CNN reconstructor has a dynamic range above 600 nm rms compared with 50 nm rms for the linear reconstructor, and reaches more than 80% Strehl at 875 nm under a variety of conditions while achieving the theoretical sensitivity limit of the sensor. The network inference takes 690 microseconds, allowing loop rates above 1 kHz.
