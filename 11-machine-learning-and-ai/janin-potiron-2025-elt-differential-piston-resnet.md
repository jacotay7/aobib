# Estimating differential pistons for the Extremely Large Telescope using focal plane imaging and a residual network

**Janin-Potiron et al. (2025)** · *A&A 704, A246* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.14577) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202556704](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202556704)

## Summary

Proposes, in numerical simulation, a deep-learning method to estimate the differential piston between the petals of the ELT M4 mirror from images of a 2x2 Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensor, as used in the ELT tomographic AO modes. A ResNet-based neural network is trained on simulated datasets, and its robustness is assessed against Strehl ratio, polychromaticity and detector noise using the rms error of the estimated piston. Temporal averaging of frames lets the differential-piston signal emerge from the turbulent speckle field and significantly reduces the error; better seeing improves accuracy, and polychromaticity degrades performance by less than 5%. The authors find that the main limitation is the need for sufficient speckle averaging rather than detector noise, and that the network can also be applied to other input images.
