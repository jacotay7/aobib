# 11 · Machine Learning & AI for AO

Machine learning entered adaptive optics early: at the start of the 1990s, neural networks trained on focal-plane images were used to sense the wavefront of the Multiple Mirror Telescope, and neural networks were tried for Shack-Hartmann processing and temporal prediction. After a long gap marked mainly by the CARMEN neural-network tomographic reconstructor tested on CANARY, the field grew quickly from about 2018 with deep learning. Current work covers convolutional and transformer networks that invert nonlinear wavefront sensors (in particular the unmodulated pyramid, now demonstrated on sky with MagAO-X), image-based and focal-plane wavefront sensing (NCPA, low-order sensing behind coronagraphs, differential piston), learned temporal prediction (LSTM/CNN predictors), reinforcement-learning controllers (from simulation to the first on-sky demonstration), neural-network-assisted PSF modelling and image restoration, and machine-learning forecasts of seeing and turbulence.

This folder collects papers where the learning method is central. Linear data-driven predictive controllers (empirical orthogonal functions, subspace predictive control, LQG identification) are generally filed under [04 · Control & Reconstruction](../04-control-and-reconstruction/README.md), and physics-based focal-plane sensing under [02](../02-wavefront-sensing/README.md) and [07](../07-high-contrast-and-extreme-ao/README.md). Entries from other folders that use machine learning are cross-listed in the index below.

## Subtopics

- **Reviews & theses** — surveys of ML for AO and doctoral theses on learned prediction and control.
- **Image-based & focal-plane wavefront sensing** — neural networks that estimate the wavefront from PSFs or focal-plane images (the 1992 MMT experiment, phase diversity, vortex-coronagraph diversity, low-order sensing, ELT petal piston).
- **NN wavefront reconstruction & tomography** — neural Shack-Hartmann reconstruction and the CARMEN open-loop tomographic reconstructor.
- **Nonlinear pyramid & Fourier-filtering WFS reconstruction** — CNN/transformer reconstructors and learned optics for the pyramid sensor, especially without modulation.
- **Reinforcement learning control** — model-free and model-based RL controllers (PO4AO, multi-agent RL), from simulation to laboratory and on sky.
- **Learned predictive control** — LSTM and CNN wavefront predictors, and telemetry-based comparisons with linear forecasting.
- **PSF estimation & image restoration** — learned phase/PSF estimation from telemetry and deep-learning multiframe deconvolution.
- **Turbulence forecasting** — ML and statistical-learning forecasts of seeing and Cn2 for observatories and optical links.
- **Microscopy, vision & communications** — ML-driven sensorless AO in microscopy and ML for free-space optical links.
