# Wavefront reconstruction and prediction with convolutional neural networks

**Swanson et al. (2018)** · *Proc. SPIE 10703 (Adaptive Optics Systems VI), 107031F* · [Free full text](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~kyros/pubs/18.spie.adaptive.pdf) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2312590](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2312590)

## Summary

An early study applying deep convolutional neural networks to adaptive optics, covering both wavefront reconstruction from wavefront-sensor data and prediction of future wavefronts. The networks are trained and tested in simulation. The authors report reconstruction accuracy comparable to state-of-the-art methods of the time, and prediction of wavefronts up to five simulation steps ahead with under 1 nm rms wavefront error. They present the work as first steps toward using deep learning in AO.
