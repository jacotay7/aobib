# Availability assessment of AO corrected optical links by machine learning

**Klotz (2023)** · *PhD thesis (HAL tel-04601901)* · [Free full text](https://theses.hal.science/tel-04601901)

## Summary

Doctoral thesis on predicting the availability of AO-corrected satellite-to-ground free-space optical links using simple turbulence characterization instruments combined with machine learning. Motivated by the need for networks of ground stations that can be reconfigured according to turbulence and weather forecasts, it proposes a methodology to predict the system power margin, without an established link, from four easily measured integrated turbulence parameters. The work emphasizes combining machine learning with physical understanding of turbulence and optical propagation, and discusses its limitations and the need for field measurements.
