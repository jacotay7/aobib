# Model-free reinforcement learning with a non-linear reconstructor for closed-loop adaptive optics control with a pyramid wavefront sensor

**Pou et al. (2022)** · *Proc. SPIE 12185, 121852U* · [Free full text](https://upcommons.upc.edu/handle/2117/385370) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2627849](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2627849)

## Summary

The paper combines a model-free reinforcement-learning (RL) predictive controller with a supervised non-linear wavefront reconstructor for AO with a pyramid wavefront sensor. The authors first analyse the additional difficulties of training an RL controller with a pyramid sensor compared with a Shack-Hartmann sensor. They then propose using a U-Net neural network as the non-linear reconstructor, with model-free RL providing the temporal prediction. In closed-loop simulations of an 8 m telescope with a 32x32 pyramid sensor, both the prediction and the non-linear reconstruction improve performance over an optimised integrator.
