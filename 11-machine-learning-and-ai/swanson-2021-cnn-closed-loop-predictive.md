# Closed loop predictive control of adaptive optics systems with convolutional neural networks

**Swanson et al. (2021)** · *MNRAS 503, 2944* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.06327) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stab632](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stab632)

## Summary

Describes a method for training deep neural networks for predictive AO control based on an adversarial prior, and demonstrates network performance in simulated closed loop rather than on pre-generated or open-loop data. The models reduce servo-lag effects and extend reliable natural-guide-star control to fainter stars, improving K-band Strehl by more than 55% over classical methods for 16th-magnitude guide stars on an 8 m telescope. The authors find that LSTM-based networks may be better suited to high-contrast cases dominated by servo lag, whereas feed-forward models are better in high-noise cases, and discuss real-time implementation.
