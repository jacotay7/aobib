# Open-loop tomography with artificial neural networks on CANARY: on-sky results

**Osborn et al. (2014)** · *MNRAS 441, 2508* · [Free full text](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/441/3/2508/1112640) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stu758](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stu758)

## Summary

Presents the first on-sky tests of the CARMEN artificial-neural-network tomographic reconstructor on the CANARY MOAO demonstrator at the William Herschel Telescope. The paper describes training of the network and its implementation as a module of the DARC real-time control system. On sky, the fully optimized Learn & Apply tomographic reconstructor outperformed CARMEN by about 5 per cent in Strehl ratio (about 15 nm rms wavefront error), which the authors attribute to limitations of the training data. Bench tests indicated that the network can outperform Learn & Apply under some conditions, for example when a high-altitude layer shifts in altitude by about 300 m.
