# Deep optics preconditioner for modulation-free pyramid wavefront sensing

**Guzmán et al. (2024)** · *Photonics Research 12, 301* · [Free full text](https://preprints.opticaopen.org/articles/preprint/Deep_Optics_Preconditioner_for_Modulation-free_Pyramid_Wavefront_Sensing/23812041) · DOI: [10.1364/PRJ.502245](https://doi.org/10.1364/PRJ.502245)

## Summary

Proposes training a passive diffractive optical layer, placed at the Fourier plane at the tip of the pyramid, to extend the linear range of the pyramid wavefront sensor without beam modulation. The single-layer optical preconditioner is optimized with an end-to-end simulation, assuming a conventional least-squares modal reconstructor. Simulations over a wide range of turbulence conditions show an improvement in aberration estimation equivalent to more than 3 lambda/D of modulation. Experimentally, the resulting 'Deep PWFS' matches a conventional pyramid sensor with 2 lambda/D modulation. The linked copy is the 2023 Optica Open preprint, whose author list and order differ from the published paper.
