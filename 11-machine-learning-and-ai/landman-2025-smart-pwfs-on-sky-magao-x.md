# Making the unmodulated pyramid wavefront sensor smart II. First on-sky demonstration of extreme adaptive optics with deep learning

**Landman et al. (2025)** · *A&A 696, L1* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.16690) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202553753](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202553753)

## Summary

Reports the first on-sky demonstration of extreme AO with an unmodulated pyramid wavefront sensor driven by a convolutional-neural-network nonlinear reconstructor, on MagAO-X at the Magellan telescope. The real-time implementation uses the TensorRT framework and runs the control loop at more than 2 kHz. The model, calibrated with internal-source data, gave stable closed-loop correction under varying conditions. On bright stars in favourable conditions it achieved nearly the same Strehl ratio as the optimized modulated sensor, and it performed better on a fainter star under strong winds.
