# Machine learning guided rapid focusing with sensor-less aberration corrections

**Jin et al. (2018)** · *Optics Express 26, 30162* · [Free full text](https://opg.optica.org/oe/fulltext.cfm?uri=oe-26-23-30162) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.26.030162](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.26.030162)

## Summary

Demonstrates rapid focusing in scattering tissue with sensorless aberration correction guided by machine learning. A convolutional neural network is trained to compute low-order Zernike aberrations directly from point spread function images, reaching about 90 percent correction accuracy with an average mean-square error of 0.06 per Zernike coefficient over 200 repetitions. Aberrations from 1 mm thick phantoms and 300 um thick mouse brain slices are compensated by loading the estimated phase on an adaptive element at the back pupil plane, with phase reconstruction taking less than 0.2 s.
