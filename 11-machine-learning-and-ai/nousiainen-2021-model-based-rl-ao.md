# Adaptive optics control using model-based reinforcement learning

**Nousiainen et al. (2021)** · *Optics Express 29, 15327* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.13685) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.420270](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.420270)

## Summary

Formulates AO closed-loop control as a model-based reinforcement learning problem and applies it in numerical simulation to a Shack-Hartmann AO system with 24 resolution elements across the aperture. The simulations show that the learned controller predicts the temporal evolution of turbulence and adapts to misregistration between the deformable mirror and the wavefront sensor. The method learns continuously on timescales of a few seconds and can therefore adjust automatically to changing conditions.
