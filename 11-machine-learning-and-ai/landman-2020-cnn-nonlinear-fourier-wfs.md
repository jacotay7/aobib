# Nonlinear wavefront reconstruction with convolutional neural networks for Fourier-based wavefront sensors

**Landman & Haffert (2020)** · *Optics Express 28, 16644* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.10560) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.389465](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.389465)

## Summary

Proposes convolutional neural networks for nonlinear reconstruction of Fourier-based wavefront sensors such as the pyramid wavefront sensor, whose intrinsic nonlinearities limit the validity range of linear reconstructors. The CNN is shown to reconstruct the nonlinear response accurately in simulation and in a laboratory implementation. Using the CNN alone as the reconstructor gave suboptimal closed-loop performance under simulated turbulence, whereas using it to estimate the nonlinear error term on top of a linear model increased the effective dynamic range. The larger dynamic range yielded higher Strehl ratios in conditions where the nonlinear error matters.
