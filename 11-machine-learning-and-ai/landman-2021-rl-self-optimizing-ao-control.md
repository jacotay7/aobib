# Self-optimizing adaptive optics control with reinforcement learning for high-contrast imaging

**Landman et al. (2021)** · *JATIS 7, 039002* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.11332) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.7.3.039002](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.7.3.039002)

## Summary

Describes how model-free reinforcement learning can optimize a recurrent-neural-network controller for closed-loop predictive AO control, targeting temporal error and vibrations in extreme AO. The approach is first verified for tip-tilt control in simulation and in the laboratory, where it mitigates vibrations and reduces residuals for power-law turbulence compared with an optimal-gain integrator, including random vibrations without online updating of the control law. Simulations then apply it to a high-order deformable mirror, showing two orders of magnitude contrast improvement at small separations under stationary turbulence and more than an order of magnitude for different wind speeds and directions. An earlier conference version appeared in Proc. SPIE 11448 (arXiv:2012.01997).
