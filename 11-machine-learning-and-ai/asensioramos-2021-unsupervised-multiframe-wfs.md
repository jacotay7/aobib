# Learning to do multiframe wavefront sensing unsupervised: Applications to blind deconvolution

**Asensio Ramos & Olspert (2021)** · *A&A 646, A100* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.01438) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202038552](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202038552)

## Summary

Proposes an unsupervised training scheme for deep-learning multiframe blind deconvolution, so that networks can be trained directly on observations instead of on outputs of classical deconvolution or on magneto-convection simulations. A physically motivated loss function derived from linear image formation and a probabilistic treatment of blind deconvolution allows end-to-end training of a model composed of three neural networks. The method is applied to stellar data from the FastCam instrument and to extended solar data from the Swedish Solar Telescope. The trained model estimates the instantaneous wavefronts, from which corrected images can be obtained with standard deconvolution, and is about three orders of magnitude faster than optimization-based deconvolution.
