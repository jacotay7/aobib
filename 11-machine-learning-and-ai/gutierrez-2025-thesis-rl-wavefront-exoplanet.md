# Reinforcement learning for wavefront command in exoplanet imaging (Apprentissage par renforcement pour la correction de surface d'onde en imagerie d'exoplanetes)

**Gutierrez (2025)** · *PhD thesis (HAL tel-05583288)* · [Free full text](https://theses.hal.science/tel-05583288) · DOI: [10.70675/12bc8d88z307az40a0z8b00za8d9df0661ac](https://doi.org/10.70675/12bc8d88z307az40a0z8b00za8d9df0661ac)

## Summary

Doctoral thesis (in French, with English abstract) developing a fully data-driven method for aberration correction from focal-plane images using deep reinforcement learning with convolutional neural networks. It reviews direct imaging, coronagraphic wavefront sensing and control, and earlier combinations of reinforcement learning with aberration correction. In a simulated space-based active-optics setting, an agent controls a pupil-plane deformable mirror from phase-diversity images to maximize a Strehl-based reward, reaching nanometric precision with robustness to noise. The method is then extended to coronagraphic imaging with single-actuator probe images and a dark-hole contrast reward, and is validated on simplified simulations of a high-contrast testbed, where it created dark holes.
