# Deep residual learning for low-order wavefront sensing in high-contrast imaging systems

**Allan et al. (2020)** · *Optics Express 28, 26267* · [Free full text](https://opg.optica.org/oe/fulltext.cfm?uri=oe-28-18-26267) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.397790](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.397790)

## Summary

Proposes deep neural networks with residual learning for non-linear low-order wavefront sensing behind a coronagraph, using the rejected starlight of a Lyot-based low-order wavefront sensor (LLOWFS). Conventional LLOWFS recovery fits Zernike coefficients with linear least squares, which limits dynamic range. The authors report that the residual-learning approach extends the usable range of the sensor by more than an order of magnitude and can improve closed-loop control for systems with large initial wavefront error. Performance is shown to hold in the low-photon regimes typical of coronagraphic exoplanet imaging.
