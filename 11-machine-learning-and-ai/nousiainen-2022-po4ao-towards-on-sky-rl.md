# Toward on-sky adaptive optics control using reinforcement learning. Model-based policy optimization for adaptive optics

**Nousiainen et al. (2022)** · *A&A 664, A71* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.07554) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202243311](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202243311)

## Summary

Introduces Policy Optimization for AO (PO4AO), a model-based reinforcement learning method that learns a dynamics model and optimizes a neural-network control policy, aimed at the temporal delay and misregistration limitations of matrix-based integrator control in extreme AO. The method is studied in numerical simulations of pyramid-sensor extreme AO for 8-m and 40-m telescopes and implemented in the laboratory on MagAO-X at the Steward Observatory. PO4AO improves coronagraphic contrast by factors of 3 to 5 within the DM control region in simulation and in the laboratory. Training takes typically 5 to 10 seconds and inference is below a millisecond, which the authors state is compatible with real-time control for extremely large telescopes.
