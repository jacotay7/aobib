# AO4ELT 3: Third Conference on Adaptive Optics for Extremely Large Telescopes (Florence, 2013) - proceedings

**Esposito & Fini (2013)** · *INAF-Osservatorio Astrofisico di Arcetri, ISBN 978-88-908876-0-4* · [Free full text](http://ao4elt3.arcetri.inaf.it/proceedings/)

## Summary

Proceedings of the third AO4ELT conference, held in Florence, Italy, on 26-31 May 2013 and organised by the Arcetri Astrophysical Observatory with LESIA and ONERA (editors S. Esposito and L. Fini). Papers are indexed by session and by author, and each has a DOI of the form 10.12839/AO4ELT3.xxxxx that resolves to a free PDF. Note that the DOIs point to the old arcetri.astro.it host; the proceedings are currently served from the arcetri.inaf.it address used here.
