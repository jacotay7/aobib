# AO4ELT 2: 2nd Conference on Adaptive Optics for Extremely Large Telescopes (Victoria, 2011) - online proceedings

**AO4ELT2 organizing committees (2011)** · *Online proceedings hosted by LESIA/ONERA* · [Free full text](https://ao4elt2.lesia.obspm.fr/spip.php%3Farticle701.html)

## Summary

Online proceedings of the second AO4ELT conference, held in Victoria (BC, Canada) on 25-30 September 2011 and co-organised by Observatoire de Paris, ONERA, NRC Herzberg and the University of Victoria. The conference had 179 registered participants and 134 submissions. The list-of-papers page links to individual PDF papers grouped by session: astronomy with AO, AO instruments and pathfinders (including invited reviews of Gemini MCAO, LBT AO, PALM-3000, GMT, TMT, E-ELT and CANARY), wavefront correctors, wavefront sensing, laser guide star systems, turbulence and other disturbances, real-time control, and modelling and post-processing.
