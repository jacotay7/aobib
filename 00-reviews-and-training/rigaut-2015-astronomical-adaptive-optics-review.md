# Astronomical Adaptive Optics

**Rigaut (2015)** · *PASP 127, 1197* · [Free full text](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1086/684512) · DOI: [10.1086/684512](https://doi.org/10.1086/684512)

## Summary

A short review of nighttime astronomical adaptive optics written for astronomers who are not AO specialists, published in the PASP review series for the International Year of Light (2015). It recalls the history of the technique, explains the principle of operation, and describes the main components: wavefront sensors, deformable mirrors, real-time control and guide stars, including laser guide stars. It then discusses the performance achieved on current telescopes, the wide-field variants of AO, and the role AO is planned to play in the extremely large telescopes.
