## Summary

Proceedings of the fourth AO4ELT conference (Lake Arrowhead, California, October 2015), published open access on the University of California's eScholarship platform as "Adaptive Optics for Extremely Large Telescopes 4 - Conference Proceedings". Each paper has a landing page with a free PDF and a DOI with the prefix 10.20353/K3T4CP1131. Topics span ELT AO systems, wavefront sensing, tomography, laser guide stars, PSF reconstruction, control and simulation.
