# Optique adaptative et télescopes géants (Adaptive optics and extremely large telescopes)

**Fusco (2023)** · *Comptes Rendus Mécanique 351(S4), 81-89* · [Free full text](https://comptes-rendus.academie-sciences.fr/mecanique/articles/10.5802/crmeca.218/) · DOI: [10.5802/crmeca.218](https://doi.org/10.5802/crmeca.218)

## Summary

This short review (in French, with an English abstract) presents the state of the art of adaptive optics for astronomical applications. It describes how AO, now installed on all large ground-based telescopes, has changed ground-based observation over the previous twenty years by overcoming a series of scientific and technical challenges. It then outlines how the concept will be applied around 2030 on the future giant telescopes to address questions such as the early universe and the search for life outside the solar system.
