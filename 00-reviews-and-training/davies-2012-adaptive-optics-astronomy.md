# Adaptive Optics for Astronomy

**Davies & Kasper (2012)** · *ARA&A 50, 305-351* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.5741) · DOI: [10.1146/annurev-astro-081811-125447](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-astro-081811-125447)

## Summary

This Annual Review considers adaptive optics from the astrophysical perspective. It summarises how AO has become part of the standard instrumentation suite at major observatories, allowing ground-based telescopes to reach the diffraction limit and spatial resolution beyond that of current space telescopes. The authors review the advances AO has enabled across a range of astrophysical topics and describe how science requirements are driving the development of the next generation of AO techniques, such as wide-field, laser-assisted and high-contrast systems. It is, for example, the first assigned reading of C. Max's UC Santa Cruz AO course (ASTR 289).
