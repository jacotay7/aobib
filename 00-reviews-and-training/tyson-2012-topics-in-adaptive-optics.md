# Topics in Adaptive Optics

**Tyson (2012)** · *InTech (open-access edited book), ISBN 978-953-307-949-3* · [Free full text](https://www.intechopen.com/books/574) · DOI: [10.5772/1086](https://doi.org/10.5772/1086)

## Summary

This open-access edited volume (editor R. K. Tyson) collects eleven chapters on diverse adaptive optics topics. Chapters cover optical turbulence profiles in the atmosphere, turbulence characterisation by moire deflectometry, direct imaging of extrasolar planets, AO-based image post-processing, closed-loop AO for high-peak-power laser facilities, the human eye and AO, an AO confocal scanning laser ophthalmoscope, improving the efficiency and measurement error of Shack-Hartmann sensors, FPGA acceleration of wavefront phase recovery, and membrane deformable mirrors. Each chapter can be downloaded individually.
