# L'optique adaptative du Télescope Canada France Hawaii et son utilisation pour l'étude des coeurs de galaxies à flambée d'étoiles (The CFHT adaptive optics system and its use for studying starburst galaxy nuclei)

**Lai (1996)** · *PhD thesis, Université Paris VII* · [Free full text](https://theses.hal.science/tel-00003538)

## Summary

This PhD thesis (in French, with an English abstract), supervised by Daniel Rouan, covers both building and using an early facility AO system. Its first part describes the integration and laboratory and on-sky characterisation of the curvature-sensing AO system of the Canada-France-Hawaii Telescope (PUEO), including the integration methodology and performance over a range of observing conditions, which agreed with predictions. The second part applies AO in the near infrared to the nuclei of starburst galaxies, presenting results for eleven galaxies obtained with the two AO instruments then open to the community. It is an example of a thesis that combines an introduction to AO with an instrument-builder's and an observer's perspective.
