# AO4ELT 7: Adaptive Optics for Extremely Large Telescopes 7th Edition (Avignon, 2023) - proceedings

**AO4ELT7 organizing committees (2023)** · *Proc. AO4ELT7 (Avignon, 2023)* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/AO4ELT7)

## Summary

Proceedings of the seventh AO4ELT conference, held at the Palais des Papes in Avignon, France, in June 2023. The papers are deposited as open-access documents in a dedicated AO4ELT7 collection of the HAL open archive (about 130 records), and each has a DOI of the form 10.13009/AO4ELT7-2023-xxx that resolves to its HAL page. Topics include ELT and GMT AO systems, wavefront sensing (including laser guide star sensors), turbulence characterisation, simulation tools such as OOPAO, and control.
