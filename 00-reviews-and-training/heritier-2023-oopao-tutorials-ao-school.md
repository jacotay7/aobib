# OOPAO tutorials and AO school notebooks

**Héritier et al. (2023)** · *OOPAO repository (GitHub, cheritier/OOPAO)* · [Free full text](https://github.com/cheritier/OOPAO/tree/master/tutorials)

## Summary

The tutorials folder of the OOPAO (Object-Oriented Python Adaptive Optics) end-to-end simulator contains scripts and notebooks for learning AO simulation. They include closed-loop examples with Shack-Hartmann, pyramid and bi-O edge wavefront sensors, two-stage AO, AO transfer functions, image formation, and how-to scripts for detectors, scintillation, spatial filtering, multiple sources, asterisms and tomography, NCPA, LIFT and SPRINT. An OAO_School sub-folder provides introductory, intermediate and advanced notebooks with questions used in an observing-with-AO school built around the PAPYRUS bench at the Observatoire de Haute-Provence. The simulator itself is described in its own entry in the simulation topic.
