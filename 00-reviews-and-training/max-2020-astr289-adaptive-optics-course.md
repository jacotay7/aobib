# ASTR 289: Adaptive Optics and its Applications (UC Santa Cruz course materials)

**Max (2020)** · *UC Santa Cruz graduate course, Winter 2020* · [Free full text](https://www.ucolick.org/~max/289/)

## Summary

Course page of Claire Max's graduate course on adaptive optics at UC Santa Cruz (Winter 2020 edition, with syllabi from 2011, 2013 and 2016). Lecture slides (PDF and PowerPoint) cover an introduction to AO, Kolmogorov turbulence, geometrical and physical optics, deformable mirrors, imaging through turbulence, signal-to-noise and detectors, error budgets, wavefront sensing, laser guide stars, control systems, wavefront reconstruction, LGS tomography (LTAO, MCAO, MOAO, GLAO), extreme AO, AO for vision science, and how to be an informed user of AO data. The page also provides homework sets, a take-home exam, turbulence animations and links to other AO tutorials.
