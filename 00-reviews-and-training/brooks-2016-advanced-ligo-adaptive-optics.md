# Overview of Advanced LIGO Adaptive Optics

**Brooks et al. (2016)** · *Applied Optics 55, 8256* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.02934) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.55.008256](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.55.008256)

## Summary

This paper gives an overview of the adaptive optics system of Advanced LIGO, called the thermal compensation system (TCS). The TCS is designed to minimise thermally induced spatial distortions of the interferometer optical modes and to correct some static curvature errors in the core optics. It combines ring-heater actuators, spatially tunable CO2 laser projectors and Hartmann wavefront sensors. The authors report that the system meets its requirement of correcting the nominal distortion to a maximum beam-weighted residual error of 5.4 nm for up to 125 W of input laser power. It is included here as an example of AO outside astronomical imaging.
