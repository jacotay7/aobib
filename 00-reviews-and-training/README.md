# 00 · Reviews, Textbooks & Training Resources

This folder collects entry points into adaptive optics as a whole rather than into one sub-problem: broad review articles, free textbooks and book chapters, university course material and lecture notes, web tutorials, hands-on notebooks for AO simulation codes, historical accounts, theses that double as introductions, and pointers to the free AO4ELT conference proceedings. Most material concerns astronomical AO, but several reviews also cover vision science, microscopy, laser beam control, optical communications and gravitational-wave detectors.

Reviews of a single sub-field (for example pyramid sensing, PSF reconstruction, AO-OCT or machine learning for AO) live in their topic folders and are cross-listed here. Simulation codes themselves are in [08](../08-modeling-error-budgets-and-simulation/README.md); this folder links only to their tutorials. Classic textbooks (Roddier, Hardy, Tyson) and several major reviews have no legitimate free full text and are listed in the paywalled list rather than here.

**Subtopics used**

- Broad reviews: general AO reviews for astronomy and beyond
- Reviews of specific areas: reviews owned here that do not fit a single topic folder, plus cross-listed sub-field reviews
- Free books & chapters: open-access edited books and introductory chapters
- Courses & lecture notes: university courses, web tutorials and lecture slides
- Tutorials & hands-on: AO summer-school lab activities and tutorial notebooks for simulation codes
- History of AO: early proposals, first light of astronomical AO, and historical snapshots
- Theses as introductions: PhD theses with broad introductory content
- Conference series & community resources: AO4ELT proceedings (editions 1-8) and resource hubs

**Start here**

- [Davies & Kasper (2012), *Adaptive Optics for Astronomy*](davies-2012-adaptive-optics-astronomy.md): ARA&A review from the astrophysical perspective
- [Hampson et al. (2021), *Adaptive optics for high-resolution imaging*](hampson-2021-adaptive-optics-high-resolution-imaging.md): primer covering astronomy, vision science and microscopy
- [Tokovinin (2001), *Adaptive optics tutorial at CTIO*](tokovinin-2001-adaptive-optics-tutorial-ctio.md): compact formula-oriented web tutorial
- [Max (2020), *ASTR 289 course materials*](max-2020-astr289-adaptive-optics-course.md): full graduate course with slides and homework
- [Hippler (2019), *Adaptive Optics for Extremely Large Telescopes*](hippler-2019-ao-extremely-large-telescopes.md): how AO is built into the ELT, GMT and TMT
- [Rousset & Fusco (2022), *Optique adaptative* (C. R. Physique)](rousset-2022-optique-adaptative-turbulence-images-astronomiques.md): 50-page state-of-the-art review (French)
- [HCIPy tutorial notebooks](por-2018-hcipy-tutorial-notebooks.md) and [OOPAO tutorials](heritier-2023-oopao-tutorials-ao-school.md): hands-on simulation in Python
