# Adaptive optics tutorial at CTIO

**Tokovinin (2001)** · *Cerro Tololo Inter-American Observatory web tutorial* · [Free full text](http://www.ctio.noirlab.edu/~atokovin/tutorial/intro.html)

## Summary

This web tutorial by Andrei Tokovinin is intended as an introduction to adaptive optics for engineers and other non-specialists, giving the formulas needed to estimate system characteristics and guide design trade-offs without deep theory. Its five parts cover atmospheric turbulence (seeing, Strehl ratio, Kolmogorov statistics, temporal evolution, isoplanatic angle and Zernike statistics); correction (partially corrected images, deformable mirror types, actuator count, servo bandwidth and stability); wavefront sensors (Shack-Hartmann, curvature, pyramid and others, with photon noise); laser guide stars (Rayleigh and sodium, cone effect, tilt indetermination); and an optional section on multi-conjugate AO. Worked examples and problems are included.
