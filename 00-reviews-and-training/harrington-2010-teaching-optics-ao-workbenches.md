# Teaching Optics and Systems Engineering With Adaptive Optics Workbenches

**Harrington et al. (2010)** · *ASP Conference Series 436 (Learning from Inquiry in Practice)* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1009.4463)

## Summary

This paper describes four adaptive optics workbenches built through education programmes associated with the Center for Adaptive Optics and related institutes, and how they are used to teach optics and systems engineering. The workbenches have been used at the CfAO AO Summer School, the Akamai Maui Short Course, in classrooms and for training laboratory staff, with activities ranging from first-year undergraduate exploration to professional training. The authors outline activities on systems thinking, characterisation, feedback control, basic optics and alignment, phase conjugation, wavefront sensing and correction, and system design, and summarise common learner outcomes.
