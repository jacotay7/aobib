# On the possibility of reducing the influence of atmospheric seeing on the image quality of stars

**Linnik (1957)** · *Optics and Spectroscopy 3, 401-402 (1957); English translation in ESO Conf. Workshop Proc. 48, 535 (1994)* · [Free full text](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1994ESOC...48..535L)

## Summary

This is an English translation, published in 1994 in an ESO conference proceedings volume with comments by J. M. Beckers, of a 1957 paper by the Soviet optician V. P. Linnik. Linnik proposes compensating atmospheric seeing by dividing the telescope primary into roughly 20 x 20 cm segments with electrically controlled piston motion, measuring the wavefront phase of each subaperture photoelectrically and driving each segment in proportion to the measured phase difference. As noted in the translator's comment, Linnik was apparently unaware of Babcock's 1953 proposal, and the paper's suggestion of using artificial light sources at 8-10 km altitude is almost certainly the first mention of artificial guide stars for AO. The three-page scan is available from NASA ADS.
