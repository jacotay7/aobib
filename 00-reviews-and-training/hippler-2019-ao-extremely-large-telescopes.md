# Adaptive Optics for Extremely Large Telescopes

**Hippler (2019)** · *Journal of Astronomical Instrumentation 8, 1950001* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.02693) · DOI: [10.1142/S2251171719500016](https://doi.org/10.1142/S2251171719500016)

## Summary

This review describes how adaptive optics is integrated into the designs of the three extremely large telescopes: ESO's Extremely Large Telescope, the Giant Magellan Telescope and the Thirty Meter Telescope. Building on three decades of experience with AO as an add-on to existing telescopes, it explains why these facilities and their instruments are designed for diffraction-limited observation from the outset, and compares the approaches taken by each project. The article ends with a short discussion of the requirements that high-contrast imaging places on AO.
