# Adaptive Optics: An Introduction

**Max (2019)** · *Author manuscript, UC Santa Cruz (ASTR 289 reading)* · [Free full text](https://www.ucolick.org/~max/289/Assigned%20Readings/Max_Adaptive_Optics_Intro_v1.pdf)

## Summary

This 14-page introductory text by Claire Max, posted on her UC Santa Cruz course pages as an assigned reading, gives an overview of adaptive optics for astronomy. It explains the role of the deformable mirror (from adaptive secondaries to MEMS devices), wavefront sensors (Shack-Hartmann, curvature and pyramid), the real-time control loop, and the need for natural or laser guide stars (Rayleigh or sodium) close to the science target. Later sections treat ideal and real telescope performance, the nature, origins and quantitative description of atmospheric turbulence, how AO improves image quality, and how turbulence strength drives AO design and performance, followed by a bibliography. The PDF is undated; its file metadata indicate 2019.
