# Optique adaptative : correction des effets de la turbulence atmosphérique sur les images astronomiques (Adaptive optics: correcting atmospheric turbulence effects on astronomical images)

**Rousset & Fusco (2022)** · *Comptes Rendus Physique 23(S1), 293-344* · [Free full text](https://comptes-rendus.academie-sciences.fr/physique/articles/10.5802/crphys.111/) · DOI: [10.5802/crphys.111](https://doi.org/10.5802/crphys.111)

## Summary

This 50-page review (in French, with an English abstract) presents the state of the art of adaptive optics for astronomy. It gives the principles of AO and describes its main components: deformable mirrors, wavefront sensors and control algorithms. It then reviews recent achievements and ongoing projects, first for direct imaging of exoplanets with extreme AO and coronagraphy using SPHERE as the example, and then for laser-assisted tomographic concepts (laser tomography AO, multi-conjugate AO and multi-object AO) that address limited sky coverage and the isoplanatic patch. The paper closes with the challenges of the giant-telescope generation, with a focus on ESO's ELT.
