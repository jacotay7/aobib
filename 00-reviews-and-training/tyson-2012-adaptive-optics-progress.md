# Adaptive Optics Progress

**Tyson (2012)** · *InTech (open-access edited book), ISBN 978-953-51-0894-8* · [Free full text](https://www.intechopen.com/books/3365) · DOI: [10.5772/46199](https://doi.org/10.5772/46199)

## Summary

This open-access edited volume (editor R. K. Tyson) contains eight chapters on recent AO developments. Topics include a dual-conjugate AO prototype for wide-field retinal imaging, devices and techniques for sensorless AO, liquid-crystal wavefront correctors, modelling and control of deformable membrane mirrors, a solar AO system, digital AO and its application to anisoplanatic imaging, AO and optical vortices, and a unified approach to analysing anisoplanatism in AO systems. Chapters are individually downloadable from the publisher.
