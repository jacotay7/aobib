# Adaptive optics for astronomy: theoretical performance and limitations

**Wilson & Jenkins (1996)** · *MNRAS 278, 39-61* · [Free full text](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1996MNRAS.278...39W) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/278.1.39](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/278.1.39)

## Summary

This paper presents a simplified model of an astronomical AO system intended as a guide for astronomers to the performance that can be expected. Image improvement is calculated as a function of the fundamental limitations imposed by anisoplanatism and by photon and detector noise in wavefront sensing. Unlike earlier studies that concentrated on Strehl ratio, the authors compute the corrected point-spread function explicitly, including its shape and encircled-energy widths, which matter for photometry and spectroscopy. The analysis is intended both to assess the benefits of AO for astronomical applications and as a tool for system design.
