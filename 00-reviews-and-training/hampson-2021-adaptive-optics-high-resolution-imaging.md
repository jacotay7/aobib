# Adaptive optics for high-resolution imaging

**Hampson et al. (2021)** · *Nature Reviews Methods Primers 1, 68* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8892592/) · DOI: [10.1038/s43586-021-00066-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43586-021-00066-7)

## Summary

This primer covers adaptive optics for high-resolution imaging across three fields: astronomy, vision science and microscopy. It begins with the general principles of AO and its main components, including methods for measuring aberrations, devices for correcting them, and how these are linked in closed-loop operation. It then presents results and applications from each field, such as cellular-resolution imaging of the living retina and deep imaging of neuronal processes in thick tissue, together with reproducibility considerations, limitations and future directions. The freely available version is the author manuscript deposited in PubMed Central.
