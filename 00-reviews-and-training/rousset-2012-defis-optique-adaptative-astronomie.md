# Les défis de l'optique adaptative en astronomie (The challenges of adaptive optics in astronomy)

**Rousset et al. (2012)** · *Photoniques 58, 32-36* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-03724199) · DOI: [10.1051/photon/20125832](https://doi.org/10.1051/photon/20125832)

## Summary

This five-page magazine article in Photoniques (in French) by researchers from LESIA (Observatoire de Paris) gives a general-audience overview of the challenges of adaptive optics in astronomy around 2012. As its title indicates, it discusses the challenges facing astronomical AO at that time, as the field prepared instruments for 8-10 m telescopes and the extremely large telescopes. It is a short, accessible text suited to French-speaking newcomers; the full text is deposited in HAL.
