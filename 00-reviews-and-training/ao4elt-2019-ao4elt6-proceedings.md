# AO4ELT 6: Adaptive Optics for Extremely Large Telescopes 6 (Québec City, 2019) - proceedings

**AO4ELT6 organizing committees (2019)** · *Online proceedings hosted by COPL, Université Laval* · [Free full text](https://ao4elt6.copl.ulaval.ca/proceedings.html)

## Summary

Proceedings page of the sixth AO4ELT conference, held at the Québec City Convention Centre, Canada, on 9-14 June 2019 (with an associated laser workshop). The page lists contributed papers grouped by session, starting with science with adaptive optics (for example MUSE laser tomography AO imaging of accreting exoplanets, Gemini North AO science cases, the wind-driven halo from SPHERE to METIS, and the LBT enhanced seeing mode), each with a link to a free PDF.
