# SPECULA tutorials (Python/GPU AO simulation framework)

**Rossi et al. (2026)** · *SPECULA documentation (Read the Docs)* · [Free full text](https://specula.readthedocs.io/en/latest/tutorials/index.html)

## Summary

The documentation of SPECULA, the Python object-oriented AO simulator from INAF-Arcetri derived from PASSATA, includes a series of step-by-step tutorials. They cover a basic and a full single-conjugate AO simulation, deformable mirror actuator step response, control stability and performance analysis, pyramid wavefront sensor calibration in the partial-correction regime, generators for vibrations and time histories, Fresnel propagation, a bi-directional optical feeder link, and an ELT-class segmented pupil with an MMSE petal reconstructor and a closed loop with a soft limiter. The framework itself is listed in the simulation topic.
