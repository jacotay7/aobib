# Subaru Telescope - History, active/adaptive optics, instruments, and scientific achievements

**Iye (2021)** · *Proceedings of the Japan Academy, Series B 97, 337-370* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.10610) · DOI: [10.2183/pjab.97.019](https://doi.org/10.2183/pjab.97.019)

## Summary

This review recounts the history, key engineering issues and selected scientific achievements of the 8.2 m Subaru Telescope, built by NAOJ during 1991-1999 and operating on Maunakea since 2000. It describes the active optics system for the thin primary mirror, which was the design backbone of the telescope's imaging performance, and the adaptive optics system with its laser guide star facility that brings the telescope to the diffraction limit. It also summarises the instruments and science highlights from cosmic reionization to exoplanets and protoplanetary disks, and closes with operational statistics, future plans and a note on culture-and-science issues in Hawaii.
