# Center for Adaptive Optics: AO resources (courses, tutorials and simulation codes)

**UC Santa Cruz Center for Adaptive Optics (2025)** · *CfAO website, UC Santa Cruz* · [Free full text](https://cfao.science.ucsc.edu/resources/)

## Summary

Resource page of the Center for Adaptive Optics at UC Santa Cruz, which organises the annual AO Summer School. It links to Claire Max's ASTR 289 AO course, Olivier Guyon's Astronomical Optics course, general optics tutorials, and a list of AO simulation and analysis codes (HCIPy, MAOS, Soapy, OOMAO, YAO, OCTOPUS, DASP, AOtools, TIPTOP and COMPASS). It serves as a starting hub for students; the summer school pages give programmes but do not host lecture slides.
