# The Adaptive Optics Summer School Laboratory Activities

**Ammons et al. (2010)** · *Learning from Inquiry in Practice, ASP Conference Series 436* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.2015)

## Summary

This paper describes the laboratory sessions introduced in 2006-2009 at the Center for Adaptive Optics AO Summer School, a week-long course for graduate students and postdocs that has trained nearly 250 participants including astronomers, vision scientists, engineers and laser-communication users. The activities are organised in three stations, Vision Science, Fourier Optics and an AO Demonstrator, aiming to build familiarity with AO technologies, optical alignment and system design, and inquiry into vision-science questions. The authors discuss the challenge of combining inquiry-based learning with complex content for a PhD-level audience and report survey results indicating that participants valued hands-on experience with a working AO system.
