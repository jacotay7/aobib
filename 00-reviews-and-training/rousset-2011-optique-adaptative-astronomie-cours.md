## Summary

Lecture material (in French) by Gérard Rousset on adaptive optics and astronomy, dated November 2011 and deposited in the Société Française d'Optique collection of the HAL open archive. Its subject keywords are adaptive optics, imaging and telescopes; the author was a member of the team behind the 1989 COME-ON prototype. It is listed here as an introductory resource for French-speaking students; the PDF is available from the HAL record.
