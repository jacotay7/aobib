# Optique adaptative et grands télescopes (Adaptive optics and large telescopes)

**Kern (1990)** · *PhD thesis, Université Paris VII* · [Free full text](https://theses.hal.science/tel-00714946)

## Summary

This PhD thesis (in French, supervised by Pierre Léna) describes the adaptive optics prototype tested on the sky in autumn 1989, which produced the first diffraction-limited astronomical images corrected by AO. It explains why large ground-based telescopes are limited by turbulence and how AO processes the wavefront before detection, then analyses the system limitations set by the number of actuators, wavefront measurement accuracy and servo bandwidth. The instrument used a 19-actuator deformable mirror and a 5x5 Shack-Hartmann sensor running at 100 Hz (servo bandwidth 9 Hz at the 0 dB open-loop point) at the coudé focus of the 1.52 m OHP telescope, reaching the diffraction limit down to 2.2 um. It combines an introduction to AO with a first-hand account of the COME-ON prototype.
