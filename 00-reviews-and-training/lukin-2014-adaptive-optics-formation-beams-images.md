# Adaptive optics in the formation of optical beams and images

**Lukin (2014)** · *Uspekhi Fizicheskikh Nauk 184, 599-640 (Russian original; English translation Physics-Uspekhi 57, 556-592)* · [Free full text](https://ufn.ru/ru/articles/2014/6/b/) · DOI: [10.3367/UFNr.0184.201406b.0599](https://doi.org/10.3367/UFNr.0184.201406b.0599)

## Summary

This review from the Zuev Institute of Atmospheric Optics (Tomsk) traces the development of adaptive optics from early ideas to applications in astronomy, high-power laser physics and medicine. It covers the first years of AO development, the principles of AO operation (direct and conjugate waves in random media, phase conjugation and system architectures), key components (wavefront sensors and reconstruction, correctors, numerical simulation), astronomical AO including partial correction, solar AO, artificial reference sources and multi-conjugate AO, and AO for forming laser beams, including high-power and military laser systems. The full 42-page Russian original is free on the journal website; the English translation in Physics-Uspekhi is free only as a partial preview.
