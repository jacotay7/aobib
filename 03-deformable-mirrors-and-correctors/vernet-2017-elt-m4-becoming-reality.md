# The E-ELT M4, on its way to becoming a reality

**Vernet et al. (2017)** · *Proc. AO4ELT5 (Tenerife, 2017), 0070* · [Free full text](https://meetings.iac.es/AO4ELT5/media/proceedings/proceeding-070.pdf) · DOI: [10.26698/ao4elt5.0070](https://doi.org/10.26698/ao4elt5.0070)

## Summary

Status report on the final design of the E-ELT M4 adaptive unit shortly before its final design review. The mirror follows the adaptive-secondary concept developed for MMT, LBT, VLT and Magellan (a thin shell carrying magnets levitating about 100 microns from voice-coil actuators on a reference structure), with changes including a shell segmented into six petals, a brazed silicon-carbide reference body, a new capacitive-sensor design, and actuator electronics grouped into gas-cooled 'bricks'. The paper describes each subsystem, compares performance analyses with results from prototypes of the critical items, and outlines the test plan for the segment prototype.
