# Development of image motion compensation system for 1.3m telescope at Vainu Bappu Observatory

**Vallapureddy et al. (2020)** · *Research in Astronomy and Astrophysics 20, 12* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.11027) · DOI: [10.1088/1674-4527/20/1/12](https://doi.org/10.1088/1674-4527/20/1/12)

## Summary

Describes a tip-tilt system developed at the Indian Institute of Astrophysics to compensate turbulence-induced image motion at the 1.3-m telescope of the Vainu Bappu Observatory, Kavalur. The instrument operates in the visible (480-700 nm) over a 1 x 1 arcmin field. On-sky tests achieved a closed-loop correction bandwidth of about 26 Hz and reduced the rms image motion by a factor of about 14; tilt-corrected images show up to about 57% improvement in resolution and a peak-intensity increase of about 2.8. The paper presents the instrument design, laboratory calibration and on-telescope performance, and compares the results with theoretical and numerical predictions.
