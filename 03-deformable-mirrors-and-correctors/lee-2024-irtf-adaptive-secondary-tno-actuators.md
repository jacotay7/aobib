# First laboratory and on-sky results of an adaptive secondary mirror with TNO-style actuators on the NASA Infrared Telescope Facility

**Lee et al. (2024)** · *Proc. SPIE 13097* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.06444) · DOI: [10.1117/12.3016132](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3016132)

## Summary

Presents IRTF-ASM-1, a 36-actuator prototype adaptive secondary mirror for the NASA Infrared Telescope Facility built with TNO hybrid variable reluctance actuators, which are more than an order of magnitude more efficient than voice-coil actuators. The mirror was developed to demonstrate the performance, operation and serviceability of the technology in an observatory environment. Calibration and performance results in a meniscus Hindle-sphere test setup are reported, together with the first on-sky results, in which stable closed-loop operation gave H-band long-exposure Strehl ratios of 35-40% under a range of seeing conditions.
