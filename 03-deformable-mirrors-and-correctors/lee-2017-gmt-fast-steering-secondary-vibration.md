# Random Vibration Analysis of the Tip-tilt System in the GMT Fast Steering Secondary Mirror

**Lee et al. (2017)** · *PASP 129, 095001* · [Free full text](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/1538-3873/aa76bd) · DOI: [10.1088/1538-3873/aa76bd](https://doi.org/10.1088/1538-3873/aa76bd)

## Summary

Analyses the tip-tilt system of the Fast Steering Secondary Mirror (FSM) of the Giant Magellan Telescope, developed in Korea (KASI and the Institute for Advanced Engineering), whose segments are disturbed by wind at the top of the secondary truss. Dynamic equations for the segment tip-tilt system are derived from force and moment equilibrium with four design parameters, and stationary random-vibration responses to wind-induced disturbances are computed in the frequency domain from the spectral density of star-image errors for off-axis and on-axis segments. A prototype on-axis FSM segment driven by piezoelectric actuators was tested at 4, 8 and 12 Hz, and the measured ratios of rms tip-tilt force to rms tip-tilt angle agree with the model.
