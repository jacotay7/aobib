# MEMS deformable mirrors in astronomical adaptive optics

**Bifano et al. (2010)** · *Proc. AO4ELT1 (Paris, 2009), 06003* · [Free full text](https://ao4elt.edpsciences.org/articles/ao4elt/pdf/2010/01/ao4elt_06003.pdf) · DOI: [10.1051/ao4elt/201006003](https://doi.org/10.1051/ao4elt/201006003)

## Summary

Reports on Boston Micromachines/Boston University MEMS deformable mirrors developed for ground- and space-based astronomical AO. The continuous-membrane devices consist of a metal-coated thin silicon membrane on arrays of up to 4096 electrostatic actuators over apertures up to 25.2 mm, with no hysteresis, sub-nanometre repeatability, up to 4 microns of stroke, surface finish below 10 nm rms and 99.8% fill factor. A segmented piston/tip/tilt device with 1 micron of piston and 600 arcseconds of tilt is also described. Device characteristics and performance measurements are presented.
