# Fast, large and controllable phase modulation using dual frequency liquid crystals

**Kirby & Love (2004)** · *Optics Express 12, 1470* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OPEX.12.001470) · DOI: [10.1364/OPEX.12.001470](https://doi.org/10.1364/OPEX.12.001470)

## Summary

Addresses the slow response of nematic liquid-crystal phase modulators by using dual-frequency liquid crystals, whose dielectric anisotropy changes sign with drive frequency so that the material can be actively driven in both directions. An all-electronic feedback system simplifies control. The authors demonstrate half-wave phase modulation at about 120 Hz with the operating point varying over nearly the full dynamic range, and 2.5 waves of phase shift at 37 Hz, about an order of magnitude faster than existing techniques for large phase shifts.
