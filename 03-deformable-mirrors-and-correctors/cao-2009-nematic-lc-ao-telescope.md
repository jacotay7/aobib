# Preliminary use of nematic liquid crystal adaptive optics with a 2.16-meter reflecting telescope

**Cao et al. (2009)** · *Optics Express 17, 2530* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.17.002530) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.17.002530](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.17.002530)

## Summary

Describes a nematic liquid-crystal adaptive optics system built for the 2.16-m reflecting telescope at Xinglong, designed and optimised with Zemax. In the laboratory, correction reduced the wavefront error to 0.2 waves peak-to-valley and 0.05 waves rms at 633 nm. On sky, tracking Pollux, real-time correction reduced the average wavefront error from 11 to 2.3 waves peak-to-valley and from 2.5 to 0.6 waves rms; the stellar image showed a sharp core but a residual halo, indicating partial correction. Limitations of the system and possible improvements are discussed.
