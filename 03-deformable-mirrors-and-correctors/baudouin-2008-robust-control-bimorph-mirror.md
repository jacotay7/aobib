# Robust control of a bimorph mirror for adaptive optics systems

**Baudouin et al. (2008)** · *Applied Optics 47, 3637* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/0804.1721) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.47.003637](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.47.003637)

## Summary

Applies robust control techniques to an adaptive optics system that includes a dynamic model of a bimorph deformable mirror, derived as a modification of the usual plate equation, together with a state-space model of the turbulent phase. A continuous-time H-infinity controller is designed in an infinite-dimensional setting, taking the frequency content of the turbulence into account. The authors report a significant improvement over traditional single-input single-output control because the method handles the multivariable nature of the problem.
