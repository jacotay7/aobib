# The adaptive secondary mirror for the Large Binocular Telescope: optical acceptance test and preliminary on-sky commissioning results

**Riccardi et al. (2010)** · *Proc. SPIE 7736 (Adaptive Optics Systems II), 77362C* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/230851613_The_adaptive_secondary_mirror_for_the_Large_Binocular_Telescope_optical_acceptance_test_and_preliminary_on-sky_commissioning_results) · DOI: [10.1117/12.858229](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.858229)

## Summary

Describes the optical acceptance test and first on-sky results of the adaptive secondary mirrors of the Large Binocular Telescope, each with 672 voice-coil force actuators whose shape is controlled through co-located capacitive sensors. The acceptance test, carried out in 2009 in the 14 m optical test tower at INAF-Arcetri, showed the thin shell could be flattened to about 14 nm rms residual surface error; the paper gives the optical layout, calibration procedures and results. It then reports early commissioning runs of the First Light Adaptive Optics (FLAO) system, in which the mirror corrected atmospheric turbulence with H-band Strehl ratios above 80%.
