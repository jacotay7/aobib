# Push-pull membrane mirrors for adaptive optics

**Bonora & Poletto (2006)** · *Optics Express 14, 11935* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.14.011935) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.14.011935](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.14.011935)

## Summary

Proposes adding push-pull capability to electrostatic membrane deformable mirrors, which normally can only pull the membrane, by placing transparent indium-tin-oxide electrodes on a glass window above the membrane. The improvement in generating surfaces is shown by comparison with a pull-only membrane mirror. The mirror is controlled in open loop from the measured response of each electrode, and an iterative strategy for handling actuator clipping is presented. Performance is evaluated through Zernike-polynomial generation and compensation of aberrations drawn from human-eye statistics.
