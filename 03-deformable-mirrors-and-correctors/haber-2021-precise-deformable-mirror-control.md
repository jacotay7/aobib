# General approach to precise deformable mirror control

**Haber & Bifano (2021)** · *Optics Express 29, 33741* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.439306) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.439306](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.439306)

## Summary

Develops an iterative, data-driven method for accurately shaping a deformable mirror: from batches of measured surface profiles the method estimates an influence matrix by multivariable least squares, computes control actions by constrained least squares, randomly perturbs them to generate new data, and repeats until convergence. Tested on a 140-actuator Boston Micromachines MEMS mirror, it achieved surface rms errors of 5-30 nm for most tested Zernike modes in a few iterations despite significant DM nonlinearities and without direct feedback control. The estimated model can also be used for single-step open-loop control.
