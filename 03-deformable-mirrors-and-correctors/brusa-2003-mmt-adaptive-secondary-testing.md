# MMT adaptive secondary: performance evaluation and field testing

**Brusa et al. (2003)** · *Proc. SPIE 4839 (Adaptive Optical System Technologies II), 691* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/230851575_MMT_adaptive_secondary_performance_evaluation_and_field_testing) · DOI: [10.1117/12.459786](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.459786)

## Summary

Reports laboratory performance and early field testing of MMT336, the 336-actuator adaptive secondary mirror of the MMT and the first mirror of its kind. Correcting at the secondary is motivated by higher throughput, lower emissivity and a simpler optical layout, and the mirror adds internal position feedback, large stroke for chopping and absolute position calibration. After more than a year of daily use in the test facility, interferometer measurements show a residual surface error of about 40 nm rms, and closed-loop operation with a Shack-Hartmann sensor gives a -3 dB bandwidth of about 30 Hz, limited by the sensor frame rate. Preliminary results on ±5 arcsec chopping at 5 Hz are shown, and the first-light run at the telescope is briefly summarised.
