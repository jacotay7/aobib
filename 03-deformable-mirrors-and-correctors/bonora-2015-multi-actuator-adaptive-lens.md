# Wavefront correction and high-resolution in vivo OCT imaging with an objective integrated multi-actuator adaptive lens

**Bonora et al. (2015)** · *Optics Express 23, 21931* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.23.021931) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.23.021931](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.23.021931)

## Summary

Introduces a transmissive multi-actuator adaptive lens that can correct wavefront aberrations up to fourth Zernike radial order and that can be integrated directly into an objective, simplifying the integration of adaptive optics into existing optical systems compared with reflective correctors. Its performance is demonstrated in a wide-field microscope with closed-loop Shack-Hartmann control. The lens was also built into the objective of a Fourier-domain OCT system and driven by image-based, wavefront-sensorless optimisation for in vivo imaging of mouse retina, correcting aberrations to near the diffraction limit.
