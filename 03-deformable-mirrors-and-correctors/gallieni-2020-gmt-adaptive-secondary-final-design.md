# GMT adaptive secondary mirrors subsystem final design

**Gallieni et al. (2020)** · *Proc. SPIE 11448 (Adaptive Optics Systems VII), 114485K* · [Free full text](https://giantmagellan.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/GMT-adaptive-secondary-mirrors-subsystem-final-design.pdf) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2562698](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2562698)

## Summary

Presents the final design of the Giant Magellan Telescope adaptive secondary mirror system: seven 1 m class deformable mirror segments, each with a hexapod positioner. Each segment uses the contactless voice-coil technology developed by AdOptica and already deployed on several 8 m class telescopes. The particular challenge for GMT is that the seven segments must work together as a single mirror. A 72-actuator subscale prototype was built to secure the final design, and its test setup and preliminary results are presented.
