# High pixel number deformable mirror concept utilizing piezoelectric hysteresis for stable shape configurations

**Huisman et al. (2020)** · *JATIS 7, 029002 (2021)* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.09338) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.7.2.029002](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.7.2.029002)

## Summary

Presents the conceptual design and early development of the Hysteretic Deformable Mirror (HDM), which uses a highly hysteretic piezoelectric material with a simple electrode layout to define individual actuator pixels. Because the material retains a large remnant deformation, the mirror shape is 'set and forget', allowing time-division multiplexing of the pixels without high refresh rates to avoid drift. The authors argue this enables scaling to very high pixel counts (128x128 or more) and densities around 100 per square millimetre. A companion paper (Schmerbauch et al. 2020, Applied Optics, linked as an alternative) models the influence functions of this hysteretic facesheet-on-piezo-layer architecture.
