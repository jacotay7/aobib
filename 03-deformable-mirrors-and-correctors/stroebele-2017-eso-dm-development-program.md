# Deformable mirrors development program at ESO

**Stroebele et al. (2017)** · *Proc. AO4ELT5 (Tenerife, 2017), 0008* · [Free full text](https://meetings.iac.es/AO4ELT5/media/proceedings/proceeding-008.pdf) · DOI: [10.26698/ao4elt5.0008](https://doi.org/10.26698/ao4elt5.0008)

## Summary

Describes the deformable-mirror development programme ESO started to prepare post-focal DMs for E-ELT instruments, since simply scaling existing technologies from a few hundred to thousands of actuators was judged neither feasible nor sufficient. The paper sets out the requirements and the development path for DMs for post-focal E-ELT AO systems, and summarises the design and status of the activities carried out by the project partners.
