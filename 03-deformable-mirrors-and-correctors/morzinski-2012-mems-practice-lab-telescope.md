# MEMS practice: from the lab to the telescope

**Morzinski et al. (2012)** · *Proc. SPIE 8253, 825304* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1202.1566) · DOI: [10.1117/12.910964](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.910964)

## Summary

Summarises about a decade of experience at the UC Santa Cruz Laboratory for Adaptive Optics as end-users of MEMS deformable mirrors, from inspecting and characterising devices to evaluating their performance in laboratory AO systems. The paper also shows on-sky MEMS wavefront correction with the 'Villages' AO system on a 1-m telescope, including open-loop control and visible-light imaging. The authors argue that the results demonstrate the maturity of MEMS technology for astronomical AO, as large MEMS-based systems such as the Gemini Planet Imager were coming online.
