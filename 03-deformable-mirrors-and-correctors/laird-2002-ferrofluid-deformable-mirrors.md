# Ferrofluid Based Deformable Mirrors - a New Approach to Adaptive Optics Using Liquid Mirrors

**Laird et al. (2002)** · *Proc. SPIE 4839* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0212189) · DOI: [10.1117/12.459065](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.459065)

## Summary

Proposes deformable liquid mirrors made from water- or oil-based ferrofluids as a low-cost route to mirrors with very many actuators. Ferrofluids respond more strongly to magnetic fields than liquid-metal amalgams and avoid passing current through the liquid, and the low reflectivity of pure ferrofluid is overcome by depositing a thin silver-colloid metal liquid-like film (MELLF) on the surface. Experimental results from a prototype deformable liquid mirror based on this combination are presented.
