# An Image Stabilization High-Resolution Camera for the Canada-France-Hawaii Telescope

**McClure et al. (1989)** · *PASP 101, 1156* · [Free full text](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1989PASP..101.1156M) · DOI: [10.1086/132592](https://doi.org/10.1086/132592)

## Summary

Describes HRCam, the image-stabilizing high-resolution camera built at the Dominion Astrophysical Observatory with the Université de Montréal and CFHT for the prime focus of the 3.6-m Canada-France-Hawaii Telescope. The instrument combines a fast guider that follows atmospheric image motion and drives a tilting mirror, a "smart" shutter that rejects periods of degraded image quality, and an aperture wheel to study the gain from stopping down the entrance pupil. The paper reviews the design and measured performance of the instrument, an early facility tip-tilt (image-stabilization) system that preceded the CFHT adaptive optics bonnette.
