# Deformable mirror interferometric analysis for the direct imagery of exoplanets

**Mazoyer et al. (2014)** · *Proc. SPIE 9148, 914846* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.03509) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2057833](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2057833)

## Summary

Characterises a 1024-actuator Boston Micromachines MEMS deformable mirror from the Paris Observatory high-contrast testbed using the interferometric bench developed at the Laboratoire d'Astrophysique de Marseille. The motivation is that coronagraphic exoplanet imaging needs residual wavefront errors well below a nanometre and limited DM surface defects, which otherwise introduce amplitude aberrations. The paper presents the bench and results on the mirror surface, the behaviour of single actuators, cross-talk between neighbouring actuators and the influence of a stuck actuator over a 10x10 actuator region, as well as the full-mirror surface shape.
