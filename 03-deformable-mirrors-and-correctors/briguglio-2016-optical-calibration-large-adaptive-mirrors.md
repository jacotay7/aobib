# Optical calibration of large format adaptive mirrors

**Briguglio et al. (2016)** · *INAF - Osservatorio Astrofisico di Arcetri technical report, ISBN 978-88-908876-1-1 (arXiv:2210.04608)* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.04608)

## Summary

Describes the sequence of operations used to optically calibrate large adaptive mirrors such as adaptive secondaries, i.e. to derive the commands needed to flatten the mirror, compensate alignment and non-common-path aberrations, and support chopping or field-stabilised operation. The paper details the actors and functional requirements of each step and stresses automation, with defined inputs, outputs and quality indicators, because mirrors with thousands of actuators make manual procedures impractical. Algorithms to evaluate the interferometric measurement noise are presented, since calibration facilities typically operate in industrial environments where noise can be a limiting factor. The approach draws on the LBT and VLT experience and targets the ELT generation.
