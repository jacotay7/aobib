# Overview of deformable mirror technologies for adaptive optics and astronomy

**Madec (2012)** · *Proc. SPIE 8447 (Adaptive Optics Systems III), 844705* · [Free full text](https://www.eso.org/sci/libraries/SPIE2012/8447-05.pdf) · DOI: [10.1117/12.924892](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.924892)

## Summary

An overview of deformable-mirror technology for astronomical adaptive optics. It first recalls the main design drivers, covering not only atmospheric compensation but also environmental conditions and mechanical constraints. It then describes the technologies available, from piezoelectric mirrors to large voice-coil adaptive secondaries and the small compact mirrors used in multi-object AO, with their pros and cons. It reviews the companies and institutes able to deliver deformable mirrors to astronomers, lessons from 25 years of development and on-sky operation, and the outlook for the field. The linked copy is the author manuscript in ESO's library.
