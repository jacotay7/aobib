# Performance of Large-Format Deformable Mirrors Constructed with TNO Variable Reluctance Actuators

**Bowens-Rubin et al. (2020)** · *Proc. SPIE 11448 (Adaptive Optics Systems VII)* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.07222) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2563659](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2563659)

## Summary

Reports laboratory performance of TNO's 57-actuator large-format deformable mirror built with a new variable-reluctance actuator that needs roughly eighty times less power than conventional voice-coil actuators. Influence functions, linearity, hysteresis, natural-shape flattening, cross-coupling, creep, repeatability and actuator lifetime were measured. Over 10 microns of stroke the authors find a linearity of 99.4% and hysteresis of 2.1%, and conclude that the technology is promising for on-sky adaptive secondary mirrors. A companion paper on ASM concepts for the APF and Keck telescopes based on this technology is linked as an alternative.
