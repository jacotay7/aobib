# A deformable secondary mirror for the VLT

**Arsenault et al. (2006)** · *Proc. SPIE 6272 (Advances in Adaptive Optics II), 62720V* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/230851590_A_deformable_secondary_mirror_for_the_VLT) · DOI: [10.1117/12.672879](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.672879)

## Summary

Presents the outcome of an ESO feasibility study, begun in 2004 with MicroGate, ADS International and INAF-Arcetri, for retrofitting a VLT 8 m telescope with a deformable secondary mirror (DSM) as part of what became the Adaptive Optics Facility. The proposed M2 unit has 1170 voice-coil force actuators with co-located capacitive position sensors and an 80 kHz internal control loop providing electronic damping. Simulations give a fitting error of 62.5 nm rms (r0 = 12.1 cm at 30° zenith) for a 2 mm thin shell, with about 1.5 kW of heat dissipation. The design targets about 50 μm stroke and sub-millisecond rise time, includes a hexapod for focusing and centring, and can switch between Nasmyth and Cassegrain configurations.
