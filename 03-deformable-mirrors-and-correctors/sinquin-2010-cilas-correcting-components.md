# Recent Progress on Correcting Components (useful for ELTs) at CILAS

**Sinquin et al. (2010)** · *Proc. AO4ELT1 (Paris, 2009), 06004* · [Free full text](https://ao4elt.edpsciences.org/articles/ao4elt/pdf/2010/01/ao4elt_06004.pdf) · DOI: [10.1051/ao4elt/201006004](https://doi.org/10.1051/ao4elt/201006004)

## Summary

Presents three correcting-component developments at CILAS relevant to ELT adaptive optics. The first is a 28x28 piezoelectric deformable mirror demonstrator with 3.5 mm actuator spacing, built for an E-ELT MCAO feasibility study and designed for small-pitch concave or convex mirrors. The second is a 50x50 prototype with 1 mm spacing based on a new 'miniDM' piezo-array family using the transverse piezoelectric effect, validating steps needed to manufacture very-high-order mirrors. The third is a tip/tilt mount prototype for the NFIRAOS MCAO system of TMT, designed to provide large angular stroke and bandwidth while carrying a heavy deformable mirror.
