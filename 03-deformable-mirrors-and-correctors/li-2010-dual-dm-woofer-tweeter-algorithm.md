# A correction algorithm to simultaneously control dual deformable mirrors in a woofer-tweeter adaptive optics system

**Li et al. (2010)** · *Optics Express 18, 16671* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.18.016671) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.18.016671](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.18.016671)

## Summary

Presents a direct slope-based algorithm that controls both mirrors of a woofer-tweeter AO system simultaneously, using a global response matrix built from the individual response matrices so that the voltages for both mirrors are computed in one step. The method was compared with a two-step sequential control in an adaptive optics scanning laser ophthalmoscope on five human eyes. Residual rms wavefront errors (0.128 vs 0.107 microns over a 7.75 mm pupil) and image power spectra were comparable for the two approaches, with the simultaneous method being more efficient. The authors note it can be applied to any woofer-tweeter AO system.
