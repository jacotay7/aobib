# Faulty actuator tolerance in deformable mirrors for Extremely Large Telescope multi-object adaptive optics

**Basden (2014)** · *MNRAS 440, 577* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.3398) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stu315](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stu315)

## Summary

Investigates how faulty actuators in the many high-order deformable mirrors required by ELT multi-object AO instruments affect AO performance, in order to judge how far faulty-actuator specifications, and hence cost, can be relaxed. End-to-end Monte Carlo simulations are used, including a map of faulty actuators from an existing deformable mirror. For actuators stuck at a fixed height, 1-2% can be faulty before significant degradation occurs, while for actuators coupled to their nearest neighbours up to about 5% can be tolerated.
