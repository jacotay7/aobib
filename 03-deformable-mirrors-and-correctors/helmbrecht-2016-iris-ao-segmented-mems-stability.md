# Long-term stability and temperature variability of Iris AO segmented MEMS deformable mirrors

**Helmbrecht et al. (2016)** · *Proc. SPIE 9909, 990981* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.04742) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2233923](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2233923)

## Summary

Investigates long-term stability and temperature sensitivity of Iris AO PTT111 segmented (piston/tip/tilt) MEMS deformable mirrors, which matters for instruments relying on open-loop correction. Three devices calibrated 13 to 29 months earlier showed only small increases in positioning error, from 1.38 nm rms after 18 months to 5.68 nm rms after 29 months. For temperature changes of up to 6.2 degrees C around room temperature, errors were 0.62-1.42 nm rms per degree C, reduced to 0.50 nm rms per degree C or less once packaging effects were removed, and individual segments deformed by at most 0.11 nm rms per degree C when heated.
