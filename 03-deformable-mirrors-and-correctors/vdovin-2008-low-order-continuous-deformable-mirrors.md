# Correction of low order aberrations using continuous deformable mirrors

**Vdovin et al. (2008)** · *Optics Express 16, 2859* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.16.002859) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.16.002859](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.16.002859)

## Summary

Analyses the static equations governing continuous deformable mirrors (the Poisson equation for membrane and bimorph mirrors, and the biharmonic equation for continuous-facesheet mirrors with push-pull actuators) to derive requirements on actuator placement for high-quality correction of low-order aberrations. The authors show that such mirrors need actuators positioned outside the correction aperture: Poisson-type mirrors require at least two outside actuators per azimuthal period of the highest aberration order to be corrected, and biharmonic-type mirrors at least four, not all on a single circle. The results provide design rules for the actuator layout of membrane, bimorph and facesheet correctors.
