# Random Vibration Testing of Microelectromechanical Deformable Mirrors for Space-based High-Contrast Imaging

**Potier et al. (2023)** · *JATIS 9, 029001* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.19495) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.9.2.029001](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.9.2.029001)

## Summary

Reports launch-environment testing of two 50x50-actuator Boston Micromachines MEMS deformable mirrors procured by NASA's Exoplanet Exploration Program, motivated by the need for space coronagraphs to use DMs with 50-96 actuators across controlled at the picometre level. The mirrors underwent functional and high-contrast imaging tests before and after exposure to flight-like random vibration. No significant functional or performance degradation was found at 10^-8 contrast levels.
