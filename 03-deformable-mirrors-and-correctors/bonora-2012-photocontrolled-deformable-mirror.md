# High resolution wavefront correction with photocontrolled deformable mirror

**Bonora et al. (2012)** · *Optics Express 20, 5178* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.20.005178) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.20.005178](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.20.005178)

## Summary

Demonstrates a photo-controlled deformable mirror (PCDM) in which the membrane is actuated optically: a light pattern from a consumer LCD display addresses a photoconductive layer, so the actuator geometry is set in software rather than by discrete fixed electrodes. This reduces driving electronics and the per-channel cost of high-resolution wavefront control. The mirror was operated with the equivalent of 36, 76 and 201 actuators simply by changing the projected light pattern, and closed-loop correction experiments are reported.
