# Voice-coil technology for the E-ELT M4 Adaptive Unit

**Gallieni et al. (2010)** · *Proc. AO4ELT1 (Paris, 2009), 06002* · [Free full text](https://ao4elt.edpsciences.org/articles/ao4elt/pdf/2010/01/ao4elt_06002.pdf) · DOI: [10.1051/ao4elt/201006002](https://doi.org/10.1051/ao4elt/201006002)

## Summary

Presents the INAF-Arcetri/Microgate/ADS design proposal for the E-ELT M4 adaptive unit based on the contactless voice-coil technology previously deployed on the MMT, LBT, Magellan and VLT adaptive mirrors. Permanent magnets glued to the back of a 2-mm-thick shell are driven by coils on a stiff reference structure, with capacitive sensors closing a co-located position loop at each actuator and dedicated parallel processors computing a centralised feed-forward of actuator forces. The design uses about 6000 actuators at roughly 30 mm spacing, and the authors argue it tolerates up to 5% failed actuators and large mechanical tolerances. A demonstration prototype addressing control of two adjacent shell segments and the modular actuator 'brick' concept is described.
