# A low cost adaptive optics system using a membrane mirror

**Paterson et al. (2000)** · *Optics Express 6, 175* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.6.000175) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.6.000175](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.6.000175)

## Summary

Presents an adaptive optics system built almost entirely from commercially available components around a 37-actuator electrostatic membrane mirror, running at frame rates up to 800 Hz on a single processor. Numerical modelling of the membrane mirror is used to optimise system parameters. Dynamic performance is studied with a diffractive wavefront generator based on a ferroelectric liquid-crystal spatial light modulator producing time-varying Kolmogorov aberrations, and correction is measured for different turbulence strengths and effective wind speeds.
