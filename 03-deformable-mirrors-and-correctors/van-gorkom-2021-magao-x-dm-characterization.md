# Characterizing deformable mirrors for the MagAO-X instrument

**Van Gorkom et al. (2021)** · *JATIS 7, 039001* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.07523) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.7.3.039001](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.7.3.039001)

## Summary

Reports the pre-integration characterisation of the three deformable mirrors of MagAO-X, the visible/near-infrared extreme-AO instrument on the Magellan Clay telescope: a 2040-actuator Boston Micromachines MEMS tweeter running at 3.63 kHz and two ALPAO mirrors serving as woofer and non-common-path corrector. Each surface was measured with a Zygo Verifire interferometer to build the response models needed to command the mirrors to desired shapes. The tweeter was flattened to 6.9 nm rms surface (0.56 nm rms within its control bandwidth) and the ALPAO mirrors to 2.1-2.2 nm rms over the beam footprint. With focus-diversity phase retrieval the integrated DMs corrected the instrument wavefront error to 18.7 nm rms. An earlier progress report (2018) is linked as an alternative.
