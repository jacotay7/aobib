# Adaptive optics with a programmable phase modulator: applications in the human eye

**Prieto et al. (2004)** · *Optics Express 12, 4059* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OPEX.12.004059) · DOI: [10.1364/OPEX.12.004059](https://doi.org/10.1364/OPEX.12.004059)

## Summary

Evaluates an optically addressed liquid-crystal spatial light modulator (Hamamatsu PPM X8267) as both an aberration generator and a corrector in an ocular adaptive optics system with a 25 Hz Hartmann-Shack sensor, at 633 and 780 nm. Single Zernike terms could be generated linearly over a wide range with negligible cross-coupling, aberrations of an artificial eye were corrected in a single step, and closed-loop correction was demonstrated in living eyes. The authors note the slow temporal response compared with deformable mirrors but highlight the large effective stroke and mode independence of the device.
