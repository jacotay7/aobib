# The University of Hawaii 2.2 Meter Fast Tip-Tilt Secondary System

**Jim et al. (2000)** · *PASP 112, 716* · [Free full text](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1086/316569) · DOI: [10.1086/316569](https://doi.org/10.1086/316569)

## Summary

Describes the image-stabilization system of the University of Hawaii 2.2-m telescope on Mauna Kea, built around a new f/31 secondary mirror mounted on a fast tip-tilt platform and used routinely for astronomical imaging and spectroscopy. The paper covers the construction and operation of the system and its capabilities. Near-infrared images show diffraction-limited cores with Strehl ratios up to 0.47, and K-band images with 0.3 arcsec FWHM are obtained routinely without deconvolution. A summary of scientific results obtained with the system and planned improvements are also given.
