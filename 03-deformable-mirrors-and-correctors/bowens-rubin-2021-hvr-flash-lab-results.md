# Performance of Large-Format Deformable Mirrors Constructed with Hybrid Variable Reluctance Actuators II: Initial Lab Results from FLASH

**Bowens-Rubin et al. (2021)** · *Proc. SPIE 11823 (Techniques and Instrumentation for Detection of Exoplanets X), 118231R* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.01693) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2593535](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2593535)

## Summary

Reports laboratory performance of FLASH, a 19-actuator, 160-mm prototype deformable mirror built by TNO with hybrid variable reluctance (HVR) actuators, which require roughly seventy-five times less power than conventional voice-coil actuators. Actuator cross-coupling, linearity, hysteresis, natural-shape flattening and drift were measured with a Zygo interferometer and capacitive sensors, and the dynamic response was characterised on sub-millisecond timescales to assess suitability for high-contrast AO. The authors conclude that the technology is suitable for on-sky adaptive secondary mirrors without active cooling.
