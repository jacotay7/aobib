# High fidelity adaptive mirror simulations with reduced order models

**Stadler et al. (2024)** · *Journal of Mathematics in Industry 14, 20* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.11088) · DOI: [10.1186/s13362-024-00158-5](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13362-024-00158-5)

## Summary

Presents a framework, developed with Microgate, for building reduced-order structural models of large adaptive mirrors so that control-system simulations can include the mirror, its supporting structure or even the telescope at acceptable computational cost. The reduced dynamic model is generated as a preprocessing step and then combined with the other system components to simulate the full adaptive mirror efficiently. The feasibility of the reduced models is analysed for Microgate's prototype of the Giant Magellan Telescope adaptive secondary mirror.
