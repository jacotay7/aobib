# Unimorph deformable mirror for space telescopes: design and manufacturing

**Rausch et al. (2015)** · *Optics Express 23, 19469* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.23.019469) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.23.019469](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.23.019469)

## Summary

Large deployable and lightweight space telescopes suffer aberrations from thermal deformation, gravity release and deployment misalignment, which an active correcting element could compensate on orbit. In an ESA-funded project the authors designed and manufactured a piezoelectrically actuated unimorph deformable mirror intended to operate in the space environment and to correct large low-order aberrations with high surface fidelity. The paper covers the design, manufacturing and performance results. A companion paper on environmental testing (thermal vacuum down to 100 K, vibration, irradiation; Optics Express 24, 1528, 2016) reports the qualification results.
