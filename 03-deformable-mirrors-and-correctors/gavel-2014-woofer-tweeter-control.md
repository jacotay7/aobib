# Woofer-tweeter deformable mirror control for closed-loop adaptive optics: theory and practice

**Gavel & Norton (2014)** · *Proc. SPIE 9148, 91484J* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.8208) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2056937](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2056937)

## Summary

Addresses closed-loop control of AO systems with two deformable mirrors in series: a large-stroke, low-order 'woofer' and a high-order, small-stroke 'tweeter'. The control must keep the system stable, prevent the mirrors from spending stroke cancelling each other, resolve actuator saturation stably, and keep each mirror on its assigned spatial-frequency range. The authors present a control architecture satisfying these criteria, derive analytic stability and performance results, and show simulations and on-sky tests with the ShaneAO system on the Lick 3-m telescope.
