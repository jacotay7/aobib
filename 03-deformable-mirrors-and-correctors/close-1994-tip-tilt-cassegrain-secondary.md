# High Resolution Imaging with a Tip-Tilt Cassegrain Secondary

**Close & McCarthy (1994)** · *PASP 106, 77* · [Free full text](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1994PASP..106...77C) · DOI: [10.1086/133346](https://doi.org/10.1086/133346)

## Summary

Presents a prototype tip-tilt Cassegrain secondary mirror on the Steward Observatory 2.3-m telescope that stabilizes atmospheric image motion. The position of the image centroid or brightest speckle is measured with a near-infrared camera and used to recenter the image with the tilting secondary, with closed-loop rates up to 100 Hz on guide stars of K <= 8. Residual image motion is reduced below 0.1 arcsec rms, removing over 90% of the low-frequency tilt power. With D/r0 about 4, long H-band exposures reached 0.19 arcsec FWHM (Strehl about 0.14), a factor of 4 sharper than uncorrected images, and the authors estimate a further factor of 2 in Strehl from reducing static optical errors.
