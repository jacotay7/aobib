# Characterisation of ALPAO deformable mirrors for the NAOMI VLTI Auxiliary Telescopes Adaptive Optics

**Le Bouquin et al. (2018)** · *Proc. SPIE 10703* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.10552) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2313442](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2313442)

## Summary

Describes the characterisation, through measurements and simulations, of the commercial ALPAO DM241 deformable mirrors used as correctors in NAOMI, the AO system for the VLTI Auxiliary Telescopes. Being part of an interferometer operating from visible to mid-infrared, the mirrors face demanding requirements on reliability, open-loop chopping, piston-free control, pupil rotation between WFS and DM, bandwidth and stroke. The paper summarises properties including creep and hysteresis and the operational scenario defined to handle them, concluding that the mirrors meet most requirements and that deviations can be handled. NAOMI is described as the first astronomical system commanded in true Zernike modes (allowing software rotation) and the first to chop with the deformable mirror.
