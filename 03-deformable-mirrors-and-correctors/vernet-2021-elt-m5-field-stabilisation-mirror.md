# ELT M5 — The Lightweight Field Stabilisation Mirror

**Vernet et al. (2021)** · *The Messenger 185, 3* · [Free full text](https://www.eso.org/sci/publications/messenger/archive/no.185-dec21/messenger-no185-3-6.pdf) · DOI: [10.18727/0722-6691/5244](https://doi.org/10.18727/0722-6691/5244)

## Summary

Describes M5, the field-stabilisation (tip-tilt) mirror of ESO's Extremely Large Telescope, which together with the adaptive M4 corrects telescope vibrations, wind shake and part of the atmospheric tip-tilt. M5 is a flat elliptical mirror of 2.2 x 2.7 m made of silicon carbide by Safran Reosc, assembled from six brazed segments with a CVD silicon cladding; the cell is built by SENER Aeroespacial. Its supports are mounted on a fast tip-tilt stage with a stroke of +/-0.5 mrad driven by three piezoelectric actuators. The article reviews the design, the manufacturing challenges and the status of segment production in 2021.
