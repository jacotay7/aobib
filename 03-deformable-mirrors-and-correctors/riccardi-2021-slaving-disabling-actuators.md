# Slaving and disabling actuators with voice-coil adaptive mirrors

**Riccardi (2021)** · *arXiv:2101.04801 (technical report)* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.04801)

## Summary

Technical report on how to handle disabled or slaved actuators in voice-coil adaptive mirrors, whose force actuators use internal capacitive metrology to close a local position loop. When actuators must be disabled or slaved, the control matrices of the mirror have to be recomputed; the report derives algorithms to do this without a new calibration. The method is relevant to the MMT, LBT, Magellan, VLT, ELT and GMT adaptive mirrors and has been used in practice on the LBT and VLT-UT4 adaptive secondary units.
