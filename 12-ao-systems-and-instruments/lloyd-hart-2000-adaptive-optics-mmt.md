# Adaptive optics for the 6.5 m MMT

**Lloyd-Hart et al. (2000)** · *Proc. SPIE 4007, 167* · [Free full text](https://home.strw.leidenuniv.nl/~kenworthy/papers/2000SPIE.4007..167L.pdf)

## Summary

The MMT adaptive optics system was designed to be the first to compensate atmospheric aberration at the telescope's secondary mirror, using a 336-actuator deformable secondary built around a thin, highly aspheric glass shell whose actuators include capacitive position sensors. The authors argue that this architecture gives optical simplicity, high throughput and low emissivity, which is particularly advantageous for thermal-infrared observations. The paper reports the construction status of the deformable secondary and of the Cassegrain wavefront-sensor package, and results of static and dynamic tests, including tracking of realistic turbulence with a sub-scale prototype.
