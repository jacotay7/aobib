# The LBT AO system on-sky results

**Esposito et al. (2011)** · *Proc. AO4ELT2 (Victoria, 2011), paper 090* · [Free full text](https://ao4elt2.lesia.obspm.fr/sites/ao4elt2/IMG/pdf/090esposito.pdf)

## Summary

The paper reports commissioning results of the first LBT natural guide star AO system (FLAO#1), carried out between May 2010 and June 2011. The system uses a 672-actuator adaptive secondary mirror and a modulated pyramid wavefront sensor with up to 30x30 subapertures. Measured performance includes H-band images with 40 mas FWHM, Strehl ratios above 90% and contrast near 1e-4 at 0.4 arcsec. Strehl ratio is given as a function of reference-star magnitude, and the achieved sky coverage is compared with that of laser guide star AO systems.
