# Ongoing and future AO projects at Subaru

**Ono et al. (2019)** · *Proc. AO4ELT6 (Québec, 2019)* · [Free full text](https://ao4elt6.copl.ulaval.ca/proceedings/401-8bP9-241.pdf)

## Summary

The paper summarises Subaru Telescope AO activities and plans for the following 5-10 years. AO188, operated since 2008 as the facility curvature SCAO system, is being upgraded with new real-time control software, a higher-power TOPTICA laser and a higher actuator-count deformable mirror, and a laser-tomography mode with four additional wavefront sensors behind AO188 is planned to improve visible correction. These upgrades also serve as technology validation for ULTIMATE-Subaru, a wide-field ground-layer AO system with four laser guide stars, four Shack-Hartmann sensors and an adaptive secondary mirror, whose conceptual design was then complete.
