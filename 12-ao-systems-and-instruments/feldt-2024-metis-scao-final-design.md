# High strehl and high contrast for the ELT instrument METIS - Final design, implementation, and predicted performance of the single-conjugate adaptive optics system

**Feldt et al. (2024)** · *Experimental Astronomy 58, 20* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.17341) · DOI: [10.1007/s10686-024-09968-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10686-024-09968-2)

## Summary

The paper presents the final design of the METIS single-conjugate AO system, a sequel to Hippler et al. (2018). It addresses the interaction with the telescope entities that take part in AO control, wavefront reconstruction with a fragmented and moving pupil, and secondary control tasks for differential image motion, non-common-path aberrations and mis-registration. Core components are a K-band pyramid wavefront sensor and a GPU-based real-time computer. Updated performance estimates, including contrast curves, are given, along with the main design decisions, analyses, and the testing and verification strategy following the METIS final design review in 2022.
