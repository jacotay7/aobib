# Image quality and high contrast improvements on VLT/NACO

**Girard et al. (2012)** · *Proc. SPIE 8447, 84470L* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.3090) · DOI: [10.1117/12.925660](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.925660)

## Summary

After ten years of operation of NACO at the VLT, a dedicated effort was made to understand and fix problems affecting its image quality and high-contrast performance. The paper reports experiments to measure and compensate non-common-path aberrations to recover the highest Strehl ratios, together with a plan to repeat these calibrations regularly. A pupil-tracking drift present since 2007 was identified and fixed in October 2011, improving suitability for angular differential imaging with focal-plane coronagraphic masks, and contrast measurements are presented. The authors note that the experience serves as a reference for later diffraction-limited VLT instruments such as SPHERE, VISIR and ERIS.
