# The MICADO first-light imager for the ELT: first steps of the SCAO system MAIT

**Clénet et al. (2024)** · *Proc. SPIE 13097, 1309728* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12814) · DOI: [10.1117/12.3018078](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3018078)

## Summary

MICADO, the ELT first-light imager, has a single-conjugate AO (SCAO) mode developed by the MICADO consortium and available at first light, alongside the MCAO mode developed by the MORFEO consortium. After the final design review, manufacturing of most SCAO optics and mechanics, the real-time computer software and the instrument control software began, ordered in steps so that a first full AO closed loop with final SCAO parts could be integrated progressively. The paper reports the first two steps, on the LESIA AO bench and in the "beta flat" configuration, and presents the status of manufacturing and first results.
