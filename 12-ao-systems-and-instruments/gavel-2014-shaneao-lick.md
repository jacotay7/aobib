# ShaneAO: wide science spectrum adaptive optics system for the Lick Observatory

**Gavel et al. (2014)** · *Proc. SPIE 9148* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.8207) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2055256](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2055256)

## Summary

ShaneAO is the high-order AO system that replaced the earlier Lick AO system on the Shane 3-m telescope, designed for diffraction-limited imaging and spectroscopy from 0.8 to 2.2 micron with a high-return-efficiency sodium beacon. It uses a woofer-tweeter pair of low- and high-order deformable mirrors with a control scheme giving full dynamic-range correction from tip-tilt to 16 cycles across the pupil, a wavefront sensor with variable pupil sampling, enhanced silver coatings developed at UC Observatories, and a mechanically rigid design for multi-hour LGS exposures. The paper presents design goals and first on-sky test results, positioning ShaneAO as a pathfinder for next-generation AO systems.
