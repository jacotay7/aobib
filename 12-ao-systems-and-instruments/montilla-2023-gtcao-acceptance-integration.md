# Laboratory Acceptance and Telescope Integration of the Gran Telescopio Canarias Adaptive Optics System

**Montilla et al. (2023)** · *Proc. AO4ELT7, Avignon* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-04419882) · DOI: [10.13009/AO4ELT7-2023-079](https://doi.org/10.13009/AO4ELT7-2023-079)

## Summary

Describes the final laboratory acceptance at the Instituto de Astrofísica de Canarias and the telescope integration of GTCAO, the single-conjugate AO system of the 10.4-m Gran Telescopio Canarias on La Palma. The system uses a 373-actuator deformable mirror conjugated to the pupil and a Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensor with 312 subapertures read by an OCAM2 camera; its requirement is 65% K-band Strehl ratio on a bright natural guide star in average conditions and at least 10% on a 14th-magnitude star. GTCAO feeds the FRIDA integral-field spectrograph, sends fast guiding and tip-tilt commands to the telescope M2, and offloads low-order modes to M1 and its segments. The bench was shipped to La Palma in June 2023 and installed on the Nasmyth B platform; the paper also outlines commissioning plans and a planned upgrade with a TOPTICA sodium laser guide star.
