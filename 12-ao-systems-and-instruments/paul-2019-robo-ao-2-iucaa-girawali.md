# Design and development of an adaptive optics system in visible and near-infrared for Inter-University Centre for Astronomy and Astrophysics 2-meter telescope

**Paul et al. (2019)** · *JATIS 5, 039002* · [Free full text](https://www.spiedigitallibrary.org/journals/Journal-of-Astronomical-Telescopes-Instruments-and-Systems/volume-5/issue-3/039002/Design-and-development-of-an-adaptive-optics-system-in-visible/10.1117/1.JATIS.5.3.039002.pdf) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.5.3.039002](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.5.3.039002)

## Summary

Describes a second Robo-AO system, working in the visible and near infrared, developed for the 2 m telescope at the Inter-University Centre for Astronomy and Astrophysics (IUCAA) Girawali Observatory in India. Robo-AO is an economical, robotic laser-guide-star AO design suited to 1–3 m telescopes. The paper presents the optomechanical design and development of the laser guide star facility and of the Cassegrain AO facility, with test results, and models the effects of different laser projection geometries. It also details an atmospheric dispersion corrector with its dispersion model and the control software, and ends with AO loop tests under laboratory-generated turbulence.
