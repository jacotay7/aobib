# First Light for the GRAVITY+ Adaptive Optics: Extreme Adaptive Optics for the Very Large Telescope Interferometer

**GRAVITY+ Collaboration et al. (2026)** · *A&A 707, A115* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.21431) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202555666](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202555666)

## Summary

Gravity Plus Adaptive Optics (GPAO) consists of four high-order AO systems equipping all 8-m Unit Telescopes of the VLT for wavefront correction of the VLTI instruments, with visible and infrared natural guide star modes and a laser guide star mode. The paper presents the design, operations and measured performance of GPAO. It illustrates the improvement with interferometric observations obtained during commissioning of the natural guide star mode at the end of 2024, including the first optical-interferometric observations of a redshift ~4 quasar, spectroscopy of a cool brown dwarf with K ~ 21, the first GRAVITY observations of a Class I young star, and sub-microarcsecond differential astrometry.
