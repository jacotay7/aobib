# ADONIS: A User-Friendly Adaptive Optics System for the ESO 3.6 Meter Telescope

**Beuzit et al. (1996)** · *ESO Conference and Workshop Proceedings 54, 57* · [Free full text](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1996ESOC...54...57B)

## Summary

ADONIS (ADaptive Optics Near Infrared System) was the upgrade of the COME-ON+ prototype at the ESO 3.6-m telescope at La Silla into a common-user instrument, and served as the precursor for VLT adaptive optics. The paper describes the main changes with respect to COME-ON+: a relocated F/45 imaging focus with a mechanical and optical interface for visitor instruments (coronagraphs, polarimeters, Fabry-Perot etalons), a new DSP-based real-time computer processing 7x7 Shack-Hartmann subapertures, and an artificial-intelligence supervisory system that sets and optimises system parameters such as the number of corrected modes and the modal control matrix. It also presents the two dedicated infrared cameras (including COMIC, 1-5 micron) and the observing procedures offered to the community. (Free scan of the ESO workshop proceedings via NASA ADS.)
