# MACAO-VLTI First Light: Adaptive Optics at the Service of Interferometry

**Arsenault et al. (2003)** · *The Messenger 112, 7* · [Free full text](https://www.eso.org/sci/publications/messenger/archive/no.112-jun03/messenger-no112-7-12.pdf)

## Summary

The Multiple Application Curvature Adaptive Optics (MACAO) programme was started by ESO in 1998 to build seven copies of a 60-element curvature AO system: four for the coudé foci of the VLT Unit Telescopes feeding the VLT Interferometer, one for SINFONI, one for CRIRES and a spare. This article describes the VLTI implementation, in which the coudé mirror M8 is replaced by a 60-element bimorph deformable mirror and a visible curvature wavefront sensor with avalanche photodiodes sits below the dichroic M9, with the aim of improving injection into the single-mode fibres of the interferometer. Design simulations predicted a K-band Strehl ratio of 0.58 on bright stars in 0.65 arcsec seeing and a limiting magnitude near V = 18. The first unit was installed on UT2 in April 2003 and first-light results are presented. A companion SPIE paper with the system design is listed as an alternative link.
