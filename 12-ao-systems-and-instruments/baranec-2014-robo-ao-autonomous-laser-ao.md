# High-efficiency Autonomous Laser Adaptive Optics

**Baranec et al. (2014)** · *ApJL 790, L8* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.8179) · DOI: [10.1088/2041-8205/790/1/L8](https://doi.org/10.1088/2041-8205/790/1/L8)

## Summary

Robo-AO is a fully autonomous laser adaptive optics and imaging system built for efficient follow-up of large surveys on 1-3 m telescopes. Using an ultraviolet Rayleigh laser guide star, it was commissioned on the Palomar 1.5-m telescope and routinely imaged more than 200 objects per night with visible-light resolution about ten times better than seeing-limited imaging. The letter describes the motivation, the robotic operation model, and the efficiency gains over conventional AO operation, and presents Robo-AO as a way to make AO imaging available for large numbers of targets.
