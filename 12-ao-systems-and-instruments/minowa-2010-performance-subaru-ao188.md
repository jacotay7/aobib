# Performance of Subaru adaptive optics system AO188

**Minowa et al. (2010)** · *Proc. SPIE 7736, 77363N* · [Free full text](https://www.naoj.hawaii.edu/Instruments/AO/references/SPIE2010_minowa_performance.pdf) · DOI: [10.1117/12.857818](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.857818)

## Summary

AO188 is the Subaru Telescope's second-generation facility AO system, a 188-element curvature-sensing system using photon-counting avalanche photodiodes and a 188-element bimorph deformable mirror at the infrared Nasmyth focus, operated in natural and sodium laser guide star modes. The paper reports natural guide star performance verified with the IRCS camera since October 2008: K-band Strehl ratios of 60-70% on R = 9 guide stars under normal seeing, operation down to R = 16.5, and an isoplanatic angle of about 30 arcsec measured in globular clusters. It also describes operating procedures and the sensitivity gain for faint-object detection. (Link is an observatory-hosted copy.)
