# The MAPS Adaptive Secondary Mirror: First Light, Laboratory Work, and Achievements

**Johnson et al. (2023)** · *Proc. SPIE 12693 (Unconventional Imaging, Sensing, and Adaptive Optics 2023), 126930F* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.14466) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2677896](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2677896)

## Summary

The MMT Adaptive optics exoPlanet characterization System (MAPS) is a comprehensive upgrade of the first-generation MMT AO system, aimed at a facility-class suite for imaging nearby exoplanets. This paper concerns the MAPS adaptive secondary mirror, which reuses legacy components of the original MMT ASM (reference body, thin shell, cold plate) but has newly designed actuators and electronics. The authors describe the design, laboratory preparation for on-sky engineering runs, results of those runs, issues discovered on sky, the follow-up laboratory investigations, and the mitigation steps taken. It is a record of practical lessons in operating and upgrading a large adaptive secondary.
