# The Adaptive Optics Facility: Commissioning Progress and Results

**Arsenault et al. (2017)** · *The Messenger 168, 8* · [Free full text](https://www.eso.org/sci/publications/messenger/archive/no.168-jun17/messenger-no168-8-14.pdf) · DOI: [10.18727/0722-6691/5019](https://doi.org/10.18727/0722-6691/5019)

## Summary

The Adaptive Optics Facility (AOF) turned VLT UT4 into an adaptive telescope by replacing its secondary with a 1172-actuator Deformable Secondary Mirror (DSM), adding the Four Laser Guide Star Facility (4LGSF), and building the GALACSI (for MUSE) and GRAAL (for HAWK-I) AO modules. This article reports commissioning status in 2017: the DSM was installed in October 2016 and the telescope returned to operations with it in non-AO mode, the 4LGSF was fully commissioned on sky (first light April 2016), and GALACSI saw first light in March 2017. It describes the test strategy, including a single-conjugate AO test mode with a 40x40 Shack-Hartmann sensor that exploits all DSM actuators and reached about 80% Strehl in K band, and the remaining steps toward ground-layer and laser-tomography operation.
