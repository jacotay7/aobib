# The Enhanced Resolution Imager and Spectrograph for the VLT

**Davies et al. (2023)** · *A&A 674, A207* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.02343) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202346559](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202346559)

## Summary

ERIS is the VLT instrument that replaced NACO and SINFONI, combining their functionality at the Cassegrain focus of UT4 and using the Adaptive Optics Facility deformable secondary mirror. It includes a new wavefront sensing module for natural and laser guide stars, an upgraded integral field spectrograph (SPIFFIER, 1-2.5 micron) and a new imager (NIX, 1-5 micron) with high-contrast options and 3-4 micron long-slit spectroscopy. The paper describes the instrument design, the AO system and its performance as measured during commissioning in 2022, after which ERIS was offered to the community.
