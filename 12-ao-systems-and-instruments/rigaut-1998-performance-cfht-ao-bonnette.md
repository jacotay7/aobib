# Performance of the Canada-France-Hawaii Telescope Adaptive Optics Bonnette

**Rigaut et al. (1998)** · *PASP 110, 152* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9712196) · DOI: [10.1086/316126](https://doi.org/10.1086/316126)

## Summary

PUEO is the curvature-sensing AO bonnette of the 3.6-m Canada-France-Hawaii Telescope. The paper presents results from its commissioning, based on more than 750 images recorded with a CCD and a near-infrared camera over 16 nights from B to H band. Performance is reported as delivered Strehl ratio, FWHM and gain, and the properties of the corrected images are related to the structure function of the compensated phase. Under median seeing PUEO gives essentially diffraction-limited images in H and K, FWHM of about 0.1 arcsec in J and I, and gains down to B with guide stars as faint as R = 14 (R = 17 in good conditions). Automatic, continuous optimisation of the modal gains during observations contributes to high operational efficiency.
