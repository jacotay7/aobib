# First Observational Results from ALFA with Natural and Laser Guide Stars

**Davies et al. (1998)** · *ESO Conf. Proc. 56 (Astronomy with Adaptive Optics, Sonthofen, 1998), 153* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9810157)

## Summary

ALFA is the sodium laser guide star AO system built by MPE and MPIA for the Calar Alto 3.5-m telescope. The paper reports results from its optimisation phase: with natural guide stars brighter than V ~ 8, K-band Strehl ratios above 60% are reached and binaries are resolved at the diffraction limit, while more typical use gives 10-15% Strehl over a wide (~2 arcmin) field with a limiting magnitude near V = 12.5. First diffraction-limited spectroscopy separates binaries 0.26 arcsec apart. With the laser as the high-order reference and a tip-tilt star 41 arcsec away, the image of the galaxy UGC 1347 improved from 1.07 to 0.40 arcsec FWHM, and remaining laser-related difficulties are discussed.
