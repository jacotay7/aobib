# Infrared wavefront sensing for adaptive optics assisted galactic center observations with the VLT interferometer and GRAVITY: operation and results

**Hippler et al. (2020)** · *Instruments 4, 20* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08414) · DOI: [10.3390/instruments4030020](https://doi.org/10.3390/instruments4030020)

## Summary

The Coudé Infrared Adaptive Optics (CIAO) systems are four near-infrared wavefront-sensing AO units at the coudé foci of the VLT Unit Telescopes that feed the GRAVITY beam combiner, enabling in particular observations of the Galactic Centre where only red or embedded guide stars are available. Each unit delivers near-diffraction-limited images so that the maximum stellar flux is injected into the GRAVITY fibres. The paper describes how CIAO is configured and operated for GRAVITY observations and presents performance data from the first three years of operation as a function of atmospheric conditions. It also details the properties of the SAPHIRA avalanche-photodiode array, used for the first time on Paranal, as a wavefront-sensor detector.
