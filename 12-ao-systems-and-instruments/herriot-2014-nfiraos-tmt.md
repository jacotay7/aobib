# NFIRAOS: First Facility AO System for the Thirty Meter Telescope

**Herriot et al. (2014)** · *Proc. SPIE 9148 (Adaptive Optics Systems IV), 914810* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.4406) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2055525](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2055525)

## Summary

NFIRAOS is the first-light AO system of the Thirty Meter Telescope: an order 60x60 multi-conjugate AO system with two deformable mirrors, normally using six sodium laser guide stars, with an NGS-only mode. It is cooled to -30 C to reduce thermal background and delivers a 2-arcmin corrected beam to up to three client instruments, relying on up to three infrared tip-tilt/focus wavefront sensors inside each instrument. The paper presents design work including automated acquisition on the IR sensors, trade studies for a common deformable-mirror size, real-time computing architectures, simplified high-order NGS wavefront sensing, and modest upgrade concepts for high-contrast imaging.
