# Astronomical Science with Adaptive Optics at the W. M. Keck Observatory

**Wizinowich (2013)** · *PASP 125, 798* · [Free full text](https://www2.keck.hawaii.edu/optics/aodocs/KAON1000aoSciencePASP.pdf) · DOI: [10.1086/671425](https://doi.org/10.1086/671425)

## Summary

The paper reviews the first dozen years of science with the Keck AO systems. The first refereed papers using Keck II natural guide star and laser guide star AO data appeared in January 2000 and May 2005, and by the end of 2012 there were 260 refereed papers based on NGS AO and 152 based on LGS AO data. The author analyses this productivity by science area and instrument, summarises the performance and limitations of the Keck AO systems (including the Keck I LGS upgrade that began science in 2012), describes developments then under way, and draws lessons on how new capabilities, especially LGS AO, broadened the user community.
