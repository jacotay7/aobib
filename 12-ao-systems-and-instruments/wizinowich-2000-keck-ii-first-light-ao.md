# First Light Adaptive Optics Images from the Keck II Telescope: A New Era of High Angular Resolution Imagery

**Wizinowich et al. (2000)** · *PASP 112, 315* · [Free full text](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1086/316543/pdf) · DOI: [10.1086/316543](https://doi.org/10.1086/316543)

## Summary

Reports the first results from the natural-guide-star adaptive optics system on the 10 m Keck II telescope, the first AO system to come on line on an 8–10 m class telescope. The authors place the system in the context of earlier natural-guide-star AO on 3–4 m telescopes and describe its initial performance. They report images with resolutions of 0.022 arcsec at 0.85 μm and 0.040 arcsec at 1.65 μm, which they describe as the highest angular resolution then obtained with a single telescope, along with performance tests on three astronomical targets.
