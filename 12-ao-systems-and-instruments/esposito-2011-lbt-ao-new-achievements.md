# Large Binocular Telescope Adaptive Optics System: New achievements and perspectives in adaptive optics

**Esposito et al. (2011)** · *Proc. SPIE 8149, 814902* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1203.2761) · DOI: [10.1117/12.898641](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.898641)

## Summary

The LBT first-light AO (FLAO) system combines a 672-actuator adaptive secondary mirror with a high-order modulated pyramid wavefront sensor. During on-sky commissioning it delivered H-band images with 40 mas resolution, Strehl ratios above 80% and contrast of order 1e-4. Using these results, the authors compare the performance and sky coverage of a natural guide star system built with state-of-the-art components with those of existing laser guide star systems on 8-10 m telescopes. They argue that the comparison suggests reconsidering the respective roles of NGS and LGS AO for 8-10 m telescopes and for ELTs.
