# The Final Design of NFIRAOS

**Andersen et al. (2019)** · *Proc. AO4ELT6 (Québec, 2019)* · [Free full text](https://ao4elt6.copl.ulaval.ca/proceedings/401-mqo6-251.pdf)

## Summary

The paper presents the final design of NFIRAOS, the first-light facility MCAO system of the Thirty Meter Telescope, developed by NRC Herzberg with TMT providing the laser guide star facility, AO executive software, wavefront-sensor detectors and deformable mirrors. The design passed its final design review in June 2018. NFIRAOS is designed to give diffraction-limited performance in J, H and K over a 34-arcsec field with 50% sky coverage at the Galactic Pole, enabled by the laser guide stars, MCAO and on-instrument near-infrared tip/tilt/focus sensors. The authors describe the subsystems and key performance budgets and design decisions.
