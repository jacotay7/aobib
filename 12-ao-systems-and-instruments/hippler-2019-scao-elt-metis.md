# Single conjugate adaptive optics for the ELT instrument METIS

**Hippler et al. (2019)** · *Experimental Astronomy 47, 65* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.07578) · DOI: [10.1007/s10686-018-9609-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10686-018-9609-y)

## Summary

METIS, the mid-infrared imager and spectrograph for the ELT, relies on a single-conjugate AO system that drives the telescope's M4 and M5 units, with demanding requirements from high-contrast exoplanet imaging. The paper presents simulations to select the optimal wavefront sensor: sky and target-sample coverage for generic visible and near-infrared sensors favour the near-infrared, and a comparison of Shack-Hartmann and pyramid sensors under realistic ELT conditions leads to the choice of a near-infrared pyramid. The impact of non-common-path aberrations, vibrations and long-term stability on SCAO and high-contrast performance is also analysed.
