# Altair at Gemini North: Full Sky Coverage Laser AO Correction at Visible Wavelengths

**Trujillo et al. (2013)** · *Proc. AO4ELT3, Florence, 13288* · [Free full text](http://ao4elt3.arcetri.inaf.it/proceedings/fulltext_13288.pdf) · DOI: [10.12839/AO4ELT3.13288](https://doi.org/10.12839/AO4ELT3.13288)

## Summary

Altair is the facility single-conjugate AO system of the Gemini North 8-m telescope, operated with natural guide stars and, since its laser guide star upgrade, with a sodium LGS. This paper describes two upgrades aimed at low-order correction over the whole sky. In the LGS + P1 "super seeing" mode, high-order aberrations are corrected on an on-axis laser guide star while tip-tilt is measured on a distant star with the telescope's peripheral wavefront sensor (P1), allowing operation anywhere between declinations +70 and -30 degrees; it was offered for science in semester 2013A with an expected image-quality gain of a factor 2-3 over seeing. The second upgrade feeds the visible GMOS spectrograph through a sodium-notch dichroic, with tip-tilt sensed by the GMOS on-instrument wavefront sensor or P1, for an expected factor of about 2 improvement at visible wavelengths.
