# Adaptive Optics at Optical Wavelengths: Test Observations of Kyoto 3DII Connected to Subaru Telescope AO188

**Matsubayashi et al. (2016)** · *PASP 128, 095003* · [Free full text](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/1538-3873/128/967/095003/pdf) · DOI: [10.1088/1538-3873/128/967/095003](https://doi.org/10.1088/1538-3873/128/967/095003)

## Summary

Reports test observations combining the Kyoto Tridimensional Spectrograph II (Kyoto 3DII), an integral-field spectrograph, with Subaru Telescope's second-generation AO system AO188, to make AO-assisted observations at optical wavelengths, where reaching a given Strehl ratio is much harder than in the near infrared. The authors built a new beam splitter that reflects light below 594 nm to the AO188 wavefront sensors and transmits light above 644 nm to Kyoto 3DII, and a mount for the spectrograph at the Nasmyth focus. In the tests the combined system gave higher spatial resolution than natural seeing even at 6500 Å, with a FWHM of 0.12 arcsec for an undersampled bright guide star.
