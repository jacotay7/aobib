# High-Resolution Images of Orbital Motion in the Trapezium Cluster: First Scientific Results from the Multiple Mirror Telescope Deformable Secondary Mirror Adaptive Optics System

**Close et al. (2003)** · *ApJ 599, 537* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0309003) · DOI: [10.1086/379150](https://doi.org/10.1086/379150)

## Summary

This paper presents the first scientific images obtained with a deformable-secondary-mirror AO system, the 6.5-m MMT AO system. H-band images of the young Orion Trapezium (theta1 Ori) cluster reach 0.07 arcsec FWHM, and stellar positions are measured to about 3 mas. Combined with earlier speckle data over a six-year baseline, the observations detect orbital motion of about 2 mas/yr in the tight theta1 Ori B2B3 and A1A2 systems. The authors discuss the dynamics of the theta1 Ori B group and the possible future ejection of its lowest-mass member. The paper documents early on-sky performance of the adaptive-secondary concept later adopted at LBT, Magellan and the VLT.
