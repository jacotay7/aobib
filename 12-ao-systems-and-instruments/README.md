# 12 · AO Systems, Instruments & History

This folder collects papers that describe adaptive optics *systems as built*: the first astronomical demonstrations, the facility single-conjugate AO systems on 3–10 m telescopes, robotic and low-cost systems on small telescopes, AO for long-baseline interferometry, and the AO architectures being built for the extremely large telescopes (ELT, TMT, GMT). Typical entries are first-light and commissioning reports, design overviews, on-sky performance characterisations, upgrade and lessons-learned papers, and overviews of the science enabled by AO facilities.

The history thread runs from Babcock's 1953 proposal through the COME-ON prototype (the first diffraction-limited astronomical images in 1989–1990) and the early curvature systems, to the 8–10 m facility era (Keck, VLT, Subaru, Gemini, LBT, MMT, Magellan). Technology-specific work is kept in sibling folders: extreme-AO and high-contrast instruments (SPHERE, GPI, SCExAO, PALM-3000, MagAO-X) live in 07, wide-field systems (MCAO/GLAO/LTAO/MOAO such as GeMS, MAD, MORFEO) in 05, laser-guide-star physics and LGS facilities in 06, and solar AO in 13. Many of those are cross-listed here.
