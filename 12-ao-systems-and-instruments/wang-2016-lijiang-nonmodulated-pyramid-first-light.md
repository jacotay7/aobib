# First light on an adaptive optics system using a non-modulation pyramid wavefront sensor for a 1.8 m telescope

**Wang et al. (2016)** · *Chinese Optics Letters 14, 100101* · [Free full text](https://www.researching.cn/ArticlePdf/m00005/2016/14/10/COL201614100101.pdf) · DOI: [10.3788/COL201614.100101](https://doi.org/10.3788/COL201614.100101)

## Summary

Reports first light of an adaptive optics system using a non-modulated pyramid wavefront sensor, integrated on the 1.8 m telescope of Yunnan Observatory at Lijiang. The letter briefly describes the structure and performance of the system and presents high-resolution images of stars. The authors report that the angular resolution approaches the diffraction limit of the 1.8 m telescope.
