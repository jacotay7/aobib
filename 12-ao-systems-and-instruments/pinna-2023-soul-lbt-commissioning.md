# SOUL at LBT: commissioning results, science and future

**Pinna et al. (2023)** · *Proc. AO4ELT7 (Avignon, 2023)* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.14447) · DOI: [10.13009/AO4ELT7-2023-082](https://doi.org/10.13009/AO4ELT7-2023-082)

## Summary

The Single conjugated adaptive Optics Upgrade for LBT (SOUL) upgraded the four LBT pyramid/adaptive-secondary SCAO systems with EMCCD detectors and improved control, and is presented as a precursor for ELT SCAO systems that combine the same technologies. After first light of the first upgraded system in September 2018, all four systems were working on sky by 2023. The paper reports control improvements and performance measured during commissioning: up to 500 corrected modes on bright stars and increased sky coverage, with K-band Strehl above 20% for reference stars with G_RP < 17, opening extragalactic targets to NGS AO. It also reviews first science results and future instruments fed by SOUL (SHARK-NIR, SHARK-VIS, iLocater).
