# AO3k at Subaru: First on-sky results of the facility extreme-AO

**Lozi et al. (2024)** · *Proc. SPIE 13097, 1309703* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.19188) · DOI: [10.1117/12.3019294](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3019294)

## Summary

The Subaru Telescope facility AO system AO188 was upgraded into AO3k (AO3000): a 64x64 (3224-actuator) ALPAO deformable mirror replacing the 188-element bimorph, a GPU-based real-time computer, a visible non-linear curvature wavefront sensor and a near-infrared wavefront sensor, with loop rates up to 2 kHz. The aim is extreme-AO-level correction for all instruments on the infrared Nasmyth platform (IRCS, IRD and SCExAO), as well as laser tomography for high Strehl with large sky coverage. The paper reports the first on-sky results obtained in 2023-2024 before the system was offered to observers, and discusses open questions for ELT-era systems such as the on-sky behaviour of high-actuator-count DMs, near-infrared wavefront sensing, rolling-shutter and persistence effects. AO3k is also positioned as a technology demonstrator for the Planetary Systems Imager on TMT.
