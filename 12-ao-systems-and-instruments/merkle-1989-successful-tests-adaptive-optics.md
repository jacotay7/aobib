# Successful Tests of Adaptive Optics

**Merkle et al. (1989)** · *The Messenger 58, 1* · [Free full text](https://www.eso.org/sci/publications/messenger/archive/no.58-dec89/messenger-no58.pdf)

## Summary

This ESO Messenger article announces the first successful tests of the VLT adaptive optics prototype "COME-ON", developed by ESO, Observatoire de Paris, ONERA and LASERDOT, at the coudé focus of the 1.52-m telescope at Observatoire de Haute-Provence on 12-23 October 1989. It explains for a general astronomical audience why atmospheric turbulence limits resolution and how a feedback loop with a wavefront sensor and a deformable mirror can compensate it. Corrected and uncorrected near-infrared images of bright stars and binaries are shown, demonstrating diffraction-limited imaging. The article also outlines the planned move of the system to the ESO 3.6-m telescope at La Silla and its role as a precursor for the VLT.
