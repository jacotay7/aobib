# The GRAVITY Coudé Infrared Adaptive Optics (CIAO) system for the VLT Interferometer

**Kendrew et al. (2012)** · *Proc. SPIE 8446* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.2945) · DOI: [10.1117/12.926558](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.926558)

## Summary

GRAVITY, the second-generation VLTI beam combiner, requires AO correction at each of the four Unit Telescopes, including in fields such as the Galactic Centre where optically bright guide stars are lacking. The GRAVITY project therefore developed four infrared Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensors (CIAO) working at 1.4-2.4 micron, together with a near-infrared-optimised real-time control system, that drive the existing MACAO deformable mirrors at the coudé foci. This paper presents the wavefront sensor design and an overview of expected AO performance. Using the newly developed SELEX/ESO SAPHIRA electron avalanche photodiode arrays, the systems were expected to reach residual wavefront errors of 400 nm or less at 500 Hz.
