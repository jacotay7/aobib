# Status of HARMONI SCAO system at mid-Final Design

**Sauvage et al. (2019)** · *Proc. AO4ELT6 (Québec, 2019)* · [Free full text](https://ao4elt6.copl.ulaval.ca/proceedings/401-RtAD-251.pdf)

## Summary

The paper describes the HARMONI single-conjugate AO (SCAO) system at an advanced design stage. SCAO uses a fast, sensitive visible pyramid wavefront sensor to drive the ELT's adaptive quaternary mirror M4 and field-stabilisation mirror M5. The authors present expected on-sky performance under varying guide-star magnitude, seeing and object extension, and tolerancing effects such as pyramid defects and chromatic residuals. Calibration and operation schemes are described, with attention to the interactions with the ELT telescope and its adaptive mirrors.
