# KAPAO: a MEMS-based natural guide star adaptive optics system

**Severson et al. (2013)** · *Proc. SPIE 8617, 861709* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.7280) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2005959](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2005959)

## Summary

KAPAO is a low-cost, remotely accessible natural guide star AO system for the Pomona College Table Mountain Observatory 1-m telescope, developed largely by undergraduates from Pomona College, Harvey Mudd College and Sonoma State University. It uses a commercial 140-actuator Boston Micromachines MEMS deformable mirror and a version of the Robo-AO control software. The project proceeded with a prototype built from off-the-shelf optics with a single visible camera (KAPAO-Alpha), in parallel with a final design using custom optics and dual-band visible/near-infrared imaging (KAPAO-Prime). The paper presents the prototype design and on-sky closed-loop tests, and plans for the final system.
