# Status and new operation modes of the versatile VLT/NACO

**Girard et al. (2010)** · *Proc. SPIE 7736, 77362N* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1009.1256) · DOI: [10.1117/12.856799](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.856799)

## Summary

The paper gives an update on NACO, then the most versatile AO-fed instrument at the VLT, about two years before its planned retirement. It describes new operation modes and experiments: a new visible wavefront sensor optimised for laser guide star operation, the increasingly used cube mode for frame selection on bright sources, and a seeing-enhancer mode without tip-tilt correction offering full sky coverage for extragalactic programmes. It also describes how the high-contrast community used NACO as a test bench for SPHERE and other second-generation planet imagers, and the work of the instrument operations team to maintain and improve performance.
