# Keck All Sky Precision Adaptive Optics

**Wizinowich et al. (2019)** · *Proc. AO4ELT6 (Québec, 2019)* · [Free full text](https://ao4elt6.copl.ulaval.ca/proceedings/401-fDmd-211.pdf)

## Summary

The paper presents the status and plans of the Keck All sky Precision Adaptive optics (KAPA) program, funded by the NSF Mid-Scale Innovation Program from 2018. KAPA comprises four key science projects (including strong-lensing cosmology, the Galactic Center, and galaxy kinematics and metallicity), an upgrade of the Keck I LGS AO facility, AO-telemetry-based PSF estimates for all science exposures, and an educational component. The focus is the facility upgrade: a new laser, wavefront sensor and real-time controller supporting laser tomography with a four-LGS asterism, and modifications to the near-infrared tip-tilt sensor for multiple natural guide star and focus measurements, all presented as pathfinders for ELTs.
