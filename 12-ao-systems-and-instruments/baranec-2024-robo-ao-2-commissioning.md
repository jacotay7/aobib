# Commissioning results from the Robo-AO-2 facility for rapid visible and near-infrared AO imaging

**Baranec et al. (2024)** · *Proc. SPIE 13097 (Adaptive Optics Systems IX), 130970G* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.00582) · DOI: [10.1117/12.3019346](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3019346)

## Summary

Robo-AO-2 is a next-generation automated laser AO system installed on the University of Hawaii 2.2-m telescope on Maunakea in 2023, designed to deliver robotic diffraction-limited visible and near-infrared imaging for large numbers of targets. It uses upgraded components, manufacturing techniques and control relative to the Robo-AO prototype, and includes a parallel reconfigurable natural guide star wavefront sensor for exploring hybrid laser/natural-star sensing. The paper presents commissioning results from 2023 and 2024.
