# Commissioning status of Subaru laser guide star adaptive optics system

**Hayano et al. (2010)** · *Proc. SPIE 7736, 77360N* · [Free full text](https://subarutelescope.org/Instruments/AO/references/SPIE2010_hayano_ao188.pdf) · DOI: [10.1117/12.857567](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.857567)

## Summary

The paper reports the commissioning status of the Subaru laser guide star AO system built around AO188. After first light with natural guide stars in a limited configuration in October 2006, the full NGS configuration went on sky in August 2008 and opened to users a month later, routinely giving K-band Strehl ratios of about 0.6-0.7 on R ~ 9-10 stars. An unexpectedly large wavefront error in the laser launch telescope was found and corrected, after which a round laser guide star of 1.2-1.6 arcsec was obtained. The authors outline the remaining commissioning steps toward LGS science operation in 2011. (Link is an observatory-hosted copy.)
