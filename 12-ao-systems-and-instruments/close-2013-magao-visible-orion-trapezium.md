# Diffraction-limited Visible Light Images of Orion Trapezium Cluster With the Magellan Adaptive Secondary AO System (MagAO)

**Close et al. (2013)** · *ApJ 774, 94* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1308.4155) · DOI: [10.1088/0004-637X/774/2/94](https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/774/2/94)

## Summary

The paper reports early visible-light results of the Magellan AO system (MagAO), which controls 250-378 modes with a 585-actuator adaptive secondary and a pyramid sensor, using the VisAO CCD camera. In 0.5-0.7 arcsec seeing, individual short exposures reach 19 mas resolution, while 60-s r'-band exposures give 23-29 mas FWHM and about 28% Strehl on bright guide stars, limited partly by telescope vibrations. Images of the Orion Trapezium resolve the 32 mas binary theta1 Ori C1/C2 in non-interferometric imaging, and relative astrometry at 0.6-5 mas precision is used to study orbital motion in the theta1 Ori B mini-cluster.
