# Adaptive optics for array telescopes using piston-and-tilt wave-front sensing

**Wizinowich et al. (1992)** · *Applied Optics 31, 6036* · [Free full text](https://lss.fnal.gov/archive/other/steward-1040.pdf) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.31.006036](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.31.006036)

## Summary

Reports a near-infrared adaptive optics system, running at about 50 Hz, that controlled the phase difference between two mirrors of the original Multiple Mirror Telescope by stabilizing the position of the interference fringe in their combined, unresolved far-field image. The integrated images reached angular resolutions better than 0.1 arcsec with fringe contrasts above 0.6. Measurements of wavefront tilt confirmed that image motion is independent of wavelength. The authors conclude that interferometric sensing of phase errors, combined with tilt sensing on each telescope, can give a stable diffraction-limited focus for segmented telescopes or telescope arrays. The linked copy is the Steward Observatory preprint, scanned in the Fermilab library's preprint archive.
