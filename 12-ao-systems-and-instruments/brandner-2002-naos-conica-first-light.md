# NAOS+CONICA at YEPUN: First VLT Adaptive Optics System Sees First Light

**Brandner et al. (2002)** · *The Messenger 107, 1* · [Free full text](https://www.eso.org/sci/publications/messenger/archive/no.107-mar02/messenger-no107-1-6.pdf)

## Summary

This article reports the first light of NAOS+CONICA (NACO), the first adaptive optics system of the VLT, on 25 November 2001 at UT4. It summarises the history of AO at ESO (COME-ON, COME-ON+, ADONIS) and the development of the Nasmyth AO system NAOS by ONERA, Observatoire de Paris and LAOG, and of the 1-5 micron camera CONICA by MPIA and MPE. NAOS uses a tip-tilt mirror, a 185-actuator deformable mirror and two Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensors, one in the visible (0.45-0.95 micron) and one in the near-infrared (0.8-2.5 micron), the latter allowing correction on red or embedded guide sources. Commissioning results include on-axis K-band Strehl ratios up to about 50% on a V = 12 star, and first science images such as the resolved T Tauri South binary. As general references for NACO, the article asks users to cite the CONICA and NAOS design papers (Lenzen et al. 1998; Rousset et al. 2000).
