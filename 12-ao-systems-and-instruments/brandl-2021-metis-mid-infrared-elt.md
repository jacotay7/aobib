# METIS: The Mid-infrared ELT Imager and Spectrograph

**Brandl et al. (2021)** · *The Messenger 182, 22* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.11208) · DOI: [10.18727/0722-6691/5218](https://doi.org/10.18727/0722-6691/5218)

## Summary

This article describes METIS, the ELT first-generation instrument covering the thermal and mid-infrared (3-13 micron). Its single-conjugate AO system enables high-contrast imaging and R ~ 100,000 integral-field spectroscopy at the diffraction limit of the ELT. The article presents the science drivers (notably exoplanets and protoplanetary disks), the conceptual design, observing modes and expected performance.
