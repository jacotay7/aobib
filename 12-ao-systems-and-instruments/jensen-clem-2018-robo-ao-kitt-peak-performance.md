# The Performance of the Robo-AO Laser Guide Star Adaptive Optics System at the Kitt Peak 2.1-m Telescope

**Jensen-Clem et al. (2018)** · *AJ 155, 32* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.08867) · DOI: [10.3847/1538-3881/aa9be6](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-3881/aa9be6)

## Summary

After moving from Palomar, Robo-AO operated every clear night at the Kitt Peak 2.1-m telescope as the first dedicated AO observatory. This paper characterises imaging performance over its first eighteen months: for median seeing of 1.44 arcsec the average Strehl ratio is 4% in i'. After post-processing, contrast under sub-arcsecond seeing reaches five and seven magnitudes at 0.5 and 1.0 arcsec for primaries with 2 <= i' <= 16. The automated reduction pipeline, with shift-and-add stages optimised for high- and low-SNR targets and a high-contrast stage for faint companions, is described.
