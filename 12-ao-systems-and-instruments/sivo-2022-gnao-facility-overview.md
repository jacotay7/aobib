# Gemini North Adaptive Optics (GNAO) facility overview and status updates

**Sivo et al. (2022)** · *Proc. SPIE 12185 (Adaptive Optics Systems VIII), 1218536* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.14519) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2629479](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2629479)

## Summary

The Gemini North Adaptive Optics (GNAO) facility is the next AO system for Gemini North, intended for survey and time-domain science in the JWST and Rubin era and optimised to feed the GIRMOS multi-object integral-field spectrograph (and any f/32 instrument). GNAO includes a new laser guide star facility with four side-launched beams, supporting a wide-field mode that improves image quality over a 2-arcmin field (with additional MOAO correction inside GIRMOS) and a narrow-field mode with near-diffraction-limited correction over 20x20 arcsec. The project also comprises a new real-time controller, facility system controller and AO bench. The paper gives an overview and the status of each product.
