# MagAO: Status and on-sky performance of the Magellan adaptive optics system

**Morzinski et al. (2014)** · *Proc. SPIE 9148* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.5098) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2057048](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2057048)

## Summary

MagAO is the adaptive optics system of the 6.5-m Magellan Clay telescope, derived from the LBT systems. It uses a modulated pyramid wavefront sensor binnable from 28x28 to 7x7 subapertures, a 585-actuator adaptive secondary mirror, up to 378 controlled modes at up to 1 kHz, and natural guide stars from R = 0 to 16. Two science cameras, VisAO (0.5-1 micron) and Clio2 (1-5 micron), observe simultaneously. The paper describes the instrument, its installation in 2012 and commissioning runs in 2012-2013, the first community science run in 2014, and the measured on-sky performance.
