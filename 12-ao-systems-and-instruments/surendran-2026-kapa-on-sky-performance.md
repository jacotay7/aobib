# On-sky capabilities and performance of the Keck All Sky Precision Adaptive optics system

**Surendran et al. (2026)** · *Proc. SPIE 14150 (Adaptive Optics Systems X)* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07769) · DOI: [10.1117/12.3105896](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3105896)

## Summary

The Keck All Sky Precision Adaptive optics (KAPA) project upgraded the Keck I AO system to laser tomography with a four-laser-guide-star asterism. The paper describes the overall architecture, the development of the tomographic reconstruction algorithm with pseudo open-loop control, its real-time implementation, and preliminary on-sky results. KAPA operates in a narrow-field mode optimised on-axis and a wide-field mode optimised over the camera field. Comparison of on-sky image quality with single-LGS operation shows a reduction of wavefront error with tomography, which the authors present as a pathfinder for ELT-class laser tomography.
