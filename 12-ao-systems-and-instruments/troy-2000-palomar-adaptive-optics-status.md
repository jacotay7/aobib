# Palomar adaptive optics project: status and performance

**Troy et al. (2000)** · *Proc. SPIE 4007, 31* · [Free full text](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/2mvcj-8jt74) · DOI: [10.1117/12.390302](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.390302)

## Summary

This paper describes the status and on-sky performance of PALAO, the JPL-built facility adaptive optics system at the Cassegrain focus of the Palomar 200-inch (5 m) Hale telescope, which achieved its first high-order lock (241 actuators) on a natural guide star in December 1998. In K band the system reached Strehl ratios up to 50% in 1.0 arcsecond seeing. Performance predictions based on real-time wavefront-sensor telemetry and on a fitted Kolmogorov atmospheric model are shown to agree with the measured science-image performance. The authors present predictions for different seeing conditions and an error budget identifying which subsystems limit performance.
