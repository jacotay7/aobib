# First Light of SINFONI at the VLT

**Bonnet et al. (2004)** · *The Messenger 117, 17* · [Free full text](https://www.eso.org/sci/publications/messenger/archive/no.117-sep04/messenger-no117-17-24.pdf)

## Summary

SINFONI combines an ESO-built adaptive optics module, derived from the MACAO curvature systems, with the MPE near-infrared integral field spectrograph SPIFFI, and was commissioned at the Cassegrain focus of VLT UT4 in 2004. The AO module uses a 60-element curvature wavefront sensor read by avalanche photodiodes and a 60-actuator bimorph deformable mirror updated at 420 Hz, and was designed for later laser guide star operation. The article describes the instrument, its observing modes and the stand-alone AO-module commissioning with an infrared test camera, where a K-band Strehl of 56% was measured at first light and Strehl versus guide-star magnitude and coherence time was characterised. Optimum performance is obtained with stars brighter than R = 12 within about 20 arcsec, with partial correction over the field for stars to R = 17.
