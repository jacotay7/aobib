# Status of MagAO and review of astronomical science with visible light adaptive optics

**Close et al. (2018)** · *Proc. SPIE 10703 (Adaptive Optics Systems VI), 107030L* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.05070) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2313107](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2313107)

## Summary

The paper reviews astronomical results obtained in the visible (below 1 micron) with adaptive optics, noting a rapid increase in visible-AO refereed papers after 2013, and gives the status of MagAO. MagAO combines a 585-actuator adaptive secondary with a 1 kHz pyramid sensor; with bright stars in 0.5 arcsec seeing, 60-s exposures reach under 30 mas resolution and about 30% Strehl at 0.62 micron. The authors summarise the factors needed for good visible performance, the upgrade of the VisAO simultaneous differential imager used for H-alpha protoplanet searches, and the science enabled at 10-30 mas resolution.
