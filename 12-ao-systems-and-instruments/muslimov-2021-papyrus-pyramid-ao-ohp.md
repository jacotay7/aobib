# Current status of PAPYRUS: the pyramid based adaptive optics system at LAM/OHP

**Muslimov et al. (2021)** · *Proc. SPIE 11876, 118760H* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.10263) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2597170](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2597170)

## Summary

PAPYRUS (Provence Adaptive optics Pyramid Run System) is a pyramid-based AO system for the coudé focus of the 1.52-m telescope at Observatoire de Haute-Provence, built largely by PhD students and postdocs from existing hardware. It is intended as an on-sky testbed for pyramid wavefront sensing and control algorithms, benefiting from the many nights available on a small telescope, and as a teaching tool for an AO summer school. Key components are a 17x17-actuator ALPAO deformable mirror with ALPAO RTC, an OCAM2K low-noise camera and a four-faced glass pyramid. The paper gives an overview, the opto-mechanical design and the project status.
