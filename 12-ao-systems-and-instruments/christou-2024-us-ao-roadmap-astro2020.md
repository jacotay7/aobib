# US Adaptive Optics Roadmap to Achieve Astro2020

**Christou et al. (2024)** · *Community white paper (arXiv:2402.06743)* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.06743)

## Summary

This community document responds to the Astro2020 decadal survey, which identified adaptive optics as a crucial technology for high-contrast imaging, for the US ELT projects (GMT and TMT, for which AO is the most important technical risk), for upgrades of existing 4-10 m telescopes, and for visible-wavelength AO. Prepared after a workshop in May 2023, it sets out suggested AO research and development priorities for funding agencies (NSF, NASA, DoE) to meet the decadal science and technical objectives, covering both stand-alone ground-based AO and AO in support of space missions. Appendices provide context on existing US AO facilities and programmes.
