# First light of the 6.5-m MMT adaptive optics system

**Wildi et al. (2003)** · *Proc. SPIE 5169 (Astronomical Adaptive Optics Systems and Applications), 17* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/230851580_First_light_of_the_65-m_MMT_adaptive_optics_system) · DOI: [10.1117/12.507687](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.507687)

## Summary

Reviews the first year of operation of the MMT adaptive optics system, the first to use an adaptive secondary mirror, which saw first light in November 2002. By the time of writing it had been used for more than 30 nights with several science cameras, reaching Strehl ratios up to 20% in H band and 98% in M band, partly limited by a control algorithm that was still being improved. The deformable secondary had needed removal from its hub only once for a malfunction. The paper describes the milestones, the operational constraints of an adaptive secondary and the steps taken to ease them, and argues that such a system can be run with modest effort.
