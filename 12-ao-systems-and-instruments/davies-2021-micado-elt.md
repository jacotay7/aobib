# MICADO: The Multi-Adaptive Optics Camera for Deep Observations

**Davies et al. (2021)** · *The Messenger 182, 17* · [Free full text](https://www.eso.org/sci/publications/messenger/archive/no.182-mar21/messenger-no182-17-21.pdf) · DOI: [10.18727/0722-6691/5217](https://doi.org/10.18727/0722-6691/5217)

## Summary

MICADO is the ELT first-light near-infrared imager, designed to image a field of nearly 1 arcmin at the diffraction limit with imaging, coronagraphy and slit spectroscopy. It is optimised to work with the laser-guide-star MCAO system of the MAORY/MORFEO consortium, and also has its own single-conjugate AO mode using one natural guide star; in an initial phase it will operate with SCAO only. The article outlines the observing modes and illustrates each with an astrophysical application, such as resolved stellar populations, high-redshift galaxies and the Galactic Centre.
