# HARMONI: the ELT's First-Light Near-infrared and Visible Integral Field Spectrograph

**Thatte et al. (2021)** · *The Messenger 182, 7* · [Free full text](https://www.eso.org/sci/publications/messenger/archive/no.182-mar21/messenger-no182-7-12.pdf) · DOI: [10.18727/0722-6691/5215](https://doi.org/10.18727/0722-6691/5215)

## Summary

This article presents HARMONI, the visible and near-infrared AO-assisted integral field spectrograph for ESO's ELT. HARMONI has a single-conjugate AO mode using a bright natural guide star and a laser tomographic AO mode using multiple laser guide stars, giving near-diffraction-limited (about 10 mas) hyper-spectral imaging over most of the sky, plus a high-contrast AO mode for exoplanet characterisation. The article summarises the instrument's spaxel scales, gratings (R ~ 3000-17000) and detectors, the roles of the different AO flavours, and representative science cases.
