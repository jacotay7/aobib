# The Giant Magellan Telescope adaptive optics program

**Bouchez et al. (2014)** · *Proc. SPIE 9148 (Adaptive Optics Systems IV), 91480W* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/277676670_The_Giant_Magellan_Telescope_Adaptive_Optics_Program) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2057613](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2057613)

## Summary

Describes the preliminary design of the adaptive optics system of the 25.4 m Giant Magellan Telescope, which is integral to the telescope and serves every planned instrument. Three first-generation modes all use a segmented adaptive secondary mirror: a natural-guide-star mode giving extreme-AO performance (under 185 nm rms total wavefront error on bright stars), a laser-tomography mode with six lasers and one off-axis natural star (better than 290 nm rms over 50% of the sky at the galactic pole), and a ground-layer mode using four natural stars around the field. A phasing system keeps primary and secondary segments aligned using edge sensors and feedback from an off-axis guide star. The paper gives predicted performance and the remaining technical challenges before construction.
