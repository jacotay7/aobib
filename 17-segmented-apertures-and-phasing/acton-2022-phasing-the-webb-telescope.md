# Phasing the Webb Telescope

**Acton et al. (2022)** · *Proc. SPIE 12180, 121800U* · [Free full text](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20230011349) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2633474](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2633474)

## Summary

Summarizes how the JWST wavefront sensing and control (WFSC) process was applied during commissioning to bring the 18 primary-mirror segments (7 degrees of freedom each) and the secondary mirror (6 degrees of freedom) from their post-deployment positions, accurate only to about 1 mm, to a diffraction-limited telescope. The sequence included segment image identification and stacking, global alignment, coarse phasing using the NIRCam dispersed Hartmann sensors, fine phasing using phase retrieval on images defocused with NIRCam weak lenses, and multi-instrument multi-field alignment. Several steps were repeated with different goals as commissioning progressed. The paper documents the on-orbit execution and results of each step.
