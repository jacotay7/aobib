# Wavefront sensing and controls for the James Webb Space Telescope

**Acton et al. (2012)** · *Proc. SPIE 8442 (Space Telescopes and Instrumentation 2012), 84422H* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/258718428_Wavefront_Sensing_and_Controls_for_the_James_Webb_Space_Telescope) · DOI: [10.1117/12.925015](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.925015)

## Summary

Describes the processes, algorithms and software developed to align the James Webb Space Telescope after deployment. JWST's secondary mirror has 6 degrees of freedom and each of its 18 primary-mirror segments has 7; after deployment these elements are placed only to within a few millimetres, leaving large wavefront errors. The wavefront sensing and control process uses images from the science instruments during commissioning to bring the telescope to diffraction-limited performance at 2 μm across the full science field. Results from flight-like end-to-end simulations of commissioning are presented.
