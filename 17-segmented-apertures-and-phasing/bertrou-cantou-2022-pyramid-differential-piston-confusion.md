# Confusion in differential piston measurement with the pyramid wavefront sensor

**Bertrou-Cantou et al. (2022)** · *A&A 658, A49* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-03551824) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202141632](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202141632)

## Summary

Investigates how the modulated pyramid wavefront sensor responds to wavefront discontinuities across the ELT spider arms in the presence of post-AO phase residuals, for a range of expected observing conditions. Using COMPASS, the authors derive sensitivity-loss and modal cross-talk coefficients and run closed-loop simulations with wavefront error criteria adapted to a fragmented pupil. They find a drastic loss of sensitivity to differential piston and non-linear modal cross-talk, which together with the limited capture range make a visible pyramid poorly suited to measuring phase discontinuities. Strategies such as optical-gain compensation, specific modulation paths and different numbers of pyramid faces help at low D/r0 (e.g. a K-band pyramid), but not for visible sensing with large residuals. The authors conclude that ELT instruments with only visible pyramids must rely on turbulence statistics for continuity, and that any other source of discontinuity would need a dedicated sensor.
