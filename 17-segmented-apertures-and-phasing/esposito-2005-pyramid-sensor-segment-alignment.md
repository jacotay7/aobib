# Pyramid sensor for segmented mirror alignment

**Esposito et al. (2005)** · *Optics Letters 30, 2572* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/7556462_Pyramid_sensor_for_segmented_mirror_alignment) · DOI: [10.1364/OL.30.002572](https://doi.org/10.1364/OL.30.002572)

## Summary

Reports what the authors describe as the first laboratory experiment using a pyramid wavefront sensor to cophase and align a segmented mirror with three degrees of freedom per segment: piston, tip and tilt. An iterative alignment procedure reached a residual wavefront error of about 10 nm, spread evenly between piston, tip and tilt. The results show that a pyramid sensor can measure all three simultaneously, which the authors present as attractive for phasing extremely large segmented telescopes.
