# Phasing segmented mirrors: a modification of the Keck narrow-band technique and its application to extremely large telescopes

**Schumacher et al. (2002)** · *Applied Optics 41, 1297* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/11463890_Phasing_segmented_mirrors_a_modification_of_the_Keck_narrow-band_technique_and_its_application_to_extremely_large_telescopes) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.41.001297](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.41.001297)

## Summary

Studies whether the narrow-band phasing technique used routinely at Keck, a Shack-Hartmann variant that correlates sub-images with simulated images, can phase extremely large telescopes with hundreds or thousands of segments. The authors develop a new algorithm in which each sub-image gives a piston-dependent value on its own, an alternative way to resolve the wavelength ambiguity that also flags problem cases, and a weighted singular-value-decomposition procedure for phasing the whole mirror. Simulations show the modified technique performs better and is precise enough for telescopes as large as 100 m.
