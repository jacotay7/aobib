# Adapting the pyramid wavefront sensor for pupil fragmentation of the ELT class telescopes

**Levraud et al. (2022)** · *Proc. SPIE 12185 (Adaptive Optics Systems VIII), 1218559* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.08822) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2629861](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2629861)

## Summary

Analyzes the spatial structure of petal modes, the differential pistons between pupil fragments of ELT-class telescopes, and how this affects their measurement by a pyramid wavefront sensor. A variant of the pyramid concept is proposed to increase sensitivity to these modes. The authors show that a single wavefront sensor cannot accurately measure turbulence and petal modes simultaneously. They propose a double-path scheme in which a second sensor dedicated to petal modes is fed with spatially filtered light to suppress residual turbulence, allowing both turbulence and petal modes to be measured and corrected.
