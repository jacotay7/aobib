# Simultaneous operation of a controllable segmented primary mirror and single conjugate adaptive optics system part 1: design concept and sensitivity analysis

**Calvin & Fitzgerald (2025)** · *JATIS 11, 019003* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.02026) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.11.1.019003](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.11.1.019003)

## Summary

Notes that speckle-based measurements of the Keck primary indicate semi-static segment surface aberrations of about 65 nm rms that the existing phasing control system does not sense. The authors propose treating the primary as a Controllable Segmented Primary (CSP) that is sensed and controlled directly through the AO system. They develop a method to separate the independently controllable CSP signal from the rest of the AO system and estimate the achievable measurement precision using models of the Keck II AO system. A companion part 2 paper reports simulated operation.
