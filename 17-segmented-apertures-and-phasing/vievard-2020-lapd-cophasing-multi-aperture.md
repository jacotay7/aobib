# Cophasing multiple aperture telescopes with linearized analytic phase diversity

**Vievard et al. (2020)** · *JATIS 6, 040501* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.10696) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.6.4.040501](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.6.4.040501)

## Summary

Introduces LAPD (Linearized Analytic Phase Diversity), a fast linearized phase-diversity algorithm for focal-plane cophasing of multiple-aperture telescopes. Phase diversity works with any aperture configuration or source extension but is usually computationally heavy; LAPD aims to reduce this load while keeping a capture range comparable to classic phase diversity. The algorithm is characterized and validated experimentally, reaching a typical performance of λ/75 rms wavefront error.
