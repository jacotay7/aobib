# On-sky multiwavelength phasing of segmented telescopes with the Zernike phase contrast sensor

**Vigan et al. (2011)** · *Applied Optics 50, 2708* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1104.3859) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.50.002708](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.50.002708)

## Summary

Reports on-sky results of the Zernike phase contrast phasing sensor (ZEUS) tested at the Very Large Telescope within the Active Phasing Experiment. A multi-wavelength scheme is implemented to extend the capture range beyond the ±λ/2 ambiguity of a single-wavelength measurement to many micrometers. The sensor successfully phased mirrors with piston errors up to ±4.0 µm on the wavefront. Results are discussed at several levels and a phasing strategy for a future Extremely Large Telescope is proposed.
