# Spectral interferometric wavefront sensing: a solution for petalometry at Subaru/SCExAO

**Deo et al. (2024)** · *Proc. SPIE 13097 (Adaptive Optics Systems IX)* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.05246) · DOI: [10.1117/12.3019419](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3019419)

## Summary

Pursues dedicated low-order wavefront sensors, or petalometers, to complement the main AO system in measuring differential piston, tip and tilt of pupil fragments created by the telescope spider. Interferometric devices measure such differential aberrations with high sensitivity but only as wrapped phases. The authors show that combining multiple spectral channels extends the capture range for petaling aberrations beyond several microns, enough to resolve one-wave wrapping errors made by the main AO. They propose implementing a petalometer using the multi-wavelength imaging mode of the VAMPIRES instrument on SCExAO, reconstructing differential piston from four spectral channels through a 7-hole non-redundant mask.
