# Optical measurements of phase steps in segmented mirrors - fundamental precision limits

**Noethe & Adorf (2007)** · *Journal of Modern Optics 54, 3* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0604056) · DOI: [10.1080/09500340600842252](https://doi.org/10.1080/09500340600842252)

## Summary

Uses Fisher information and the Cramér-Rao bound to derive the fundamental precision with which the height of a phase step between mirror segments can be estimated from a limited number of photons. Four sensor types are compared: a Shack-Hartmann sensor with a small cylindrical lenslet centred on a segment border, a modified Mach-Zehnder interferometer, a Foucault test and a curvature sensor. Bounds are computed under ideal narrowband, photon-noise-limited conditions and compared with the device-independent quantum statistical limit. For the Shack-Hartmann case the effects of broadband light, finite sampling and atmospheric seeing are also examined.
