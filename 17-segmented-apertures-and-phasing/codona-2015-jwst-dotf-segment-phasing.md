# James Webb Space Telescope segment phasing using differential optical transfer functions

**Codona & Doble (2015)** · *JATIS 1, 029001* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.08004) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.1.2.029001](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.1.2.029001)

## Summary

Proposes using the differential optical transfer function (dOTF), a non-iterative image-based wavefront sensing method that needs two star images differing by one small pupil change, for JWST segment phasing. Two ways of introducing the pupil modification are described: a small (<λ/4) displacement of a single segment actuator and small misalignments of NIRCam's filter wheel. The actuator method would also allow MIRI and NIRISS to be used for phasing, enable frequent alignment monitoring, and help calibrate non-common-path aberrations between cameras. Large segment discontinuities can be measured with dOTFs in two filter bands, and the dOTF's pupil amplitude and phase estimate could seed the planned iterative phase retrieval.
