# Sensing and control of segmented mirrors with a pyramid wavefront sensor in the presence of spiders

**Schwartz et al. (2018)** · *Proc. AO4ELT5 (Tenerife, 2017)* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.08839) · DOI: [10.26698/AO4ELT5.0015](https://doi.org/10.26698/AO4ELT5.0015)

## Summary

Studies the island/petal problem on the ELT, where the spiders and the segmented M4 isolate areas of the pupil on the wavefront sensor. Because the pyramid WFS is poorly sensitive to differential piston, closed-loop differential pistons settle near integer multiples of the sensing wavelength, typically one to ten times it, and vary rapidly. Three mitigation approaches are compared in end-to-end simulations: using only pyramid information (for example with reduced modulation), phase closure of the DM commands assuming a continuous correction over the pupil, and pair-wise slaving of edge actuators across the spiders. Pair-wise slaving performs best, adding only 20-45 nm rms in quadrature for 0.45-0.85 arcsec seeing.
