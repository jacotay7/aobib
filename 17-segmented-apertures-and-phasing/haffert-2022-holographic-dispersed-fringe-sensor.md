# Phasing the Giant Magellan Telescope with the holographic dispersed fringe sensor

**Haffert et al. (2022)** · *JATIS 8, 021513* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.03615) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.8.2.021513](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.8.2.021513)

## Summary

Introduces the Holographic Dispersed Fringe Sensor (HDFS), a single pupil-plane hologram that interferes light from the segments onto distinct focal-plane locations to sense piston and petal modes on fragmented apertures such as GMT, ELT and TMT. Such modes are poorly sensed by Shack-Hartmann and pyramid wavefront sensors because the gaps exceed the Fried parameter. Simulations indicate differential piston below 10 nm rms for guide stars up to about 13th magnitude in J+H. Laboratory tests with MagAO-X and the GMT phasing testbed reached 5 nm rms on the Magellan aperture and 50 nm rms on a GMT-like segmented aperture while the pyramid sensor compensated simulated median-seeing turbulence. The arXiv preprint is titled "The Holographic Dispersed Fringe Sensors (HDFS): phasing the Giant Magellan Telescope".
