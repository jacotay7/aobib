# Sparse aperture differential piston measurements using the pyramid wave-front sensor

**Arcidiacono et al. (2016)** · *Proc. SPIE 9909, 99096K* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.00390) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2232374](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2232374)

## Summary

Reports a laboratory experiment at Shanghai Astronomical Observatory to test the ability of the pyramid wavefront sensor to measure differential piston on a sparse aperture. The longer-term aim is closed-loop phasing of the primary mirrors of a sparse Fizeau imaging telescope with a pyramid WFS. Tests covered flattening the wavefront with a deformable mirror and measuring the differential-piston signal on a two-pupil configuration. The experiment also characterized achromatic double pyramids manufactured in the SHAO optical workshop.
