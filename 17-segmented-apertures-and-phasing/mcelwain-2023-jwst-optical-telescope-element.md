# The James Webb Space Telescope Mission: Optical Telescope Element Design, Development, and Performance

**McElwain et al. (2023)** · *PASP 135, 058001* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.01779) · DOI: [10.1088/1538-3873/acada0](https://doi.org/10.1088/1538-3873/acada0)

## Summary

Gives an overview of the JWST Optical Telescope Element: requirements, architecture, development history, on-orbit performance and lessons learned. The 6.6 m primary consists of 18 controllable, low areal density hexagonal segments that were aligned and phased in orbit using image-based wavefront sensing and control algorithms. The paper describes how the passively cooled, deployable segmented telescope was developed over more than two decades and how it performs in orbit. The authors present JWST as a demonstration of segmented-aperture space telescopes and a path toward larger ones.
