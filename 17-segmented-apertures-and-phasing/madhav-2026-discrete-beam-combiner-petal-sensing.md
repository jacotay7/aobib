# Sensing ELT petal modes with a discrete beam combiner: an end-to-end theoretical model

**Madhav et al. (2026)** · *arXiv:2609.13575 (preprint)* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13575)

## Summary

Develops a theoretical model of a single-mode photonic discrete beam combiner (DBC) configured to sense the six petal pistons of the ELT pupil, which is split into petals by six 0.5 m wide spider arms. The model covers the coupled-mode description of a 2D evanescently coupled waveguide lattice with six inputs and 45 outputs, its visibility-to-pixel matrix, the pupil remapping of one sub-aperture per petal, and a five-dimensional petal-mode basis. Optimizing input spacing and interaction length to minimize the matrix condition number gives a well-conditioned device (condition number 10.4). The modeled sensor recovers petal pistons with unit linear gain over the full ±λ/2 (±800 nm) unambiguous range with cross-talk below 1e-3.
