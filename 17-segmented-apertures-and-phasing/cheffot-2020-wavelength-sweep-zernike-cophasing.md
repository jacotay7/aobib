# Measuring the cophasing state of a segmented mirror with a wavelength sweep and a Zernike phase contrast sensor

**Cheffot et al. (2020)** · *Optics Express 28, 12566* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.06603) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.390576](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.390576)

## Summary

Evaluates a coarse-phasing technique in which a Zernike phase contrast sensor is combined with a wavelength sweep to obtain a large capture range without moving individual segments. Because no segment modulation is required, the technique could monitor the phasing state of a segmented telescope in parallel with science observations. Tests on a dedicated bench achieve 112 nm rms precision. Simulations are used to explain a known error of the method and to suggest improvements.
