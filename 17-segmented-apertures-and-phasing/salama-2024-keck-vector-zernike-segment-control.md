# Keck Primary Mirror Closed-loop Segment Control Using a Vector-Zernike Wavefront Sensor

**Salama et al. (2024)** · *ApJ 967, 171* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.08728) · DOI: [10.3847/1538-4357/ad3b99](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/ad3b99)

## Summary

Reports the first on-sky closed-loop control of segmented primary-mirror pistons using a Zernike wavefront sensor, installed on the AO-corrected beam of Keck II. The vector-ZWFS uses a metasurface focal-plane mask that applies two different phase shifts to orthogonal polarizations, producing two pupil images and extending the dynamic range relative to a scalar ZWFS. The sensor is intended to monitor and correct segment co-phasing errors in parallel with science observations. Closed-loop segment piston control improved the Strehl ratio on the NIRC2 science camera by up to 10 percentage points, and the paper analyzes the loop performance and its effect on science data.
