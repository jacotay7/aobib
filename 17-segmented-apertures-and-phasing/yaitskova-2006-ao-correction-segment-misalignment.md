# Adaptive Optics Correction of the Wavefront Distortions Induced by Segments Misalignment in Extremely Large Telescope

**Yaitskova & Vérinaud (2006)** · *Proc. SPIE 6267, 62672N* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0606401) · DOI: [10.1117/12.670195](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.670195)

## Summary

Analyzes how well an adaptive optics system can correct wavefront errors caused by misaligned segments of an extremely large telescope primary, using the residual wavefront rms and the phase power spectral density. An analytical model and end-to-end simulations give qualitatively equal results. Both show that the geometric matching between the segmentation pattern and the actuator/subaperture layout of the AO system matters. The authors also show that the design of the wavefront sensor is critical.
