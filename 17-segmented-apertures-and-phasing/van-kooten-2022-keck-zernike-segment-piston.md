# On-sky Reconstruction of Keck Primary Mirror Piston Offsets Using a Zernike Wavefront Sensor

**van Kooten et al. (2022)** · *ApJ 932, 109* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.02241) · DOI: [10.3847/1538-4357/ac6ba2](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/ac6ba2)

## Summary

Presents the first on-sky results of an adaptive-optics-fed Zernike wavefront sensor (ZWFS) on a segmented-aperture telescope, operating in H band inside the Keck Planet Imager and Characterizer (KPIC) on Keck II. Primary-mirror segments were pistoned by known amounts and the resulting wavefront was measured both with the ZWFS and with a modified Gerchberg-Saxton phase retrieval on defocused NIRC2 images. For three segments pistoned by 400 nm of optical path difference the two methods give 408 ± 23 nm and 394 ± 46 nm respectively. Measurements were limited to about 100 nm OPD by insufficient averaging of AO residuals, and simulations are used to explain a systematic offset in the ZWFS reconstructions.
