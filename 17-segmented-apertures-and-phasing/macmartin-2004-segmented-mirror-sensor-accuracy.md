# Measurement accuracy in control of segmented-mirror telescopes

**MacMartin & Chanan (2004)** · *Applied Optics 43, 608* · [Free full text](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/9t70q-d6x22) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.43.000608](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.43.000608)

## Summary

Estimates the contribution of sensor noise to errors in controlling the out-of-plane degrees of freedom of highly segmented primary mirrors. Mechanical edge-sensor measurements of relative segment motion are compared with optical wavefront information, using data from the Keck telescopes to obtain realistic edge-sensor noise levels. The authors conclude that mechanical sensors will be more accurate than wavefront information for the telescope concepts then under consideration, so supplemental wavefront sensors are not required for real-time figure control. They also find that control errors from sensor noise will not significantly degrade seeing-limited or diffraction-limited observations.
