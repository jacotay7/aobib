# Redundant apodization for direct imaging of exoplanets. II. Application to island effects

**Leboulleux et al. (2022)** · *A&A 666, A91* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.00295) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202243413](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202243413)

## Summary

Proposes making coronagraphs robust to island-effect aberrations, the differential pistons, tips and tilts on pupil petals caused by the low-wind effect or AO petaling, instead of only sensing and correcting them. Following a method first developed for primary-mirror segmentation errors, Redundant Apodized Pupils (RAP) are designed at the petal scale and then duplicated and rotated to match the pupil's petal geometry. Applied to the six-petal ELT pupil, the designs reach 1e-6 contrast in a dark region from 8 to 40 λ/D. Both amplitude and phase apodizers are robust to differential piston between petals with minimal degradation of coronagraphic PSF and contrast, and are more robust to petal-level tip-tilt than apodizers designed for the whole pupil.
