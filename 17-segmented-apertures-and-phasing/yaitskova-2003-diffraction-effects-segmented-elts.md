# Analytical study of diffraction effects in extremely large segmented telescopes

**Yaitskova et al. (2003)** · *JOSA A 20, 1563* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/10597567_Analytical_study_of_diffraction_effects_in_extremely_large_segmented_telescopes) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.20.001563](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.20.001563)

## Summary

Analyses the diffraction effects of a segmented aperture with a very large number of segments, as planned for extremely large telescopes, using an analytical calculation of the PSF for Keck-type hexagonal segmentation. The paper focuses on effects that produce speckles or a regular pattern of diffraction peaks: random piston and tip-tilt errors on each segment, gaps between segments, and segment edge distortion. For each case it gives formulas and typical values for the Strehl ratio, the relative intensity of higher-order diffraction peaks and the average speckle intensity.
