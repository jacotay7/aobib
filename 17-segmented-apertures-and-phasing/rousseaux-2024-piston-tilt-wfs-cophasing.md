# A piston and tilt wavefront sensor dedicated to the cophasing of segmented optics in low light level conditions

**Rousseaux et al. (2024)** · *Optics and Lasers in Engineering 181, 108412* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-04652184) · DOI: [10.1016/j.optlaseng.2024.108412](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.optlaseng.2024.108412)

## Summary

Presents an interferometer that measures piston, tip and tilt of segmented mirrors by combining an optical grating with a microlens array. Because it collects all the incident light and works over a broad spectral band, the device is suited to low-light-level operation, for example cophasing segmented primaries such as that of JWST. Tests on a 37-segment deformable mirror reached an accuracy better than λ/100. The measurement is self-referenced, and closure relations allow measurement errors to be estimated from the data themselves.
