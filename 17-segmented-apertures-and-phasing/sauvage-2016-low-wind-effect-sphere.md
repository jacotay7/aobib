# Tackling down the low wind effect on SPHERE instrument

**Sauvage et al. (2016)** · *Proc. SPIE 9909, 990916* · [Free full text](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/qjm8c-q3050) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2232459](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2232459)

## Summary

After more than a year of SPHERE operations at the VLT, this paper analyses two unexpected limitations of its extreme-AO system SAXO. The first is spatial: under some low-wind conditions, wavefront aberrations that do not follow Kolmogorov statistics appear as phase discontinuities across the telescope spiders (the low wind effect, or island effect) and are poorly seen and corrected by the AO system, degrading the PSF. The second is temporal: high-amplitude vibrations on low-order modes caused by the telescope environment and the AO behaviour. For each, the authors develop and test on sky a mitigation (including a new operating procedure for the low wind effect) and assess its performance, and they discuss the implications for the E-ELT.
