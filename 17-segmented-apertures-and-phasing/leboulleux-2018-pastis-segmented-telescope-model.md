# Pair-based Analytical model for Segmented Telescopes Imaging from Space for sensitivity analysis

**Leboulleux et al. (2018)** · *JATIS 4, 035002* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.00870) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.4.3.035002](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.4.3.035002)

## Summary

Introduces PASTIS (Pair-based Analytical model for Segmented Telescopes Imaging from Space), an analytical model linking segment-level aberrations of a segmented primary to coronagraphic contrast, for building error budgets on segment alignment and stability. The model is validated against end-to-end simulations and found accurate enough at the exo-Earth detection level. A fast error-budget method is developed that accounts for the different impact of each segment on final performance. It is applied to a specific aperture to derive static and quasi-static requirements on local segment aberrations, and to identify the dominant modes for a given coronagraph geometry.
