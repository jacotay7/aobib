# A strategy for sensing the petal mode in the presence of AO residual turbulence with the pyramid wavefront sensor

**Levraud et al. (2024)** · *A&A 682, A84* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.07570) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202346929](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202346929)

## Summary

Studies why the pyramid wavefront sensor cannot measure the petal mode, the differential piston between pupil fragments separated by ELT spiders that would reduce ELT resolution to that of a 15 m telescope, and how to recover the measurement. Using a simplified pupil, the authors consider a second sensing path separated from the turbulence-correcting loop. An unmodulated pyramid is sensitive to petal modes but inaccurate in the presence of atmospheric residuals. Based on the petal mode's power spectral density, they add a pinhole spatial filter around the pyramid tip that reduces the first-path residuals seen by the unmodulated pyramid and restores its accuracy, and demonstrate the system with end-to-end simulations.
