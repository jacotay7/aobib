# Advanced wavefront reconstruction methods for segmented Extremely Large Telescope pupils using pyramid sensors

**Hutterer et al. (2018)** · *JATIS 4, 049005* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.4.4.049005) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.4.4.049005](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.4.4.049005)

## Summary

The thick secondary-mirror supports (spiders) of extremely large telescopes fragment the pupil seen by the wavefront sensor, so that phase information is disconnected across the spider shadows and data are lost on some subapertures; the result is differential pistons between pupil segments that many existing reconstructors cannot handle. The authors propose a split approach that reconstructs the segment piston modes separately from the rest of the wavefront, and introduce two methods for directly reconstructing the segment pistons from pyramid-sensor data. Because the pistons are treated separately, reconstruction algorithms developed for non-segmented pupils can then be used for the remaining wavefront. End-to-end simulations for the ELT first-light instrument METIS show accurate, stable and fast reconstruction. The article is published open access under a CC BY 4.0 licence.
