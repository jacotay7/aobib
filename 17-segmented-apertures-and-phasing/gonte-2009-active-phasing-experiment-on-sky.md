# On-sky Testing of the Active Phasing Experiment

**Gonté et al. (2009)** · *The Messenger 136, 25* · [Free full text](https://www.eso.org/sci/publications/messenger/archive/no.136-jun09/messenger-no136-25-31.pdf)

## Summary

Describes ESO's Active Phasing Experiment (APE), built to gain experience in controlling segmented primary mirrors in preparation for the European Extremely Large Telescope. APE placed an active segmented mirror conjugated to the VLT pupil and compared four optical phasing sensors: a curvature sensor, a pyramid sensor, a Shack-Hartmann sensor and a sensor based on a modified Mach-Zehnder interferometer. The article describes the instrument design and its installation at the VLT visitor focus for on-sky tests, and summarizes the results obtained with the different phasing techniques.
