# Control and Alignment of Segmented-Mirror Telescopes: Matrices, Modes, and Error Propagation

**Chanan et al. (2004)** · *Applied Optics 43, 1223* · [Free full text](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/hvqvw-y7j63) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.43.001223](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.43.001223)

## Summary

Constructs and analyzes the control matrix of the active control system for the primary mirror of a generalized segmented-mirror telescope, starting from the Keck design and scaling to up to 1000 segments. An alternative edge-sensor geometry to the one used at Keck is also considered. The paper examines how sensor noise propagates through the matrix into mirror modes and the consequences for both seeing-limited and diffraction-limited observations. The related problem of optically aligning such a primary mirror is analyzed in terms of the distinct but related matrices that govern it.
