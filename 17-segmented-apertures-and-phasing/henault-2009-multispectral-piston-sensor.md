# Multi-spectral piston sensor for co-phasing giant segmented mirrors and multi-aperture interferometric arrays

**Hénault (2009)** · *Journal of Optics A 11, 125503* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/0908.4040) · DOI: [10.1088/1464-4258/11/12/125503](https://doi.org/10.1088/1464-4258/11/12/125503)

## Summary

Presents the optical design and theory of a multi-spectral piston sensor intended for co-phasing giant segmented mirrors of future Extremely Large Telescopes and multi-aperture interferometric arrays. Numerical simulations indicate that direct piston and tip-tilt measurements are feasible with accuracies close to 20 nm and 10 nanoradians respectively, compatible with co-phasing requirements. The method appears to be perturbed by uncorrected atmospheric seeing.
