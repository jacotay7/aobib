# The Giant Magellan Telescope Phasing System: algorithms and performance simulations

**Quirós-Pacheco et al. (2017)** · *Proc. AO4ELT5 (Tenerife, 2017)* · [Free full text](https://meetings.iac.es/AO4ELT5/media/proceedings/proceeding-145.pdf) · DOI: [10.26698/AO4ELT5.0145](https://doi.org/10.26698/AO4ELT5.0145)

## Summary

Describes the phasing strategy for the 25.4 m Giant Magellan Telescope, whose seven 8.4 m primary segments are matched by a segmented Gregorian adaptive secondary, so M1-M2 segment pairs must be phased to a small fraction of the wavelength in diffraction-limited modes. The first part presents a revised phasing and active-optics control strategy that combines several wavefront-sensing channels of the Acquisition, Guiding and Wavefront Sensing (AGWS) system to keep the field phased. The second part evaluates the effect of wind buffeting on phasing performance in laser tomography AO mode, where edge sensors on M1 and M2 sense segment piston disturbances and drive fast compensation with the adaptive secondary.
