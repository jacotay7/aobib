# Controlling petals using fringes: discontinuous wavefront sensing through sparse aperture interferometry at Subaru/SCExAO

**Deo et al. (2022)** · *Proc. SPIE 12185, 285* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.02898) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2629865](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2629865)

## Summary

Demonstrates re-purposing non-redundant sparse aperture masking interferometers as low-order wavefront sensors for petaling, complementing the high-order pyramid WFS on SCExAO at Subaru. Low-wind and petaling effects split the PSF core into several lobes and are poorly corrected by common wavefront sensors. Far-field interferograms from a 7-hole mask are used to directly retrieve petaling aberrations that are almost invisible to the main AO loop. A visible dual-band mode with two disjoint 25 nm channels overcomes the one-wavelength ambiguity of fringe tracking without interfering with near-infrared coronagraphy, and on-sky engineering results show petal measurement well beyond the range of a single-wavelength design.
