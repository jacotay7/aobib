# First lab results of segment/petal phasing with a pyramid wavefront sensor and a novel holographic dispersed fringe sensor (HDFS) from the Giant Magellan Telescope high contrast adaptive optics phasing testbed

**Hedglen et al. (2022)** · *Proc. SPIE 12185 (Adaptive Optics Systems VIII), 1218516* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.03614) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2629538](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2629538)

## Summary

Presents the GMT High-Contrast Adaptive Optics phasing Testbed (p-HCAT), which uses the MagAO-X instrument to test segment phase sensing together with AO control for GMT natural-guide-star AO, where seven 8.4 m segments separated by gaps larger than 30 cm must be co-phased. First closed-loop piston-control results with one GMT segment are reported. The pyramid WFS alone failed to control segment piston in simulated 0.6 and 1.2 arcsec seeing, attributed to non-linear modal cross-talk and poor pixel sampling of the segment gaps. Using the HDFS as a second-channel piston sensor while the pyramid controlled all other modes held piston to within 50 nm rms with a ±10 µm dynamic range in simulated 0.6 and 1.2 arcsec seeing. The arXiv version is titled "The Giant Magellan Telescope high contrast adaptive optics phasing testbed (p-HCAT): lab tests of segment/petal phasing with a pyramid wavefront sensor and a holographic dispersed fringe sensor (HDFS) in turbulence".
