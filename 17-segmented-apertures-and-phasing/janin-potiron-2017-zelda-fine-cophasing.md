# Fine cophasing of segmented aperture telescopes with ZELDA, a Zernike wavefront sensor in the diffraction-limited regime

**Janin-Potiron et al. (2017)** · *A&A 603, A23* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.05501) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201730686](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201730686)

## Summary

Adapts ZELDA, a Zernike wavefront sensor developed for measuring residual aberrations in exoplanet imagers, to fine cophasing of segmented apertures with diffraction-limited images. The earlier ZEUS concept was designed for seeing-limited operation and is not suited to this final phasing step. The paper introduces an analysis scheme using per-segment piston, tip and tilt estimators and gives probabilistic predictions of closed-loop convergence as a function of the initial wavefront error. Simulations show that the sensor retrieves segment piston, tip and tilt simultaneously and unambiguously, supporting its use for nanometric segment alignment, including for exoplanet imaging.
