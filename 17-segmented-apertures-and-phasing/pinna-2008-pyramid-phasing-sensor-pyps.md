# The Pyramid Phasing Sensor (PYPS)

**Pinna et al. (2008)** · *Proc. SPIE 7012 (Ground-based and Airborne Telescopes II), 70123D* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/241512079_The_pyramid_phasing_sensor_PYPS) · DOI: [10.1117/12.790519](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.790519)

## Summary

Presents PYPS, the pyramid wavefront sensor for phasing and aligning segmented mirrors built for the Active Phase Experiment (APE). The paper describes its opto-mechanical design and laboratory results at Arcetri before integration on the APE bench. A closed piston-correction loop under emulated turbulence (D/r0 = 33 at 700 nm, V/D = 1.9 Hz) reached a final piston error of 10 nm rms on the wavefront. Two filtering techniques that average out the atmospheric disturbance faster cut the required cophasing time by two orders of magnitude, and first results with a synthetic interaction matrix reached a similar final piston error.
