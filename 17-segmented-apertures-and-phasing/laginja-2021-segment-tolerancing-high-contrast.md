# Analytical tolerancing of segmented telescope co-phasing for exo-Earth high-contrast imaging

**Laginja et al. (2021)** · *JATIS 7, 015004* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.06288) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.7.1.015004](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.7.1.015004)

## Summary

Presents an analytical method for computing segment-level wavefront error tolerances needed to detect exo-Earths with coronagraphs on segmented space telescopes, replacing ad hoc Monte Carlo studies of spatially uncorrelated segment phasing errors. The approach relates the mean dark-hole contrast to the standard deviation of each segment's wavefront error, yielding spatially dependent requirements. It extends the PASTIS matrix-based propagation model and is checked against end-to-end Monte Carlo simulations. Applied to Apodized Pupil Lyot Coronagraph designs for LUVOIR, it broadly agrees with earlier studies while relaxing requirements for a significant subset of segments.
