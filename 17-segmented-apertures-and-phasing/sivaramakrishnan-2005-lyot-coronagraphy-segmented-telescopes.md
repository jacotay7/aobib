# Lyot Coronagraphy on Giant Segmented-Mirror Telescopes

**Sivaramakrishnan & Yaitskova (2005)** · *ApJ 626, L65* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0505040) · DOI: [10.1086/431460](https://doi.org/10.1086/431460)

## Summary

Studies Lyot-style coronagraphy (classical, band-limited and Gaussian occulters) on extremely large, highly segmented telescopes. While larger diameter always helps high-dynamic-range science, diffraction from inter-segment gaps sets a floor on the achievable extinction of on-axis starlight. An analytical expression is derived for how coronagraphic suppression degrades with gap size for segments in a periodic array. A simple Lyot stop suppresses the central PSF peak but leaves gap-induced satellite images; a reticulated Lyot mask that also blocks the gaps reduces them to about 5e-9 contrast on a 30 m telescope with 10 mm gaps, at the cost of a brighter central peak.
