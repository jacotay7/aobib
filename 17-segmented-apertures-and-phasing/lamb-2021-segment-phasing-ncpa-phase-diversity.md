# Simultaneous estimation of segmented telescope phasing errors and non-common path aberrations from adaptive-optics-corrected images

**Lamb et al. (2021)** · *MNRAS 505, 3347* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.14609) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stab1247](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stab1247)

## Summary

Investigates phase diversity at the science focal plane of a segmented telescope with AO, both with an internal artificial source and with AO-corrected images of a bright star. Simulations of the Keck/NIRC2 system show that segment phasing errors can be disentangled from non-common-path aberrations (NCPA), which matters because any correction applied to the primary should contain minimal NCPA contribution. Reliable estimates require about a one-minute sequence of short-exposure AO-corrected images, with about 75 defocused frames obtained through a slight modification of the AO loop. Applying the estimates to the primary and deformable mirror reduces the phasing-error and NCPA wavefront errors by up to 67% and 65% respectively.
