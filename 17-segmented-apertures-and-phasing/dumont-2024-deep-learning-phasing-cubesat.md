# Phasing segmented telescopes via deep learning methods: application to a deployable CubeSat

**Dumont et al. (2024)** · *JOSA A 41, 489* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.18712) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.506182](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.506182)

## Summary

Develops a neural-network method to measure co-phasing errors of a small, segmented, deployable CubeSat telescope for Earth observation, where volume and power constraints exclude dedicated phasing hardware or heavy computation. The network estimates phasing errors from images of a point source. It reaches the targeted performance level, typically a wavefront error below 15 nm rms for a diffraction-limited visible imager. Robustness to high-order aberrations and noise is tested and results are compared with existing state-of-the-art techniques.
