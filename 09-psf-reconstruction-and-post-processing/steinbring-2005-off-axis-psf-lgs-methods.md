# Characterizing the Adaptive Optics Off-Axis Point-Spread Function. II. Methods for Use in Laser Guide Star Observations

**Steinbring et al. (2005)** · *PASP 117, 847* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0505208) · DOI: [10.1086/431725](https://doi.org/10.1086/431725)

## Summary

Second paper in a series on characterizing the off-axis AO PSF, here for laser-guide-star observations, where the PSF variation across the field is more complex than for a natural guide star because a natural star is still required for low-order correction. Six methods for estimating the off-axis LGS AO PSF are discussed, based on calibration images of dense star fields that map PSF changes with field position, augmented with AO telemetry and simple simulations. The methods are tested with the laser guide star AO system of the Lick Observatory Shane telescope.
