# Recent Results and Perspectives for Precision Astrometry and Photometry with Adaptive Optics

**Lu et al. (2010)** · *Proc. SPIE 7736, 77361I* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1008.1293) · DOI: [10.1117/12.859737](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.859737)

## Summary

This proceedings paper reviews the state of infrared astrometry and photometry of crowded stellar fields with adaptive optics on large ground-based telescopes. It reports that relative astrometric accuracies below 0.2 mas have been demonstrated over 10-20 arcsec fields at 55-95 mas resolution, together with relative photometric accuracies of about 3% and absolute accuracies of 5-20%. The author discusses recent improvements and the remaining limitations, such as PSF knowledge and distortion. Science applications discussed include orbits of brown dwarfs and exoplanets, the Galactic Center supermassive black hole and its stellar environment, and the initial mass function in young clusters.
