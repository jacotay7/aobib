# Point spread function reconstruction for SOUL + LUCI LBT data

**Simioni et al. (2022)** · *JATIS 8(3), 038003* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.01563) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.8.3.038003](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.8.3.038003)

## Summary

Reports on PSF reconstruction software for AO observations, tested for the first time with pyramid wavefront-sensor telemetry, using data from the SOUL single-conjugate AO upgrade of the Large Binocular Telescope with the LUCI near-infrared camera. Using only telemetry and a careful calibration of the instrument response, PSFs of bright on-axis point sources are reconstructed from two independent datasets. The reconstructed PSFs agree with observed ones to within 2% in Strehl ratio and 4.5% in FWHM, and the core encircled energy is recovered to 4% in the worst case. The authors also evaluate the effect on morphological fits of a compact galaxy and outline extensions to anisoplanatism, low signal-to-noise and MCAO.
