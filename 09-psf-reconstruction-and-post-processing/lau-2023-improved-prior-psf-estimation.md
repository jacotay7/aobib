# Improved prior for adaptive optics point spread function estimation from science images: Application for deconvolution

**Lau et al. (2023)** · *A&A 673, A72* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.09422) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202245471](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202245471)

## Summary

The paper improves the marginal approach for estimating an analytical AO PSF model from science images of resolved extended objects, as the first step of a two-step blind deconvolution. Using simulations, the authors first quantify the accuracy on PSF parameters needed for a resilient deconvolution and show that existing marginal algorithms do not reach it, particularly for small objects. They then introduce a new object model (prior) within the marginal algorithm, which improves the estimation of the required PSF parameters and the resulting deconvolution.
