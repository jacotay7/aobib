# Point-spread function reconstruction of adaptive-optics imaging: meeting the astrometric requirements for time-delay cosmography

**Chen et al. (2021)** · *MNRAS 508, 755* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.11060) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stab2587](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stab2587)

## Summary

Applies PSF reconstruction to Keck laser-guide-star AO images of the strongly lensed quasar J0659+1629 to meet the requirements of time-delay cosmography, where astrometric errors on lensed images and poor knowledge of PSF wings propagate into the inferred Hubble constant. Using the reconstructed PSF, the authors obtain astrometric precision better than 1 milliarcsecond on the multiple quasar images. They also subtract all point-like images with residuals consistent with the noise level, separating the light of the lensed host and the foreground deflector. The method is presented as applicable to other science cases with several nearby point sources.
