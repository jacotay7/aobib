# AIROPA III: Testing Simulated and On-Sky Data

**Turri et al. (2022)** · *JATIS 8, 039002* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.00548) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.8.3.039002](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.8.3.039002)

## Summary

AIROPA is a software package for astrometric and photometric analysis of AO images that combines PSF fitting with PSF reconstruction, using knowledge of instrument performance and atmospheric turbulence to predict the long-exposure PSF across the field. The paper reports tests of AIROPA on simulated and on-sky Keck images of the Galactic Center. The method accounts reliably for static aberrations internal to the instrument. On sky, however, it does not significantly improve accuracy, which the authors attribute possibly to uncalibrated telescope aberrations.
