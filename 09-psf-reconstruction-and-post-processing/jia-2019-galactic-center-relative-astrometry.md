# The Galactic Center: Improved Relative Astrometry for Velocities, Accelerations, and Orbits near the Supermassive Black Hole

**Jia et al. (2019)** · *ApJ 873, 9* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.02491) · DOI: [10.3847/1538-4357/ab01de](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/ab01de)

## Summary

The paper presents improved relative astrometry for stars within the central half parsec of the Galaxy from Keck AO data taken between 1995 and 2017. Improvements include correcting local astrometric distortions, adding a magnitude-dependent additive error, more careful removal of stellar confusion, and jackknife estimates of velocity and acceleration uncertainties. The median proper-motion uncertainty is 0.05 mas/yr for 1184 stars in the central 10 arcsec, and 24 accelerating sources are detected. The systematic error on the black hole position derived from the orbit of S0-2 is halved and the north-south drift of the reference frame is reduced by a factor of 4.
