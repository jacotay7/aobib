# Off-axis point spread function characterization in laser guide star adaptive optics systems

**Beltramo-Martin et al. (2018)** · *MNRAS 478, 4642* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.05198) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/sty1103](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/sty1103)

## Summary

The paper models the signature of anisoplanatism on the PSF for wide-field (about 20 arcsec) single-conjugate LGS AO observations, where angular, focal and tilt anisoplanatism elongate off-axis PSFs. A generalized model based on a point-wise decomposition of the phase is proposed that accounts for the non-stationarity of LGS systems; it agrees with end-to-end simulations to within 0.1% on Strehl ratio, FWHM and fraction of variance unexplained, at lower computational cost than previous models. The authors study the sensitivity to the Cn2(h) profile and find that, for a 10 m telescope, 1% accuracy on PSF morphology requires at least 7 layers with heights known to 200 m and weights to 10%. The model is verified with data from the HeNOS four-laser testbed.
