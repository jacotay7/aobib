# AIROPA IV: Validating point spread function reconstruction on various science cases

**Terry et al. (2023)** · *JATIS 9(1), 018003* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.05489) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.9.1.018003](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.9.1.018003)

## Summary

Tests the AIROPA off-axis PSF reconstruction package on six independent on-sky datasets from Keck II/NIRC2, extracting stellar astrometry, photometry and fit residuals. The study varies atmospheric conditions, the number and placement of PSF reference stars, stellar crowding and telescope position angle, and compares a static single-PSF model with a spatially variable PSF that includes instrumental aberrations and atmospheric turbulence. For most metrics the variable-PSF mode gives little or no improvement, with at best about 13% smaller astrometric uncertainty, although one dataset shows a roughly tenfold photometric improvement. The authors attribute the limited gains to instrumental aberrations not captured by afternoon AO bench calibrations.
