# Temporal variability and statistics of the Strehl ratio in adaptive-optics images

**Gladysz et al. (2008)** · *PASP 120, 1132* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/0801.4889) · DOI: [10.1086/592787](https://doi.org/10.1086/592787)

## Summary

The paper studies the temporal variability and statistics of the instantaneous Strehl ratio, defined as the peak intensity of single short exposures, using the 3.63 m AEOS telescope with a high-order AO system. The phase variance computed from reconstructed wavefronts is analysed alongside. The Maréchal approximation is tested and used to explain the observed negative skewness of the Strehl ratio distribution, and the phase-variance estimate is shown to follow a three-parameter Gamma distribution. Simple scaling of the reconstructed wavefronts is shown to strongly affect the shape of the Strehl distribution.
