# Physics-based model of the adaptive-optics-corrected point spread function. Applications to the SPHERE/ZIMPOL and MUSE instruments

**Fétick et al. (2019)** · *A&A 628, A99* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.02200) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201935830](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201935830)

## Summary

The paper introduces a model of the long-exposure AO PSF (often called PSFAO) intended for any AO system and seeing condition, matching both the corrected core and the turbulent halo. It parameterizes the residual phase power spectral density with five parameters for circularly symmetric PSFs and seven for asymmetric ones, one of which is directly the Fried parameter r0. The model is fitted to end-to-end OOMAO simulations and to on-sky PSFs from SPHERE/ZIMPOL and from MUSE narrow-field mode, matching core and halo with sub-percent relative error. The fitted r0 correlates at 97% with the RTC estimate on ZIMPOL and follows the expected wavelength dependence on MUSE.
