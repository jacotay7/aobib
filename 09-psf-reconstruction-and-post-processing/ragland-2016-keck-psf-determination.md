# Point spread function determination for Keck adaptive optics

**Ragland et al. (2016)** · *Proc. SPIE 9909 (Adaptive Optics Systems V), 99091P* · [Free full text](https://www.spiedigitallibrary.org/conference-proceedings-of-spie/9909/99091P/Point-spread-function-determination-for-Keck-adaptive-optics/10.1117/12.2231814.pdf) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2231814](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2231814)

## Summary

An overview of the program to bring PSF reconstruction to Keck AO science observations, motivated by the limits that poor PSF knowledge places on AO photometry, astrometry, morphology, kinematics and faint-companion detection. The paper describes how the reconstruction algorithms were implemented and the design of prototype operational tools for automated PSF reconstruction. On-sky performance is assessed by comparing reconstructed PSFs with PSFs measured on the NIRC2 science camera. The authors highlight the need to know the control-loop performance, to map the telescope pupil accurately onto the deformable mirror and the science-instrument pupil, and to account for telescope segment piston errors, and they close with lessons learned and near-term plans.
