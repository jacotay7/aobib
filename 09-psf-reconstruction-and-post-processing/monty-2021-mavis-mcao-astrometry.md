# Towards realistic modelling of the astrometric capabilities of MCAO systems: detecting an intermediate mass black hole with MAVIS

**Monty et al. (2021)** · *MNRAS 507, 2192* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.13199) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stab2199](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stab2199)

## Summary

MAVIS, an MCAO visible imager and spectrograph for the VLT Adaptive Optics Facility, has an astrometric requirement of 150 microarcseconds (goal 50). The authors develop MAVISIM, an image simulator including three major astrometric error sources: high- and low-order PSF spatial variability, tip-tilt residuals and static field distortion. With these terms alone, an accuracy of 50 microarcseconds is recovered for stars brighter than m = 19 in 30 s using PSF-fitting photometry. Using an N-body model of an NGC 3201-like cluster with a central 1500 solar-mass black hole, they show that its dynamical signature could be detected from proper motions in the inner few arcseconds.
