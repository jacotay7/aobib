# Accounting for the anisoplanatic point spread function in deep wide-field adaptive optics images

**Cresci et al. (2005)** · *A&A 438, 757* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0504467) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361:20052890](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361:20052890)

## Summary

The authors determine and account for the anisoplanatic PSF in deep Ks-band NACO images of the SWAN survey at the VLT, which comprises 42 fields around bright AO guide stars covering about 30 square arcminutes. They build a parametric model of the PSF variation across the field, providing a model PSF for every detected galaxy using only readily available data and without assumptions about the stability of the isoplanatic angle during a night. Tests with simulated galaxy profiles show reliable recovery of morphological parameters up to Ks of about 20.5. The model is applied to five fields to derive AO morphologies of 55 galaxies brighter than Ks = 20.
