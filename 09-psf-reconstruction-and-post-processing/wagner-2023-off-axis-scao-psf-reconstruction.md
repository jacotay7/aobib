# Off-axis point spread function reconstruction for single conjugate adaptive optics

**Wagner et al. (2023)** · *JOSA A 40, 1382* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.07905) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.488843](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.488843)

## Summary

The paper presents an algorithm to reconstruct the PSF in directions away from the guide star in a single-conjugate AO system on an extremely large telescope, focusing on the anisoplanatic and generalized fitting error contributions. An SCAO PSF reconstruction algorithm is combined with a time-dependent atmospheric tomography method to obtain direction-dependent post-AO PSFs. End-to-end simulations show qualitatively good agreement with PSFs computed from the simulated wavefront and stable behavior with respect to imprecise knowledge of the atmospheric parameters.
