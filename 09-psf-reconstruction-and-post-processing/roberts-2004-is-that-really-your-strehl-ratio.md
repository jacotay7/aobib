# Is that really your Strehl ratio?

**Roberts et al. (2004)** · *Proc. SPIE 5490 (Advancements in Adaptive Optics), 504* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/253843024_Is_that_really_your_Strehl_ratio) · DOI: [10.1117/12.549115](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.549115)

## Summary

Examines how much measured Strehl ratios depend on the algorithm used to compute them, since Strehl ratio is the most common, and often misused, AO performance metric. The authors made a series of simulated PSFs with known Strehl ratios and sent them to members of the project, who measured them blind with their own methods. Each round of comparison exposed effects that were investigated in the next round. The paper reports the scatter among the measurements and gives recommendations for computing an accurate Strehl ratio.
