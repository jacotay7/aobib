# What is limiting near-infrared astrometry in the Galactic Centre?

**Fritz et al. (2010)** · *MNRAS 401, 1177* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/0909.2592) · DOI: [10.1111/j.1365-2966.2009.15707.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2966.2009.15707.x)

## Summary

The paper systematically analyses error sources for high-precision astrometry in AO near-infrared images of the crowded Galactic Center field. At the 100 microarcsecond level the most important limitations are imperfectly subtracted seeing halos of neighbouring stars, residual image distortions and unrecognized confusion with fainter background sources. Further contributions come from PSF estimation uncertainty, photon noise, coordinate transformations, chromatic atmospheric refraction, differential tilt jitter and anisoplanatism. Residual distortions limit stars as bright as K = 14, while halos of surrounding stars limit fainter stars; the authors suggest that techniques to estimate or reconstruct the seeing halo could help.
