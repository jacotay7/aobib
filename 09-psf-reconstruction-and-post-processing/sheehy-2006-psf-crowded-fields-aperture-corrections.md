# Constraining the Adaptive Optics Point-Spread Function in Crowded Fields: Measuring Photometric Aperture Corrections

**Sheehy et al. (2006)** · *ApJ 647, 1517* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0604551) · DOI: [10.1086/505524](https://doi.org/10.1086/505524)

## Summary

Because the AO PSF depends on seeing, wind speed and guide-star brightness, the authors estimate it from the science data themselves for crowded star fields. They model the modulation transfer function of the main components of the imaging system and constrain the model with the power spectrum of the image, from which the PSF is reconstructed. Applied to Keck laser guide star AO data, the method yields reasonable fit parameters and a useful PSF approximation. Comparison with HST/NICMOS shows photometric accuracy of a few percent for Strehl ratios as low as 4%.
