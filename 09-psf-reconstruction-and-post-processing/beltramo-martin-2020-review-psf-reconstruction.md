# Review of PSF reconstruction methods and application to post-processing

**Beltramo-Martin et al. (2020)** · *Proc. SPIE 11448, 114480A* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-03240495) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2560805](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2560805)

## Summary

This review compiles and classifies techniques for determining the point spread function (PSF) of adaptive optics observations, with emphasis on PSF reconstruction from AO telemetry and other pupil-plane-based approaches. It addresses the spatial, temporal and spectral variability of the AO PSF and its complex structure as the main obstacles to post-processing. The authors summarize the main messages from a large body of references at a high level. The paper also covers applications of reconstructed or modeled PSFs to post-processing, in particular PSF fitting for photometry/astrometry and deconvolution.
