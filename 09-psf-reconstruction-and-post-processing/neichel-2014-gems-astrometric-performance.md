# Astrometric performance of the Gemini multiconjugate adaptive optics system in crowded fields

**Neichel et al. (2014)** · *MNRAS 445, 500* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.0719) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stu1766](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stu1766)

## Summary

The paper assesses the astrometric performance and limitations of the GeMS MCAO system with the GSAOI camera on Gemini South, using deep single-epoch images, multi-epoch data and distortion calibration. For single-epoch, undithered data an astrometric error below 0.2 mas is reached for exposures longer than one minute, provided enough stars are available to remove high-order distortions. Multi-epoch observations show an additional systematic error of about 0.4 mas, the dominant term of the error budget. This term is attributed to time-variable distortion induced by gravity flexure.
