# Pushing point-spread function reconstruction to the next level: application to SPHERE/ZIMPOL

**Beltramo-Martin et al. (2020)** · *MNRAS 494, 775* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.10267) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/staa525](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/staa525)

## Summary

The paper applies the PRIME PSF reconstruction method, combining pupil-plane telemetry and focal-plane measurements, to SPHERE/ZIMPOL observations of the globular cluster NGC 6121, with upgrades to both the PSF model and the fitting procedure. The authors show that PRIME keeps the PSF fitting residual below 0.2% over 2 hours of observation while using only 30 s of AO telemetry, which bears on telemetry storage requirements for PSF-R on 30-40 m telescopes. They also test PRIME on faint stars to determine the precision needed on the initial-guess parameters for convergence to the optimal solution.
