# High-precision astrometry with MICADO at the European Extremely Large Telescope

**Trippe et al. (2010)** · *MNRAS 402, 1126* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/0910.5114) · DOI: [10.1111/j.1365-2966.2009.15940.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2966.2009.15940.x)

## Summary

The paper identifies statistical and systematic effects that limit the astrometric accuracy achievable with MICADO, the near-infrared imager proposed for the 42 m E-ELT. These are grouped as instrumental (e.g. geometric distortion), atmospheric (e.g. chromatic differential refraction) and astronomical (e.g. reference source selection). Several effects act at the 100 microarcsecond level, above the theoretical statistical accuracy of the telescope, and must be controlled by instrument design or dedicated calibration. If this is done, the authors conclude that accuracies of 40 microarcseconds or better per epoch are achievable.
