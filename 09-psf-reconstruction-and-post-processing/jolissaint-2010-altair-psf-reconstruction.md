# Adaptive optics point spread function reconstruction: lessons learned from on-sky experiment on Altair/Gemini and pathway for future systems

**Jolissaint et al. (2010)** · *Proc. SPIE 7736, 77361F* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1007.2458) · DOI: [10.1117/12.857670](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.857670)

## Summary

The authors report an on-sky PSF reconstruction experiment with Altair, the adaptive optics system of the Gemini North telescope, in bright on-axis natural guide star mode. The PSF-R method is shown to work as a system performance diagnostic tool. However, the reconstructed PSFs did not match the observed ones because of telescope and system aberrations not included in the model, and the probable origins of this discrepancy are discussed. The paper closes with proposals for PSF-R approaches for future systems using multiple natural and laser guide stars.
