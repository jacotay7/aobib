# Direction dependent point spread function reconstruction for multi-conjugate adaptive optics on giant segmented mirror telescopes

**Wagner et al. (2022)** · *Astronomy and Computing 40, 100590* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.11429) · DOI: [10.1016/j.ascom.2022.100590](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ascom.2022.100590)

## Summary

The paper presents a storage-efficient PSF reconstruction algorithm for multi-conjugate adaptive optics on giant segmented mirror telescopes such as the ELT, where the post-AO PSF varies across the field. It combines an existing single-conjugate PSF reconstruction method with an atmospheric tomography algorithm to obtain direction-dependent PSF estimates. Tests in an end-to-end simulation show qualitatively good agreement with PSFs computed directly from the simulated incoming wavefront, with reasonable runtime and memory consumption.
