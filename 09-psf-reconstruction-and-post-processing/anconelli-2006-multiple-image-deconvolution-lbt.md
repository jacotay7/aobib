# Deconvolution of multiple images with high dynamic range and an application to LBT LINC-NIRVANA

**Anconelli et al. (2006)** · *A&A 460, 349* · [Free full text](https://www.aanda.org/articles/aa/pdf/2006/46/aa5836-06.pdf) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361:20065836](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361:20065836)

## Summary

The standard Richardson-Lucy method performs poorly on astronomical images containing objects with very different angular scales and brightnesses. The authors extend a regularized Richardson-Lucy approach, derived with the split gradient method by adding a penalty term to the Csiszár divergence, to the deconvolution of multiple images, aimed at the Fizeau interferometric images of LINC-NIRVANA at the Large Binocular Telescope. The method is tested on a model young binary star with a circumbinary dusty ring. Results are good when the PSFs are known exactly, while with approximate PSFs the reconstruction accuracy depends on the magnitude difference between the components.
