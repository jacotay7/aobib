# Improving Galactic Center Astrometry by Reducing the Effects of Geometric Distortion

**Yelda et al. (2010)** · *ApJ 725, 331* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.0064) · DOI: [10.1088/0004-637X/725/1/331](https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/725/1/331)

## Summary

The paper derives a new geometric distortion model for the Keck II NIRC2 narrow camera, built for the first time from on-sky measurements and made publicly available. Applied to widely dithered images, it reduces residuals in stellar separations by a factor of about 3 relative to previous models. Together with corrections for differential atmospheric refraction, the model is used to tie the infrared reference frame to the radio Sgr A* rest frame via SiO masers, giving a frame about 2-3 times more accurate and stable than earlier ones. Sgr A* is localized to 0.6 mas in position and 0.09 mas/yr in velocity.
