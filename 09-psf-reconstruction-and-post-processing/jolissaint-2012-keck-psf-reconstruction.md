# First Successful Adaptive Optics PSF Reconstruction at W. M. Keck Observatory

**Jolissaint et al. (2011)** · *Proc. AO4ELT2 (Victoria, 2011)* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1202.3486)

## Summary

This proceedings paper presents results of the PSF reconstruction project for the Keck II and Gemini North AO systems in natural guide star mode. Initial tests identified the determination of static aberrations and of the optical turbulence parameters as the most critical aspects of PSF-R, motivating a dedicated observing campaign. The authors show that a deformable-mirror-based seeing monitor reaches about 10% accuracy, that phase diversity works on sky sources, and that the assumption of residual phase stationarity largely holds. With these elements they report a successful PSF reconstruction for Keck II in bright natural guide star mode.
