# Astrometry with MCAO: HST-GeMS proper motions in the globular cluster NGC 6681

**Massari et al. (2016)** · *A&A 595, L2* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.05923) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201629336](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201629336)

## Summary

This letter tests for the first time the multi-epoch astrometric capabilities of GeMS/GSAOI by measuring proper motions in the globular cluster NGC 6681. Existing HST/ACS proper motions are used to build a distortion-free reference at the GeMS epoch, which is used to measure and correct the time-variable distortions of each GeMS exposure. Comparing the corrected GeMS images with first-epoch HST/ACS images gives a relative proper motion of the Sagittarius dwarf galaxy of (4.09, -3.41) mas/yr, consistent with HST-only values within 0.03 mas/yr and with comparable precision (0.43 mas/yr rms). The authors conclude that high-quality proper motions can be measured over 85 x 85 arcsec fields with MCAO.
