# The MICADO first light imager for the ELT: the PSF Reconstruction Software

**Grazian et al. (2026)** · *Proc. SPIE (Adaptive Optics Systems X), 2026* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11593) · DOI: [10.1117/12.3102300](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3102300)

## Summary

This paper reports the status of the PSF reconstruction software of MICADO, the first-light near-infrared imager of the ELT, designed to reconstruct the PSF for all observations from AO telemetry alone in both SCAO and MCAO modes. The SCAO PSF-R pipeline is being completed and has been validated using ERIS at the VLT and SOUL+LUCI at the LBT. The authors report percent-level accuracy on axis and 5-20% off axis. Initial science simulations indicate that the software meets the precision required for MICADO data analysis.
