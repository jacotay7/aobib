# Marginalized myopic deconvolution of adaptive optics corrected images using Markov chain Monte Carlo methods

**Yan et al. (2023)** · *JATIS 9, 048004* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-04051187) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.9.4.048004](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.9.4.048004)

## Summary

Marginal myopic deconvolution estimates the PSF and the object and noise hyperparameters with the object marginalized out, before deconvolving with these estimates; previous maximum a posteriori implementations did not provide uncertainties on the parameters. The authors instead use the minimum mean square error estimator, computed together with its uncertainties by a Markov chain Monte Carlo algorithm. The method is validated with realistic simulations in astronomical and satellite-observation contexts. Results are shown on a VLT/SPHERE/ZIMPOL observation and on a ground-based LEO satellite observation with ONERA's ODISSEE AO bench at the Côte d'Azur Observatory 1.52 m telescope.
