# Joint estimation of atmospheric and instrumental defects using a parsimonious point spread function model. On-sky validation using state of the art worldwide adaptive-optics assisted instruments

**Beltramo-Martin et al. (2020)** · *A&A 643, A58* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.00994) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202038679](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202038679)

## Summary

The paper tests the parsimonious analytical AO PSF model of Fétick et al. (2019) on 4812 on-sky PSFs from seven AO systems, using a common fitting algorithm, and extends it to jointly estimate atmospheric parameters, AO performance and static aberrations. Over the sample the model reaches about 4% error on Strehl ratio and FWHM. The retrieved Fried parameter follows known seeing statistics, the expected wavelength dependence on MUSE and no field dependence on GSAOI. The framework is used to retrieve low-wind-effect differential piston/tip/tilt on SPHERE, in agreement with ZELDA measurements, and segment piston errors on Keck II.
