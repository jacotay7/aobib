# Accurate photometry with adaptive optics in the presence of anisoplanatic effects with a sparsely sampled PSF

**Schödel (2010)** · *A&A 509, A58* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/0912.1219) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/200912808](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/200912808)

## Summary

The paper addresses systematic photometric errors caused by anisoplanatism in dense AO stellar fields when only one or very few PSF reference stars are available, as in the Galactic Center. Two approaches based on local PSF fitting with StarFinder are presented: merging locally extracted PSF cores with wings from the brightest star, and Wiener deconvolution with the brightest-star PSF followed by local PSF extraction on the deconvolved image. Tested on real and artificial images, the deconvolution-based method limits systematic effects to about 2-5% for point sources and about 10% for diffuse emission over a 28 x 28 arcsec H-band field. The effect of deconvolution on noise properties and formal uncertainties is analysed.
