# Blind and robust reconstruction of adaptive optics point spread functions for asteroid deconvolution and moon detection

**Berdeu et al. (2024)** · *Proc. SPIE (Adaptive Optics Systems IX), 2024* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.08585) · DOI: [10.1117/12.3019874](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3019874)

## Summary

The paper presents a method to reconstruct the AO PSF blindly from images of asteroids, without priors on the instrument or the asteroid shape, in order to deconvolve the main body and detect faint nearby moons hidden in the halo. PSF core parameters are first estimated assuming a sharp-edged flat object, allowing deconvolution of the main body. The faint PSF extensions are then reconstructed by robust penalized optimization that rejects outliers such as cosmic rays, bad pixels and moons, and moons are detected in the residuals. The method is applied to VLT/SPHERE and Keck/NIRC2 data and improves moon signal compared with existing moon-enhancement algorithms.
