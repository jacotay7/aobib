# The Strehl Ratio in Adaptive Optics Images: Statistics and Estimation

**Soummer & Ferrari (2007)** · *ApJ 663, L49* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/0706.1718) · DOI: [10.1086/519080](https://doi.org/10.1086/519080)

## Summary

Intensity statistics in AO images are usually modeled with a modified Rician distribution, but this model is inappropriate at the central point of the image for high to very high correction levels. Since the central intensity determines the Strehl ratio, the authors study its distribution specifically. They show that it can be modeled by a non-central Gamma distribution, which provides a basis for statistically sound Strehl ratio estimation.
