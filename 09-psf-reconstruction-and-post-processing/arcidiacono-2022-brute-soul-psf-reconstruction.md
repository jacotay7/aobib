# BRUTE, PSF Reconstruction for the SOUL pyramid-based Single Conjugate Adaptive Optics facility of the LBT

**Arcidiacono et al. (2022)** · *Proc. SPIE 12185, 1218540* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.03278) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2629001](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2629001)

## Summary

The paper describes BRUTE (Blind Reconstruction Using TElemetry), a PSF reconstruction algorithm for the LBT LUCI instrument fed by the SOUL pyramid-based single-conjugate AO system. The method uses only synchronous wavefront sensor telemetry and a few asynchronous calibrations. Rather than computing an optical transfer function and associated filters, it reconstructs a temporal series of wavefront maps and the corresponding instantaneous PSFs, which are then combined. The algorithm was tested in the laboratory and on sky for several SOUL configurations, guide star magnitudes and seeing conditions.
