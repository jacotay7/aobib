# Asymmetries in adaptive optics point spread functions

**Madurowicz et al. (2019)** · *JATIS 5(4), 049003* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.12981) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.5.4.049003](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.5.4.049003)

## Summary

Explains the asymmetry observed along a preferred axis in AO PSFs. When phase errors from high-altitude turbulence scintillate through Fresnel propagation, the resulting amplitude errors can be spatially offset from the residual phase errors left by a delayed AO correction, and these correlated errors produce an asymmetric image. An analytic open-loop model gives the asymmetry strength for a single aberration mode and generalizes to two dimensions via Fourier decomposition, with dependence on the wind-driven correction offset and layer altitude. The asymmetry is strongest for slow wind and distant layers, the brighter lobe points opposite to the wind direction, and the model is checked against Gemini Planet Imager data and end-to-end simulations.
