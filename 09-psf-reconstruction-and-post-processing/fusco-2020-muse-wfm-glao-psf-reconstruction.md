# Reconstruction of the ground-layer adaptive-optics point spread function for MUSE Wide Field Mode observations

**Fusco et al. (2020)** · *A&A 635, A208* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.04028) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202037595](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202037595)

## Summary

The authors describe an operational PSF reconstruction approach for laser-assisted ground-layer AO in the MUSE Wide Field Mode on the VLT Adaptive Optics Facility. The method uses a Fourier description of the GLAO correction, driven by a small set of atmospheric parameters from the AO real-time computer, and adds MUSE-specific instrumental effects such as pixel size and internal aberrations. It was validated with end-to-end simulations, re-optimized after a sensitivity analysis, and then commissioned on more than one year of on-sky MUSE data. The algorithm meets the MUSE requirements of a few percent accuracy on PSF FWHM and on the Moffat kurtosis parameter, is publicly available, and is run routinely for each observation.
