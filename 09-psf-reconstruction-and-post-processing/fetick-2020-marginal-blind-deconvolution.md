# Blind deconvolution in astronomy with adaptive optics: the parametric marginal approach

**Fétick et al. (2020)** · *MNRAS 496, 4209* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.11160) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/staa1813](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/staa1813)

## Summary

The paper addresses the lack of PSF knowledge in post-processing of AO-corrected images by estimating the PSF from the science image itself. The authors show that joint estimation of object and PSF parameters suffers from severe limitations and propose instead a marginalized estimation of the parameters of a parametric PSF model, with the object integrated out. The estimated PSF is then used for deconvolution, forming a complete blind deconvolution procedure. The method is evaluated on simulations and applied to VLT/SPHERE/ZIMPOL images of the asteroid (4) Vesta.
