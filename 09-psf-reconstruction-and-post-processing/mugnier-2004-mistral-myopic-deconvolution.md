# MISTRAL: a myopic edge-preserving image restoration method, with application to astronomical adaptive-optics-corrected long-exposure images

**Mugnier et al. (2004)** · *JOSA A 21, 1841* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/8220499_Mistral_a_myopic_edge-preserving_image_restoration_method_with_application_to_astronomical_adaptive-optics-corrected_long-exposure_images) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.21.001841](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.21.001841)

## Summary

Describes MISTRAL, a Bayesian deconvolution method for images corrected by adaptive optics. The noise model includes both photon and detector noise, and the regularization is suited to objects that mix sharp edges with smooth areas. Because the PSF is imperfectly known, it is estimated jointly with the object under soft constraints that encode prior knowledge of the PSF, a so-called myopic rather than blind approach. The method is validated in simulation and illustrated on experimental data from several AO systems and telescopes, some of which had already supported published astrophysical results.
