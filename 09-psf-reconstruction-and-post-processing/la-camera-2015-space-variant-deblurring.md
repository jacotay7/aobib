# A method for space-variant deblurring with application to adaptive optics imaging in astronomy

**La Camera et al. (2015)** · *A&A 579, A1* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.01684) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201525610](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201525610)

## Summary

The paper proposes a deblurring method for AO images whose PSF varies across the field, given a set of samples of the space-variant PSF. The image domain is partitioned into approximately isoplanatic regions, each deconvolved with suitable methods including a correction of boundary effects, and the effectiveness of this correction and of using partially overlapping sections is demonstrated. Applied to simulated images of a stellar system with spatially variable PSF, the method gives good photometric quality from aperture photometry on the deblurred images. It is implemented in the IDL package Patch.
