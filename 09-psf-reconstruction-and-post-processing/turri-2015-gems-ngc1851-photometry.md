# Toward Precision Photometry for the ELT Era: The Double Subgiant Branch of NGC 1851 Observed with the Gemini/GeMS MCAO System

**Turri et al. (2015)** · *ApJ 811, L15* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.01764) · DOI: [10.1088/2041-8205/811/2/L15](https://doi.org/10.1088/2041-8205/811/2/L15)

## Summary

This letter analyses Ks-band GeMS/GSAOI images of the central region of the globular cluster NGC 1851 to test precise near-infrared photometry with MCAO in crowded fields. The authors present what they describe as the deepest ground-based Ks photometry in crowded fields at the time and combine it with HST/ACS F606W data in a colour-magnitude diagram. They detect the knee of the lower main sequence at Ks of about 20.5 and the double subgiant branch of the cluster. The detection is used as a demonstration of the photometric accuracy of GeMS in crowded fields.
