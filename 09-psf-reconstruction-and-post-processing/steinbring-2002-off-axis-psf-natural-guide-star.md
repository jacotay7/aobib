# Characterizing the Adaptive Optics Off-Axis Point-Spread Function. I. A Semi-Empirical Method for Use in Natural Guide Star Observations

**Steinbring et al. (2002)** · *PASP 114, 1267* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0207261) · DOI: [10.1086/343217](https://doi.org/10.1086/343217)

## Summary

The paper describes a semi-empirical technique to determine the AO PSF at the position of a science target 10-30 arcsec from the guide star. Calibration images of dense star fields are used to measure how the PSF changes with field position, and this information is applied to contemporaneous guide-star images to predict the off-axis PSF at the time of the science observation. Tests used natural-guide-star AO systems on the CFHT and the Lick Shane telescope together with simple atmospheric simulations. At 25 arcsec off axis, the error on the predicted FWHM drops from 60% using the guide star alone to 33% using a dense-field image and to 19% with the combined method.
