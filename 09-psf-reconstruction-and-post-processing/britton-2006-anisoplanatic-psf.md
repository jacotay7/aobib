# The Anisoplanatic Point-Spread Function in Adaptive Optics

**Britton (2006)** · *PASP 118, 885* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0603170) · DOI: [10.1086/505547](https://doi.org/10.1086/505547)

## Summary

The paper derives a model that combines observations of the guide star with an analytic formulation of anisoplanatism to predict the AO PSF anywhere in the field, accounting for aperture diameter, wavelength, angular offset, zenith angle and turbulence profile. Predictions are compared with narrowband 2.12 and 1.65 micron Palomar AO images of a 21 arcsec binary, using contemporaneous MASS/DIMM turbulence profiles. Predicted companion Strehl ratios agree with measurements within a few percent and the PSFs within 10%, whereas isoplanatic-angle predictions are strongly discrepant. Differential photometry to 1 part in 1000 and differential astrometry to 1 mas are obtained, with the astrometric error dominated by differential tilt jitter.
