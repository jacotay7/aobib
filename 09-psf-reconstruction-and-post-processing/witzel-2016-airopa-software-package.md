# The AIROPA software package: milestones for testing general relativity in the strong gravity regime with AO

**Witzel et al. (2016)** · *Proc. SPIE 9909, 99091O* · [Free full text](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/2y3jk-6z077) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2233872](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2233872)

## Summary

This paper introduces AIROPA (Anisoplanatic and Instrumental Reconstruction of Off-axis PSFs for AO), a software package developed by the UCLA Galactic Center Group to model the spatial variability of the PSF in Keck/NIRC2 adaptive-optics images. The motivation is that field-dependent PSF changes from atmospheric anisoplanatism and instrumental aberrations are a limiting systematic for astrometry and photometry of stars orbiting the Galactic Center black hole. AIROPA builds on modified versions of StarFinder and Arroyo and takes turbulence profiles, instrumental aberration maps (from phase diversity) and the science images as inputs to produce field-dependent PSFs for PSF fitting. The authors report that it reproduces observed Strehl-ratio differences across the field of up to about 5% in K band and reduces PSF-fitting residuals by at least a factor of three compared with single-PSF analysis.
