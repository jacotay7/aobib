# Optimal Stellar Photometry for Multi-conjugate Adaptive Optics Systems Using Science-based Metrics

**Turri et al. (2017)** · *AJ 153, 199* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.00422) · DOI: [10.3847/1538-3881/aa63ed](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-3881/aa63ed)

## Summary

Using deep J and Ks GeMS/GSAOI exposures of NGC 1851, the paper develops an optimal strategy for PSF-fitting stellar photometry in crowded MCAO fields, judged by science-based metrics such as the width of the main-sequence turn-off. The GeMS PSF shows significant spatial and temporal variability that must be accounted for. Most of the PSF variation occurs within the control radius of the MCAO system, and the best photometry is obtained when the PSF radius matches this scale. Photometric calibration is identified as a critical issue for future MCAO systems on TMT and the ELT, and the resulting colour-magnitude diagrams reach Ks of about 22.
