# AIDA: an adaptive image deconvolution algorithm with application to multi-frame and three-dimensional data

**Hom et al. (2007)** · *JOSA A 24, 1580* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3166524/) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.24.001580](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.24.001580)

## Summary

The paper presents AIDA, a reimplementation and extension of the MISTRAL myopic deconvolution method of Mugnier and co-workers, written in Numerical Python with a constrained conjugate-gradient minimizer. The method jointly estimates the object and the PSF within a Bayesian framework with an edge-preserving object prior, and AIDA extends it to multi-frame data (several PSFs or several objects) and to three-dimensional data sets. It includes a scheme that automatically balances the maximum-likelihood term against object regularization, reducing the manual tuning needed to obtain satisfactory reconstructions. The authors validate it on synthetic data over a range of signal-to-noise ratios and show that it is effective on experimental data from adaptive-optics-equipped telescopes and from wide-field microscopy. The linked copy is the NIH author manuscript in PubMed Central.
