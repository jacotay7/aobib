# Holographic imaging of crowded fields: high angular resolution imaging with excellent quality at very low cost

**Schödel et al. (2013)** · *MNRAS 429, 1367* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1110.2261) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/sts420](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/sts420)

## Summary

Presents a speckle-holography method optimised for crowded stellar fields, in which the instantaneous PSF of each short-exposure frame is extracted iteratively and, optionally, from several reference stars at once. This yields accurate, high signal-to-noise PSFs per frame and sensitive, high-Strehl reconstructed images. The method is tested with several instruments and targets from the N to the I band. The authors find that holographic imaging can match or exceed current AO systems in PSF cosmetics, stability and Strehl ratio, particularly at short near-infrared and optical wavelengths, outperforms lucky imaging by using the full PSF, and can also be combined with AO imaging when the corrected PSF is unstable.
