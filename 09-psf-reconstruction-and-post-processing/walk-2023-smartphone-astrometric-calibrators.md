# Smartphone screens as astrometric calibrators

**Walk et al. (2023)** · *J. Astron. Instrum. 12, 2350009* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.04961) · DOI: [10.1142/S2251171723500095](https://doi.org/10.1142/S2251171723500095)

## Summary

Geometric optical distortion is a major term in the astrometric error budget of AO-equipped large telescopes and must be calibrated. The authors investigate smartphone OLED screens as low-cost, stably illuminated and quickly reconfigurable astrometric calibration sources that can probe different spatial frequencies of an instrument's distortion. Characterizing a Samsung S20 screen, they measure a pixel placement error of 189 ± 15 nm RMS. They conclude that at this level milliarcsecond astrometric accuracy is achievable for modern astronomical instruments.
