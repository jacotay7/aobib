# Anisoplanatism effect on the E-ELT SCAO point spread function. A preserved coherent core across the field

**Clénet et al. (2015)** · *A&A 583, A102* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-02423539) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201425469](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201425469)

## Summary

Simulates the effect of angular anisoplanatism on the single-conjugate AO (SCAO) point spread function of the European ELT, in terms of both Strehl ratio and PSF profile as a function of off-axis distance. Three tools of increasing fidelity are used and cross-checked: analytical formulae for the Strehl ratio degradation, a Fourier-based code, and a GPU-based end-to-end simulation. Besides the expected loss of performance with field angle, the SCAO PSF retains a diffraction-limited coherent core even at off-axis distances as large as 60 arcsec, where Strehl ratios are very low. The Airy-like pattern remains visible even when the turbulence outer scale exceeds the telescope diameter. The authors suggest this argues for preserving the optical quality of common-path optics over a wide field, which could give SCAO-fed instruments some astrometric capability.
