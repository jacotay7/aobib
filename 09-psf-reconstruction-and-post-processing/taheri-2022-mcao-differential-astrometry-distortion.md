# Optimal Differential Astrometry for Multiconjugate Adaptive Optics. I. Astrometric Distortion Mapping using On-sky GeMS Observations of NGC 6723

**Taheri et al. (2022)** · *AJ 163, 187* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.12547) · DOI: [10.3847/1538-3881/ac5747](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-3881/ac5747)

## Summary

Using GeMS/GSAOI observations of the core of NGC 6723, the authors study the on-sky astrometric performance of MCAO in preparation for ELT and TMT instruments. They develop methods to map the astrometric distortion fields of the observing system at high spatial resolution and to separate time-variable and static components. Applied to GSAOI data, the maps reveal the effect of the field rotator at the subpixel level and the distortion due to the GeMS-GSAOI optical design. The pipeline yields proper motions with an uncertainty floor of 45 microarcseconds per year, used to measure the proper-motion dispersion profile of the cluster from 12000 stars.
