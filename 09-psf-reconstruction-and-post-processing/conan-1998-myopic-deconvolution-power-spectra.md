# Myopic deconvolution of adaptive optics images by use of object and point-spread function power spectra

**Conan et al. (1998)** · *Applied Optics 37, 4614* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/5565962_Myopic_deconvolution_of_adaptive_optics_images_by_use_of_object_and_point-spread_function_power_spectra) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.37.004614](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.37.004614)

## Summary

Argues that adaptive optics correction is usually partial, so deconvolution is needed to reach the diffraction limit, and that the deconvolution must be regularized. The paper presents a regularized method with a positivity constraint that uses prior knowledge of the object, namely an estimate of its local mean and a model of its power spectral density. The method is then extended to an unknown PSF, using similar prior information on the PSF's power spectrum, which makes it myopic. Results are shown on both simulated and experimental data.
