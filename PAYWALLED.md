# Paywalled works worth adding

Important adaptive-optics papers, books and chapters found while building this registry
for which **no legitimate free full text was found as of October 2026**, so they are not
entries in the topic folders. Inclusion here only means our search did not find a free copy;
one may exist or appear later. If you find a free copy (arXiv, author page, institutional
repository, open-access re-release), add an entry and remove the item from this list.
