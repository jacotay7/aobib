# Adaptive optics for structured illumination microscopy

**Débarre et al. (2008)** · *Optics Express 16, 9290* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.16.009290) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.16.009290](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.16.009290)

## Summary

Implements wavefront-sensorless adaptive optics in a structured illumination microscope and analyses how aberrations affect its image formation. Aberrations are found to fall into two groups: those that affect imaging of the illumination pattern and those that do not. A set of aberration modes suited to this microscope is derived and used for efficient correction, with each mode optimised independently through sequential maximisation of an image-quality metric. Corrected imaging is demonstrated on fixed fluorescent specimens, with further improvement from differential aberration imaging to reduce background fluorescence.
