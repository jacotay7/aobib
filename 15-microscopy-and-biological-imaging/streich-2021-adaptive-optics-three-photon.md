# High-resolution structural and functional deep brain imaging using adaptive optics three-photon microscopy

**Streich et al. (2021)** · *Nature Methods 18, 1253* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8490155/) · DOI: [10.1038/s41592-021-01257-6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-021-01257-6)

## Summary

Describes an intravital imaging approach combining three-photon excitation, modal sensorless (indirect) adaptive optics and active electrocardiogram gating for deep imaging in the mouse brain. The sensorless scheme is designed to be robust at the low signal-to-noise ratios typical of deep scattering tissue and allows correction over large axial ranges. Near-diffraction-limited imaging of deep cortical spines and subcortical dendrites is shown to a depth of 1.4 mm, at the edge of the CA1 hippocampus. Deep-layer calcium imaging of astrocytes, including fibrous astrocytes in the corpus callosum, is also demonstrated.
