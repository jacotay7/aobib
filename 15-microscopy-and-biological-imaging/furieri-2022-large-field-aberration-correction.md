# Aberration measurement and correction on a large field of view in fluorescence microscopy

**Furieri et al. (2022)** · *Biomedical Optics Express 13, 262* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8803008/) · DOI: [10.1364/BOE.441810](https://doi.org/10.1364/BOE.441810)

## Summary

Presents a plug-and-play module for aberration measurement and correction across a large field of view in fluorescence microscopy. Wavefront correction uses direct wavefront reconstruction by spinning-pupil aberration measurement and a deformable lens controlled in closed loop. The lens corrects aberrations at the centre of the field, and the residual aberrations towards the edges are removed by anisoplanatic deconvolution. Results are shown for a widefield and a light-sheet fluorescence microscope, indicating that correction over the full field is achievable with a compact transmissive module in the detection path.
