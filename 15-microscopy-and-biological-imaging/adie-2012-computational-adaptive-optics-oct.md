# Computational adaptive optics for broadband optical interferometric tomography of biological tissue

**Adie et al. (2012)** · *PNAS 109, 7175* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3358872/) · DOI: [10.1073/pnas.1121193109](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1121193109)

## Summary

Presents computational adaptive optics, in which aberrations are corrected in the reconstructed tomogram of a broadband interferometric imaging system rather than in the optical beam. Based on Fourier optics, aberrations of a virtual pupil are corrected using Zernike polynomials. Combined with interferometric synthetic aperture microscopy, this allows reconstruction (within the single-scattering limit) with focal-plane resolution at all depths. Demonstrations on tissue phantoms with sub-resolution particles and ex vivo rat lung tissue show correction of data acquired with a strongly astigmatic beam, which also gave a more uniform depth-dependent signal.
