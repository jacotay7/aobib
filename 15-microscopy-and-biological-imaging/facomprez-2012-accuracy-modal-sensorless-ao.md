# Accuracy of correction in modal sensorless adaptive optics

**Facomprez et al. (2012)** · *Optics Express 20, 2598* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.20.002598) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.20.002598](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.20.002598)

## Summary

Theoretical and experimental study of the parameters that govern correction accuracy in modal sensorless adaptive optics for microscopy, using two-photon fluorescence imaging as the example. The authors show that with a suitable number of measurements, precise correction is possible for aberrations up to about 2 rad rms without optimising the choice of correction modes. They analyse the photon budget for shot-noise-limited signals and find that 10^4 to 10^5 photons suffice for complete correction. Guidelines are given for choosing an optimal correction algorithm for given experimental conditions.
