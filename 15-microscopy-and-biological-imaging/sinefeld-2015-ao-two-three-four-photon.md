# Adaptive optics in multiphoton microscopy: comparison of two, three and four photon fluorescence

**Sinefeld et al. (2015)** · *Optics Express 23, 31472* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4692258/) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.23.031472](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.23.031472)

## Summary

Demonstrates an adaptive optics system driven by nonlinear feedback from three- and four-photon fluorescence. Excitation pulses come from soliton self-frequency shifting of a 1550 nm fibre femtosecond laser, and correction uses a 1020-segment MEMS spatial light modulator perturbed with an orthogonal Walsh basis and a modified three-point phase-shifting interferometry scheme. Correction improves three-photon signal from fluorescent beads, and two-, three- and four-photon signals are compared in the same system using a dye pool. The signal improvement from aberration correction is found to grow exponentially with the order of the nonlinearity.
