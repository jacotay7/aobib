# Dynamic super-resolution structured illumination imaging in the living brain

**Turcotte et al. (2019)** · *PNAS 116, 9586* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6511017/) · DOI: [10.1073/pnas.1819965116](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1819965116)

## Summary

Applies super-resolution structured illumination microscopy (SIM) to in vivo brain imaging, addressing three-dimensional imaging in optically heterogeneous tissue that is constantly moving. Image acquisition and reconstruction are optimised to combat sample motion, and adaptive optics corrects sample-induced aberrations. The authors image the brains of live zebrafish larvae and mice and observe the dynamics of dendrites and dendritic spines at nanoscale resolution.
