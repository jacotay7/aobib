# Adaptive wavefront correction in two-photon microscopy using coherence-gated wavefront sensing

**Rueckel et al. (2006)** · *PNAS 103, 17137* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1634839/) · DOI: [10.1073/pnas.0604791103](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0604791103)

## Summary

Demonstrates adaptive wavefront correction in two-photon microscopy based on coherence-gated wavefront sensing (CGWS), in which the wavefront of backscattered light from the focal region is selected by low-coherence interferometry and measured, and a deformable mirror applies the correction. Resolution and signal improve substantially, including in living biological specimens, and a nearly diffraction-limited focus is restored even for strong aberrations. The authors argue the approach applies to samples with a wide range of scattering properties and could allow real-time pixel-by-pixel correction at fast scan rates.
