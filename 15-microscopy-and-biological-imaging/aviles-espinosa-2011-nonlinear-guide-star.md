# Measurement and correction of in vivo sample aberrations employing a nonlinear guide-star in two-photon excited fluorescence microscopy

**Aviles-Espinosa et al. (2011)** · *Biomedical Optics Express 2, 3135* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3207382/) · DOI: [10.1364/BOE.2.003135](https://doi.org/10.1364/BOE.2.003135)

## Summary

Shows that sample-induced aberrations can be measured in a nonlinear microscope using the localised two-photon excited fluorescence spot as a nonlinear guide star. The wavefront emitted from this point source is recorded with a Shack-Hartmann sensor, and the aberrations are compensated with a deformable mirror in a single step. The technique is applied to fixed and in vivo biological samples, in some cases improving the total collected signal by more than an order of magnitude.
