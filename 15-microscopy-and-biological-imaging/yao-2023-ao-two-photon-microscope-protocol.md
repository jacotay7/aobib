# Construction and use of an adaptive optics two-photon microscope with direct wavefront sensing

**Yao et al. (2023)** · *Nature Protocols 18, 3732* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11033548/) · DOI: [10.1038/s41596-023-00893-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41596-023-00893-w)

## Summary

Step-by-step protocol for building, calibrating and operating a two-photon microscope with guide-star-assisted adaptive optics that restores diffraction-limited resolution to depths close to 900 µm in the mouse cortex. The wavefront is measured directly from a guide star formed by exciting a red-shifted dye in the blood serum. The design mainly uses commercially available components, with CAD models supplied for custom parts, and is modular. Demonstrations include imaging somatostatin-expressing neurons 700 µm below the pia, calcium dynamics of layer 5b neurons and thalamocortical glutamate transmission; the protocol is stated to take about 30 days for users with graduate-level optics expertise.
