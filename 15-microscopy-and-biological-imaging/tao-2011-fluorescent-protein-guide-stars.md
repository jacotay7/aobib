# Adaptive optics microscopy with direct wavefront sensing using fluorescent protein guide stars

**Tao et al. (2011)** · *Optics Letters 36, 3389* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/51610930_Adaptive_optics_microscopy_with_direct_wavefront_sensing_using_fluorescent_protein_guide_stars) · DOI: [10.1364/OL.36.003389](https://doi.org/10.1364/OL.36.003389)

## Summary

Introduces direct wavefront sensing in tissue using structures labelled with fluorescent proteins as guide stars, demonstrated with an adaptive optics confocal microscope imaging mouse brain tissue. A dendrite and the cell body of a neuron labelled with yellow fluorescent protein serve as guide stars without injecting any other fluorescent marker. Photobleaching effects are analysed. In fixed mouse tissue at 70 μm depth, correction increases image contrast and improves signal intensity about threefold.
