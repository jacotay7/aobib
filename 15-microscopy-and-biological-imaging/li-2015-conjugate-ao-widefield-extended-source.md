# Conjugate adaptive optics in widefield microscopy with an extended-source wavefront sensor

**Li et al. (2015)** · *Optica 2, 682* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OPTICA.2.000682) · DOI: [10.1364/OPTICA.2.000682](https://doi.org/10.1364/OPTICA.2.000682)

## Summary

Describes a fast closed-loop adaptive optics implementation for widefield (non-scanning) microscopy that requires no guide star, using the sample itself as the reference. It is based on a high-resolution, single-shot wavefront sensor compatible with extended samples. The correction is applied in a conjugate AO configuration to enlarge the corrected field of view, and a fast algorithm identifies sample-induced aberrations using illumination from an arbitrarily shaped source. The principle is presented together with proof-of-concept experiments.
