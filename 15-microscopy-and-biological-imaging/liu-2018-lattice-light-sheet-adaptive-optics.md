# Observing the cell in its native state: Imaging subcellular dynamics in multicellular organisms

**Liu et al. (2018)** · *Science 360, eaaq1392* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6040645/) · DOI: [10.1126/science.aaq1392](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaq1392)

## Summary

Combines lattice light-sheet microscopy with adaptive optics (AO-LLSM) to image subcellular dynamics noninvasively within multicellular organisms. Sample-induced aberrations are measured from a two-photon-excited fluorescent guide star created within the specimen and corrected in both the excitation and detection paths, so that aberration-free, high spatiotemporal resolution volumetric imaging is possible across large multicellular volumes. Applications include endocytosis, organelle remodelling during mitosis, and the migration of axons, immune cells and metastatic cancer cells in vivo, chiefly in zebrafish. The authors use the data to examine phenotypic diversity of cells across organisms and developmental stages.
