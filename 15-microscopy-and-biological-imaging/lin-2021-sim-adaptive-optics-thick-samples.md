# Subcellular three-dimensional imaging deep through multicellular thick samples by structured illumination microscopy and adaptive optics

**Lin et al. (2021)** · *Nature Communications 12, 3148* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8149693/) · DOI: [10.1038/s41467-021-23449-6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-23449-6)

## Summary

Combines adaptive optics with three-dimensional structured illumination microscopy (SIM), which otherwise suffers loss of resolution and reconstruction artefacts in samples thicker than a single cell. The system achieves 150 nm lateral and 570 nm axial resolution at a depth of 80 µm through Caenorhabditis elegans. Adaptive optics is shown to improve three-dimensional resolution, especially axially, and to reduce artefacts across a variety of biological samples.
