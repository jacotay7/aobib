# Adaptive optical microscopy for neurobiology

**Rodríguez & Ji (2018)** · *Current Opinion in Neurobiology 50, 83* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5984664/) · DOI: [10.1016/j.conb.2018.01.011](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.conb.2018.01.011)

## Summary

Short review of adaptive optics applied to optical microscopy in neuroscience, aimed at recovering diffraction-limited imaging deep within living tissue. It summarises how aberration correction has been used to improve both structural and functional imaging of neurons in several model organisms. Highlighted examples include diffraction-limited imaging over large volumes in larval zebrafish brains, resolving dendritic spines more than 600 µm deep in the mouse brain, and more accurate measurement of orientation tuning of thalamic boutons in mouse visual cortex. The free copy is the PubMed Central author manuscript.
