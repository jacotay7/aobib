# An adaptive optics module for deep tissue multiphoton imaging in vivo

**Rodríguez et al. (2021)** · *Nature Methods 18, 1259* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9090585/) · DOI: [10.1038/s41592-021-01279-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-021-01279-0)

## Summary

Presents a compact adaptive optics module that can be added to two- and three-photon fluorescence microscopes to measure and correct tissue-induced aberrations. Aberrations are measured with a zonal, indirect (sensorless) frequency-multiplexed method, with both modulation for measurement and the correction carried out by a single high-speed deformable mirror. With the module, the authors resolve synaptic structures in deep cortical and subcortical regions of the mouse brain. They also demonstrate high-resolution imaging of neuronal structures and somatosensory-evoked calcium responses deep in the mouse spinal cord in vivo. The free copies are the PubMed Central author manuscript and the bioRxiv preprint.
