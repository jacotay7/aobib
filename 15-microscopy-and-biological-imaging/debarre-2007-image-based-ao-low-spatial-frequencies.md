# Image based adaptive optics through optimisation of low spatial frequencies

**Débarre et al. (2007)** · *Optics Express 15, 8176* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.15.008176) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.15.008176](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.15.008176)

## Summary

Presents a wavefront-sensorless adaptive optics scheme for incoherent imaging systems in which correction maximises an image-quality metric based on low spatial frequency content. Images are acquired with different aberration biases applied, and the correction is estimated from this sequence. Expanding aberrations in Lukosz modes makes the effect of each mode separable, so each mode can be optimised independently as a quadratic maximisation requiring three image measurements per mode. The scheme is demonstrated in an incoherent transmission microscope, and the authors show that the range of spatial frequencies in the metric tunes the sensitivity to aberration magnitude.
