# Adaptive illumination based on direct wavefront sensing in a light-sheet fluorescence microscope

**Wilding et al. (2016)** · *Optics Express 24, 24896* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.24.024896) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.24.024896](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.24.024896)

## Summary

Develops a method for adaptive correction of phase aberrations in the illumination arm of a light-sheet fluorescence microscope. Aberrations present in the sample are detected by direct wavefront sensing on epi-fluorescence light. This measurement is then used to correct the light-sheet illumination with a spatial light modulator operating in feed-forward mode. Improved axial resolution is demonstrated by imaging Tg(fli:GFP) zebrafish embryos.
