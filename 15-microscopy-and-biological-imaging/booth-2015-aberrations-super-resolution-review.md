# Aberrations and adaptive optics in super-resolution microscopy

**Booth et al. (2015)** · *Microscopy 64, 251* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4711293/) · DOI: [10.1093/jmicro/dfv033](https://doi.org/10.1093/jmicro/dfv033)

## Summary

Review of how optical aberrations affect the main families of super-resolution fluorescence microscopy and how adaptive optics can compensate them. The authors discuss single-molecule switching (localization) microscopy, stimulated emission depletion (STED) and structured illumination microscopy (SIM) separately, explaining how each is sensitive to aberrations in a different way because of its image-formation mechanism. Implementations of adaptive optics for each technique are described, including the beam paths that must be corrected and suitable image-quality metrics.
