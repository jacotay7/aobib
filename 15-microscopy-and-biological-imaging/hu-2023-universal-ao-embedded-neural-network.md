# Universal adaptive optics for microscopy through embedded neural network control

**Hu et al. (2023)** · *Light: Science & Applications 12, 270* · [Free full text](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41377-023-01297-x) · DOI: [10.1038/s41377-023-01297-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41377-023-01297-x)

## Summary

Proposes a physics-based, machine-learning-assisted wavefront-sensorless adaptive optics control method (MLAO) intended to transfer across microscope modalities. A specially constructed neural network, whose architecture reflects the physics of microscope image formation, is embedded in the AO control loop; it is orders of magnitude simpler than earlier neural-network approaches. The method is demonstrated on two-photon, three-photon and widefield 3D structured illumination microscopes, where it outperformed commonly used modal sensorless methods. It remained robust to 3D sample structure, specimen motion, low signal-to-noise ratio and activity-induced fluorescence fluctuations, and the network's internal configuration can be interpreted physically.
