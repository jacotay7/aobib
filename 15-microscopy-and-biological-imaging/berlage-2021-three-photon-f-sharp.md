# Deep tissue scattering compensation with three-photon F-SHARP

**Berlage et al. (2021)** · *Optica 8, 1613* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OPTICA.440279) · DOI: [10.1364/OPTICA.440279](https://doi.org/10.1364/OPTICA.440279)

## Summary

Extends focus-scanning holographic aberration probing (F-SHARP) scattering compensation to three-photon excitation. The authors report that the three-photon version converges faster than comparable two-photon techniques and works reliably on densely labelled samples where two-photon approaches fail. The method is applied to imaging dendritic spines on GFP-labelled layer 5 neurons in an anesthetised mouse.
