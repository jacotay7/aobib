# Live imaging using adaptive optics with fluorescent protein guide-stars

**Tao et al. (2012)** · *Optics Express 20, 15969* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3601654/) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.20.015969](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.20.015969)

## Summary

Describes an adaptive optics microscope with direct Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensing that uses genetically expressed fluorescent proteins as guide stars for live imaging. Imaging of Drosophila embryos shows correction of aberrations and near-diffraction-limited images of medial sections of large embryos. GFP-Polo-labelled centrosomes, not visible before correction, are clearly resolved afterwards, and four-dimensional time-lapse imaging with correction of dynamic aberrations is demonstrated. The work identifies GFP-tagged centrosome proteins (Polo and Cnn) as suitable biological guide stars.
