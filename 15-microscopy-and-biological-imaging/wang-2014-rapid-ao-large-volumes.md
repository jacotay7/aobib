# Rapid adaptive optical recovery of optimal resolution over large volumes

**Wang et al. (2014)** · *Nature Methods 11, 625* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4069208/) · DOI: [10.1038/nmeth.2925](https://doi.org/10.1038/nmeth.2925)

## Summary

Demonstrates adaptive correction of complex aberrations at high numerical aperture using a descanned, laser-induced guide star and direct wavefront sensing, with a 14 ms update time. Scanning the guide star and descanning its emission averages the wavefront over a region, and the fast measurement allows the correction to follow the rapid spatial variation of aberrations in biological specimens. Diffraction-limited imaging is recovered over large volumes (more than 240 µm per side). The method is applied to imaging fine neuronal processes and subcellular dynamics in the zebrafish brain.
