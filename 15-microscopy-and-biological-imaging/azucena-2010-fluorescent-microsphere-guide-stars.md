# Wavefront aberration measurements and corrections through thick tissue using fluorescent microsphere reference beacons

**Azucena et al. (2010)** · *Optics Express 18, 17521* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3408921/) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.18.017521](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.18.017521)

## Summary

Uses fluorescent microspheres injected into Drosophila embryos as artificial guide stars for direct wavefront measurement with a Shack-Hartmann sensor in a microscope, borrowing the astronomical laser-guide-star concept. The measured wavefront error is corrected in 10 ms steps by applying its inverse on a MEMS deformable mirror in the imaging path. When imaging through 100 µm of tissue the Strehl ratio improved by a factor of 2 on average and up to 10. The isoplanatic half-width was measured to be about 19 µm, giving a corrected field of view of about 38 µm around the guide star.
