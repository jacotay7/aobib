# Field of view advantage of conjugate adaptive optics in microscopy applications

**Mertz et al. (2015)** · *Applied Optics 54, 3498* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4629780/) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.54.003498](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.54.003498)

## Summary

Compares, theoretically and experimentally, two placements of the deformable mirror in an adaptive optics microscope: conjugate to the objective pupil (pupil AO) and conjugate to the main source of aberrations (conjugate AO). The comparison uses the simplified case of spatially variant aberrations produced by a well-defined phase screen, with attention to the resulting corrected field of view. Conjugate AO is found to give a substantially larger corrected field. The authors note that this is well known in astronomy and aim to recast it for the optical microscopy community.
