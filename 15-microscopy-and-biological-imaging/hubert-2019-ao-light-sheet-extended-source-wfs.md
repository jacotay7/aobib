# Adaptive optics light-sheet microscopy based on direct wavefront sensing without any guide star

**Hubert et al. (2019)** · *Optics Letters 44, 2514* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-02163939) · DOI: [10.1364/OL.44.002514](https://doi.org/10.1364/OL.44.002514)

## Summary

Presents an adaptive optics light-sheet fluorescence microscope with closed-loop aberration correction in the emission path based on direct wavefront sensing, without iterative optimisation and without a guide star. The design uses a modified Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensor that works with extended sources, such as the images produced by optical-sectioning microscopes. The authors characterise the sensor performance and demonstrate contrast improvement on neuronal structures in depth in the ex vivo adult Drosophila brain.
