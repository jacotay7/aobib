# Practical sensorless aberration estimation for 3D microscopy with deep learning

**Saha et al. (2020)** · *Optics Express 28, 29044* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7679184/) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.401933](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.401933)

## Summary

Addresses the estimation of optical aberrations from volumetric intensity images as a step in sensorless adaptive optics for 3D microscopy. Because ground-truth experimental training data are hard to obtain, the authors train neural networks only on simulated data and show that they give accurate predictions on real experimental images. The approach is validated on simulated data and on experimental datasets from two microscopy modalities and compared with non-learned methods. The study also examines the predictability of individual aberration modes, finding that wavefront symmetry plays a key role, and releases an open-source Python implementation.
