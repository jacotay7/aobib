# Scattering compensation by focus scanning holographic aberration probing (F-SHARP)

**Papadopoulos et al. (2017)** · *Nature Photonics 11, 116* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.17169/refubium-29928) · DOI: [10.1038/nphoton.2016.252](https://doi.org/10.1038/nphoton.2016.252)

## Summary

Presents F-SHARP, a non-invasive method for measuring the scattered electric-field point spread function (amplitude and phase) inside a turbid sample in three dimensions. The technique scans a probe focus relative to a reference focus and uses the nonlinear (two-photon) signal to interferometrically recover the E-field PSF, which is then used to optically cancel sample-induced distortions. Imaging capabilities are demonstrated on a variety of samples, including through vertebrate brains and through thinned skull in vivo. The free copy is the version deposited in the Freie Universität Berlin repository (Refubium).
