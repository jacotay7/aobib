# Adaptive optics in microscopy

**Booth (2007)** · *Phil. Trans. R. Soc. A 365, 2829* · [Free full text](http://www.its.caltech.edu/~bi177/private/Adaptive%20optics%20in%20microscopy-2007-PhilTrans2829.full.pdf) · DOI: [10.1098/rsta.2007.0013](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsta.2007.0013)

## Summary

A review of adaptive optics for optical microscopy, where aberrations, often caused by the specimen itself, reduce resolution and contrast. It explains why microscope AO departs from the schemes used in astronomy, and discusses the sources and effects of aberrations and their correction, particularly in confocal and two-photon microscopes. Methods of direct wavefront sensing, indirect (sensorless) aberration measurement and the available correction devices are covered. The review also covers related uses of adaptive optics in optical data storage, optical tweezers and micro- and nanofabrication. The linked copy is hosted on a Caltech course page.
