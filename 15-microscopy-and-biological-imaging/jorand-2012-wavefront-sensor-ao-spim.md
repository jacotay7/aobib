# Deep and clear optical imaging of thick inhomogeneous samples

**Jorand et al. (2012)** · *PLoS ONE 7, e35795* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3338470/) · DOI: [10.1371/journal.pone.0035795](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0035795)

## Summary

Combines selective plane illumination microscopy (SPIM) with wavefront-sensor-based adaptive optics (waoSPIM). Aberrations in the detection path are measured directly with a Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensor, using fluorescent beads in the sample as point-source emitters, and corrected with a deformable mirror. The method is used to correct distortions in three-dimensional imaging and to improve images from deep within thick, optically inhomogeneous biological samples.
