# Deep learning-driven adaptive optics for single-molecule localization microscopy

**Zhang et al. (2023)** · *Nature Methods 20, 1748* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10630144/) · DOI: [10.1038/s41592-023-02029-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-023-02029-0)

## Summary

Develops deep-learning-driven adaptive optics for single-molecule localization microscopy (SMLM), motivated by the inconsistent response of conventional image-quality metrics used in iterative sensorless AO in tissue. A trained deep neural network monitors individual single-molecule emission patterns, infers their shared wavefront distortion, and passes the estimate through a dynamic filter to drive a deformable mirror in near real time. The method estimates and compensates 28 wavefront deformation modes simultaneously. It improves the resolution and fidelity of 3D SMLM through brain tissue specimens more than 130 µm thick.
