# Adaptive optics enables 3D STED microscopy in aberrating specimens

**Gould et al. (2012)** · *Optics Express 20, 20998* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3635694/) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.20.020998](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.20.020998)

## Summary

First implementation of adaptive optics in stimulated emission depletion (STED) microscopy. Three-dimensional STED imaging of thick specimens is hindered by sample-induced aberrations that distort the depletion focus. The authors apply aberration correction to enable 3D super-resolution imaging under strongly aberrating conditions such as those introduced by thick biological tissue.
