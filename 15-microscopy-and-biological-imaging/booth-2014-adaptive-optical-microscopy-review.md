# Adaptive optical microscopy: the ongoing quest for a perfect image

**Booth (2014)** · *Light: Science & Applications 3, e165* · [Free full text](https://www.nature.com/articles/lsa201446) · DOI: [10.1038/lsa.2014.46](https://doi.org/10.1038/lsa.2014.46)

## Summary

Review of adaptive optics in high-resolution optical microscopy, where aberrations arise from the refractive-index structure of the specimen rather than from the atmosphere. It reviews recent developments in adaptive microscopy methods, including aberration measurement with and without a wavefront sensor, and their applications. Advances across a range of microscope modalities are covered, with emphasis on applications that require images from deep within biological tissue. The article closes with a discussion of prospects for the field.
