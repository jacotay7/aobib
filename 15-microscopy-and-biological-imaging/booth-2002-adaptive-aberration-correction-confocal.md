# Adaptive aberration correction in a confocal microscope

**Booth et al. (2002)** · *PNAS 99, 5788* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC122854/) · DOI: [10.1073/pnas.082544799](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.082544799)

## Summary

Demonstrates an adaptive confocal fluorescence microscope that corrects system- and specimen-induced aberrations. Aberrations are measured with a modal wavefront sensor that directly estimates Zernike mode amplitudes and, like the confocal microscope, has axial selectivity; correction is applied with a deformable membrane mirror. The paper discusses why microscopy requires wavefront-sensing approaches different from those used in astronomy. Corrected images of biological specimens show improved contrast and restoration of axial resolution.
