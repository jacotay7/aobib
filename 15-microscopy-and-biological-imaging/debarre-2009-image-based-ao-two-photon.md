# Image-based adaptive optics for two-photon microscopy

**Débarre et al. (2009)** · *Optics Letters 34, 2495* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3320043/) · DOI: [10.1364/OL.34.002495](https://doi.org/10.1364/OL.34.002495)

## Summary

Demonstrates wavefront-sensorless aberration correction in a two-photon excited fluorescence microscope. Based on an analysis of the image-formation process, the authors develop a correction scheme that improves image quality while requiring minimal additional exposure of the sample. As a result the correction induces little photobleaching. Improved visibility of small structures is shown in a range of fresh and fixed biological tissues. The free copy is the PubMed Central author manuscript.
