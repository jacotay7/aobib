# Direct wavefront sensing for high-resolution in vivo imaging in scattering tissue

**Wang et al. (2015)** · *Nature Communications 6, 7276* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4490402/) · DOI: [10.1038/ncomms8276](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms8276)

## Summary

Extends adaptive optics based on direct imaging of a laser-induced guide star's wavefront to tissues that strongly scatter visible light. The key step is the use of near-infrared guide stars, whose emission is scattered less, so that a Shack-Hartmann measurement remains possible deep in tissue. The method enables in vivo two-photon morphological and functional imaging down to 700 µm inside the mouse brain.
