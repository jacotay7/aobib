# Adaptive optics improves multiphoton super-resolution imaging

**Zheng et al. (2017)** · *Nature Methods 14, 869* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7749710/) · DOI: [10.1038/nmeth.4337](https://doi.org/10.1038/nmeth.4337)

## Summary

Improves multiphoton structured illumination microscopy by measuring optical aberrations with a nonlinear guide star and correcting them with a deformable mirror. The method is demonstrated on bead phantoms, cells in collagen gels, nematode larvae and embryos, Drosophila brain and zebrafish embryos. At depths of about 250 µm, peak intensity increased up to 40-fold and resolution was recovered to about 176 nm laterally and 729 nm axially.
