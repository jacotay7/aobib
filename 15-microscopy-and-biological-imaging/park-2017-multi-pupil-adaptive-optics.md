# Large-field-of-view imaging by multi-pupil adaptive optics

**Park et al. (2017)** · *Nature Methods 14, 581* · [Free full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5482233/) · DOI: [10.1038/nmeth.4290](https://doi.org/10.1038/nmeth.4290)

## Summary

Introduces multi-pupil adaptive optics (MPAO), which performs spatially independent wavefront correction for several subregions of the field simultaneously. This gives correction over a 450 × 450 µm² field of view, nine times the area of conventional pupil adaptive optics. The independent control of each subregion also enables three-dimensional imaging of nonplanar surfaces. MPAO is applied to in vivo structural and functional imaging in the mouse brain.
