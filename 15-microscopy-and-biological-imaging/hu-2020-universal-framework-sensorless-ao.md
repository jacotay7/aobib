# A universal framework for microscope sensorless adaptive optics: Generalized aberration representations

**Hu et al. (2020)** · *APL Photonics 5, 100801* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0022523) · DOI: [10.1063/5.0022523](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0022523)

## Summary

Proposes a common framework that encompasses the main families of wavefront-sensorless adaptive optics used in microscopes, including modal, pupil-segmentation zonal and pixelated piston-based methods. A general model of aberration representation is introduced so that these schemes can be compared systematically. Through modelling and experiments in a two-photon microscope, sensorless schemes with different representations are compared on simulated and sample-induced aberrations. The results indicate that different representations suit different experimental scenarios, informing the choice of method for a given application.
