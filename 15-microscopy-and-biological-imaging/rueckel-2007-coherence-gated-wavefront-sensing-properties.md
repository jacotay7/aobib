# Properties of coherence-gated wavefront sensing

**Rueckel & Denk (2007)** · *JOSA A 24, 3517* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/5870128_Properties_of_coherence-gated_wavefront_sensing) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.24.003517](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.24.003517)

## Summary

Analyses coherence-gated wavefront sensing (CGWS), which measures wavefront aberrations inside strongly scattering tissue so they can be corrected with adaptive optics, for example to restore diffraction-limited light microscopy. The authors build a model based on ray tracing of ballistic light scattered from discrete scatterers, describing how performance depends on coherence length, scatterer density, coherence-gate position and polarization. The model is evaluated with Monte Carlo simulation and checked against experimental measurements. They show in particular that circularly polarized light allows all aberrations needed for adaptive correction to be sensed correctly.
