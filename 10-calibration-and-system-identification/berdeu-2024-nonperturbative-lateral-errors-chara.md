# Open loop calibration and closed loop non-perturbative estimation of the lateral errors of an adaptive optics system: examples with GRAVITY+ and CHARA experimental data

**Berdeu et al. (2024)** · *Proc. SPIE 13097, 130970T* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.06569) · DOI: [10.1117/12.3019843](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3019843)

## Summary

Presents an improved perturbative open-loop method for quickly assessing large lateral DM/WFS errors by correlating the measured response to a few two-dimensional spatial modes with a synthetic model. It then introduces a model-free, non-perturbative closed-loop method based on the temporal correlation of 2D spatial frequencies in the deformable-mirror commands, which does not affect science observations and is sparse in Fourier space. Results are shown on the GRAVITY+ development bench (1432 actuators) and on sky with CHARA and GRAVITY/CIAO, demonstrating applicability to non-standard geometries such as hexagonal 60-actuator systems.
