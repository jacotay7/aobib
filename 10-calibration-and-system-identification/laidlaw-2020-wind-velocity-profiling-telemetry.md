# Automated wind velocity profiling from adaptive optics telemetry

**Laidlaw et al. (2020)** · *MNRAS 491, 1287* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-03259636) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stz3062](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stz3062)

## Summary

Temporal control techniques require the wind velocity as a function of altitude, which can in principle be extracted from spatio-temporal SLODAR analysis of AO telemetry, but the limited altitude resolution of current systems makes it hard to separate layers automatically. The paper introduces a technique for automated wind-velocity profiling from AO telemetry. It is demonstrated with simulated and on-sky centroid data from CANARY on the William Herschel Telescope, and on-sky profiles are compared with contemporaneous Stereo-SCIDAR measurements and with ECMWF forecasts. The software is released as open source.
