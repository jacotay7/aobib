# Non-Common Path Aberrations correction for the GTC Adaptive Optics system: calibration and compensation of static aberrations

**Montilla et al. (2019)** · *Proc. AO4ELT6 (Québec, 2019)* · [Free full text](https://ao4elt6.copl.ulaval.ca/proceedings/401-rp7W-311.pdf)

## Summary

Describes the NCPA calibration of the GTC AO system (373-actuator DM, 312-subaperture Shack-Hartmann WFS) needed to deliver the expected K-band Strehl ratio to the FRIDA instrument. Phase diversity is implemented by introducing known aberrations with the deformable mirror and recording the aberrated images on the science camera, which avoids moving the instrument detector but makes the result sensitive to errors in the DM and camera models. The paper discusses the modelling and calibration issues encountered, the practical implementation of phase diversity in the laboratory, and results of optical-bench tests.
