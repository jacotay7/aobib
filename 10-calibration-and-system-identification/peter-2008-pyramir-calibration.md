# PYRAMIR: Calibration and Operation of a Pyramid Near-Infrared Wavefront Sensor

**Peter et al. (2008)** · *PASP 120, 872* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.0137) · DOI: [10.1086/590479](https://doi.org/10.1086/590479)

## Summary

Investigates the calibration and operational behaviour of PYRAMIR, a near-infrared pyramid wavefront sensor at the Calar Alto 3.5-m telescope, under intrinsic error sources (diffraction at the pyramid edges, detector readout noise) and external ones (a Gaussian pupil illumination during calibration, an optically resolved reference source, non-common-path aberrations). The effect of three reference-source sizes on the calibration is measured, and the response is quantified in terms of modal cross-talk and aliasing. The tip-tilt behaviour of the system is examined separately. From these measurements the authors derive a procedure to optimize the calibration and set-up of a pyramid-based AO system and compute total error budgets to choose the number of calibrated modes.
