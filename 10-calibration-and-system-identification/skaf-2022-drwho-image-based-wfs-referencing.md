# On-sky validation of image-based adaptive optics wavefront sensor referencing

**Skaf et al. (2022)** · *A&A 659, A170* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.14997) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202141514](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202141514)

## Summary

Presents DrWHO, an algorithm that compensates static and quasi-static aberrations such as NCPA by updating the wavefront-sensor reference at every iteration, moving the AO loop's convergence point towards a better focal-plane image. References are computed with an iterative lucky-imaging approach that favours high-quality focal-plane frames, so the method needs neither a model nor a linear, calibrated WFS. Simulations show convergence towards correction of 82% of the NCPA. On-sky tests on SCExAO at Subaru over a 10-minute run at 750 nm show a 15.7% improvement in a flux-concentration metric of PSF quality.
