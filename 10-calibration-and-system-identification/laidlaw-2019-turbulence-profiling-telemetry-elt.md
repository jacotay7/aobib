# Optimizing the accuracy and efficiency of optical turbulence profiling using adaptive optics telemetry for extremely large telescopes

**Laidlaw et al. (2019)** · *MNRAS 483, 4341* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.05860) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/sty3285](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/sty3285)

## Summary

Optical turbulence profiles can be measured from AO telemetry by fitting an analytic covariance model to the cross-covariance of Shack-Hartmann centroids. The paper shows that restricting the fit to a covariance-map region of interest improves accuracy and introduces a technique that uses this region to measure and compensate WFS misalignments. Covariance-matrix and covariance-map fitting are compared using simulations and on-sky CANARY data from the William Herschel Telescope, with on-sky profiles compared against contemporaneous Stereo-SCIDAR measurements. The covariance-map approach reduces fitting time for an ELT-scale system by a factor of 72, and the software is released as open source.
