# Calibration of NAOS and CONICA static aberrations. Application of the phase diversity technique

**Blanc et al. (2003)** · *A&A 399, 373* · [Free full text](https://www.aanda.org/articles/aa/pdf/2003/07/aa2912.pdf) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361:20021655](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361:20021655)

## Summary

Describes and evaluates a phase-diversity wavefront sensor used to measure the static (non-common-path) aberrations of the VLT adaptive optics system NAOS and its camera CONICA. The paper compiles the main limitations of the technique, examining systematic errors from the experimental implementation and design restrictions, from imperfect knowledge of the system, and from the estimation algorithm. The effect of each error source on the wavefront estimate is assessed with numerical and experimental data. The study sets out the verifications and calibrations needed for accurate results and is intended as a practical guide to applying phase diversity. The calibration results and resulting performance gain on NAOS-CONICA are given in the companion paper by Hartung et al. (2003).
