# Pathfinder first light: alignment, calibration, and commissioning of the LINC-NIRVANA ground-layer adaptive optics subsystem

**Kopon et al. (2014)** · *Proc. SPIE 9148, 914828* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.5855) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2056737](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2056737)

## Summary

Describes the alignment and calibration of the Pathfinder, the ground-layer wavefront sensor of the LINC-NIRVANA MCAO system for one side of the Large Binocular Telescope, which uses 12 pyramid sensors to optically co-add light from natural guide stars and drives the LBT adaptive secondary mirror. As part of a modular commissioning strategy, the Pathfinder was integrated and tested on its own during seven runs at the LBT in 2013. The paper covers integration with the telescope control system, alignment to the telescope optical axis, off-sky closed-loop AO calibration and finally on-sky closed-loop operation. It presents the alignment scheme and off-sky calibration test that led to the first on-sky closed-loop images.
