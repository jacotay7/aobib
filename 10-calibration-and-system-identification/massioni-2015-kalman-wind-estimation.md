# Adaptive distributed Kalman filtering with wind estimation for astronomical adaptive optics

**Massioni et al. (2015)** · *JOSA A 32, 2353* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-01225941) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.32.002353](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.32.002353)

## Summary

Shows that the phase estimate of a Kalman filter running on an AO system can be exploited to estimate the wind velocity of frozen-flow turbulent layers, and that this estimate can be fed back to update the filter, making it adaptive. The adaptive controller is implemented with a distributed Kalman filter in a realistic simulation of a laser-guide-star MCAO system on a 30 m telescope, with negligible added computation. The wind estimation is compared with a previously published SLODAR wind profiler, and the impact of turbulence-profile quantisation is assessed. A main finding is that the adaptive distributed Kalman filter is more sensitive to profile quantisation than the static minimum-mean-square-error estimator.
