# Atmospheric turbulence profiling with SLODAR using multiple adaptive optics wavefront sensors

**Wang, Schöck & Chanan (2008)** · *Applied Optics 47, 1880* · [Free full text](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/eg5tz-c2f68) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.47.001880](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.47.001880)

## Summary

The SLODAR method recovers the vertical turbulence profile from time-averaged spatial cross-correlations of wavefront slopes measured by Shack-Hartmann sensors looking at different guide stars. This paper applies it to data from the Palomar multiple guide star unit (MGSU), a test bed built to study tomographic multi-guide-star AO, and describes the data reduction from observations made in 2006. It also uses temporally delayed cross-correlations to estimate the wind velocities of the turbulent layers. The authors show that this wind profiling improves the height resolution of SLODAR in addition to providing layer velocities.
