# On-sky results for adaptive optics control with data-driven models on low-order modes

**Sinquin et al. (2020)** · *MNRAS 498, 3228* · [Free full text](https://iogs.hal.science/hal-03169410) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/staa2562](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/staa2562)

## Summary

LQG tip-tilt regulators built on stochastic models identified from AO telemetry compensate atmospheric disturbance, windshake and vibrations and can be periodically retuned. This paper extends the approach to more low-order modes: a global stochastic dynamical model of a chosen number of turbulent low-order modes is identified from wavefront-sensor data by data-driven modelling, while higher modes use second-order autoregressive priors. The resulting LQG regulator combines a dedicated tip-tilt loop with a deformable mirror without mode decoupling. Performance is evaluated on CANARY in July 2019 through H-band Strehl ratios and replay of on-sky telemetry.
