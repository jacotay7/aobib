# Online estimation of the wavefront outer scale profile from adaptive optics telemetry

**Guesalaga et al. (2017)** · *MNRAS 465, 1984* · [Free full text](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article-pdf/465/2/1984/13479902/stw2548.pdf) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stw2548](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stw2548)

## Summary

Describes an online method to estimate the profile of the wavefront outer scale, L0(h), from AO loop data of facility instruments, motivated by its influence on the estimation of Cn2(h), r0, isoplanatic angle and coherence time and on wide-field AO performance. Cross-correlation functions of slopes from two or more wavefront sensors are fitted with a linear combination of simulated theoretical layers at different altitudes and outer-scale values. The authors analyse limitations, including insensitivity to outer scales much larger than the telescope diameter, the maximum number of observable layers, and the minimum data length needed while keeping turbulence stationary. The method is applied to telemetry from the Gemini South multi-conjugate AO system GeMS, with five wavefront sensors and two deformable mirrors, to derive L0 statistics.
