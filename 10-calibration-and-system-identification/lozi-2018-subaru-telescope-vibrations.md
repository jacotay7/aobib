# Characterizing vibrations at the Subaru Telescope for the Subaru coronagraphic extreme adaptive optics instrument

**Lozi et al. (2018)** · *JATIS 4, 049001* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.08296) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.4.4.049001](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.4.4.049001)

## Summary

Studies vibrations affecting SCExAO at the Subaru Telescope, the most vibration-sensitive instrument on the telescope. Vibrations, mostly below 10 Hz, were observed for all targets and, using accelerometers, traced to the telescope itself rather than the instrument. They are related to the encoder pitch of the altitude and azimuth drives, with frequencies scaling with rotation speed, plus a strong altitude-axis vibration near transit when the altitude rate falls below 0.12 arcsec/s; the 10 Hz telescope control loop amplifies them between 4 and 6 Hz. The authors show the vibration frequencies can be predicted from the target coordinates alone, providing input for predictive wavefront control.
