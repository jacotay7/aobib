# Deconstructing turbulence and optimising GLAO using imaka telemetry

**Lai et al. (2018)** · *Proc. SPIE 10703, 107036D* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-02340523) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2313356](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2313356)

## Summary

Uses global slope covariance matrices from the 'imaka GLAO system on the UH 2.2 m telescope to reconstruct the ground-layer strength, the integrated turbulence and the vertical turbulence profile, compared with simultaneous Maunakea MASS/DIMM profiles. A method is developed to compute the phase structure function directly from slope covariances in the telemetry, allowing tests of Kolmogorov/von Karman models and of the spatial-frequency content of turbulence, such as excess high-frequency power from dome and tube seeing. The paper also introduces the AIR-FLOW portable turbulence sensor project for scanning dome and telescope-tube turbulence.
