# Determination of the optical turbulence parameters from the adaptive optics telemetry: critical analysis and on-sky validation

**Jolissaint et al. (2018)** · *Applied Optics 57, 7837* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/327632348_Determination_of_the_optical_turbulence_parameters_from_the_adaptive_optics_telemetry_critical_analysis_and_on-sky_validation) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.57.007837](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.57.007837)

## Summary

Within the Keck PSF-reconstruction project, identifies as exhaustively as possible the systematic and random errors that affect estimates of seeing and outer scale derived from deformable-mirror telemetry, and ways to keep them below 10%. Seeing from the improved DM-based method was compared with more than 70 nearly simultaneous seeing measurements from open-loop PSFs on the same optical axis, agreeing to about 10%. Comparison with a DIMM/MASS seeing monitor located away from the telescope showed weak, sometimes no, correlation. The authors conclude that DM-based seeing can be accurate if all error terms are handled, while seeing from non-collocated monitors is of limited use for this purpose.
