# Auto-calibration and performance monitoring of GRAVITY+ Adaptive Optics system with physics-based methods and inverse problem approaches

**Berdeu et al. (2025)** · *Proc. AO4ELT8 (Viña del Mar, 2025)* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-05361914) · DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.21104330](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21104330)

## Summary

Overview of the calibration and monitoring software developed for the GRAVITY+ AO systems, whose deformable mirrors, wavefront sensors and pupil move relative to each other across the VLT infrastructure. Five modules are presented with on-sky performance: a pseudo-synthetic interaction-matrix module that compensates system rotation using a physical DM model, estimators of WFS/DM lateral mis-registration for open-loop bootstrapping and closed-loop auto-alignment, a spot monitor for faint-star acquisition, an atmospheric and performance monitor including a multi-layer wind profiler, and a pupil monitor. The work is presented as a prefiguration of operational needs for AO on giant telescopes.
