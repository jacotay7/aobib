# Estimating Atmospheric Wind Speeds From Gemini Planet Imager AO Telemetry

**Du et al. (2024)** · *Proc. SPIE 13097, 130974E* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.01193) · DOI: [10.1117/12.3020607](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3020607)

## Summary

The effective wind velocity sets the atmospheric coherence time and hence how fast an AO system must correct; fast high-altitude layers cause the wind-driven halo that limits coronagraphic contrast. The paper presents two methods for estimating the effective wind velocity from pseudo-open-loop wavefront-sensor slopes: one based on spatio-temporal covariance maps and one based on the power spectral density of the defocus term. Results are shown on simulated data and, preliminarily, on Gemini Planet Imager AO telemetry. It builds on an earlier covariance-map study by Levinstein et al. (2022).
