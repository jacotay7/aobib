# Towards higher resolution profiling using filtered laser-based adaptive optics telemetry

**Béchet (2022)** · *Proc. SPIE 12185 (Adaptive Optics Systems VIII)* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-04970424) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2630488](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2630488)

## Summary

Laser-guide-star AO telemetry contains information on the vertical profiles of Cn2, outer scale and wind speed, usually extracted from cross-covariance maps of Shack-Hartmann slopes, but low-order modes are poorly constrained in LGS systems. This study analyses, in a unified formalism, filtering strategies that build local derivative estimates (curvatures or higher-order derivatives) from the slopes before computing covariances. Such filtering strongly reduces sensitivity to the outer scale and sharpens the cross-covariance peaks, which simplifies layer detection and may improve the vertical resolution of SLODAR-type profiling. An automated profiling algorithm is presented with results on simulated data and on-sky data from the VLT Adaptive Optics Facility.
