# Calibration and precompensation of noncommon path aberrations for extreme adaptive optics

**Sauvage et al. (2007)** · *JOSA A 24, 2334* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0703077) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.24.002334](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.24.002334)

## Summary

Non-common path aberrations are static aberrations not seen by the AO wavefront sensor and therefore not corrected in closed loop, which limits extreme AO performance. The paper presents experimental validation, on the ONERA AO bench, of procedures for measuring and pre-compensating NCPA. Measurement relies on refined phase-diversity algorithms, and pre-compensation uses a pseudo-closed-loop scheme that avoids depending on an accurate model of the wavefront sensor. The resulting images reach a Strehl ratio of 98.7% at 632.8 nm.
