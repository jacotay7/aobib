# Validation through simulations of a Cn2 profiler for the ESO/VLT Adaptive Optics Facility

**Garcia-Rissmann et al. (2015)** · *MNRAS 448, 2594* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.05525) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stv169](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stv169)

## Summary

The VLT Adaptive Optics Facility uses multiple wavefront sensors with a deformable secondary mirror and four laser guide stars for GLAO and LTAO modes, whose performance benefits from knowledge of turbulence stratification. This work validates, entirely through end-to-end simulations, a Cn2 profiler adapted to the AOF specifications. Because absolute calibration depends on r0 and L0, only normalised profiles are tested, along with sensitivity to input-parameter uncertainties and to different turbulence distributions. The profiler includes a correction for unseen turbulence important for the GRAAL mode, and simulations with typical Paranal profiles show that input features are reliably identified for all AOF modes.
