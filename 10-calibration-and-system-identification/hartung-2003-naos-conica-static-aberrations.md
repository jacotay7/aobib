# Calibration of NAOS and CONICA static aberrations. Experimental results

**Hartung et al. (2003)** · *A&A 399, 385* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-03801094) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361:20021744](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361:20021744)

## Summary

Presents the implementation and results of non-common-path aberration calibration for NAOS-CONICA, the first adaptive optics system of the VLT. The aberrations of the CONICA camera optics and the NAOS beam splitters lie outside the AO loop and are measured separately from the AO wavefront sensor using phase diversity. For each instrument configuration the measured aberrations are expressed as sets of Zernike coefficients and passed to the AO system, which pre-compensates them. The paper details the instrumental implementation of phase diversity, the calibration results, and the resulting gain in overall optical performance. It is the companion to Blanc et al. (2003), which analyses the phase-diversity method and its error sources.
