# Analysis of GeMS tip-tilt on-sky data: LQG implementation for vibration rejections

**Leboulleux et al. (2015)** · *Proc. AO4ELT4 (Los Angeles, 2015)* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-04023215) · DOI: [10.20353/K3T4CP1131621](https://doi.org/10.20353/K3T4CP1131621)

## Summary

A 37 Hz vibration of the Gemini South secondary mirror detected in January 2015 limits the performance of GPI and GeMS. Using GeMS tip-tilt on-sky data, the paper analyses its impact and proposes the tip-tilt control strategy first tested on CANARY and used on SPHERE, which identifies a sum of second-order autoregressive disturbance models from telemetry and uses them in an LQG controller. Analysis in replay mode predicts a gain of 15 to 20 mas compared with the integral controller, ahead of implementation in the GeMS real-time computer.
