# Innovative Calibration Strategies for Large Adaptive Telescopes with Pyramid Wave-Front Sensors

**Héritier (2019)** · *PhD thesis, Aix-Marseille Université* · [Free full text](https://theses.hal.science/tel-02390861)

## Summary

Doctoral thesis on calibrating AO systems of large adaptive telescopes equipped with pyramid wavefront sensors, where there is often no external calibration source and DM/WFS mis-registrations evolve during observations. The first part develops and validates on sky a pseudo-synthetic model of the LBT AO system (pyramid WFS and adaptive secondary), showing an interaction matrix from the model performs as well as a measured one. The second part studies online identification of mis-registration parameters, comparing an invasive approach that dithers a few well-chosen signals on the DM with a non-invasive approach that estimates a noisy interaction matrix from closed-loop telemetry. The thesis also discusses the pyramid WFS optical gains in this context.
