# Impact of DM misregistration for NCPA compensation in HARMONI's SCAO system

**Harvey et al. (2023)** · *Proc. AO4ELT7 (Avignon, 2023)* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-04402856) · DOI: [10.13009/AO4ELT7-2023-064](https://doi.org/10.13009/AO4ELT7-2023-064)

## Summary

In HARMONI's SCAO mode a calibration deformable mirror and a low-order DM are used together to calibrate and correct NCPA between the wavefront-sensor path and the science detectors, but the optical design rotates the low-order DM with respect to the calibration DM and places them several metres apart. Monte Carlo simulations quantify how residual wavefront errors between the two DMs depend on rotation, shift and magnification mis-registrations. When controlling the first 50 Zernike modes, residuals stay at or below 3% for all rotation angles and pupil shifts up to half a DM pitch, and small magnification errors contribute little. The results support using the low-order DM to compensate NCPA and deliver a flat wavefront to the pyramid WFS.
