# Dynamic Calibration of Adaptive Optics Systems: A System Identification Approach

**Chiuso et al. (2010)** · *IEEE Trans. Control Syst. Technol. 18, 705* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/224608457_Dynamic_Calibration_of_Adaptive_Optics_Systems_A_System_Identification_Approach) · DOI: [10.1109/TCST.2009.2023914](https://doi.org/10.1109/TCST.2009.2023914)

## Summary

Reports work on building accurate dynamic models of the actuation (deformable mirror) and sensing (wavefront sensor) of an adaptive optics system. State-of-the-art system identification and model-reduction techniques are adapted to the AO problem. The results use real data collected under various operating conditions on an ESO demonstrator then operating at Paranal.
