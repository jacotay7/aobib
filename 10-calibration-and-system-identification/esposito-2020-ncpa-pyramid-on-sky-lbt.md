# On-sky correction of non-common path aberration with the pyramid wavefront sensor

**Esposito et al. (2020)** · *A&A 636, A88* · [Free full text](https://nrc-publications.canada.ca/eng/view/ft/?id=50360676-5949-40da-9bef-1dcb0fa7bf80) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201937033](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201937033)

## Summary

NCPA are usually corrected by adding static offsets to the wavefront-sensor signals, which assumes a linear sensor described by a fixed interaction matrix; this does not hold for the pyramid WFS, whose response changes with correction quality. The paper presents a method to operate a pyramid (or other non-linear) WFS in closed loop with a wavefront offset that stays stable as star brightness, seeing and wind speed vary. The method is detailed analytically and numerically and tested at the LBT, in daytime and on sky, with the FLAO system and the LUCI2 instrument. On-sky results show NCPA fully nulled and diffraction-limited images with about 70% Strehl ratio in H band under variable seeing.
