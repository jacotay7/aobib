# ESCAPE: Efficient Synthesis of Calibrations for Adaptive optics through Pseudo-synthetic and Empirical methods

**Taylor et al. (2024)** · *Proc. SPIE 13097 (Adaptive Optics Systems IX)* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.06974)

## Summary

Describes the calibration strategy used to commission the refurbished convex adaptive secondary mirror of the 6.5 m MMT within the MAPS upgrade, where no calibration source can illuminate both the ASM and the wavefront sensor. ESCAPE combines the DO-CRIME on-sky interaction-matrix measurement with the SPRINT approach for computing pseudo-synthetic calibration matrices, continuously regenerating them from empirical feedback in open or closed loop to follow quasi-static system changes. Measuring the interaction matrix in the background during closed-loop operation also provides an estimate of the pyramid WFS optical gains. The paper gives the mathematical basis, testbed validation and preliminary on-sky results at MMT.
