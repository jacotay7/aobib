# A new calibration strategy for adaptive telescopes with pyramid WFS

**Héritier et al. (2018)** · *MNRAS 481, 2829* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.04848) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/sty2485](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/sty2485)

## Summary

Adaptive telescopes with large deformable mirrors inside the telescope usually lack an artificial source for interaction-matrix measurement, and DM/WFS mis-registrations evolve during operation. The paper investigates pseudo-synthetic calibration, in which mis-registration parameters are estimated and injected into a synthetic model to generate noise-free interaction matrices, for the pyramid-WFS AO systems of the Large Binocular Telescope. A synthetic model of the LBT AO system is developed, and a procedure to fit mis-registration parameters to an experimental interaction matrix is introduced. An interaction matrix generated from the model was tested on the real system in high-order mode and gave slightly better performance than the measured one.
