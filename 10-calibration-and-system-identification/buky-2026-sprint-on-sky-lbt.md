# DM/WFS mis-registration tracking: Implementation and on-sky validation of SPRINT at LBT

**Buky et al. (2026)** · *Proc. SPIE 14150 (Adaptive Optics Systems X)* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27941) · DOI: [10.1117/12.3104960](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3104960)

## Summary

Reports the implementation of the SPRINT mis-registration tracking method in the real-time computer of the Large Binocular Telescope AO system. Telescopes with integrated deformable mirrors see their DM/WFS alignment evolve during operation, and untracked mis-registrations can cause loop instability. The paper presents daytime and on-sky tests showing that rotation, shift and magnification mis-registrations can be recovered during observations. SPRINT is noted as the baseline approach for several ELT instruments.
