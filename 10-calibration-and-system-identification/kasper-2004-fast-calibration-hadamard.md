# Fast calibration of high-order adaptive optics systems

**Kasper et al. (2004)** · *JOSA A 21, 1004* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/8516534_Fast_calibration_of_high-order_adaptive_optics_systems) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.21.001004](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.21.001004)

## Summary

Presents a calibration method for adaptive optics systems that greatly reduces the time needed to measure the interaction matrix, or equivalently improves its signal-to-noise ratio. Actuators are pushed in optimized Hadamard patterns rather than one at a time, and for a given wavefront-sensor noise level the required time does not grow with the number of actuators. The authors highlight its value for high-order systems and for adaptive secondary mirrors on telescopes with no Gregorian focus, where calibration must use a natural star and atmospheric turbulence adds measurement noise.
