# DO-CRIME: Dynamic On-sky Covariance Random Interaction Matrix Evaluation, a novel method for calibrating adaptive optics systems

**Lai et al. (2021)** · *MNRAS 501, 3443* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.14705) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/staa3874](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/staa3874)

## Summary

Adaptive secondary mirrors on Cassegrain telescopes offer no focus for an artificial calibration source, so their interaction matrices must be obtained on sky, usually by adjusting a synthetic model parametrically (shift, magnification, rotation). This paper proposes measuring the interaction matrix ab initio from the AO telemetry stream by applying random patterns with diagonal command covariance on the deformable mirror and correlating them with the wavefront-sensor measurements. The method is developed for the adaptive secondary upgrade of the 'imaka wide-field AO system on the UH 2.2 m telescope and is shown to work on sky with the existing 'imaka testbed.
