# Optical calibration and performance of the adaptive secondary mirror at the Magellan telescope

**Briguglio et al. (2018)** · *Scientific Reports 8, 10835* · [Free full text](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-018-29171-6) · DOI: [10.1038/s41598-018-29171-6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-29171-6)

## Summary

Describes the optical calibration of the adaptive secondary mirror of the Magellan AO system (MagAO) on the 6.5-m Clay telescope, carried out within the MagAO-2K upgrade. The authors developed procedures to obtain high signal-to-noise interferometric measurements of the mirror modes despite dome convection and telescope vibrations, which were used to build an improved control matrix with up to 500 modes. An updated slaving algorithm improves control of actuators vignetted by the central obscuration, and a new flattening command reaches 22 nm RMS surface error. The paper reports the optical quality of the MagAO mirror for the first time and shows on-sky performance obtained with the new calibrations.
