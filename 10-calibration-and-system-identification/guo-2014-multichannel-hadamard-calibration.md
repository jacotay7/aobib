# Multichannel-Hadamard calibration of high-order adaptive optics systems

**Guo et al. (2014)** · *Optics Express 22, 13792* · [Free full text](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.22.013792) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.22.013792](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.22.013792)

## Summary

Presents the multichannel-Hadamard method for measuring the interaction matrix of high-order AO systems. The deformable-mirror actuators are first divided into channels according to their coupling, and a voltage-oriented Hadamard scheme is then applied to these channels. The procedure is detailed for a 595-element AO system, and the optimal channel division is studied by numerical simulation. Experiments comparing it with Hadamard-only and multichannel-only schemes show that the combined method improves interaction-matrix measurement.
