# Focal-plane Cn2(h) profiling based on single-conjugate adaptive optics compensated images

**Beltramo-Martin et al. (2018)** · *MNRAS 481, 2349* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.10712) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/sty2399](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/sty2399)

## Summary

Anisoplanatic PSF modelling for wide-field science with single-conjugate AO requires the Cn2(h) profile, which is not accessible from SCAO telemetry and which external profilers measure with discrepancies above 10%. The paper presents Focal Plane Profiling (FPP), which retrieves Cn2(h) from post-AO focal-plane images of stars in the field. It shows that FPP can reach a 1% level of accuracy on the profile and studies how accuracy depends on calibration-star magnitude and position. On a Keck baseline, photometry and astrometry errors due to PSF mis-modelling reach 1% and 50 microarcsec with a single H=14 star at 20 arcsec, and the concept is validated with images from the NRC-Herzberg HeNOS testbed.
