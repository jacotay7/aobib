# Atmospheric turbulence profiling using multiple laser star wavefront sensors

**Cortés et al. (2012)** · *MNRAS 427, 2089* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.1999) · DOI: [10.1111/j.1365-2966.2012.22076.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2966.2012.22076.x)

## Summary

Describes data preprocessing, SLODAR analysis and wind-profiling techniques for GeMS, the Gemini MCAO system. Slopes from the five laser-guide-star Shack-Hartmann sensors are combined with the commands of the three deformable mirrors to reconstruct pseudo-open-loop slopes, from which turbulence profiles are derived. The SLODAR method is adapted to closed-loop, multi-LGS operation and characterises up to 16 layers between 0 and 19 km, and wind profiling provides layer speed and direction as well as a cross-check of the SLODAR results. Results from 2011 observations are presented, along with issues such as Rayleigh fratricide and centroid gain variations.
