# Vibrations modelling at LBTO utilizing telemetry data

**Coronel et al. (2019)** · *Proc. AO4ELT6 (Québec, 2019)* · [Free full text](https://ao4elt6.copl.ulaval.ca/proceedings/401-VFLS-251.pdf)

## Summary

Non-turbulent perturbations in modern telescopes arise from equipment such as fans and cooling pumps and from wind- or tracking-induced mechanical vibrations, and mitigating them requires accurate models. The paper models vibrations as a sum of continuous-time damped oscillators, equivalent to discrete-time second-order autoregressive models, and estimates their parameters from sampled data by maximum likelihood. The method is applied to telemetry from the Large Binocular Telescope Observatory to estimate the vibration frequency peaks.
