# The calibration procedure of the LINC-NIRVANA ground and high layer WFS

**Arcidiacono et al. (2018)** · *Proc. SPIE 10703, 107034R* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.01657) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2313152](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2313152)

## Summary

Describes the calibration of the ground-layer and high-layer wavefront sensors of LINC-NIRVANA, a layer-oriented MCAO module at the Large Binocular Telescope with two twin channels. Field derotation is mechanical for the ground-layer sensors and optical (K-mirror) for the high-layer sensors, which rotates the pupil images on the sensors and therefore changes the projection of the deformable mirrors onto them. To handle this, the soft real-time computer loads the interaction matrix corresponding to the current rotation in one-degree steps. The calibrations were performed in daytime only, using optical fibre sources.
