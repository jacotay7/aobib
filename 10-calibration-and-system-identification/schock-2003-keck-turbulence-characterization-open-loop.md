# Atmospheric turbulence characterization with the Keck adaptive optics systems. I. Open-loop data

**Schöck et al. (2003)** · *Applied Optics 42, 3705* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/10655153_Atmospheric_turbulence_characterization_with_the_Keck_adaptive_optics_systems_I_Open-loop_data) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.42.003705](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.42.003705)

## Summary

Investigates methods for measuring atmospheric turbulence with the adaptive optics systems of the W. M. Keck Observatory using open-loop wavefront-sensor data. The main difficulties are separating instrumental from atmospheric effects and calibrating the devices accurately, so the paper concentrates on practical analysis issues. Two methods, differential-image-motion structure functions and Zernike decomposition of the wavefront, give values of r0 in excellent agreement with long-exposure images, with wavefront-sensor calibration as the main source of error. Outer-scale estimates agree between methods and with values found at other sites, of the order of tens of metres.
