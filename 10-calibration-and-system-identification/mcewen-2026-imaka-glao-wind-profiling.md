# Wavefront Profiling via correlation of GLAO open loop telemetry

**McEwen et al. (2026)** · *arXiv:2608.10786* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10786)

## Summary

Applies a wind-profiling method to open-loop telemetry from the 'imaka ground-layer AO demonstrator on the University of Hawaii 2.2-m telescope, identifying turbulent-layer velocities from spatio-temporal cross-correlations between multiple wavefront sensors. The derived layer velocities are compared with nearby anemometer data and with meteorological model predictions of upper-level winds. The strengths and limits of the profiler are tested by injecting simulated layers into real telemetry and checking their recovery. Results from four characteristic observing runs in winter and summer are reported, including the fraction of data giving viable estimates, and the authors discuss applying the technique to other multi-conjugate AO systems.
