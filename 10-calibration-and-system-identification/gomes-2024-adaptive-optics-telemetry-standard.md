# Adaptive Optics Telemetry Standard: Design and specification of a novel data exchange format

**Gomes et al. (2024)** · *A&A 686, A7* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.08300) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202348486](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202348486)

## Summary

Introduces the Adaptive Optics Telemetry (AOT) format, a FITS-based standard for exchanging AO telemetry from visible/infrared ground-based observatories to support large-scale telemetry mining and analysis. The format is designed around two use cases, atmospheric turbulence parameter estimation and PSF reconstruction, and covers natural and laser guide-star, single- and multi-WFS systems with Shack-Hartmann or pyramid sensors. Data fields, types, units and dimensions are fully specified. A Python package for conversion, reading, writing and exploration of AOT files is released, and the format is demonstrated with data from five instruments on different telescopes.
