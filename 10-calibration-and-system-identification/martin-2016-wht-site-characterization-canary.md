# William Herschel Telescope site characterization using the MOAO pathfinder CANARY on-sky data

**Martin et al. (2016)** · *Proc. SPIE 9909, 99093P* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.06313) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2231437](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2231437)

## Summary

Tomographic AO requires the statistical turbulence parameters, which CANARY retrieves from its own telemetry with the Learn & Apply algorithm by model-fitting the WFS measurement covariance matrix as a function of the Cn2 profile, outer-scale profile and system mis-registration. The paper explores an upgrade, Learn 3 Steps (L3S), which separates the identification of altitude layers from the ground layer to mitigate slow convergence of the empirical covariance matrices. For nominal conditions L3S reaches the same tomographic error with five times fewer frames than Learn & Apply. Applied to CANARY data from five nights in September 2013, it gives a median total seeing of 0.67 arcsec, outer scale of 8.9 m and wind speed of 3.07 m/s, and characterises layers above 20 km missed by the tomographic reconstruction.
