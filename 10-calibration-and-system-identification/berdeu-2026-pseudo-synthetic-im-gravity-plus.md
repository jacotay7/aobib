# Pseudo-Synthetic Interaction Matrix for the GRAVITY+ Adaptive Optics system: modeling and calibration of the ALPAO deformable mirror influence functions and coupling

**Berdeu et al. (2026)** · *Proc. SPIE (Adaptive Optics Systems X)* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-05702155) · DOI: [10.1117/12.3100827](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3100827)

## Summary

The four GRAVITY+ AO systems on the VLT share a 43x43 ALPAO deformable mirror (1432 actuators) whose resolution exceeds the sampling of all Shack-Hartmann sensing modes, so an accurate forward model of the DM is needed to build pseudo-synthetic interaction matrices that are recomputed as the system rotates. The paper describes how a super-resolved two-dimensional model of the mean influence function is derived from the manufacturer's low-resolution measurements. It then explains how the actuator coupling matrix is calibrated on the system using the VLT internal beacon. The calibrated coupling allows nearly pure low-order modes to be produced in open loop on the DM.
