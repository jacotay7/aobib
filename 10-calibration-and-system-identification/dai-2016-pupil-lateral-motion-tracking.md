# Experiments of two pupil lateral motion tracking algorithms using a Shack-Hartmann sensor

**Dai et al. (2016)** · *Journal of Modern Optics 64, 127* · [Free full text](https://hal-obspm.ccsd.cnrs.fr/obspm-02268044) · DOI: [10.1080/09500340.2016.1212415](https://doi.org/10.1080/09500340.2016.1212415)

## Summary

Pupil stability limits the performance and operational stability of AO systems. The paper analyses two methods to measure lateral pupil shift: one uses the fluxes in the outer-edge sub-apertures of a Shack-Hartmann sensor, the other uses the real-time interaction matrix of the AO system. Both are verified experimentally with a 9x9 Shack-Hartmann sensor. After two correction steps, the residual pupil shift is reduced to less than 5% of a sub-aperture with either algorithm.
