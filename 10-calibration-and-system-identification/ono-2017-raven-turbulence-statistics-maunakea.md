# Statistics of turbulence parameters at Maunakea using the multiple wavefront sensor data of RAVEN

**Ono et al. (2017)** · *MNRAS 465, 4931* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.05049) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stw3083](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stw3083)

## Summary

Presents statistics of Cn2 and outer-scale vertical profiles at Maunakea estimated with a SLODAR method from on-sky telemetry of RAVEN, the MOAO demonstrator at the Subaru telescope. Profiles are obtained by fitting theoretical auto- and cross-correlations of multiple Shack-Hartmann measurements to the observed ones with a Levenberg-Marquardt algorithm, for which analytic derivatives of the phase structure function are developed. The median total seeing is 0.46 arcsec with 54.3% of Cn2 in the ground layer, and the median outer scale is 25.5 m, increasing with altitude. Results agree with the literature except for the ground layer.
