# Self-calibration of adaptive optics systems. Application to the THEMIS solar telescope

**Béchet et al. (2026)** · *arXiv:2608.29593 (submitted to A&A)* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29593)

## Summary

Treats interaction-matrix calibration as the estimation of an affine model linking deformable-mirror commands to wavefront-sensor measurements, fitted in the weighted least-squares sense to data acquired with random probe commands. Several estimators are derived in closed form, fitting either the raw WFS data or differences between successive frames, together with expressions for their mean squared errors. Forgetting factors give simple recurrence rules so the model can be learned continuously at the AO loop rate, on an internal source or on sky in open or closed loop. Applied to telemetry from the AO system of the THEMIS solar telescope, the simple-differences estimator gives the lowest error and is simpler to implement than the widely used push-pull method. A shorter conference version of the same work appeared in Proc. SPIE 14150, 141502N (2026).
