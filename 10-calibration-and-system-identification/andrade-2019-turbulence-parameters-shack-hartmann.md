# Estimation of atmospheric turbulence parameters from Shack-Hartmann wavefront sensor measurements

**Andrade et al. (2019)** · *MNRAS 483, 1192* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.08396) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/sty3181](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/sty3181)

## Summary

Studies the estimation of the Fried parameter and outer scale from AO telemetry by fitting the variances of Zernike coefficients reconstructed from Shack-Hartmann measurements. The non-orthogonality of Zernike derivatives introduces modal cross-coupling and the finite sensor resolution introduces aliasing, both of which bias the variances. Simulations show cross-coupling is the dominant bias, and an iterative algorithm is presented to overcome it. For outer scales of 4-32 m and SNR of 10 or more on the variances, both parameters are recovered with sub-percent accuracy.
