# SPRINT for WFAO systems

**Agapito et al. (2024)** · *Proc. SPIE 13097, 130975P* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.15336) · DOI: [10.1117/12.3018656](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3018656)

## Summary

Extends the SPRINT mis-registration identification strategy from single-conjugate AO to wide-field AO systems with several wavefront sensors and deformable mirrors. In such systems more elements can move relative to each other, and the sub-aperture/actuator registration of an altitude-conjugated DM depends on pupil size, conjugation, guide-star position and field rotation. The paper investigates whether all parameters should be identified simultaneously or in successive steps working on one DM at a time.
