# SPRINT: System Parameters Recurrent INvasive Tracking, a fast and least-cost online calibration strategy for adaptive optics

**Héritier et al. (2021)** · *MNRAS 504, 4274* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.11261) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stab1177](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stab1177)

## Summary

Presents SPRINT, a strategy to track DM/WFS mis-registrations during operation of AO systems with pre-focal deformable mirrors, such as those of the ELT. Signals are acquired on sky by invasively dithering a few modes on the DM, before or during science operation, and fed to a pseudo-synthetic model of the AO system to estimate the mis-registration parameters. The authors introduce a method to compute the modes most sensitive to each mis-registration, which reduces the number of signals required and the impact on the science path. Simulations show accurate parameter estimation with only a few well-chosen signals, robustness to noise and turbulence conditions, and similar behaviour for pyramid and Shack-Hartmann WFS.
