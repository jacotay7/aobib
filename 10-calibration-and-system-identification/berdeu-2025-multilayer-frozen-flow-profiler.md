# Multi-layer frozen profiler from single conjugated adaptive optics telemetry: novel approach and potential applications

**Berdeu et al. (2025)** · *Proc. AO4ELT8 (Viña del Mar, 2025)* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-05361919) · DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.21104487](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21104487)

## Summary

Presents a turbulence profiler that works on single-conjugate AO telemetry, demonstrated with the GRAVITY+ AO systems on the VLT. Assuming the atmosphere is a mixture of frozen-flow layers, but without assuming their structure function, the method jointly retrieves the wind speed, direction and Cn2 weight of the main turbulent layers; layer heights remain unknown because SCAO lacks stereoscopic information. The model fits standard Paranal conditions with up to eight independent layers and can refine the real-time atmospheric monitor (r0, tau0, seeing, residual variances). The authors conclude a single frozen-flow layer is usually unrealistic and suggest applications such as debiasing telemetry-based mis-registration estimators and piston reconstruction for interferometry.
