# Quantifying telescope phase discontinuities external to adaptive optics systems by use of phase diversity and focal plane sharpening

**Lamb et al. (2017)** · *JATIS 3, 039001* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-01678522) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.3.3.039001](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.3.3.039001)

## Summary

Proposes and applies two focal-plane methods, phase diversity and a form of image sharpening, to estimate pupil-plane phase discontinuities that the AO system does not sense. For the VLT, the simulated low wind effect affecting SPHERE is estimated with both methods, which are shown to be complementary, and single-image phase diversity using the natural defocus of the SPHERE differential tip-tilt sensor imager is also demonstrated. Phase diversity estimates the low wind effect to within 30 nm RMS, within the tolerance for a 1e-6 SPHERE contrast target. For Keck, 153 nm RMS of simulated segment piston is estimated to within 29 nm RMS from a single defocused NIRC2 image, and correcting it would raise the Strehl ratio by 12%.
