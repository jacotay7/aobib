# Estimation of the lateral mis-registrations of the GRAVITY+ adaptive optics system. Perturbative method with open-loop modal correlation and non-perturbative method with temporal correlation of closed-loop telemetry

**Berdeu et al. (2024)** · *A&A 687, A157* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.07423) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202449311](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202449311)

## Summary

The GRAVITY+ AO systems have moving components between the deformable mirrors and wavefront sensors, requiring lateral mis-registrations to be monitored and corrected during operation. For acquisition, a perturbative method applies two-dimensional modes and correlates the response with a reference interaction matrix, estimating large lateral errors in open loop in a single fit with sub-subaperture resolution. In closed loop, a non-perturbative method searches the telemetry for temporal-correlation patterns produced by noise propagation through the loop; it is not absolute but drives the system towards negligible mis-registration with limited turbulence bias. Both methods are validated in simulation and on the GRAVITY+ development bench with Shack-Hartmann sensors.
