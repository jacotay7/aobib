# Determining the interaction matrix using starlight

**Wildi & Brusa (2004)** · *Proc. SPIE 5490 (Advancements in Adaptive Optics), 164* · [Free full text](https://web.archive.org/web/20260307212821/https://www.astro.unige.ch/~wildif/publications/5490-102.pdf) · DOI: [10.1117/12.552280](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.552280)

## Summary

Proposes measuring the interaction matrix of an adaptive optics system on the telescope using natural guide stars, motivated by the MMT adaptive secondary mirror. With a Cassegrain adaptive secondary, the usual approach of calibrating with an artificial source is impossible once the mirror is mounted, and the MMT matrix had been measured in the lab with a different optical setup; the authors suggest this may be why image quality could not be improved by using more than the original 52 modes. They present the on-sky measurement method and simulations showing it can reach a high signal-to-noise ratio. The linked copy is the authors' PDF as archived by the Internet Archive, since the original page has moved.
