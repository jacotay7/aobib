# Integrated turbulence parameters' estimation from NAOMI adaptive optics telemetry data

**Morujão et al. (2023)** · *A&A 678, A193* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.15178) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202346952](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202346952)

## Summary

Tests whether turbulence parameters can be estimated from the low-resolution wavefront sensors of NAOMI, the AO modules of the four VLTI Auxiliary Telescopes. Seeing is estimated with a modified and optimised algorithm that fits measured modal variances to theoretical von Karman variances by chi-squared minimisation, while simultaneously estimating the remaining (uncorrected) and measurement errors. A Monte Carlo method is proposed to estimate the statistical uncertainty. On simulated data the seeing is recovered to per-cent accuracy with a 20 s horizon, and on-sky data from 2018-2020 give a median seeing of 0.76 arcsec, with no dependence on the Auxiliary Telescope position on the platform.
