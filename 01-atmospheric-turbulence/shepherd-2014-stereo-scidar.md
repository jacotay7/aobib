# Stereo-SCIDAR: optical turbulence profiling with high sensitivity using a modified SCIDAR instrument

**Shepherd et al. (2014)** · *MNRAS 437, 3568* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.3465) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stt2150](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stt2150)

## Summary

Proposes Stereo-SCIDAR, a generalised SCIDAR variant in which the scintillation pattern of each star of a double-star target is imaged on a separate detector. Separating the pupil images increases tolerance to magnitude difference, removes the normalisation errors that require recalibration in classical SCIDAR, raises the signal-to-noise ratio of the cross-covariance by at least a factor of two (up to 16 for a 3-magnitude difference) and eases real-time wind-profile reconstruction. Response functions are computed and performance is assessed with Monte Carlo simulation. The technique is demonstrated with data from the 2.5-m Nordic Optical Telescope and the 1.0-m Jacobus Kapteyn Telescope on La Palma.
