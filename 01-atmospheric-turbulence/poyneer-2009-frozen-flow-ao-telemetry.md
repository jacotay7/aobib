# Experimental verification of the frozen flow atmospheric turbulence assumption with use of astronomical adaptive optics telemetry

**Poyneer et al. (2009)** · *JOSA A 26, 833* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/24251883_Experimental_verification_of_the_frozen_flow_atmospheric_turbulence_assumption_with_use_of_astronomical_adaptive_optics_telemetry) · DOI: [10.1364/JOSAA.26.000833](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSAA.26.000833)

## Summary

Tests whether atmospheric turbulence follows the frozen-flow (Taylor) hypothesis, using closed-loop deformable-mirror telemetry from the Altair system at Gemini North and the Keck AO system. The detection algorithms come from the predictive Fourier control framework and look for translating layers in the temporal behaviour of Fourier modes. Frozen flow is detected more than 94% of the time, usually as one to three layers carrying 20–40% of the controllable phase power. Layer velocities vary by less than 0.5 m/s per axis over 10 s intervals, which the authors interpret as stable enough for predictive control to identify and track the conditions.
