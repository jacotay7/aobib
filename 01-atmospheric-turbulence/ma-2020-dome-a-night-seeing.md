# Night-time measurements of astronomical seeing at Dome A in Antarctica

**Ma et al. (2020)** · *Nature 583, 771* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.15365) · DOI: [10.1038/s41586-020-2489-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2489-0)

## Summary

Reports the first direct night-time seeing measurements at Dome A, obtained with a Differential Image Motion Monitor at a height of 8 m. Seeing as low as 0.13 arcsec was recorded, with statistics comparable to those at 20 m height at Dome C. The boundary layer was below 8 m 31% of the time, when the median seeing was 0.31 arcsec, consistent with free-atmosphere seeing. Seeing and boundary-layer thickness correlate strongly with the near-surface temperature gradient, consistent with a median boundary-layer thickness of about 14 m.
