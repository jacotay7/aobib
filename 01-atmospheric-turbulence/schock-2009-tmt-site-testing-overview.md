# Thirty Meter Telescope Site Testing I: Overview

**Schöck et al. (2009)** · *PASP 121, 384* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/0904.1183) · DOI: [10.1086/599287](https://doi.org/10.1086/599287)

## Summary

Overview of the Thirty Meter Telescope site-testing programme, which over five years measured atmospheric properties at five candidate sites: Cerros Tolar, Armazones and Tolonchar in northern Chile, San Pedro Mártir in Mexico and the 13 North site on Mauna Kea. The paper describes the candidate selection and the instrumentation deployed. Site testing concluded with Armazones and Mauna Kea 13N retained for further consideration. The authors state that all five candidates are excellent sites and none is the single obvious choice. This is the first paper of the TMT site-testing series.
