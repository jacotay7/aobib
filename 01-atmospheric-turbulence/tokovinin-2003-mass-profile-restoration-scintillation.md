# Restoration of turbulence profile from scintillation indices

**Tokovinin et al. (2003)** · *MNRAS 343, 891* · [Free full text](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/343/3/891/1123465) · DOI: [10.1046/j.1365-8711.2003.06731.x](https://doi.org/10.1046/j.1365-8711.2003.06731.x)

## Summary

Presents the algorithm used by the Multi-Aperture Scintillation Sensor (MASS) to measure atmospheric turbulence by statistical analysis of stellar light-flux fluctuations. Scintillation indices are computed in four concentric ring apertures and their pairwise combinations, and are fitted to a model with a small number of turbulent layers, either at fixed or at floating altitudes. The weighting functions, noise properties and optimisation of the aperture geometry are derived, and the method is tested with simulations and real data. The authors report a vertical resolution of about Δh/h ~ 0.5, layer-intensity errors near 10% of the integrated intensity, and measurement of the free-atmosphere seeing and isoplanatic angle to within a few per cent.
