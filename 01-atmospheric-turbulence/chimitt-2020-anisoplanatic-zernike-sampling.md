# Simulating anisoplanatic turbulence by sampling intermodal and spatially correlated Zernike coefficients

**Chimitt & Chan (2020)** · *Optical Engineering 59, 083101* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.11210) · DOI: [10.1117/1.OE.59.8.083101](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.OE.59.8.083101)

## Summary

Presents a propagation-free method for simulating imaging through anisoplanatic turbulence by drawing Zernike coefficients that are correlated both between modes and across the field. The authors establish the equivalence between the angle-of-arrival correlation of Basu, McCrae and Fiorino (2015) and the multi-aperture correlation of Chanan (1992), which defines the covariance matrix from which coefficients are sampled. Fast and scalable sampling strategies are proposed, turning the wave-propagation problem into a sampling problem. The simulator is reported to agree with theory and with real turbulence data while being much faster than split-step propagation simulators.
