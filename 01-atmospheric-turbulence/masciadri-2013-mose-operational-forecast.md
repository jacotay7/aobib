# MOSE: operational forecast of the optical turbulence and atmospheric parameters at European Southern Observatory ground-based sites - I. Overview and vertical stratification of atmospheric parameters at 0-20 km

**Masciadri et al. (2013)** · *MNRAS 436, 1968* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.6775) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stt1708](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stt1708)

## Summary

Overview of the MOSE (MOdeling ESO Sites) project, which assessed the feasibility of forecasting classical atmospheric parameters (wind, temperature, humidity) and optical turbulence (Cn2 profiles, seeing, isoplanatic angle, coherence time) above Cerro Paranal and Cerro Armazones. This first paper evaluates how well the Meso-NH mesoscale model reconstructs the vertical stratification of atmospheric parameters over the first 20 km. The authors report good model performance, especially for wind speed, and argue that such forecasts, available hours in advance, can support AO operations and the estimation of the coherence time.
