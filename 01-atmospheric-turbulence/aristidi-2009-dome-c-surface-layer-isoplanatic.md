# Dome C site testing: surface layer, free atmosphere seeing, and isoplanatic angle statistics

**Aristidi et al. (2009)** · *A&A 499, 955* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/0904.2982) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/200810953](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/200810953)

## Summary

Analyses 3.5 years of site-testing data from three DIMMs at different heights at Dome C. Statistics of seeing and isoplanatic angle are given, along with their characteristic fluctuation time of about 30 minutes at 8 m. Using the three DIMMs as a surface-layer profiler, the layer is found to have a sharp upper boundary with a median height of 23-27 m, and the free-atmosphere seeing has a median of 0.36 arcsec. Outside the summer season the surface layer contributes about 95% of the total turbulence.
