# Numerical modeling of atmospherically perturbed phase screens: new solutions for classical fast Fourier transform and Zernike methods

**Carbillet & Riccardi (2010)** · *Applied Optics 49, G47* · [Free full text](https://www-n.oca.eu/carbillet/enseignement/M2-Optique/cours-1/Carbillet+Riccardi-wfgeneration.pdf) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.49.000G47](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.49.000G47)

## Summary

Addresses known weaknesses of the two classical methods for generating atmospheric phase screens. FFT-based screens lack low spatial frequencies; adding subharmonics is a known fix, but no criterion existed for how many are needed, so the authors define two criteria and show they work in practice. Zernike-based screens behave poorly at high spatial frequencies because of numerical instability, which the authors overcome with an algorithm based on an alternative definition of the Zernike polynomials using the recurrence relations of Jacobi polynomials. The methods are implemented in the freely distributed CAOS software package.
