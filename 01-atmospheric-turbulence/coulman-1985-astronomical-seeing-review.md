# Fundamental and Applied Aspects of Astronomical "Seeing"

**Coulman (1985)** · *ARA&A 23, 19* · [Free full text](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1985ARA%26A..23...19C) · DOI: [10.1146/annurev.aa.23.090185.000315](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev.aa.23.090185.000315)

## Summary

Review article in Annual Review of Astronomy and Astrophysics covering the state of knowledge on astronomical seeing up to 1982. It gives a historical account and treats both the optics and the meteorology of seeing. It also covers the calculation of system performance from microthermal data, optical methods for measuring seeing, and techniques for minimizing the image-degrading effects of the atmosphere. An extensive bibliography gives access to a literature that is otherwise scattered across astronomy, optics, radio science and meteorology.
