# Simulation of Time-dependent Karhunen-Loeve Phase Screens: an Ergodic Approach

**Mathar (2025)** · *arXiv:2510.12861* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.12861)

## Summary

Proposes an alternative to the frozen-screen approximation for time-dependent phase screens. Instead of dragging a pupil across a large static screen, the structure function is constructed in a three-dimensional volume (a sphere, for computational efficiency), two-dimensional planar cuts are taken through it, and the cut is moved along its normal to produce a time sequence. The manuscript develops the linear algebra for populating the volume with phase statistics of the von Karman model using a Karhunen-Loeve expansion.
