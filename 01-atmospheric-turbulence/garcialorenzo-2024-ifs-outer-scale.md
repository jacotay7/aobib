# Integral field spectroscopy supports atmospheric optics to reveal the finite outer scale of the turbulence

**García-Lorenzo et al. (2024)** · *A&A 687, A40* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.19337) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/202348364](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202348364)

## Summary

Introduces a technique to estimate the wavefront outer scale L0 from seeing-limited integral field spectroscopy by measuring how image quality varies with wavelength across synthetic band images. The measured variation is compared with Tokovinin's (2002) analytical expression combining seeing and L0. Applied to MUSE Wide Field Mode observations at Paranal, the method validates that expression empirically, while remaining discrepancies are discussed in terms of seeing uncertainties and dome-induced turbulence. The paper also outlines how L0 and dome seeing can be obtained as by-products of routine IFS observations.
