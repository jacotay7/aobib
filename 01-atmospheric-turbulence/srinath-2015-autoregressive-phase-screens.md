# Computationally efficient autoregressive method for generating phase screens with frozen flow and turbulence in optical simulations

**Srinath et al. (2015)** · *Optics Express 23, 33335* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.05424) · DOI: [10.1364/OE.23.033335](https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.23.033335)

## Summary

Presents a sample-based autoregressive method for generating and evolving atmospheric phase screens, with a single parameter per Fourier mode controlling the balance between frozen-flow and stochastic ('boiling') components. The method addresses limitations of Fourier-based screens such as periodicity and missing low-spatial-frequency power. AO simulations fed with AR screens show markedly increased closed-loop residual temporal power for small amounts of added stochastic content, compared with pure translating screens. Preliminary fits to AO telemetry suggest the AR model represents real conditions better than pure frozen flow.
