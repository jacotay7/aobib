# Simultaneous Measurements of the Fried Parameter r_0 and the Isoplanatic Angle theta_0 using SCIDAR and Adaptive Optics - First Results

**Weiss et al. (2002)** · *ASP Conf. Ser. 266 (Astronomical Site Evaluation in the Visible and Radio Range), 86* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0101474)

## Summary

Reports a simultaneous turbulence measurement campaign at the Calar Alto 1.23-m and 3.5-m telescopes, combining a SCIDAR instrument with the ALFA adaptive optics system. The Fried parameter r0 and the isoplanatic angle theta0 were derived from both instruments, with SCIDAR also providing Cn2 profiles, and ALFA data taken in closed and open loop. The paper compares the values obtained from the two systems at several times over three nights.
