# Characterization of the ground layer of turbulence at Paranal using a robotic SLODAR system

**Butterley et al. (2020)** · *MNRAS 492, 934* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.04751) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stz3498](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stz3498)

## Summary

Describes a robotic SLODAR instrument at Cerro Paranal that measures the vertical profile of turbulence in 8 resolution elements up to 100-500 m altitude. Statistics from 875 nights between 2014 and 2018 show a highly variable ground layer, with most of its strength in the median case concentrated in the first 50 m. SLODAR seeing agrees with the Paranal DIMM and with seeing derived from the VLT UT1 active-optics Shack-Hartmann sensor after accounting for instrument heights. Full correction of the turbulence between 10 m and 500 m would improve the median seeing from 0.689 to 0.481 arcsec at 500 nm.
