# Multiwavelength active-optics Shack-Hartmann sensor for monitoring seeing and turbulence outer scale

**Martinez (2014)** · *A&A 572, A14* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.4911) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201424476](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201424476)

## Summary

Studies how multiwavelength long-exposure images can be used to derive the seeing and turbulence outer scale simultaneously from the FWHM of seeing-limited images taken at a telescope focus. Such estimates differ from those of external site monitors because of pointing, height above ground and dome contributions. The paper proposes a modified active-optics Shack-Hartmann sensor that records spot PSFs at several wavelengths, so that real-time r0 and L0 can be obtained at the telescope focus independently of field stabilisation. The concept is discussed in the context of extremely large telescope design.
