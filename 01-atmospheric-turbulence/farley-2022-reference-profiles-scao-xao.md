# Reference optical turbulence and wind profiles for single conjugate and extreme adaptive optics

**Farley (2022)** · *MNRAS 517, 2966* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.14931) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stac2880](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stac2880)

## Summary

Presents a method to select a small number of reference turbulence and wind profiles from a large dataset for single-conjugate and extreme AO simulations, assuming that performance correlates with r0, theta0 and tau0. Profiles are chosen so that they jointly follow the distributions of these integrated parameters. The equivalent-layers compression method is extended to wind profiles. Applied to Stereo-SCIDAR data from Paranal, five profiles are extracted, and analytical AO simulation shows that they span a corresponding range of corrected Strehl ratios.
