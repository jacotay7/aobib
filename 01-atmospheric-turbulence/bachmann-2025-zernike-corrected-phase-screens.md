# Accurate Zernike-corrected phase screens for arbitrary power spectra

**Bachmann et al. (2025)** · *Optical Engineering 64, 058102* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.04826) · DOI: [10.1117/1.OE.64.5.058102](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.OE.64.5.058102)

## Summary

Addresses the accuracy of phase screens used in split-step solutions of the stochastic parabolic equation for wave propagation through random media. The authors introduce hybrid phase screens that combine Zernike and Fourier components to represent arbitrary refractive-index power spectra, including the low spatial frequencies poorly sampled by FFT screens. The screens are benchmarked for accuracy and efficiency against established methods.
