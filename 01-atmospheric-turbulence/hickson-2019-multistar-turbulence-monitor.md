# Multistar turbulence monitor: a new technique to measure optical turbulence profiles

**Hickson et al. (2019)** · *MNRAS 485, 2532* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.04307) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stz568](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stz568)

## Summary

Describes a technique that derives turbulence profiles from sequences of short-exposure images of a star field taken with a small telescope. Differential motion between all pairs of star images gives longitudinal and transverse tilt structure functions over a range of angular separations, which are fitted to turbulence-model predictions using Markov-chain Monte Carlo optimisation. The method estimates the lower-atmosphere profile, total and free-atmosphere seeing and the outer scale. It is verified with Monte Carlo simulations and illustrated with data from the AST3-2 telescope at Dome A, Antarctica.
