# Optical turbulence forecast for ground-based astronomy and free-space optical communication

**Masciadri et al. (2025)** · *Proc. SPIE 13619, 136190E* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.16845) · DOI: [10.1117/12.3060357](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3060357)

## Summary

Summarises the state of the art of optical-turbulence forecasting research by the authors' group, placed in the international context. Earlier work concentrated on whether optical turbulence and its vertical distribution could be predicted; current efforts address the accuracy achievable at different time scales, the efficiency of forecasting methods and the contribution of statistical techniques such as auto-regression and machine learning. The contribution also discusses applications to free-space optical communication alongside ground-based astronomy.
