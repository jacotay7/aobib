# Chromatic Anisoplanatism in Adaptive Optics: A detailed comparison of theory with numerical simulations

**Femenía-Castellá & Devaney (2026)** · *arXiv:2609.06693* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06693)

## Summary

Validates the theory of chromatic anisoplanatism, the error arising because atmospheric refraction makes rays at the sensing and science wavelengths traverse different paths, using geometric-optics Monte Carlo simulations. An 8-layer approximation of the median Cerro Armazones profile with Kolmogorov and von Karman statistics is used for pupils from 4.2 to 39 m, sensing at 589 nm and observing at 1.25 microns. Simulated phase variances match theory, with a slight overestimate by theory in the von Karman case; Strehl losses exceed 30% at zenith angles of 70 degrees or more. The large set of realisations shows strongly non-Gaussian distributions of phase variance and Strehl ratio with long tails.
