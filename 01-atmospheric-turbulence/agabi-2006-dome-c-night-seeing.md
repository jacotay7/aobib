# First Whole Atmosphere Nighttime Seeing Measurements at Dome C, Antarctica

**Agabi et al. (2006)** · *PASP 118, 344* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0510418) · DOI: [10.1086/498728](https://doi.org/10.1086/498728)

## Summary

Reports night-time site-testing results from the first winter-over at Concordia station, Dome C, based on seeing and isoplanatic-angle monitoring and in-situ balloon soundings of Cn2 profiles. The atmosphere is divided into a 36-m surface layer responsible for 87% of the turbulence and a very stable free atmosphere above it with a median seeing of 0.36 +/- 0.19 arcsec at 30 m elevation. A DIMM on an 8.5-m tower measured a median seeing of 1.3 +/- 0.8 arcsec, reflecting the strong surface layer.
