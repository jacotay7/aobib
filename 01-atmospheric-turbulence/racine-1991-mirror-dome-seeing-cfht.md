# Mirror, dome, and natural seeing at CFHT

**Racine et al. (1991)** · *PASP 103, 1020* · [Free full text](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1991PASP..103.1020R) · DOI: [10.1086/132920](https://doi.org/10.1086/132920)

## Summary

Correlates a large, homogeneous set of HRCam image-quality measurements at the CFHT prime focus with thermal sensor data to identify and quantify local seeing. Mirror seeing is found to be an important contributor, amounting to FWHM = 0.40 arcsec per (degree C)^(6/5) when the primary mirror is warmer than the dome air. Dome seeing is marginally significant at about 0.1 arcsec per (degree C)^(6/5) of inside-outside temperature difference, and optical aberrations of the primary and HRCam limit image quality to 0.38 arcsec. The median natural seeing on Mauna Kea at the CFHT site is found to be 0.43 +/- 0.05 arcsec.
