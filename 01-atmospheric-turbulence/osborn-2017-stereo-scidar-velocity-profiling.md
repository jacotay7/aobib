# Turbulence velocity profiling for high sensitivity and vertical-resolution atmospheric characterization with Stereo-SCIDAR

**Osborn et al. (2017)** · *MNRAS 464, 3998* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.02624) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stw2685](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stw2685)

## Summary

Compares turbulence velocity profiles from the Stereo-SCIDAR on the 2.5-m Isaac Newton Telescope (La Palma) with Global Forecast System model winds and nearby radiosondes, in order to validate the instrument's automated turbulence velocity identification. The turbulence velocities are found to be consistent with wind velocities. The paper shows that velocity information can increase the sensitivity of Cn2 profiles, since weak layers can be detected through their velocity vectors, and can refine layer altitudes beyond the native resolution. Examples of complex velocity structure within layers caused by wind shear are also presented.
