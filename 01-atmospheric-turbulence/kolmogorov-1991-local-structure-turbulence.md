# The local structure of turbulence in incompressible viscous fluid for very large Reynolds numbers

**Kolmogorov (1991)** · *Proc. R. Soc. Lond. A 434, 9* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/265443806_The_local_structure_of_turbulence_in_incompressible_viscous_fluid_for_very_large_Reynolds_numbers) · DOI: [10.1098/rspa.1991.0075](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.1991.0075)

## Summary

English translation, published in a 1991 Royal Society issue marking fifty years of Kolmogorov's turbulence theory, of his 1941 paper in Doklady Akademii Nauk SSSR. Treating velocity components as random fields, it introduces the hypotheses of local homogeneity and isotropy at very large Reynolds numbers and the similarity hypotheses, under which small-scale statistics depend only on the energy dissipation rate and the viscosity. A consequence is that, in the inertial range, the velocity structure function grows as the two-thirds power of separation. This scaling underlies the Kolmogorov refractive-index and phase statistics (the 5/3 power law for phase) used throughout adaptive optics.
