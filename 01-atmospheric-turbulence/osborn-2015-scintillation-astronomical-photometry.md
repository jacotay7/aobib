# Atmospheric scintillation in astronomical photometry

**Osborn et al. (2015)** · *MNRAS 452, 1707* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.06921) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stv1400](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stv1400)

## Summary

Describes stellar scintillation from its physical origin to its effect on high-precision photometry of bright targets. The authors show that Young's (1967) approximation tends to underestimate median scintillation noise at several major observatories, and that median or real-time turbulence profiles give better estimates. New equations for scintillation noise are provided, including for extremely large telescopes, where the noise is lower than previously assumed. They note that scintillation and shot noise share the same exposure-time dependence and that their ratio depends only weakly on telescope diameter.
