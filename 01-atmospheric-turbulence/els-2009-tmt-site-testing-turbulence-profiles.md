# Thirty Meter Telescope Site Testing VI: Turbulence Profiles

**Els et al. (2009)** · *PASP 121, 527* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/0904.1865) · DOI: [10.1086/599384](https://doi.org/10.1086/599384)

## Summary

Reports the vertical distribution of optical turbulence above the five TMT candidate sites, measured with MASS-DIMM profilers for at least two years and with acoustic SODARs. At all sites the lowest 200 m dominate the total seeing, with weaker ground layers at the Chilean sites. The two northern-hemisphere sites have weaker turbulence above 500 m, and Mauna Kea 13N has the weakest 16-km turbulence, explaining its large isoplanatic angle. The influence of the jet stream and near-ground wind, and seasonal and nocturnal variations, are discussed.
