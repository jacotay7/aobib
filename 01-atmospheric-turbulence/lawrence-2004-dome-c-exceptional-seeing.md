# Exceptional astronomical seeing conditions above Dome C in Antarctica

**Lawrence et al. (2004)** · *Nature 431, 278* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/8342956_Exceptional_astronomical_seeing_conditions_above_Dome_C_in_Antarctica) · DOI: [10.1038/nature02929](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature02929)

## Summary

Reports wintertime seeing measurements at Dome C on the Antarctic plateau, made with a MASS (multi-aperture scintillation sensor) and an acoustic SODAR. The median seeing is 0.27 arcsec, and below 0.15 arcsec 25% of the time, compared with 0.5–1.0 arcsec at the best mid-latitude sites and 1.8 arcsec at the South Pole. The authors estimate that a telescope at Dome C would compete with one 2 to 3 times larger at the best mid-latitude observatories, and discuss the implications for high-angular-resolution astronomy and adaptive optics from Antarctic sites. Later campaigns refined these results, in particular for the turbulent surface layer.
