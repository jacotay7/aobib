# Statistics of turbulence profile at Cerro Tololo

**Tokovinin et al. (2003)** · *MNRAS 340, 52* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0209432) · DOI: [10.1046/j.1365-8711.2003.06231.x](https://doi.org/10.1046/j.1365-8711.2003.06231.x)

## Summary

Reports three months of continuous monitoring of the turbulence profile and seeing at Cerro Tololo in 2002, with about 28000 low-resolution profiles from a new MASS single-star monitor and seeing from a DIMM. The median seeing was 0.95 arcsec, with 60% contributed by the first 500 m and a median free-atmosphere seeing of 0.55 arcsec. Turbulence above 12 km is always present, and a calm four-day period with 0.2-0.3 arcsec free-atmosphere seeing was observed. The paper estimates ground-layer AO gains, studies correlations with tropopause and ground winds, and characterises turbulence bursts lasting from 15 minutes near the ground to 1-2 hours at high altitude.
