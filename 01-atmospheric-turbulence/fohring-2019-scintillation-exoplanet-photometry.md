# Atmospheric scintillation noise in ground-based exoplanet photometry

**Föhring et al. (2019)** · *MNRAS 489, 5098* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.02004) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stz2444](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stz2444)

## Summary

Demonstrates the impact of atmospheric scintillation on ground-based exoplanet transit photometry through contemporaneous turbulence profiling and transit observations. A strong correlation is found between measured intensity variations and the scintillation predicted from optical turbulence profiles, showing that a profiler can model scintillation noise on another telescope at the same site. For median La Palma conditions scintillation dominates for targets brighter than about V=10.1 on a 0.5-m telescope and V=11.7 on a 4.2-m telescope. MCMC analysis quantifies the effect of scintillation on the uncertainties of derived planet parameters.
