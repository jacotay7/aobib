# Atmospheric coherence times in interferometry: definition and measurement

**Kellerer & Tokovinin (2007)** · *A&A 461, 775* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0610207) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361:20065788](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361:20065788)

## Summary

Addresses the diverse definitions of the atmospheric time constant t0 used in interferometry and adaptive optics. The authors derive a formula for the structure function of the fringe phase (piston) in a long-baseline interferometer and review existing techniques for measuring the time constant and their shortcomings. They show that the standard AO time constant, with minor modification, quantifies the piston coherence time, and express fringe-tracking residuals and visibility loss in terms of it. A new measurement method based on fast variations of defocus is proposed and shown in simulation to be feasible with a 35-cm telescope.
