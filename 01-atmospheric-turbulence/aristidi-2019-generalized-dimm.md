# A generalized differential image motion monitor

**Aristidi et al. (2019)** · *MNRAS 486, 915* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.07093) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stz854](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stz854)

## Summary

Presents the Generalized Differential Image Motion Monitor (GDIMM), a compact automatic instrument based on a 28-cm telescope with a three-hole entrance mask. It measures four turbulence parameters: seeing, isoplanatic angle, coherence time and wavefront coherence outer scale. The paper describes the instrument, data processing and error budget. Statistics from 3.5 years of continuous monitoring at the Calern Observatory (France) are reported.
