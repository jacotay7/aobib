# The ESO differential image motion monitor

**Sarazin & Roddier (1990)** · *A&A 227, 294* · [Free full text](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1990A%26A...227..294S)

## Summary

Reviews the theory of differential image motion measurements and describes the ESO Differential Image Motion Monitor (DIMM), which measures wavefront slope differences between two small sub-pupils and is therefore insensitive to telescope tracking errors. The relation between the variance of differential motion and the Fried parameter is derived from the angle-of-arrival covariance of Kolmogorov turbulence. Seeing estimates from the monitor are compared with simultaneous image-quality measurements on a large telescope and found to agree. The DIMM described here became a standard seeing monitor for site testing.
