# Monitoring daytime and nighttime optical turbulence profiles with the PML instrument

**Aristidi et al. (2020)** · *arXiv:2002.04947* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.04947)

## Summary

Reports recent developments of the Profiler of Moon Limb (PML), which uses fluctuations of the lunar or solar limb to retrieve the refractive-index structure constant profile Cn2(h) and the wavefront coherence outer scale profile L0(h). The atmosphere is divided into 33 layers with altitude resolution from 100 m near the ground to 2 km at high altitude, and profiles are produced every 3 minutes during both day and night. Results obtained at the Plateau de Calern (France) are presented.
