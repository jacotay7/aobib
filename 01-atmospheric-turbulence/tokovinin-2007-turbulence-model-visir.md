# Testing turbulence model at metric scales with mid-infrared VISIR images at the VLT

**Tokovinin et al. (2007)** · *MNRAS 378, 701* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/0704.0470) · DOI: [10.1111/j.1365-2966.2007.11803.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2966.2007.11803.x)

## Summary

Probes the structure of atmospheric turbulence from centimetre to metre scales using simultaneous mid-infrared (VISIR) and visible imaging at the VLT. The data depart significantly from the Kolmogorov model and are fitted by a von Karman model with an outer scale of order 30 m, with clear signs of phase structure function saturation across the 8-m aperture. Image quality is found to improve towards the infrared faster than the standard lambda^(-1/5) scaling, and the authors suggest that 30-m apertures could be diffraction-limited without adaptive optics at wavelengths longer than about 8 microns.
