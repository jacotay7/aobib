# Turbulence nowcast for the Cerro Paranal and Cerro Armazones observatory sites

**Milli et al. (2020)** · *Proc. SPIE 11448* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.05674) · DOI: [10.1117/12.2561364](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2561364)

## Summary

Explores an empirical, data-driven route to short-term forecasting of optical turbulence that exploits spatial diversity between two nearby sites: Cerro Paranal, host of the VLT, and Cerro Armazones, future host of the ELT. Using simultaneous turbulence measurements at the two sites, the study examines the correlation between the high-altitude turbulence measured above each of them. The work is part of ESO efforts to obtain short-term turbulence forecasts that support the scheduling of VLT observations and prepare ELT operations.
