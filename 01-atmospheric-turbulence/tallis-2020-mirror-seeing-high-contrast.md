# Effects of mirror seeing on high-contrast adaptive optics instruments

**Tallis et al. (2020)** · *JATIS 6, 015002* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.04649) · DOI: [10.1117/1.JATIS.6.1.015002](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JATIS.6.1.015002)

## Summary

Quantifies the effect of mirror seeing on the Gemini Planet Imager using science observations, AO telemetry and environmental data from the GPI Exoplanet Survey between 2014 and 2017. GPI performance is best when the primary mirror is in thermal equilibrium with the outside air. Spatial and temporal power spectral densities of Fourier modes show increased spatial PSD amplitude in the inertial range when M1 is out of equilibrium, and possible deviations from Kolmogorov and single-layer frozen-flow models. The paper concludes with an assessment of temperature control and ventilation at Gemini South.
