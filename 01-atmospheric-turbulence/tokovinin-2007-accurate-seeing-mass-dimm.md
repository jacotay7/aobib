# Accurate seeing measurements with MASS and DIMM

**Tokovinin & Kornilov (2007)** · *MNRAS 381, 1179* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/0708.0195) · DOI: [10.1111/j.1365-2966.2007.12307.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2966.2007.12307.x)

## Summary

Studies the absolute accuracy of seeing measurements with the Differential Image Motion Monitor (DIMM) and the Multi-Aperture Scintillation Sensor (MASS), which together allow the ground-layer seeing to be estimated from total and free-atmosphere seeing. The analysis combines analytical theory, numerical simulation and experiments. A modification of MASS processing to compensate for partially saturated scintillation is developed, and DIMM is shown to be potentially biased by optical aberrations such as defocus and by propagation effects. With biases controlled, both methods reach about 10% absolute accuracy.
