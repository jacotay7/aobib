# Optical turbulence profiling with Stereo-SCIDAR for VLT and ELT

**Osborn et al. (2018)** · *MNRAS 478, 825* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.02585) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/sty1070](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/sty1070)

## Summary

Reports results from the first 18 months of the Stereo-SCIDAR operated by Durham University and ESO at Cerro Paranal, about 20 km from the ELT site. From 83 nights over 22 months, the median seeing is 0.64 arcsec with half the turbulence below 2 km and 40% below 600 m; median coherence time and isoplanatic angle are 4.18 ms and 1.75 arcsec. Profiles of turbulence strength and velocity were compared with the surface-layer SLODAR, MASS-DIMM and the ECMWF forecast model, with correlation coefficients between 0.61 (isoplanatic angle) and 0.84 (seeing).
