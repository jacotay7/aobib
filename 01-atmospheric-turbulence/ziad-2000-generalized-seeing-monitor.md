# From the grating scale monitor to the generalized seeing monitor

**Ziad et al. (2000)** · *Applied Optics 39, 5415* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/5497001_From_the_grating_scale_monitor_to_the_generalized_seeing_monitor) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.39.005415](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.39.005415)

## Summary

Describes the generalized seeing monitor (GSM), a successor to the grating scale monitor developed at Nice University to estimate the turbulence outer scale L0 from the normalized covariance of angle-of-arrival fluctuations. The GSM uses four identical small-telescope modules measuring the angle of arrival at four points of the wavefront with millisecond time resolution. Spatio-temporal analysis of these signals gives the seeing, the outer scale and the wavefront speed, and scintillation gives the isoplanatic angle. The paper analyses the instrument's performance and accuracy and presents results from site-testing campaigns at La Silla, Oukaïmeden, Maidanak, Cerro Pachón and Cerro Paranal.
