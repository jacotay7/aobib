# FATE: an operational automatic system for optical turbulence forecasting at the Very Large Telescope

**Masciadri et al. (2024)** · *Proc. SPIE 13097, 130972T* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.05133) · DOI: [10.1117/12.3018041](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.3018041)

## Summary

Reports progress on FATE, an operational automatic system delivering forecasts of astroclimatic and atmospheric parameters to support service-mode science operations at the Very Large Telescope. The project was selected through an ESO open call for tender with defined technical specifications. The paper presents the goals of the service once integrated into VLT operations, current forecast performance and project status. FATE is intended to prepare the way for optical turbulence forecasting at the ELT.
