# Atmospheric scintillation at Dome C, Antarctica: implications for photometry and astrometry

**Kenyon et al. (2006)** · *PASP 118, 924* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0604538) · DOI: [10.1086/505409](https://doi.org/10.1086/505409)

## Summary

Presents low-resolution turbulence profiles above Dome C measured with a MASS instrument on 25 nights in March-May 2004. Apart from the lowest layer, Dome C shows much less turbulence than Cerro Tololo and Cerro Pachón, and the integrated turbulence at 16 km is always below the Chilean medians. From these profiles the authors compute scintillation noise for photometry and the atmospheric error in narrow-angle differential astrometry, estimating a gain of about 3.6 relative to the mid-latitude Chilean sites. The estimates are described as preliminary because average wind profiles were used.
