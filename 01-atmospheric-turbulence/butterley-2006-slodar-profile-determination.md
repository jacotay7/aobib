# Determination of the profile of atmospheric optical turbulence strength from SLODAR data

**Butterley et al. (2006)** · *MNRAS 369, 835* · [Free full text](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/369/2/835/3101559) · DOI: [10.1111/j.1365-2966.2006.10337.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2966.2006.10337.x)

## Summary

Develops the data analysis for SLODAR, in which the vertical profile of optical turbulence is measured from the cross-covariance of Shack-Hartmann wavefront slopes on the two stars of a double star. The theoretical response of the system to a thin turbulent layer is derived as a function of layer altitude and of the spectral power index of the phase aberrations. The turbulence profile is then estimated by fitting these impulse responses to the measured cross-covariances, and the statistical uncertainty and altitude resolution of the recovered profile are analysed. The paper also shows how the total integrated turbulence strength (seeing) can be obtained from the same data, giving the fraction of turbulence above the maximum sensed altitude, and accounts for centroid noise and the form of the structure function.
