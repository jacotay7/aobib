# LOLAS: an optical turbulence profiler in the atmospheric boundary layer with extreme altitude resolution

**Avila et al. (2008)** · *MNRAS 387, 1511* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/0805.4784) · DOI: [10.1111/j.1365-2966.2008.13386.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2966.2008.13386.x)

## Summary

Presents the Low Layer SCIDAR (LOLAS), an instrument for measuring optical-turbulence profiles in the atmospheric boundary layer with high altitude resolution. It applies the generalised SCIDAR concept on a dedicated 40-cm telescope observing widely separated double stars, giving profiles with 12-m resolution for a 200-arcsec pair. The system includes autoguiding, autofocus and image stabilisation algorithms. First results were obtained at Mauna Kea as part of the Gemini ground-layer turbulence monitoring campaign and are presented as a demonstration rather than a site characterisation.
