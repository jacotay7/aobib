# 01 · Atmospheric Turbulence & Site Characterization

Adaptive optics exists to correct the wavefront distortions produced by optical turbulence in the
Earth's atmosphere, so almost every AO design and performance estimate starts from a statistical
model of that turbulence: the Kolmogorov / von Karman phase statistics, the integrated parameters
(Fried parameter r0, isoplanatic angle theta0, coherence time tau0, outer scale L0), and the
vertical distribution of turbulence strength and wind speed, the Cn2(h) and V(h) profiles. This folder collects the
literature on how these quantities are described theoretically, how they are measured at
observatories, how they vary in space and time, and how they are simulated and forecast.

The folder covers turbulence theory and modal statistics, the outer scale, isoplanatism, temporal
behaviour and the frozen-flow hypothesis, and stellar scintillation. It also covers the instruments used to monitor seeing
and profile turbulence (DIMM, MASS, SCIDAR/Stereo-SCIDAR, SLODAR, lunar-limb and newer
small-telescope profilers), major site-testing campaigns (Paranal, TMT candidate sites, Antarctic
plateau, La Palma, Cerro Tololo), reference profiles for AO simulation, mesoscale and
general-circulation-model forecasting, phase-screen generation, and dome/mirror seeing. Many of
the classic theory papers (Fried, Noll, Hufnagel, Roddier 1981, Greenwood) are not
freely available and are therefore listed in the repository's paywalled list instead. Profiling from AO telemetry is
mostly in topic 10, and machine-learning forecasting is in topic 11; entries from those folders that are relevant here are cross-listed in the index below.

## Subtopics

- **Turbulence theory & phase statistics**: reviews of seeing, structure-function tests, Zernike/Karhunen-Loeve statistics
- **Outer scale**: measurements and evidence of a finite L0
- **Isoplanatism & anisoplanatism**: theta0 measurements, anisoplanatic and chromatic errors
- **Temporal statistics & frozen flow**: seeing variability, coherence time, layer velocities
- **Scintillation**: stellar intensity fluctuations and their impact on photometry
- **Cn2 profiling & seeing monitors**: DIMM, MASS, SCIDAR, SLODAR, PML, RINGSS, SHIMM and comparisons between them
- **Site testing campaigns**: TMT, Dome C / Dome A, Cerro Tololo, La Palma
- **Reference profiles for AO design**: compressing measured profiles for simulation
- **Turbulence forecasting**: Meso-NH (MOSE, FATE), ECMWF-based forecasts, nowcasting
- **Phase-screen simulation**: infinite, autoregressive, Zernike-corrected and time-dependent screens
- **Dome & local seeing**: mirror and dome seeing measurements and sensors
