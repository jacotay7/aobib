# The Effects of the Turbulent Atmosphere on Wave Propagation

**Tatarskii (1971)** · *Israel Program for Scientific Translations, Jerusalem (NTIS TT 68-50464)* · [Free full text](https://ntrl.ntis.gov/NTRL/dashboard/searchResults/titleDetail/TT6850464.xhtml)

## Summary

English translation, edited by J. W. Strohbehn, of Tatarskii's 1967 monograph on wave propagation in the turbulent atmosphere, supplemented with material from a 1970 preprint on the Markov-process approximation; it succeeds his 1959 monograph, translated in 1961 as *Wave Propagation in a Turbulent Medium*. The first chapter develops random fields with stationary increments and structure functions, the Kolmogorov theory of small-scale turbulence, and the microstructure of the temperature and refractive-index fields, together with experimental data on atmospheric turbulence. Later chapters treat scattering of electromagnetic and sound waves by turbulence and line-of-sight propagation of short waves using geometrical optics and the method of smooth perturbations (Rytov), giving phase and amplitude fluctuations and their structure functions for plane and spherical waves. Applications include aperture averaging, scintillation of extended sources, image motion in telescopes and experimental data on stellar scintillation, and a final chapter covers strong fluctuations with field-theoretical methods and the Markov approximation. The 488-page scanned NTIS report can be downloaded free from the National Technical Reports Library page.
