# Directly Characterizing Dome Seeing: Differential Image Motion Sensor Using Multisources (DIMSUM)

**Kurmus et al. (2023)** · *arXiv:2311.03692* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.03692)

## Summary

Introduces DIMSUM, a setup for directly characterising dome seeing by measuring differential image motion of multiple sources with strobed imaging that freezes index-of-refraction fluctuations on thermal time scales. The apparatus was installed in the Auxiliary Telescope of the Vera C. Rubin Observatory. Early results show a correlation between characteristic differential image motion and local temperature fluctuations. The authors present the results as preliminary and outline further tests.
