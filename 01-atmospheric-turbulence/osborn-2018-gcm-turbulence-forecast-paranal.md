# Atmospheric turbulence forecasting with a general circulation model for Cerro Paranal

**Osborn & Sarazin (2018)** · *MNRAS 480, 1278* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.08005) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/sty1898](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/sty1898)

## Summary

Compares turbulence profiles and integrated astro-meteorological parameters from a forecast model based on the ECMWF general circulation model with Stereo-SCIDAR measurements at Paranal. The model needs no spatial nesting and is therefore fast to run on the latest forecasts. It is statistically consistent with the measurements, with a correlation of 0.98 between median profiles, and contemporaneous free-atmosphere seeing, isoplanatic angle and coherence time correlate at 0.64, 0.40 and 0.63. Applications discussed include smart scheduling, remote site characterisation from forecast archives and optimisation of wide-field AO.
