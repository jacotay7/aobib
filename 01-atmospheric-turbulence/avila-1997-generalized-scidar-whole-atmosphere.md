# Whole atmospheric-turbulence profiling with generalized scidar

**Avila et al. (1997)** · *Applied Optics 36, 7898* · [Free full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/5588455_Whole_atmospheric-turbulence_profiling_with_generalized_scidar) · DOI: [10.1364/AO.36.007898](https://doi.org/10.1364/AO.36.007898)

## Summary

Reports the first on-sky implementation of the generalized SCIDAR technique, which had previously been proposed and tested in the laboratory. Classical SCIDAR analyses double-star scintillation in the telescope pupil and is insensitive to turbulence below a few kilometres; the generalized version analyses the scintillation in a plane conjugated below the pupil so that low-altitude layers also produce a measurable signal. The instrument was used on the Nordic Optical Telescope and the William Herschel Telescope at La Palma to measure vertical profiles of the refractive-index structure constant Cn2(h). The authors measure the spatial autocorrelation of double-star scintillation for several positions of the analysis plane and report agreement with theoretical expectations.
