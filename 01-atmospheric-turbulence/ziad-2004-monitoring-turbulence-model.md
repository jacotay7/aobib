# Towards the monitoring of atmospheric turbulence model

**Ziad et al. (2004)** · *A&A 414, L33* · [Free full text](https://hal.science/hal-00419019) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361:20031770](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361:20031770)

## Summary

Proposes a method to test atmospheric turbulence models by reconstructing the phase structure function from simultaneous measurements of the longitudinal angle-of-arrival covariance at several baselines with the Generalized Seeing Monitor (GSM). The same technique gives the first estimates of the outer scale from GSM data that do not rely on an assumed turbulence model. Preliminary reconstructed structure functions are presented and compared with the theoretical predictions of the commonly used turbulence models (e.g. Kolmogorov and von Karman).
