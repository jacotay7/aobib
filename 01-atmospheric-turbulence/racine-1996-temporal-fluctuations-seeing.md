# Temporal Fluctuations of Atmospheric Seeing

**Racine (1996)** · *PASP 108, 372* · [Free full text](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1996PASP..108..372R) · DOI: [10.1086/133732](https://doi.org/10.1086/133732)

## Summary

Examines the amplitude and characteristic time of seeing fluctuations produced by the free atmosphere using 414 SCIDAR Cn2 profiles obtained on Mauna Kea. The mean ratio of two seeing values separated by a time interval grows with an e-folding time of about 17 minutes and saturates at about 1.6. The paper discusses implications for the design and use of seeing-responsive facilities, including adaptive optics and queue-scheduled observing.
