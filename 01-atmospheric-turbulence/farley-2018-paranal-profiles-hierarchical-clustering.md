# Representative optical turbulence profiles for ESO Paranal by hierarchical clustering

**Farley et al. (2018)** · *MNRAS 481, 4030* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.07551) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/sty2536](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/sty2536)

## Summary

Addresses how to compress large turbulence-profile datasets into small sets of profiles usable in Monte Carlo AO simulations while minimising information loss. Hierarchical clustering is used to partition the profiles according to their structure, and one profile is extracted from each cluster. Applied to more than 10000 Stereo-SCIDAR profiles from 83 nights at ESO Paranal, two extraction methods each yield 18 profiles describing the dataset's variability. The authors show that this variability is difficult to capture with small sets of profiles defined by integrated parameters such as seeing.
