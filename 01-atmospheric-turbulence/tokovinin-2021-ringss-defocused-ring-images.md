# Measurement of turbulence profile from defocused ring images

**Tokovinin (2021)** · *MNRAS 502, 794* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.13734) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/staa4049](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/staa4049)

## Summary

Presents the concept of the Ring-Image Next Generation Scintillation Sensor (RINGSS), a turbulence monitor that analyses sequences of defocused, ring-like images of a bright star recorded with a small telescope. Statistics of intensity fluctuations in the ring and of its radial motion yield a low-resolution turbulence profile, the total seeing and the atmospheric time constant. The processing algorithm is developed and tested by numerical simulation, with corrections for finite exposure time and partially saturated scintillation. A 0.13-m prototype was tested on the sky; the approach is proposed as a portable monitor and as an upgrade for existing seeing monitors.
