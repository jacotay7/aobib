# From Differential Image Motion to Seeing

**Tokovinin (2002)** · *PASP 114, 1156* · [Free full text](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1086/342683/pdf) · DOI: [10.1086/342683](https://doi.org/10.1086/342683)

## Summary

Reviews and extends the theory of the differential image motion monitor (DIMM) and gives more accurate coefficients for computing the Fried parameter from the measured variance of differential image motion. Numerical simulations show that a DIMM measures Zernike tilts rather than image centroids, as is usually assumed. The biases caused by CCD readout noise and by finite exposure time are modelled, and correction of the exposure-time bias by interlacing short and long exposures is validated with real turbulence and wind profiles. The paper also shows that a finite turbulence outer scale reduces the image size in large telescopes by 10% or more relative to standard theory, and provides formulae for the FWHM and half-energy diameter of the atmospheric PSF including the outer scale.
