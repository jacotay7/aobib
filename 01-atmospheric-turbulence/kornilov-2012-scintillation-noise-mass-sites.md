# Comparison of the scintillation noise above different observatories measured with MASS instruments

**Kornilov et al. (2012)** · *A&A 546, A41* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1208.3824) · DOI: [10.1051/0004-6361/201219954](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201219954)

## Summary

Uses stellar scintillation data collected with MASS instruments at 11 astronomical sites to estimate the scintillation noise of large telescopes for both fast photometry and long exposures. The noise is mostly set by turbulence and wind in the upper atmosphere and is comparable at all sites, slightly lower at Mauna Kea and highest at Tolonchar. The classical Young formula is found to underestimate scintillation noise. Temporal variations on 1-2 hour scales and seasonal trends are described, with some correlation between nearby observatories.
