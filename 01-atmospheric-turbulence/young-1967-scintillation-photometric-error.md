# Photometric error analysis. VI. Confirmation of Reiger's theory of scintillation

**Young (1967)** · *AJ 72, 747* · [Free full text](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1967AJ.....72..747Y) · DOI: [10.1086/110303](https://doi.org/10.1086/110303)

## Summary

Confirms Reiger's (1963) theory of stellar scintillation with additional observations and discusses its implications for photoelectric photometry. The scintillation amplitude is found to be nearly independent of wavelength, proportional to the 3/2 power of airmass and, at low frequencies, to the -2/3 power of telescope aperture. The scintillation power spectrum behaves as a smeared version of the telescope diffraction pattern, so a central obstruction increases high-frequency scintillation noise. The resulting scintillation-noise approximation ('Young's formula') has been widely used to estimate photometric noise.
