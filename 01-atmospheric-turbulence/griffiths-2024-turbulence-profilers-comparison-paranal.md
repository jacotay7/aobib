# A comparison of next-generation turbulence profiling instruments at Paranal

**Griffiths et al. (2024)** · *MNRAS 529, 320* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.09144) · DOI: [10.1093/mnras/stae434](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stae434)

## Summary

Reports a six-night campaign at Cerro Paranal in 2023 comparing two new site monitors, RINGSS and the 24-hour Shack-Hartmann Image Motion Monitor (24hSHIMM), with the Stereo-SCIDAR and a MASS-DIMM. Integrated parameters are compared through ratios, bias, unbiased RMS error and correlation; seeing, free-atmosphere seeing and coherence time agree well, while the isoplanatic angle correlates less although medians agree. Profiles from the small-telescope instruments are compared with the 100-layer Stereo-SCIDAR profiles, with RINGSS and SHIMM found to improve on MASS-DIMM. First continuous day-night monitoring at Paranal shows a strong diurnal seeing cycle, with a median daytime seeing of 2.65 arcsec.
