# Nowcasting the turbulence at the Paranal Observatory

**Milli et al. (2019)** · *arXiv:1910.13767* · [Free full text](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.13767)

## Summary

Investigates forecasting optical turbulence conditions (seeing, coherence time and ground-layer fraction) at Paranal over the next two hours, termed turbulence nowcasting, to support short-term scheduling of VLT service-mode observations. The study uses historical data from the Paranal Astronomical Site Monitoring combined with ancillary data in a machine-learning framework. Strengths and shortcomings of the approach are discussed, together with perspectives for the Extremely Large Telescope.
