# European Extremely Large Telescope Site Characterization I: Overview

**Vernin et al. (2011)** · *PASP 123, 1334* · [Free full text](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1086/662995/pdf) · DOI: [10.1086/662995](https://doi.org/10.1086/662995)

## Summary

Overview of the atmospheric characterization of candidate sites for the European Extremely Large Telescope, carried out under the European Union's Sixth Framework Programme. Candidate sites in Chile, Argentina, Morocco and the Canary Islands were monitored with a common set of standard instruments and procedures, including seeing and turbulence-profile monitors and meteorological stations. The paper describes the campaigns and methods and summarises the results for the parameters considered, in the context of the eventual selection of Cerro Armazones near Paranal. Two companion papers cover the adaptive-optics-related parameters and the climatology in more detail.
