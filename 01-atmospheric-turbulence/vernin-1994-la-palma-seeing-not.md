# Optical seeing at La Palma Observatory. II. Intensive site testing campaign at the Nordic Optical Telescope

**Vernin & Muñoz-Tuñón (1994)** · *A&A 284, 311* · [Free full text](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1994A%26A...284..311V)

## Summary

Presents an intensive four-night site-testing campaign at the Observatorio del Roque de los Muchachos (La Palma) in July 1990 combining SCIDAR at the Nordic Optical Telescope focus, free-flying balloons and an instrumented mast. On three of four nights the image size was better than 0.7 arcsec, with a mean SCIDAR seeing of 0.76 arcsec, and the surface-layer contribution was small. Comparison of focal and whole-atmosphere seeing indicates that NOT dome and mirror seeing do not significantly degrade images. Balloon soundings provide speckle lifetime, isoplanatic angle (usually below 2 arcsec) and precipitable water vapour, and show few well-defined turbulent layers.
